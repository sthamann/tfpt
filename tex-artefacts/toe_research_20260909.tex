\subsection*{1. A charged field from the original filled microscopic sea}
Fix the original complex, number-conserving QWZ strip at width eight,
mass one and holonomy sector $r=1$. Its one-particle space has dimension
$16N$ over $\mathbb C$; it is not the compiler's sixteen real Majoranas.
Write $P_N$ for its negative-energy projector of rank $8N$ and
$\Omega_N$ for the actually filled sea. Set
\[
 D_N=\frac{N}{2\pi}h_N,\qquad
 \mathcal H_N=d\Gamma(D_N)-\operatorname{Tr}(P_ND_N),\qquad
 Q_N=\mathcal N-8N.
\]
Here $\mathcal H_N\geq0$ on the full finite Fock space, including charged
sectors. For a smooth periodic function $g$ use the physical top row,
not an auxiliary charge register:
\[
 (R_Ng)(x,y)=N^{-1/2}e^{-i\pi x/(2N)}g(x/N)
       \delta_{y,7}\chi_+,\qquad \Psi_N(g)=a_N(R_Ng),
 \quad \chi_+=2^{-1/2}(1,1)^T.
\]
These fields obey exact CAR, have charge $-1$, and are supported on the
sampled support of $g$. Their adjoints have charge $+1$.
For $k=j-\tfrac14$, $p=2\pi k/N$, $\rho=1-\cos p$ and the normalized
top-row mode $r_{N,j}$, the original strip action gives
\[
 \|D_Nr_{N,j}\|\leq |k|,\qquad
 \|(D_N+k)r_{N,j}\|
 \leq\frac{\pi k^2}{N}+\frac{2\pi^2|k|^3}{3N^2}.
\]
Indeed $h(p)\chi_+$ on the top row has the two orthogonal components
$-\sin p\,\chi_+$ and $\rho\chi_-$; its norm is
$2|\sin(p/2)|$. This is a bound on the actual microscopic action,
not a fitted edge dispersion.

The geometric edge quasi-mode has amplitude proportional to
$\rho^{7-y}\chi_+$ and residual $\rho^8$. For
$|j|\leq M_N=\min(\lfloor N/8\rfloor,\lfloor\sqrt N\rfloor)$,
projection onto $P_N$ for
$j\geq1$ and onto $1-P_N$ for $j\leq0$ produces an isometry $J_N$ with
$P_NJ_N=J_NP_{\rm cur}$ exactly and
$\|J_Ne_j-r_{N,j}\|\leq4\rho_j$.
The discarded occupation of the raw top-row vector is at most
$9\rho_j^2$, hence $O(N^{-4})$ for each fixed label.
If $g=\sum_j f_je^{2\pi ijx}$, the locality-to-polarization comparison is
\[
 \|R_Ng-J_N\Pi_{M_N}f\|
 \leq4\rho_*\|\Pi_{M_N}f\|_2+
             \sum_{|j|>M_N}|f_j|\longrightarrow0.
\]
The last term is an $\ell^1$ alias tail; replacing it by an unqualified
$\ell^2$ tail would not justify sampled locality.

Second quantization relative to the filled sea maps the target vacuum,
particles and holes into the same source Fock space. With
$a_j=b_j$ for $j\leq0$ and $a_j=d_j^*$ for $j\geq1$, the target is
\[
 H=\sum_j |j-\tfrac14|\,n_j^{\rm exc},\qquad
 Q=\sum_{j\leq0}n_j^b-\sum_{j\geq1}n_j^d.
\]
The resulting contractions intertwine both field adjoints and charge in
the limit on the common finite-energy core. Orthogonality of momentum
residuals bounds the $k$-excitation generator error by $k$ times the
one-particle error, not by the $8N$ sea occupation. Since each excitation
costs at least $1/4$, $k\leq\lfloor4E\rfloor$ on an energy-$E$ core.
For $S_m=\sum_j|j-\tfrac14|^m|f_j|$ one also obtains the uniform bounds
\[
 \|\Psi_N(g)\|\leq S_0,\quad
 \|[\mathcal H_N,\Psi_N(g)]\|\leq S_1,\quad
 \text{time-transport error}\leq
 |t|\left(\frac{\pi S_2}{N}+\frac{2\pi^2 S_3}{3N^2}\right),
\]
with transported test function
$g_t(x)=e^{it/4}g(x-t/(2\pi))$ in the source convention.
All fixed smooth finite field words and their vacuum moments follow by
bounded-product convergence. The top-edge charge-sector minima are
\[
 E_{\rm top}(q)=q^2/2-q/4,\qquad q\in\mathbb Z.
\]
They are not minima over both edges of the whole cylinder.
The microscopic holonomy term has not been removed to fit a target.

\subsection*{2. Neutral multipoint limits, without changing the source}
For the same strip and filled projector, the alternating four-word
$U_a^*U_bU_c^*U_d$ cancels the outer history ramps. Normal ordering is
the actual finite-sea trace subtraction. Positive heat regularization,
Jensen and Cauchy--Schwarz give uniform $L^p$ bounds for $1<p<2$ on the
full four-endpoint torus, including collisions. They promote the
separated-point limit to an $L^1$ limit.
At physical $\delta=4N^{-3/4}$ an auxiliary Fourier window of order
$N^{1/4}\log N$ improves the normalized grid error to
$O(N^{-3/4}\log^3N)$; the interpolation contribution is
$O(N^{-5/8})$. This changes only the comparison window, not the physical
source. Ordered analytic branches retain, for example, the quarter-circle
phase $e^{i\pi/8}$. Pair-Gram positivity uses the corresponding reversed
pair ordering; it is not a proof of Osterwalder--Schrader positivity.
The even-word grid estimate for length $L<28$ has bound
$O(N^{(L-28)/32}\log^3N)$; higher collision control is not thereby proved.
