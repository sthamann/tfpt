\paragraph{The common reference.}
Fix $L\ge3$, $N=L^3$, $a,m>0$, $u,\nu\ge0$, $J\ge0$, a time step
$\delta>0$, $T\ge3$ and $\beta=T\delta$. Every site's charge remains
in $\mathbb Z^8$. The original signed hopping and potential are
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 H_{\rm hop}&=\kappa I-JA_{\rm sign},&\quad \kappa&=48NJ,\\
 D(n)&=\frac1N\sum_x e(n_x)+\nu\sum_{\langle xy\rangle}e(n_x-n_y),
 &e(p)&=\tfrac12p^TGp,\\
 V(n,\phi)&=D(n)+\tfrac12\phi^T A(n)\phi,
 &A(n)&=-\Delta_a+m^2+2u\,\operatorname{diag}e(n_x).
 \end{aligned}
\]
The $48N$ directed elementary shifts include their original phases.
Thus $0\le H_{\rm hop}\le2\kappa I$. With $K_\phi=\sum_x\pi_x^2/2$,
write the same symmetric primitive transfer as
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 S=e^{-\delta K_\phi/2}e^{-\delta V/2},\qquad
 Q=S^*e^{-\delta H_{\rm hop}}S,\qquad Q_D=S^*S,\qquad C=SS^*,\\
 Z=\operatorname{Tr}Q^T,\qquad
 Z_D=\operatorname{Tr}Q_D^T=\operatorname{Tr}C^T,\qquad
 \theta=\beta\kappa.
 \end{gathered}
\]
All traces in the next theorem may be restricted to any fixed total-charge
sector. Scalar mass and charge coercivity give $0<Z_D<\infty$.
Crucially, $Z_D$ includes \emph{all} constant charge profiles and their
charge-dependent scalar determinants, not just the zero profile.

\paragraph{Full-reference theorem and proof.}
Let $Z_{\rm abs}$ be the sum of moduli before cancellation, resolved into
elementary hopping words, initial charge profiles and scalar integrals.
Then
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{e^{-\theta}Z_D\ \le\ Z\ \le\ Z_{\rm abs}\ \le\ Z_D,
 \qquad \frac{Z_{\rm abs}}{Z}\le e^{\theta}.}
 \label{eq:r27-reference}
\end{equation}
Every elementary hop changes the complete profile, hence
$\langle n|A_{\rm sign}|n\rangle=0$. Scalar spectral Jensen gives
$p_n=\langle n|e^{-\delta H_{\rm hop}}|n\rangle\ge e^{-\delta\kappa}$.
Pinching $Q$ into its charge blocks $P_nQP_n=p_nQ_D(n)$ contracts the
Schatten $T$ norm, so
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}Q^T\ge\sum_n\operatorname{Tr}(P_nQP_n)^T
 \ge e^{-\theta}\sum_n\operatorname{Tr}Q_D(n)^T.
\]
For a finite partition, pinching is an average of unitary conjugations;
triangle inequality proves contraction. For the countably infinite carrier,
use a finite set of charge blocks and its complement, discard the latter's
nonnegative trace contribution, then increase the set monotonically.
This is a trace-power statement, not the generally false operator
inequality $e^{-\delta\kappa}Q_D\le Q$.

For the absolute bound replace phases by bare translations only in the
majorant. Each unsigned slice word $U_j$ is a sector-preserving unitary,
and $C$ has nonnegative scalar integral kernels. Schatten H\"older yields
\[
 0\le\operatorname{Tr}(U_1C\cdots U_TC)
 \le\prod_{j=1}^T\|U_jC\|_T=Z_D.
\]
The left side equals the sum of moduli of the corresponding original
profile-resolved weights, not the modulus of an already cancelled trace.
At total order $k$, the slice allocations and channel choices have combined
weight $e^{-\theta}\theta^k/k!$. Their positive sum is finite and equals
one, proving the absolute bound and justifying Tonelli followed by the
signed rearrangement. As a second upper comparison, the unsigned hopping
transfer is a positive contraction, so its partition is at most $Z_D$.
These arguments concern the finite regulator, not a limit interchange.

The norm tools are standard; see
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0601063}{Perez-Garcia, Wolf, Petz and Ruskai}
and \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0204049}{Hansen and Pedersen}.
The full-reference application above removes the extra charge heat-trace
factor of Round~26 without changing the original model. It still permits
exponential growth $e^{48\beta NJ}$ and is not an efficient sign cure.

\paragraph{Three explicit error budgets.}
The exact Gram inverse satisfies $\|G^{-1}\|_\infty=31$, so
$D(n)\ge a_c\|n\|^2$, $a_c=1/(62N)$. On the neutral sector define
\[
 \Theta(s)=\operatorname{Tr}_{\rm neutral}e^{-sD}
 \le\left(1+\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{s a_c}}\right)^{8N}.
\]
Let $K$ bound all charge coordinates at \emph{sampled slice boundaries}
and $P$ bound total elementary hopping order. If $P+2>\theta$,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \rho_{\rm charge}&\le T e^\theta
   e^{-\delta a_c(K+1)^2/2}\Theta(\beta/2)^{1/T},\\
 \rho_{\rm hops}&\le
  \frac{\theta^{P+1}}{(P+1)!}\frac1{1-\theta/(P+2)}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
For the first estimate, insert the boundary projection $R_K$ in the
joint trace. It commutes with $C$, not generally with hopping. H\"older
gives $Z_Dp_D(K)^{1/T}$, with $p_D=\operatorname{Tr}(R_KC^T)/Z_D$.
In the neutral sector $Z_D\ge Z_{\phi,0}$ and scalar Gaussian comparison
gives $p_D\le e^{-\beta a_c(K+1)^2/2}\Theta(\beta/2)$; then take the
$T$th root and the union over boundaries. No intermediate state inside
a retained hopping word is discarded using this boundary estimate.

For a quadratic memory truncation with operator error $\epsilon_R$,
retain the full eliminated determinant. If
$\eta_R=\epsilon_R/(\delta m^2)<1$, Round~25/26 comparison gives
\[
 B_R=\frac{\epsilon_R |S_{\rm ret}|Tg_m(0)}{2(1-\eta_R)},
 \qquad \rho_{\rm memory}\le e^\theta(e^{B_R}-1).
\]
Here $g_m(0)$ is the exact diagonal inverse of the temporal mass operator,
or a certified upper bound. Restrict histories first and approximate the
retained scalar factors second. Then
$\rho=\rho_{\rm charge}+\rho_{\rm hops}+\rho_{\rm memory}$ bounds the
relative partition error. If $\rho<1$, the approximate partition is positive;
bounded one-time selfadjoint charge observables of norm at most one have
normalized error at most $2\rho/(1-\rho)$. Arbitrary signed multi-time
insertions and reflection positivity of truncated weights are not covered.

\paragraph{Reproducible certificates and actual remaining cost.}
The planner uses integers and fractions, with upward roots,
$e^\theta\le3^{\lceil\theta\rceil}$,
$e^{-d}\le2^{-\lfloor d\rfloor}$ and
$e^B-1\le B/(1-B)$ for $0\le B<1$.
At $N=27,T=3,\beta=1,J=1/10$, full memory and a $1\%$ omission target,
the published Round~26 $K=4096,P=962$ improve to $K=2670,P=354$.
Applying the same first-passing-integer search to the old inequalities
gives $K=4035,P=956$: the gain is not just a finer search grid.

A genuine nonzero-memory example uses $L=9$, cells of side $3$, $a=m=1$,
arbitrary $u,\nu\ge0$, $T=33$, $\delta=3/4$, $\beta=99/4$ and
$J=1/866052$, hence $\theta=1$. There are $513$ retained sites and
$27$ interiors of $8$ sites. The cell gap is $4$, giving exactly
$q_b=1/4$. A temporal comparison mass squared $8/9\le1$ gives exactly
$q_0=1/2$. The cyclic tail certificate at $R=12$ retains $25$ of the
$33$ quadratic time offsets and bounds the summed memory error by
$0.2045\%$. With $K=12235,P=5$ the three omission budgets sum to less
than $1\%$. The full determinant remains a full-history function.
This is a declared weak-total-hopping example, not a fitted coupling.

\paragraph{The original sign obstruction and physical frontier remain.}
For overlapping original hops $A=W_{xy,e_2}$ and $B=W_{yz,e_3}$,
the word $ABA^*B^*$ closes with phase $-1$ because $e_2^TGe_3=1$.
Its actual hopping matrix-element product is $-J^4$ for $J>0$.
Diagonal configuration rephasing telescopes around this loop and cannot
remove it. This excludes that particular stoquastic rephasing, not all
bases, regroupings or simulation methods. High-dimensional charge sums,
full-history determinants, volume-uniform interacting continuum and
real-time reconstruction remain open, as do a derived common parent,
chiral completion, gravity and physical initial-state selection. No RH
or empirical claim is made.
