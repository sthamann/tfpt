\subsection{Typed objects and prior local results}

The integral compiler commutant, its positive cone, the sixteen-real-mode
quadratic candidate, the seventy-state interacting chain, the filled QWZ
strip and the compact-U(1) rotor parent are different objects. In particular,
the quadratic candidate has eight complex modes and a 256-dimensional full

open four-site, eight-mode model; it is not an unbounded winding rotor or
the internal Dirac operator.

The compiler-order audit distinguishes a unique minimal family-inner order
from its unique maximal overorder, with integral indices four and four.
These solve different selection problems. The Hermitian $2\times2$ formula
\[
 X=\begin{pmatrix}t+z&x-iy\\x+iy&t-z\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 \det X=t^2-x^2-y^2-z^2
\]
provides an exact Lorentz-cone representation, not a derived spacetime
metric, action or quantum-gravity measure. Under the matched-center and
Clock-normalizer hypotheses, the actual eight-complex-mode source excludes
a unital $M_2(\mathbb C)$ identification carrying its whole Clock action.
The active $M_3$ corner accommodates two distinct slow frequencies; a closed
autonomous $M_2$ automorphism flow has only one positive Bohr frequency.

Other local controls delimit apparent simplifications. One-sided quaternion
defects can be scalar, and Gram whitening can erase the relative rates.
A unique Hilbert--Schmidt relational vector need not give a nontrivial
physical reduced state. A four-point $D_4$-symmetric conservative real
generator retains independent edge and diagonal weights. On the scalar
circle, full rotation/reflection symmetry and differential order at most
two imply $aD^2+b$; fourfold symmetry alone does not.

The direct positive point-evaluation perturbation of $|D|$ is not a closed
form in the audited setting; its positive Fourier-cutoff family has the
free norm-resolvent limit. A negative renormalized coupling gives a
nontrivial family with an unselected parameter. Under the direct rotor
identification its selected vector need not have finite $D^2$ energy.
Adding a positive $\eta D^2$ term changes the form domain and introduces
an unselected $\eta$. The free Dirichlet--Neumann operator $|D|=\sqrt{D^2}$
is an exact bridge, but not an identification of the two time generators.

Finally, for $u^*=-u$ and $u^2=-I$,
\[
 (I+nu)/\sqrt{1+n^2}=\exp(\arctan(n)u).
\]
An integral index $(1+n^2)^4$ of the unnormalized lattice action is not
entropy production of this unitary, nor a derived physical clock.

\subsection{Reconstruction contracts that must remain distinct}

For a pure eigenstate and a specified Hermitian family $O_i$, the covariance
form obeys
\[
 c^{\mathsf T}\Gamma c
 =\|(K-\langle K\rangle)\psi\|^2,\qquad K=\sum_i c_iO_i.
\]
A one-dimensional nonconstant kernel identifies one generator direction,
not the origin of the family or state. For a faithful mixed state,
$\operatorname{Var}_\rho(K)=0$ instead forces $K$ to be scalar. Thermal
reconstruction therefore needs its own stationarity-response contract.
Adding separately measured eigenstate covariance matrices is not the same
as mixing the eigenstates before computing one covariance matrix.

For a fixed-$N$ system with a Hamiltonian of at most two-body order, the
complete two-particle reduced density matrix determines its energy
expectation. If the ground state $\rho_0$ is unique with gap $\Delta>0$,
\[
 H-E_0I\succeq\Delta(I-\rho_0)
\]
implies uniqueness of the positive state completion having the same
two-particle data. More generally,
\[
 1-\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma\rho_0)
 \leq\frac{\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma H)-E_0}{\Delta}.
\]
This conditional argument is not a general uniqueness statement for
arbitrary excited states. Likewise
$\log((I-C)C^{-1})$ reconstructs the quadratic quasifree one-body reader,
not every interacting state with that covariance.

A different sufficient finite contract starts with a fully specified
selfadjoint strictly positive Hilbert-space operator $T$, a simple largest
eigenvalue $\lambda_0$ and a positive step size $\delta$:
\[
 H=-\delta^{-1}\log(T/\lambda_0).
\]
It yields a unique ground ray and energy differences. It does not determine
$T$ or $\delta$ from symmetry or a common fixed vector. Computing
$T=e^{-\delta H_{70}}$ first and then inverting it is circular as an origin
argument. A completely positive channel is not this type of operator.

\subsection{A covariant event channel with an actual boundary response}

Let $K$ be the Hermitian one-particle lift of the existing edge $E_{14}$.
It obeys $K^3=K$. Define
\[
 U(\theta)=I+(\cos\theta-1)K^2-i\sin\theta K,\qquad
 U_k=O^kU(\theta)O^{-k},\qquad
 \Phi_\theta(\rho)=\frac16\sum_{k=0}^5U_k\rho U_k^*.
\]
This is a covariant, trace-preserving completely positive map. Averaging
the Hamiltonians first instead preserves the $P/Q$ split. The two operations
must not be confused. For the invariant hidden one-particle state $Q/3$,
writing $c=\cos\theta$,
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}(P\Phi_\theta(Q/3))
 =\frac{35-34c-c^2}{108}.
\]
At $\theta=\pi/2$ the value is $35/108$, without retaining an orientation
record. The pulse itself acts only on carrier modes. With $R_B$ the
original boundary projector and $h$ the unchanged source one-body
Hamiltonian, $\rho'=\Phi_{\pi/2}(Q/3)$ obeys
\[
 R_B\rho'=0,\qquad
 \operatorname{Tr}(R_Bh\rho'h)=\frac1{24},
\]
and hence
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}(R_Be^{-ihs}\rho'e^{ihs})
 =s^2/24+O(s^3).
\]
The initial hidden state and the vacuum have identical accessible vacuum
marginals, but this stipulated protocol separates them. The vacuum remains
vacuum because the lifted operations preserve particle number. This is a
conditional positive readout result, not a source-derived preparation or
implementation. Covariance of density matrices also does not establish
a stronger Gauss-law physical-state constraint.

\subsection{New continuation: transfer succeeds but state selection fails}

A possible, explicitly added continuous event model is
\[
 \mathcal L(X)=-i[h,X]+r(\Phi_{\pi/2}(X)-X),\qquad r>0.
\]
The event-only semigroup is the Poisson mixture of $\Phi^n$; the rate $r$
is unselected. With the Hilbert--Schmidt inner product,
\[
 \operatorname{Re}\langle X,(\Phi-I)X\rangle
 =-\frac1{12}\sum_k\|[U_k,X]\|_{\rm HS}^2.
\]
The Hamiltonian part has zero real quadratic form. Consequently the fixed
space of $\mathcal L$ is the common commutant of all $U_k$ and $h$.
The three distinct eigenvalues of each $U_k$ make its commutant equal to
that of $K_k$.

Exact linear equations give dimensions ten for the edge-only commutant
and five after adding $h$. The joint algebra is
\[
 \mathbb C I_6\oplus M_2(\mathbb C).
\]
Indeed the boundary plane whose three coefficients sum to zero has a
rank-two projector $R$ with $K_kR=0$ and $hR=R$. The two normalized positive
states $R/2$ and $(I-R)/6$ are stationary and distinct. The explicit five
fixed directions exhaust the computed commutant.

Thus the event mechanism transmits a signal but does not select a unique
one-particle state. Full Fock number conservation adds further obstructions.
Its 64-dimensional channel superoperator is not Hilbert--Schmidt-selfadjoint,
so it is not the strictly positive selfadjoint $T$ in the logarithmic
transfer theorem. This is a typed exclusion, not a claim that no different
source-derived transfer or detailed-balance representation can work.

\subsection{Proposed follow-ups and discriminating research directions}
\label{sec:universalraum-followups-20260912}

These are proposed investigations, not newly completed results or launched
worker tasks. Their common acceptance rule is a forward derivation without
importing the desired Hamiltonian, its eigenstates or target couplings.
A genuinely independently derived primitive action may itself be the
Hamiltonian; it need not be artificially required to be a different object.

\paragraph{F0: Independent reproduction.}
Obtain and pin the original 17- and 66-family certificates before using
their strongest claims as local premises; then audit the non-Slater and
cyclotomic irreducibility certificates. Keep the 133-dimensional product
extension separate. Success means the same exact ranks, coefficient fields
and class definitions, with numerical conditioning reported separately.
Unavailable originals mean ``not reproduced'', not ``false''.

\paragraph{F1: Freeze the elementary grammar.}
For one original finite sector, map each primitive rule to its operator,
phase, support, adjoint and composition. Distinguish physical operations
from gauge identifications and elementary generators from composite words.
A source-derived filtration or equivalent primitive rule must explain why
composition does not automatically introduce independently weighted energy
terms. Reject a cutoff chosen only to enforce the desired covariance rank.

\paragraph{F2: Derive process type and measure.}
Construct one source step or collar without the target generator as input.
Declare whether the output is a Hamiltonian, a positive Hilbert-space
transfer or a reduced quantum channel. Derive weights, step duration and
any traced environment from the stated inputs. Test alternative weights
with identical symmetry and fixed vector. A freely chosen metric in
$d^*Wd$, pulse angle or event rate remains an additional datum. Channel
positivity does not by itself license a Hamiltonian or Markov logarithm.

\paragraph{F3: State selection and correlation content.}
Compute the exact fixed algebra or ground space of the F2 candidate.
Use eigenstate covariance, thermal stationarity or full two-particle data
only under their respective contracts. Test identical one-body data with
different four-point responses, and identical ground rays with different
energy ratios. Repetition of the current channel and free flow cannot
remove their common fixed algebra. Any operation removing that algebra
must be independently supplied by the source, not by a target-state penalty.

\paragraph{F4: Operational response and costs.}
For a source-derived intervention, compare admissible preparations with
the same initial accessible marginal at the original boundary. Bound the
remainder in the conditional $s^2/24+O(s^3)$ signal on an explicit finite
time interval, and include preparation energy, precision and sampling
costs. Preserve the no-intervention control. Exact cyclic rank alone is
not successful physical control, and arbitrarily small edge asymmetry
must not be compensated by unaccounted unlimited precision.

\paragraph{F5: Source-to-model maps.}
An explicit map between the successful candidate and H70, the rotor parent
or the charged seam must preserve the relevant adjunction, charge/Clock
action, states, dynamics and readouts on named domains. Matching spectra,
dimensions or group names alone is insufficient. An open chain must not
be identified with a winding rotor, nor a free source with an interacting
state without the additional operation being derived.

\paragraph{Four exploratory hypotheses, not established mechanisms.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \emph{Positive frustrated selection:} derive the unavoidable positive
minimum and relative defect weights from the source. The failed equal-weight
H70 selector is not revived by adjusting its weights to the target.
\item \emph{A genuine collar transfer:} derive a positive transfer directly
from the actual boundary construction and its composition. Reflection
positivity must not be inferred merely from a positive Gram matrix.
\item \emph{An internal relational reference:} look for an already present
source interaction coupling the untouched boundary plane while respecting
the correct joint symmetry. Appending an arbitrary environment or projector
does not establish source provenance.
\item \emph{Thermal or multi-state readout:} exploit additional independently
obtained state responses for robust reconstruction. Optimizing a synthetic
temperature for conditioning does not select a cosmic temperature.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{F6: Separate downstream acceptance.}
The half-charge, common $3+1$D parent, chiral, continuum, coupling and
gravity obligations remain separate after any finite success. No new RH
or factoring mechanism is proposed in this roadmap; future arithmetic
work must first consult the existing source-bound proof and counterexample
registers. A later Hyl\ae an application could test order-independent
consolidation on frozen inputs while preserving distinguishability and
recall, but requires an actual implementation map and separate authorization.
