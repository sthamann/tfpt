\paragraph{Reference snapshot policy.}
All experimental reference values used by the verification suite are pinned to a specific
publication date or data release.  Where a DOI or arXiv identifier exists, it is recorded
in the bibliography below and in the suite's \texttt{sm\_inputs\_mz.json} policy file.
Online-only resources (NuFIT, PDG online tables) carry a snapshot date.
This ensures that results are reproducible even if upstream databases update:
the suite always evaluates against the pinned snapshot, and any future reference update
triggers a re-run rather than a silent drift.
