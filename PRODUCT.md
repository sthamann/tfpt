# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Explain TFPT and its connected research so readers can understand what the theory determines, how each result is obtained and what its stated scope means. Preserve an inspectable route to original papers, ledger statements and verification evidence.

## Users

The user explicitly asks for an understandable introduction before mathematical detail, followed by substantial explanations that readers can follow into the original research. No narrower demographic has been established.

## Operating Context

The public website is https://www.fixpoint-theory.com/. Its existing implementation is a Next.js app under website/. The theory atlas is the sole current research navigation source; original papers and the ledger determine scientific scope. The website carries a dated publication and research snapshot.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Keep mathematical consequences, declared inputs, conditional physical readers, assigned textures and empirical measurements distinct. Preserve completed scoped results and precise negative results. Script counts describe an inventory and do not establish independent physical confirmations. Calculation precision, measurement uncertainty and physical identification have separate meanings.

Frozen prediction values and their history must not change as a side effect of explanation or design work. Dimensional calibration, mass scheme, branch hypotheses and external ranges remain attached to their respective readouts.

## Brand Commitments

Retain TFPT, its existing logo and its original attribution. The user explicitly requested a visual and immersive explanation with geometries and guided changes, and rejected unreadable records, broken dark surfaces and numerical overload without context.

## Evidence on Hand

Original source material and verifications are already in the repository. website/public/research/ contains the public research snapshot. website/public/verification/predictions_frozen.json is the frozen prediction register. The website guide links individual calculations to their corresponding source sections. Local UI captures support particular reviewed surfaces, not a claim that every research result has been independently reverified.

## Product Principles

- Begin from the reader's question and make the requested outcome findable.
- Explain actual operations and carry their outputs into the next step.
- Keep shared dependencies visible across particle, electromagnetic, cosmological and quantum branches.
- Place a result's assumptions and original evidence at the relevant step.
- Treat a source edit, a rendered check and a public deployment as different completion states.
