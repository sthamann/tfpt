\begin{abstract}
We consolidate finite algebraic and analytic controls, four attributed
research-task reports on state-to-generator reconstruction, and new exact
tests of covariant edge operations. The latter yield an original-boundary
signal under an explicitly additional event channel, but their fixed

data is distinguished from deriving those data from a primitive source.
