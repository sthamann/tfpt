2. Even a complete undecorated CP transfer does not fix marked responses

Use convention E_O(X)=sum_ij O_ij A_j X A_i*, E=E_I.
Given the ACTUAL A_i and field matrix O, every finite ordered response is
obtained by composing these E_O with all intervening E powers and the
actual boundary/Perron data. No Wick assumption is required.

For an exact implication countertest let the auxiliary space be C2 and
A_0=I/sqrt(2), A_j=sigma_j/sqrt(6), j=x,y,z.
Then E(I)=I, E(sigma_j)=sigma_j/3 and the unique stationary density is
l=I/2. The tensor already spans M2 at length1 and is primitive.
Set B_0=(A_0+A_x)/sqrt(2), B_x=(A_0-A_x)/sqrt(2),
B_y=A_y, B_z=A_z. For EVERY X, sum B_i X B_i*=E(X).
Thus the full transfer, Perron data, normalization and mixing eigenvalues
are identical. Yet for the SAME marked physical O=|0><0|,
  tr(l E_O^A(I))=1/2,  tr(l E_O^B(I))=1/3.
The difference occurs before higher-word reconstruction is attempted.

The Kraus rotation is a physical-basis rotation. If O and every mark are
transported with it, the sources are equivalent. Holding O fixed tests
exactly the missing marked correspondence; it is not a new unmarked
theory or a pair satisfying the entire original TFPT master data.
No claim of physical locality, OS positivity or TFPT embedding is made
for this auxiliary countertest. Its sole conclusion is that E_I alone
does not determine E_O at fixed physical labels.

3. Finite positivity and separation do not give uniform transfer mixing

For 0<e<1/2 let
  P_e=[[1-e,e],[e,1-e]], D=diag(1,-1).
All entries of P_e are strictly positive; Alg(P_e,D)=M2 for every e.
The eigenvalues are 1 and lambda=1-2e. At e_L=1/(4L), the Perron gap
is 1/(2L), tending to0. The positive transfer Hamiltonian -log(P_e)
also has a gap tending to0. These are NOT parent-Hamiltonian gaps.
No identification of these gaps with the original Dadm is permitted.

4. The same failure persists for normalized local parent projectors

To avoid relying on a transfer/parent gap identification, take an actual
injective MPS implication counterfamily, with d=D_aux=2 fixed:
  a=sqrt(1-e), b=sqrt(e),
  A_0=|0>(a<0|+b<1|), A_1=|1>(b<0|+a<1|).
For 0<e<1/2 the four length2 products span M2: both overlaps a,b
are nonzero and the two row vectors have determinant a^2-b^2>0.
E is unital and its nonzero eigenvalues are 1 and lambda=1-2e.
Its stationary density l=[[1/2,ab],[ab,1/2]] is faithful.

On a periodic ring of L sufficiently large, the MPS amplitudes are
  psi(i_1,...,i_L)=product_j sqrt(P_e[i_j,i_(j+1)]).
The squared norm is tr(P_e^L)=1+lambda^L. Let h be the ORTHOGONAL
projector onto the complement of the length3 MPS image, and
H_L(e)=sum_j h_(j,j+1,j+2). This is the canonical range3 parent,
with local terms norm1, independent of L at each fixed e. Injectivity
at length2 gives its unique periodic ground line for L>=4: the cited
Theorem10 requires ring size>=2L0 and interaction length>L0, here L0=2.

All expectations below are divided by ||psi||^2. For Z=diag(1,-1),
M=sum_j Z_j, the ground state has <M>=0 and
  <Z_0 Z_r>=(lambda^r+lambda^(L-r))/(1+lambda^L),
  <M^2>=L (1+lambda)/(1-lambda) (1-lambda^L)/(1+lambda^L).
These follow by the full periodic Markov weight, not an independent
site approximation. The nonzero trial vector M psi is orthogonal to
the unique ground line. Each h_j annihilates psi, and commutes with
Z outside its three sites. Therefore
  <M H_L M>=sum_j ||h_j (Z_j+Z_(j+1)+Z_(j+2)) psi||^2 <=9L.
The variational principle gives the DIRECT PARENT bound
  gap H_L(e) <= 9 (1-lambda)/(1+lambda)
                      * (1+lambda^L)/(1-lambda^L).

At e=e_L=1/(4L), lambda^L tends to exp(-1/2), so this bound tends
to0 as O(1/L). All bond/physical dimensions, blocking length, local
interaction range and projector norms stay fixed. Every member is
injective and has positive classical transfer and separated basis.
The tensor changes with L. Thus finite-volume injectivity alone does
not imply a UNIFORM gap over changing tensors, even for canonical
normalized parents. This does not contradict the fixed-tensor FNW
gap theorem, and is not a counterexample to a specified TFPT tensor.
