000001 | PAIR INDEX AND THE RELATIVE SEAM DETERMINANT
000002 | ============================================
000003 | 
000004 | 25 October 2026. Independent analytic review. No Atlas, index, paper or
000005 | existing contract was changed. This note reviews only the ordinary Fredholm
000006 | determinant and its precise TFPT scope.
000007 | 
000008 | Verdict
000009 | -------
000010 | 
000011 | The proposed theorem is correct after making its hypotheses and orientation
000012 | explicit.
000013 | 
000014 | Let P and Q be orthogonal projections on the same Hilbert space, assume
000015 | Q-P is trace class, and put
000016 | 
000017 |   k=Tr(Q-P).                                                (1)
000018 | 
000019 | Then k is an integer pair index with the sign convention in (1), and for
000020 | every |z|<1,
000021 | 
000022 |   det_F[(I-zQ)(I-zP)^(-1)] = (1-z)^k.                      (2)
000023 | 
000024 | The difference Q-P need not be positive or finite rank. In particular, a
000025 | primitive value k=1 fixes the scalar factor 1-z without selecting a primitive
000026 | line, a defect vector or a rank-one transfer operator.
000027 | 
000028 | If Q=UPU* for a unitary U satisfying Q-P trace class and if C is a positive
000029 | contraction with C-P trace class, the exact three-factor cancellation gives
000030 | 
000031 |   det_F[(I-z UCU*)(I-zC)^(-1)] = (1-z)^k.                  (3)
000032 | 
000033 | Thus the existing faithful KMS theorem extends from the old unit shift and its
000034 | integer powers to every unitary in the same restricted polarization class.
000035 | This is a genuine strengthening of the present contract. It weakens the need
000036 | for the specially named primitive ray only when the requested output is the
000037 | single scalar determinant response.
000038 | 
000039 | It does not reconstruct the primitive vector, transfer spectrum, state
000040 | preparation or the operator U_Sigma^min. It also does not derive the original
000041 | coefficient 5/4. An ordinary pair index is integral. The original formula uses
000042 | the already separate carrier weight B gamma=5/4 multiplying the logarithm.
000043 | 
000044 | 1. Projection-pair proof and sign
000045 | ---------------------------------
000046 | 
000047 | Write
000048 | 
000049 |   A_Q(z)=I-zQ,       A_P(z)=I-zP,
000050 |   R(z)=A_Q(z) A_P(z)^(-1).                                (4)
000051 | 
000052 | For |z|<1 both inverses exist. Moreover
000053 | 
000054 |   R(z)-I=-z(Q-P)A_P(z)^(-1)                               (5)
000055 | 
000056 | is trace class, so its ordinary Fredholm determinant is defined.
000057 | 
000058 | Direct differentiation gives
000059 | 
000060 |   R'(z)R(z)^(-1)
000061 |     =A_Q(z) [(P-Q)/(1-z)] A_Q(z)^(-1).                    (6)
000062 | 
000063 | For completeness, differentiate A_Q A_P^(-1), multiply on the right by
000064 | A_P A_Q^(-1), and use
000065 | 
000066 |   A_P^(-1)P=P/(1-z),       QA_Q^(-1)=Q/(1-z).
000067 | 
000068 | Since P-Q is trace class, cyclicity against the bounded invertible A_Q gives
000069 | 
000070 |   Tr[R'R^(-1)]
000071 |     =Tr(P-Q)/(1-z)
000072 |     =-k/(1-z).                                             (7)
000073 | 
000074 | The Fredholm Jacobi formula and det_F R(0)=1 now imply on the analytic branch
000075 | in the unit disk
000076 | 
000077 |   log det_F R(z)=k log(1-z),                               (8)
000078 | 
000079 | which is (2).
000080 | 
000081 | The sign is fixed by the stated order of the relative pair. On the actual old
000082 | negative sea
000083 | 
000084 |   P=1_(B_a<0),       u=M_z,       uPu*=P+Pi_0,
000085 | 
000086 | one has Q-P=Pi_0 and k=+1, hence 1-z. For the complementary positive Hardy
000087 | projection the same u gives k=-1 and the ordered factor is (1-z)^(-1).
000088 | Reversing U or reversing the ordered determinant also reverses k.
000089 | 
000090 | The integrality in (1) is the standard index theorem for a trace-class pair
000091 | of projections. With the Avron, Seiler and Simon convention
000092 | ind(P,Q)=Tr(P-Q), the present k equals -ind(P,Q). The bibliographic source is
000093 | 
000094 |   J. Avron, R. Seiler, B. Simon,
000095 |   The Index of a Pair of Projections,
000096 |   Journal of Functional Analysis 120 (1994), 220-237,
000097 |   DOI 10.1006/jfan.1994.1031.
000098 | 
000099 | The determinant identity itself follows from the short calculation above and
000100 | does not require a separate finite-rank reduction.
000101 | 
000102 | 2. Exact extension from P to any trace-class covariance
000103 | --------------------------------------------------------
000104 | 
000105 | Assume now
000106 | 
000107 |   0 <= C <= I,       C-P in L1,
000108 |   Q=UPU*,            Q-P in L1.                            (9)
000109 | 
000110 | The phrase any U below always means a unitary satisfying the second condition
000111 | in (9). It does not mean every unitary on the Hilbert space.
000112 | 
000113 | Put
000114 | 
000115 |   A_C=I-zC,       A_UC=I-zUCU*.
000116 | 
000117 | Then UCU*-C is trace class because
000118 | 
000119 |   UCU*-C=U(C-P)U*+(Q-P)+(P-C).                            (10)
000120 | 
000121 | The relative operator has the exact factorization
000122 | 
000123 |   A_UC A_C^(-1)
000124 |    =[A_UC A_Q^(-1)]
000125 |     [A_Q A_P^(-1)]
000126 |     [A_P A_C^(-1)].                                      (11)
000127 | 
000128 | Every factor is identity plus trace class. The first factor is the unitary
000129 | conjugate of
000130 | 
000131 |   A_C A_P^(-1),
000132 | 
000133 | while the third factor is its inverse. Fredholm determinant multiplicativity
000134 | and unitary invariance cancel their determinants exactly. The middle factor is
000135 | the projection pair in (2). This proves (3) without commutativity of C with P
000136 | or U and without a sign assumption on UCU*-C.
000137 | 
000138 | Positivity of C is the natural covariance hypothesis and ensures the displayed
000139 | inverses for |z|<1. Algebraically, the same proof only needs bounded C and an
000140 | open z domain on which the inverses exist.
000141 | 
000142 | 3. Smooth scalar winding on the actual circle
000143 | ----------------------------------------------
000144 | 
000145 | Let P=P_- be the negative Fourier projection on L2(S1), and let U=M_u for a
000146 | smooth scalar function u:S1->U(1). Write
000147 | 
000148 |   u(theta)=sum_j u_j exp(i j theta).
000149 | 
000150 | For one Fourier monomial, [P,M_(z^j)] is a partial isometry of rank |j|.
000151 | Consequently
000152 | 
000153 |   ||[P,M_u]||_1 <= sum_j |j| |u_j|.                       (12)
000154 | 
000155 | The right side is finite for smooth u. Since
000156 | 
000157 |   UPU*-P=[U,P]U*,                                         (13)
000158 | 
000159 | the projection pair is trace class. The Toeplitz pair-index theorem, with the
000160 | negative-sea orientation used here, gives
000161 | 
000162 |   Tr(UPU*-P)=wind(u).                                      (14)
000163 | 
000164 | Equations (3) and (14) therefore yield
000165 |
