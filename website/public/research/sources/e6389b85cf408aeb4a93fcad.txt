000087 | 3. The smallest exact finite-wall test

000091 |   N=[[0,-1],[1,0]], Z=diag(1,-1), S=NZ=[[0,1],[1,0]].

000093 | Thus N^2=-I, Z^2=S^2=I, NZ=S, ZN=-S, and S anticommutes with N and Z.
000094 | At one actual tangential Fourier value p, take finite-dimensional constant
000095 | Phi=Phi*, a smooth real periodic scalar sigma(r), and the scalar
000096 | antiperiodic domain on the circle of length 2ell. Set
000097 | 
000098 |   Q=N partial_r+Z sigma(r)Phi,
000099 |   D=Q+pS.
000100 | 
000101 | Internal tensor identities are implicit; Phi commutes with N,Z,S. Q and D
000102 | are selfadjoint on H1_antiperiodic by bounded perturbation of N partial_r.
000103 | They have compact resolvent. On H2_antiperiodic, with the corresponding
000104 | first-derivative boundary trace, direct multiplication gives
000105 | 
000106 |   {S,Q}=0,
000107 |   Q^2=-partial_r^2+sigma(r)^2 Phi^2+S sigma'(r)Phi,
000108 |   D^2=Q^2+p^2 I.                                      (1)
000109 | 
000110 | In particular the derivative term has the PLUS sign. To test an exact
000111 | lambda_D=+/-p it suffices to solve Q psi=0. Multiplying this equation by N
000112 | gives
000113 | 
000114 |   partial_r psi=sigma(r)(S Phi)psi,
000115 |   psi(r)=exp(S Phi F(r))psi(0),
000116 |   F(r)=integral_0^r sigma(u)du.                         (2)
