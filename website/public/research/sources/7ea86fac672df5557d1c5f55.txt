000194 | \begin{theorem}[Invariance of the defect lexicography]
000195 | Let $\mathcal C$ be the category of nontrivial admissible one-sided spectral bordisms modulo unitary
000196 | intertwiners and contractible stabilizations. Let
000197 | \begin{equation}
000198 | \mathfrak D:\mathcal C\to\mathbb N^k
000199 | \end{equation}
000200 | be the canonical defect filtration. If $F:\mathcal C\to\mathcal C'$ is an equivalence of admissible
000201 | presentations and for every coordinate there is a strictly increasing map
000202 | $\psi_i:\mathbb N\to\mathbb N$ with
000203 | \begin{equation}
000204 | d'_i(F(B))=\psi_i(d_i(B)),
000205 | \end{equation}
000206 | then $B$ is a lexicographic minimizer of $\mathfrak D$ if and only if $F(B)$ is a lexicographic
000207 | minimizer of $\mathfrak D'$.
000208 | \end{theorem}
000209 | 
000210 | \begin{proof}
000211 | Strictly increasing coordinate maps preserve and reflect the first coordinate at which two defect
000212 | vectors differ. Therefore $\mathfrak D(B)<_{\rm lex}\mathfrak D(C)$ if and only if
000213 | $\mathfrak D'(F(B))<_{\rm lex}\mathfrak D'(F(C))$. Hence minimizers are preserved by equivalence.
000214 | Existence follows because $\mathbb N^k$ is well founded under lexicographic order. Uniqueness is
000215 | then reduced to classification of the final minimized stratum by the one-sided boundary datum up to
000216 | unitary equivalence.
000217 | \end{proof}
