000443 | The preceding appendix showed that a literal compression of the pure projector
000444 | covariance cannot simultaneously retain the exact factor 1-z and be strictly

000449 | Circle assumptions and orientation
000450 | ----------------------------------
000451 | Let B be the self-adjoint old circle operator and
000452 | 
000453 |   P=1_(B<0),
000454 |   u=M_z.
000455 | 
000456 | Assume the orientation actually used in the old circle dictionary,
000457 | 
000458 |   u P u* = P+Pi,
000459 |   Pi=|psi_0><psi_0|,
000460 |   P psi_0=0.

000462 | For B=-i partial_theta on the twisted Fourier domain with spectrum n+a,
000463 | 0<a<1, multiplication by z sends n to n+1. Hence uPu* adds exactly the unique
000464 | mode with eigenvalue a in [0,1), proving this orientation. Reversing B or the
000465 | shift reverses the index and gives (1-z)^(-1), so the orientation is an essential
000466 | hypothesis rather than a notation-free fact.

000481 | Trace-class control
000482 | -------------------
000483 | First,
000484 | 
000485 |   uCu*-C
000486 |    = u(C-P)u*-(C-P)+(uPu*-P)
000487 | 
000488 | is trace class: the first two terms are trace class by assumption and unitary
000489 | invariance of L^1, while the last term is Pi of rank one. Therefore
000490 | 
000491 |   R_C-I = -z(uCu*-C)A_C^(-1)
000492 | 
000493 | is trace class and det_F(R_C) is defined.
000494 | 
000495 | The factorization
000496 | 
000497 |   R_C
000498 |    = (u A_C A_P^(-1) u*)
000499 |      (u A_P u* A_P^(-1))
000500 |      (A_P A_C^(-1))

000502 | is an exact multiplication identity. Each factor is I plus trace class. Indeed,
000503 | 
000504 |   A_C A_P^(-1)-I = -z(C-P)A_P^(-1),
000505 |   A_P A_C^(-1)-I =  z(C-P)A_C^(-1),
000506 | 
000507 | and the middle factor differs from I by rank one.

000509 | Exact determinant invariance
000510 | ----------------------------
000511 | Fredholm-determinant multiplicativity and unitary invariance give
000512 | 
000513 |   det_F(u A_C A_P^(-1)u*) = det_F(A_C A_P^(-1)),
000514 |   det_F(A_P A_C^(-1))     = det_F(A_C A_P^(-1))^(-1).

000516 | Thus the first and third determinants cancel. For the middle factor, the old
000517 | orientation gives

000519 |   u A_P u* = I-z(P+Pi)=A_P-zPi.

000521 | Since Ppsi_0=0, A_P^(-1)psi_0=psi_0, and hence
000522 | 
000523 |   u A_P u* A_P^(-1)=I-zPi,
000524 |   det_F(u A_P u* A_P^(-1))=1-z.
000525 | 
000526 | Therefore, for every |z|<1,
000527 | 
000528 |   det_F R_C = det_F R_P = 1-z.

000531 | polarization class. It does not require C itself to contain an empty or filled
