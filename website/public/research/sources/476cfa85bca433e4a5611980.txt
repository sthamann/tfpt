000084 | 2. Direct genus one Szego proof
000085 | ===============================
000086 | 
000087 | For p=(x,y_x) and q=(z,y_z), define
000088 | 
000089 |   S_part(p,q)=
000090 |     [A(x)B(z)+B(x)A(z)]/[2(x-z)]
000091 |     (dx/y_x)^(1/2) (dz/y_z)^(1/2).                     (8)
000092 | 
000093 | The signs of A and B are chosen coherently so that A B=y on E. Formula (8)
000094 | has the defining properties of the alpha Szego kernel:
000095 | 
000096 |   * It is a half form in each variable with spin multipliers D_A.
000097 | 
000098 |   * At p=q, its only pole has unit residue because both ratios tend to one.
000099 | 
000100 |   * At q=h p, the apparent x=z pole cancels between the two numerator terms.
000101 | 
000102 |   * It has no other poles.
000103 | 
000104 | If S_part and the normalized Szego kernel differed, their difference for
000105 | fixed q would be a holomorphic section of alpha. Equation (7) makes that
000106 | difference zero. Hence (8) is the actual NS--NS elliptic Szego kernel.
000107 | 
000108 | Now use y=A B. The two terms in (8) simplify exactly as
000109 | 
000110 |   A(x)B(z)/sqrt(y_x y_z)=g(x)/g(z),
000111 |   B(x)A(z)/sqrt(y_x y_z)=g(z)/g(x).                    (9)
000112 | 
000113 | Equations (8) and (9) prove (2). This is a direct genus one argument from
000114 | pole, multipliers and H0(E,alpha)=0. It does not import a genus two theorem.
000115 | 
000116 | For comparison, the same algebraic shape for a hyperelliptic even spin
000117 | partition is displayed in E. D'Hoker, M. Hidding and O. Schlotterer,
000118 | "Cyclic products of Szego kernels and spin structure sums", JHEP 05 (2023)

000120 | 
000121 |   https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.09069
000122 | 
000123 | That reference treats genus two. It is a primary literature check of the
000124 | partition formula, not the proof of the genus one identity used here.
