000030 | ## 1. Actual current products supply Jordan multiplication
000031 | 
000032 | For real symmetric A, define the existing local field
000033 | 
000034 |     T_A(z) = (1/2) sum_ij A_ij :h_i(z) h_j(z):.
000035 | 
000036 | The standard vacuum Wick rule gives
000037 | 
000038 |     T_A(z) T_B(w) = (Tr(AB)/2)/(z-w)^4
000039 |                    + 2 T_(A o B)(w)/(z-w)^2 + O((z-w)^(-1)),
000040 |     A o B = (AB+BA)/2.
000041 | 
000042 | Proof: two complete pairings give Tr(AB)/2. The four single contractions
000043 | cancel the prefactor 1/4, leaving h(z)^T AB h(w). At the double pole the fields
000044 | are evaluated at w and their symmetric normal product kills the antisymmetric
000045 | part of AB. Thus h^T(AB+BA)h/2 = 2 T_(A o B). This holds for arbitrary A,B,
000046 | not only for the checked projectors. The simple pole is intentionally not
000047 | identified with just a derivative: for noncommuting matrices it can contain
000048 | additional antisymmetric weight-three terms.
000049 | 
000050 | This uses standard free-boson Wick algebra, not a new general theorem. A
000051 | primary reference for the normalizations is Paul Ginsparg, *Applied Conformal
000052 | Field Theory*, section 2.3, equations (2.16)-(2.17):
000053 | https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9108028.
000054 | 
000055 | Consequently repeated extraction of the weight-two double-pole coefficient
000056 | generates exactly the corresponding iterated Jordan products as local fields
000057 | in this stipulated source algebra. This is a field/OPE identity, not the claim
000058 | that multiplying scalar measurement outputs implements an OPE.
