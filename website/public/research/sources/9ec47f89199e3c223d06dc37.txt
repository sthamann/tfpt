000020 |   Pc=[[0,0,1],[1,0,0],[0,1,0]],

000023 | carries h_a is exactly

000027 | This is a column transport in the existing differential frame. It is not
000028 | an independently chosen identification of three equal-dimensional fibers.

000030 | 
000031 |   G_a^(-*) G_a^(-1)=G^(-*) G^(-1).

000042 | read by J E^(b-1), and a scalar cable matrix and a matrix in the one common

000072 | 3. EXACT COEFFICIENT ALGEBRA
