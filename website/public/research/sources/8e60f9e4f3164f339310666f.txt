000053 | ===============================================
000054 | 
000055 | Use a complex Hilbert product conjugate-linear in the first variable and
000056 | linear in the second.  Creation a^*(v) is linear in v and annihilation a(v) is

000058 | 
000059 |   Xi=a^*(v_1)a^*(v_2)a^*(v_3)Omega

000061 | spans K=det(V).  The Hilbert adjoint gives the bra
000062 | 
000063 |   R(Xi): K -> C,             R(Xi)(xi)=<Xi,xi>.                (3)
000064 | 
000065 | For fixed Xi, (3) is complex-linear in xi, so R(Xi) belongs to K^*.  The Riesz
000066 | map R:K->K^* is conjugate-linear.  Under a unitary symmetry g,
000067 | 
000068 |   g|K=det(g|V),              g|K^*=det(g|V)^(-1).              (4)
000069 | 
000070 | This is the precise reason that beta=i I_V gives -i on K and +i on K^*.

000079 | complex-linear statement uses covectors phi_j in V^* and the canonical

000082 |   det(V)^* tensor det(V) -> C,
