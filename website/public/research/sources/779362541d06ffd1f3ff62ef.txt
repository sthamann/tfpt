000051 | 2. Correct minimization theorem
000052 | 
000053 | Let C:S -> B be any functor and d:B -> N^k an isomorphism-invariant defect,
000054 | with the original lexicographic order on N^k.
000055 | Write d_seed=d o C. The unconditional conclusion is
000056 | 
000057 |   s minimizes d_seed iff C(s) minimizes d on im(C).
000058 | 
000059 | Proof. Both comparisons are exactly comparisons of values d(C(s')).
000060 | Passing from the image to all B needs an extra condition: at least one
000061 | global minimizer must lie in im(C). Even then, recovering s uniquely needs
000062 | classification of the relevant fiber of C, not just its image.
000063 | 
000064 | Consequently well-foundedness proves existence of a minimum defect vector
000065 | in any nonempty allowed image. It does not prove that its fiber has one
000066 | isomorphism class. A nonempty subset of the positive integers has a least
000067 | element k>=1; it need not contain1. Unit spectral flow requires either its
000068 | own axiom or an admissible unit-flow witness.
000069 | 
000070 | The manuscript makes the first statement correctly at section002,
000071 | lines194-216: uniqueness is reduced to classification of the last stratum.
000072 | It does not provide that classification in the later proof at269-306.

000074 | admissible bordisms without proving the necessary image condition.
