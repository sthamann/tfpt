000003 | \label{lem:norm-stable-gap-parent-path}
000004 | \label{thm:eta-zero-parent-domination}
000005 | Fix finite volume $V$ and let $T_V$ be the primitive blocked admissible transfer operator with
000006 | Perron vector $v_V^\ast$. Let $H_{\mathrm{par}}(V)$ be the blocked admissible parent Hamiltonian of
000007 | \Cref{lem:parent-gap-from-transfer-block}, with
000008 | \[
000009 | m_0(V):=\operatorname{gap}(H_{\mathrm{par}}(V))>0.
000010 | \]
000011 | Let the physical admissible Hamiltonian be written as
000012 | \[
000013 | H_{\mathrm{adm}}(V)
000014 | =
000015 | H_{\mathrm{par}}(V)+K_\Sigma(V)+K_c(V)+K_\Theta(V)+K_g(V)+H_{\mathrm{tr,pos}}(V),
000016 | \]
000017 | where each added term is positive semidefinite and vanishes on the Perron vacuum line generated by
000018 | $v_V^\ast$. Then
000019 | \[
000020 | H_{\mathrm{adm}}(V)\ge H_{\mathrm{par}}(V).
000021 | \]
000022 | Consequently the admissible Hamiltonian has the same vacuum line and satisfies
000023 | \[
000024 | \operatorname{gap}(H_{\mathrm{adm}}(V))
000025 | \ge
000026 | \operatorname{gap}(H_{\mathrm{par}}(V))
000027 | =
000028 | m_0(V).
000029 | \]

000036 | \]
000037 | is positive semidefinite by construction and annihilates the Perron vacuum line, hence the operator
000038 | inequality is immediate and the ground state is unchanged. The gap bound then follows from the
