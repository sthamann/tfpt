000059 |   Pc=[[0,0,1],[1,0,0],[0,1,0]],

000063 | transport on a common lifted sheet,

000065 |   H_ij=G(y_i)G(y_j)^(-1),

000072 |

000076 | 
000077 |   spec(Pc)={1,omega,omega^2},   tr(Pc)=tr(M_c)=0.

000082 |   Pc P_(omega^2)=omega^2 P_(omega^2),

000086 | canonical family basis with the three coefficient slots, the displayed e_i
000087 | are not individual Pc eigenvectors.  Their use therefore does not silently
