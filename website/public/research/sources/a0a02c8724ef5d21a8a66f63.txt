000130 | 3. Boundary-subsystem proposition
000131 | ---------------------------------
000132 | 
000133 | Let A_CAR^+(O) be the bounded even positive-time local CAR algebra of the
000134 | actual fold. Let A_P^+(O) be the positive-time algebra of one complete
000135 | operational seed S_P. Assume a compatible family of injective unital
000136 | *-homomorphisms
000137 | 
000138 |   iota_O : A_CAR^+(O) -> A_P^+(O)                         (1)
000139 | 
000140 | with the following properties:
000141 | 
000142 |   iota respects isotony and locality;
000143 |   omega_P(iota(A))=omega_CAR(A);
000144 |   iota(tau_CAR,t(A))=tau_P,t(iota(A));
000145 |   iota(Theta_CAR(A))=Theta_P(iota(A));
000146 |   iota carries the given seam action/class to the restricted P1 seam.
000147 | 
000148 | Write q_CAR and q_P for the OS sesquilinear forms on positive-time histories.
000149 | Define on the OS quotient of the CAR histories
000150 | 
000151 |   V_iota [F]_CAR := [iota(F)]_P.                          (2)
000152 | 
000153 | State and reflection covariance give
000154 | 
000155 |   q_P(iota(F),iota(G))=q_CAR(F,G).                        (3)
000156 | 
000157 | Consequently every CAR null vector maps to a P1 null vector, (2) is well
000158 | defined and extends to an isometry
000159 | 
000160 |   V_iota : H_CAR -> K_CAR,
000161 |   K_CAR=closure{iota(F) Omega_P}.                          (4)
000162 | 
000163 | Time covariance gives
000164 | 
000165 |   V_iota exp(-t H_CAR)=exp(-t H_P)V_iota                 (5)

000167 | on the reconstructed cyclic subsystem. Reflection and the seam action obey
000168 | the analogous restricted intertwining relations. This proof uses the OS
000169 | forms themselves. It does not assume that an arbitrary unbounded field acts
000170 | by left multiplication on the OS quotient.

000173 | restricted net has exactly the form used in the paper's definition of
