000115 | 3. Polynomial bound for every octic mode
000116 | ----------------------------------------
000117 | 
000118 | Consider a degree-r normal-ordered current monomial of total mode m,
000119 | 
000120 |   W_m=sum_(n_1+...+n_r=m) :h^(c_1)_n1 ... h^(c_r)_nr: .    (3)

000126 |   Q=sum_(n_i<0) |n_i|.

000128 | Normal ordering places the positive modes on the right. Acting on P_E, the
000129 | term vanishes unless

000142 | After all positive modes have acted, the negative modes can raise the energy
