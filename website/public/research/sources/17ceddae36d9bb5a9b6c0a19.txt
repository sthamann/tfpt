000004 | Question
000005 | ========
000006 | 
000007 | Can the original Moebius cut and Pin gluing be connected to the already
000008 | present marked P1 and C2/C12 covers without choosing a new bundle, a new
000009 | state, or a global Cartan field on the compact normal S2?

000011 | Verdict
000012 | =======
000013 | 
000014 | Yes, at one precise and useful level.  The pair of Pin quarter sectors on
000015 | the existing NS orientation cover and the polarized real first cohomology of
000016 | 
000017 |   C2: y^2=x^4-1
000018 | 
000019 | carry the same real two dimensional marked dihedral representation.  The
000020 | map x:C2->P1 is already the double cover branched at the four flavor marks
000021 | mu4.  Thus there is an exact monodromy carrier from the original Pin cut and
000022 | gluing to the marked flavor P1.  It does not pass through the normal S2 and
000023 | does not require a global Cartan choice there.
000024 |

000026 | the full two dimensional Green covariance with the original P1 boundary
000027 | state or Hardy covariance.  That state, time and field map remains open.

000029 | The changed premise
000030 | ===================
000031 | 
000032 | The earlier search often asked for a global map from the compactified normal
000033 | S2, with its degree one determinant line, to the flavor P1.  The original
000034 | paper does not ask for such a map.  It constructs two outputs from the seam:
000035 | 
000036 |   * the compact normal S2 with L2=O(1), and
000037 |   * the compact seam quotient P1 with four mu4 marks.
000038 | 
000039 | The smallest common object required for the current question is therefore
000040 | the representation carried by the cut and gluing operators.  It need not be
000041 | a common base surface.  This weaker premise is sufficient for the exact
000042 | bridge below and introduces no geometry.

000044 | Corrected source boundary: deck reflection is not the Calderon polarization
000045 | ============================================================================
000046 | 
000047 | The archived source starts from D_+=gamma_n(partial_n+B_Sigma) and constructs
000048 | 
000049 |   M_tilde=M_+ union_Sigma M_+^op,
000050 |   Sigma=Fix(tau_dbl),
000051 |   D=D_+ union_Sigma D_+^op,
000052 | 
000053 | It then claims on the Cauchy data
000054 | 
000055 |   i_C=2P_C-I=R_tau_dbl.                                (0)
000056 | 
000057 | This equality is not a positive bridge.  It was already disproved in the
000058 | scoped 15 October contract.  On a compact product Dirac collar the local
000059 | geometric deck lift conjugates the two-face graph projector instead of being
000060 | 2P_C-I.  More generally, the principal symbol of 2P_C-I depends on tangential
000061 | cotangent direction, while a local deck lift is pointwise in the bundle.
000062 | Defining R_tau:=2P_C-I makes the equation tautological and loses the claimed
000063 | geometric identity.
000064 | 
000065 | The corrected operator slots are therefore separate:
000066 | 
000067 |   * geometric deck tau_dbl,
000068 |   * operational reflection Theta,
000069 |   * Calderon polarization P_C.
000070 | 
000071 | The full two-face Cauchy graph remains positive and reconstructs the signed
000072 | boundary operator B.  It does not identify any of these three slots.  No new
000073 | requirement Theta_x=R_tau is made here.

000075 | Positive bridge, prioritized test: NS Pin pair and polarized C2 cohomology
000076 | ============================================================================
000077 | 
000078 | On the existing NS orientation cover the original Pin operator is
000079 | 
000080 |   A=J T1,        A^2=-I.
000081 | 
000082 | The two Pin sectors are the A eigenlines with eigenvalues +i and -i.  The
000083 | linear reflection R satisfies
000084 | 
000085 |   R A R=-A=A^{-1}.
000086 | 
000087 | Hence, in the ordered (+i,-i) basis,
000088 | 
000089 |   A=diag(i,-i),       R=[[0,1],[1,0]].                 (1)
000090 | 
000091 | This is the standard real D4 representation after complexification.
000092 | 
000093 | Now use only the existing parity cover C2 and its already marked base map:
000094 | 
000095 |   p:C2->P1_x,   p(x,y)=x,
000096 |   Q2(x,y)=(i x,y),
000097 |   kappa(x,y)=(bar x,bar y).
000098 | 
000099 | The branch divisor of p is exactly mu4.  Infinity has two unramified points.
000100 | For the holomorphic differential eta=dx/y,
000101 | 
000102 |   Q2* eta=i eta,
000103 |   Q2* bar(eta)=-i bar(eta),
000104 |   kappa eta=bar(eta),
000105 |   kappa Q2* kappa=(Q2*)^{-1}.                           (2)
000106 | 
000107 | Equations (1) and (2) give the explicit intertwiner
000108 | 
000109 |   Pin +i line  -> C eta,
000110 |   Pin -i line  -> C bar(eta),
000111 |   A            -> Q2* on H1(C2,R),
000112 |   R            -> kappa on H1(C2,R).
000113 | 
000114 | This is stronger than matching two eigenvalues.  In the real basis
000115 | 
000116 |   a=Re(eta),       b=Im(eta),
000117 | 
000118 | write the intersection form, quarter rotation and real reflection as
000119 | 
000120 |   Omega=[[0,1],[-1,0]],
000121 |   Q=[[0,1],[-1,0]],
000122 |   K=[[1,0],[0,-1]].
000123 | 
000124 | Then, exactly,
000125 | 
000126 |   Q^T Omega Q=Omega,
000127 |   K^T Omega K=-Omega,
000128 |   K Q K=Q^{-1}.
000129 | 
000130 | Thus Q preserves the symplectic intersection pairing, while K is
000131 | the expected anti-symplectic reflection.  For the positive Hodge L2 metric
000132 | g, normalized to the identity in this basis,
000133 | 
000134 |   Q^T g Q=g,          K^T g K=g.
000135 | 
000136 | So the full real carrier is positively polarized and the two generators are
000137 | Hodge isometries.  There is one essential type distinction.  The linear
000138 | pullback K swaps H^{1,0}=C eta with H^{0,1}=C bar(eta).  It does not preserve
000139 | the positive holomorphic line by itself.  If C denotes coefficient
000140 | conjugation, the anti-linear operation C K fixes each of those quarter
000141 | eigenlines.  This reproduces the two different NS facts: the linear Green
000142 | Clifford reflection swaps the quarter sectors, while the anti-linear
000143 | reflection preserves each sector.  It still does not identify their Green
000144 | covariance or Fock state with the Hodge polarization.
000145 | 
000146 | The comparison is made in the category of real representations.  Pullback by
000147 | kappa is complex linear on complex-valued cohomology and swaps the two Hodge
000148 | types; the separate coefficient conjugation is anti-linear.  Neither is
000149 | being confused with the linear Clifford factor in the Green positivity form.
000150 | On the four marked points, kappa acts as z->1/z,
000151 | because every mark lies on the unit circle.  Thus it has exactly the same
000152 | marked permutation as the paper's D4 reflection.  Globally, one is an
000153 | antiholomorphic real structure and the other is the holomorphic Moebius map
000154 | z->1/z.  The proved dictionary is the marked real representation and not an
000155 | identity of those two maps on all of P1.
000156 | 
000157 | This is more than a count of four points.  It preserves the quarter
000158 | eigenvalues, the reflection conjugation law, and the actual branch marking.
000159 | It also explains why one fixed Pin eigenline is insufficient: reflection
000160 | exchanges the two conjugate cohomology lines just as the Green reflection
000161 | exchanges the two Pin sectors.
