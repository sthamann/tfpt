000422 | of that value and holds for every beta>0. Since n_l is strictly decreasing,
000423 | 
000424 |   D_beta:=u C_beta u^*-C_beta

000426 | is diagonal with eigenvalues d_l=n_(l-1)-n_l>0. Moreover
000427 |

000431 | u C_beta u^* are faithful contractions: no eigenvalue is zero or one. For
000432 | |z|<1 the relative operator

000434 |   R_beta(z)=(I-z u C_beta u^*)(I-z C_beta)^(-1)

000436 | differs from the identity by trace class. Its determinant exists, and the
