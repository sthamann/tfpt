000027 | 1. THE ORIGINAL FUNCTION HAS THE REQUIRED DIVISOR AND DEGREE
000028 | 
000029 | On
000030 | 
000031 |   C: eta^2=1-w^6
000032 | 
000033 | define
000034 | 
000035 |   z=eta/(1-w^3).
000036 | 
000037 | Then
000038 | 
000039 |   z^2=(1+w^3)/(1-w^3).                                       (1)
000040 | 
000041 | At q_a, where w^3=-1, eta is a local coordinate and the denominator is
000042 | nonzero.  Thus z has a simple zero at every q_a.  At p_a, where w^3=1, the
000043 | same identity shows a simple pole.  Therefore
000044 | 
000045 |   div(z)=D_q-D_p.                                              (2)
000046 | 
000047 | There are three poles, so z:C->P1 has degree three.  Solving (1) gives
000048 | 
000049 |   w^3=(z^2-1)/(z^2+1).
000050 | 
000051 | The only branch values are z=+1,-1,+i,-i, all on the unit circle.  Hence over
000052 | the open disk D={|z|<1}, the map is an unramified three-sheeted cover.  Since
000053 | D is simply connected, its inverse image is the disjoint union of three
000054 | disks, and each restriction maps biholomorphically to D.  The boundary branch
000055 | points are measure-zero endpoints for the Hardy traces and do not alter the
000056 | interior shift index.

000062 | 
000063 |   H_S=H^2(C_D,S_0),
