000074 | 
000075 | The algebra in (1) is immediate from eta^2=(1-w^3)(1+w^3).  At

000078 | 1-w^3 has a double zero, so z has a simple pole.  Hence

000080 |     div(z)=q_0+q_1+q_2-p_0-p_1-p_2.

000114 | 2.1 THE HARDY NORM IS FIXED BY THE HALF-FORM
000115 | 
000116 | This Hardy realization does not require a fitted measure.  On each open lifted
000117 | disc, dz has no zero and the theta characteristic S_0 has a holomorphic
000118 | half-form e with e^2=dz.  For psi=f(z)e, and likewise after tensoring with a
000119 | unitary flat frame of L,
000120 | 
000121 |     integral_boundary |psi|^2
000122 |       = integral_|z|=1 |f(z)|^2 |dz|.
000123 | 
000124 | Thus the source spin structure supplies the ordinary Hardy norm, while the
000125 | unitary coefficient metric is the same in the relative and reference cables.
000126 | Changing e by its other square root changes no norm or determinant line.
000127 | 
000128 | At each of the four branch points on |z|=1, a local coordinate u has
000129 | z-z_0=a u^3.  Hence dz has a zero of order two and e has a zero of order one.
000130 | Those boundary points have measure zero in the Hardy norm and create no
000131 | additional interior evaluation defect or H^1 point term.  This proves the
000132 | natural half-form Hardy realization used in (6).  It does not identify that
000133 | Hardy polarization with the physical Calderon polarization of D_rel and
000134 | D_ref; that remains the operator premise stated in section 7.

000138 |     index(z|H_q)=-3.

000144 | (6) gives B_q^(-1), exactly as in (4).  This is one line.  Its inverse power

000147 | For any degree-zero line L, apply the same shift to S_0 tensor L and to
000148 | L.  Their cokernel determinants obey
000149 | 
000150 |     B(S_0 tensor L)=Nm_Dq(S_0) tensor B(L).
