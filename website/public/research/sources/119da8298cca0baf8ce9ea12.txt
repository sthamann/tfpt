000077 | $\omega_\beta$ be the restriction \eqref{eq:omega-def} of the
000078 | vacuum-sector rotational Gibbs state.  Then:
000079 | \begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\alph*)}]
000080 | \item $\omega_\beta$ has a rotational $\beta$-KMS extension to
000081 |   $\B$.
000082 | \item The maps
000083 |   \[
000084 |     E_I=\frac14\sum_{r=0}^{3}\alpha_r:
000085 |     \B(I)\to\A(I)
000086 |   \]
000087 |   are the unique normal faithful conditional expectations.  They
000088 |   form a net expectation $E$, and
000089 |   \[
000090 |     \widetilde\omega_\beta
000091 |     =\omega_\beta\circ E
000092 |     =\Phi_\beta^{\B}.
000093 |   \tag{4.1}\label{eq:main-extension}
000094 |   \]
000095 |   This is the unique $\Z_4$-invariant state extension of
000096 |   $\omega_\beta$, hence in particular the unique
000097 |   $\Z_4$-invariant rotational KMS extension.
000098 | \item Every rotational $\beta$-KMS extension of $\omega_\beta$ is
000099 |   $\Z_4$-invariant.  Consequently
000100 |   $\widetilde\omega_\beta$ is the unique rotational KMS extension
000101 |   without an invariance qualifier.
