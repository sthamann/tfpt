000096 | 4. Two incidence transforms on the SAME E6 carrier
000097 | 
000098 | Let N have rows the 1120 E6 cuts and columns the 120 E7 cuts, with N(E,F)=1
000099 | for E subset F. Let M have the same rows and columns the 7560 tetrahedra,
000100 | with M(E,T)=1 for E in T. Both are actual Weyl intertwiners because their
000101 | entries are defined by root inclusions. Both are independent of field-lift
000102 | phases, which cancel in conjugation of root-support projectors.
000103 | 
000104 | Two distinct E6 cuts share an E7 precisely when their A2 normals share one
000105 | root line. Their generated normal system is then A3 and their intersection
000106 | is D5. The shared root line determines exactly one E7; the A3 hull determines
000107 | exactly one tetrahedron. There are 45360 such unordered pairs, partitioned
000108 | both into 7560 sets of six tetrahedron edges and 120 sets of C(28,2) edges.
000109 | Therefore
000110 | 
000111 |     N N^T = A1 + 3 I,            M M^T = A1 + 27 I.            (4.1)
000112 | 
000113 | The diagonal entries count 3 and 27 incidences. Every off-diagonal entry is
000114 | one exactly for the D5 relation, zero otherwise. The checker tests EVERY
000115 | entry of both identities, and the unique tetrahedron of every D5 edge.
