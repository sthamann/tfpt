000098 | 1. The canonical order-two intermediate
000099 | -----------------------------------------
000100 | 
000101 | Use the fixed standard target coordinates in which A3 is the usual D3 root
000102 | lattice on the last three coordinates.  Then
000103 | 
000104 |   K={z in Z8:
000105 |        sum_(i=0)^4 z_i is even,
000106 |        sum_(i=5)^7 z_i is even}.
000107 | 
000108 | Choose the marked Z4 glue generator
000109 | 
000110 |   g=(1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2).
000111 | 
000112 | It has order four modulo K: 2g=(1,...,1) is not in K, whereas
000113 | 4g=(2,...,2) is in K.  A cyclic group of order four has one subgroup of
000114 | order two, namely <2g>.  Its intermediate lattice is
000115 | 
000116 |   L_2=K union (K+2g).
000117 | 
000118 | For an integral vector z, membership in K means that the first-five and
000119 | last-three coordinate sums are both even.  Membership in K+2g means that
000120 | both sums are odd.  Their union is therefore
000121 | 
000122 |   L_2={z in Z8: sum_i z_i is even}=D8.                    (1)
000123 | 
000124 | Equation (1) proves the full lattice identity.  It is not inferred from the
000125 | number of roots.

000128 | glue degree is
