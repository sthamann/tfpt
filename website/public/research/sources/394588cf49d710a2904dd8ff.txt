000046 | 2. Class A: event orders without a background geometry
000047 | 
000048 | INPUT: an unlabeled finite strict partial order P=(E,<). A precedence relation,
000049 | transitivity and absence of cycles are assumed; causal order is not derived
000050 | from consistency in THIS class. There are no rates, amplitudes or metric.

000052 | JOINT CONSEQUENCE: cumulative occurrence states are the order ideals J(P).
000053 | They form a distributive lattice under union and intersection. Its nonzero
000054 | join-irreducibles are exactly principal ideals down(e), recovering the events
000055 | and their order. Maximal one-event-at-a-time ideal chains are the linear
000056 | extensions. Thus one relational datum specifies events, cumulative states and
000057 | allowed histories together. These are occurrence states, not quantum states
000058 | or physically stored memories; the event order remains an input.

000060 | COUNTERWORLDS: at each ideal I a fixed-P Markov growth law independently

000062 | weights, but need not select them. For a<c with an isolated b, even the first
000063 | choice a versus b has a free probability. There is no order automorphism
000064 | exchanging them. The full product of invariant Markov simplices has dimension
000065 |  beta(P)=sum_[I](number of enabled-event orbits under Aut(P)_I - 1).
000066 | The sum runs over Aut(P) orbits of nonterminal ideals. The checker obtains
000067 | unlabeled-poset counts1,2,5,16 at sizes1..4. At size3, four classes have beta0

000070 | unit-step, invariant Markov class, not uniqueness of durations or a world.
