000051 | 3. The first operator identity, before any spectrum fitting

000053 | Let C be an orthogonal projection, U a unitary on the same Hilbert space,
000054 | and S=C U C restricted to C H. Direct multiplication gives
000055 | 
000056 |  C-S* S = C U* (1-C) U C = ((1-C) U C)* ((1-C) U C).
000057 | 
000058 | Thus S is an isometry precisely when (1-C) U C=0. This says that the
000059 | actual seam action leaves the positive Cauchy-data subspace invariant.
