000018 | 2. Complete ordered response and nested reconstruction
000019 | 
000020 | Let D be a common invariant complex vector domain for an alphabet A_a.
000021 | For infinite alphabets use coefficientwise finite words and a well-defined
000022 | formal sum with finite contribution to each coefficient. Assume P^2=P,
000023 | R^2=R, PR=RP=P and P D,R D subset D. Finite-rank original finite-energy
000024 | readouts satisfy this. Hilbert positivity additionally requires orthogonal

000027 | Let x_a be formal free NONCOMMUTING variables commuting with operator
000028 | coefficients. Define T=sum x_a A_a, M=I-T and G_P=P M^-1 P.
000029 | All inverses in this section are formal in the word-length completion.
000030 | The constant term of M is I. Thus M^-1=sum T^n exists coefficientwise,
000031 | and the coefficient of x_a1 ... x_an is exactly P A_a1 ... A_an P.
000032 | This statement concerns every finite word, with no depth truncation.
000033 | 
000034 | For Q=I-P, write D_Q=Q-QTQ on QD. It has constant identity Q.
000035 | The exact Schur expression on PD is
000036 | 
000037 |   F_P(M)=P-PTP-PTQ D_Q^-1 QTP,
000038 |   G_P=F_P(M)^-1.
000039 | 
000040 | All these formal inverses exist. Crucially the variables within the memory
000041 | term KEEP THEIR ORDER. E.g. for T=xL+yJ+zJdagger with JP=PJ=P,
000042 | and L=[[K,Bdagger],[B,D]], J=diag(I,J_Q), it is

000044 |   F_P= (1-y-z)I_P-xK
000045 |        -x Bdagger (I_Q-xD-yJ_Q-zJ_Qdagger)^-1 x B.
