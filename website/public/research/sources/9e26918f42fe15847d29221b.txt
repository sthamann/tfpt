000001 | TWO-FACE RESPONSE OF THE GIVEN FREE QUARTER GRAPH
000002 | 2 November 2026. A conditional source calculation, not a physical F1 closure.
000003 | 
000004 | The source is the original geometric Cauchy graph used in R074, on two
000005 | labelled faces, G(p)=[[1-C(p),X(p)],[X(p),C(p)]], p=n+alpha, n in Z,
000006 | C(p)=1/(1+exp(beta*p)), X(p)=1/(2 cosh(beta*p/2)). The graph is a
000007 | projection. Its identification with a full physical covariance remains
000008 | an additional premise. It is not the Higgs-coupled matrix graph.
000009 | 
000010 | For two scalar face functions a,b, let A=diag(M_a,M_b). Fourier conventions
000011 | are normalized so that Parseval has no extra 2pi factor. The full response
000012 | Q(a,b)=||[A,G]||_HS^2 is sum_k (a_k,b_k)^* N_k (a_k,b_k). For k!=0,
000013 | 
000014 |  N_k=|k| [[coth(beta|k|/2), -csch(beta|k|/2)],
000015 |           [-csch(beta|k|/2), coth(beta|k|/2)]].                 (1)
000016 | 
000017 | Proof for k>0; k<0 follows by shifting the summation index. Put
000018 | C=C(p), C'=C(p-k), X=X(p), X'=X(p-k). Direct block expansion gives
000019 | 
000020 |  N11=sum_n[(C-C')^2+X^2+X'^2], N12=-2 sum_n X X'.          (2)
000021 | 
000022 | Using X^2=C(1-C), the two exact pointwise identities are
000023 | 
000024 |  (C-C')^2+X^2+X'^2=coth(beta*k/2)(C'-C),
000025 |  2XX'=csch(beta*k/2)(C'-C).                                (3)
000026 | 
000027 | They hold for every beta>0, real alpha and k>0. The total sum telescopes:
000028 | sum_n(C(n+alpha-k)-C(n+alpha))=k, since the Fermi tails are 1 and 0.
000029 | All summands in (2) decay at both ends. Thus (1) is an infinite-mode
000030 | identity; finite numerical windows in the accompanying checker are controls.
000031 | 
000032 | For k=0, N_0=d[[1,-1],[-1,1]], d=2 sum_n C_n(1-C_n).       (4)
000033 | The previously derived R066 quarter charge variance gives sum C_n(1-C_n)
000034 | =1/(8pi) at beta=4pi and alpha=1/4 or 3/4. Therefore d=1/(4pi) there.
000035 | This special identity is not asserted for arbitrary alpha or beta.
000036 | 
000037 | The harmonic Dirichlet-to-Neumann operator of a flat strip of width
000038 | L=beta/2, with outward derivatives and the same boundary normalization,
000039 | has (1) for k!=0, and N_strip,0=L^-1[[1,-1],[-1,1]]. At the original
000040 | beta=4pi its zero mode is twice (4). Thus, with the normalized orthogonal
000041 | projector Pi_0,-=1/2[[1,-1],[-1,1]] on the constant relative face vector,
000042 | 
000043 |  N_source = N_strip - (1/(2pi)) Pi_0,-,
000044 |  (1/2) N_strip <= N_source <= N_strip.                      (5)
000045 | 
000046 | The inequalities are quadratic-form inequalities: nonzero-mode matrices
000047 | are identical and positive; on the zero mode the common direction is zero
000048 | for both, and the positive relative eigenvalue is halved. For a selfadjoint
000049 | generator, its normal-ordered variance is Q/2, not Q. This does not assert
000050 | that the physical action equals either form. It reuses R066 and R074, does
000051 | not provide a new independent prediction or derive a spatial dimension.
