000202 | %=============================================================================
000203 | 
000204 | This appendix records the coefficient bookkeeping used in the suite and provides a
000205 | heuristic motivation for the CFE coefficient policy. It is not presented as a full
000206 | background-field derivation. In the declared scheme, the one-loop effective potential
000207 | for the electromagnetic coupling modulus is taken to have the form:
000208 | \begin{equation}
000209 |     U(\alpha) = \frac{1}{4}(F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu})_{\text{eff}} 
000210 |         + \text{quantum corrections}
000211 | \end{equation}
000212 | 
000213 | The adopted structure, including the Chern--Simons contribution from 11D reduction, is:
000214 | \begin{equation}
000215 |     U(\alpha) = \frac{A}{4}\alpha^4 - \frac{2}{3}A\cthree^3\alpha^3 
000216 |         - 8A\bone\cthree^6 L\,\alpha + \mathcal{O}(\alpha^5)
000217 |     \label{eq:potential}
000218 | \end{equation}
000219 | where $L = \ln(1/\phiz)$ encodes the geometric scale.
000220 | 
000221 | The modulus-closure condition $\delta\Gamma_{\mathrm{eff}}/\delta\sigma=0$ reduces to
000222 | $\partial U/\partial\alpha = 0$ (monotonic $\alpha(\sigma)$), giving:
000223 | \begin{equation}
000224 |     \frac{\partial U}{\partial\alpha} = A\alpha^3 - 2A\cthree^3\alpha^2 
000225 |         - 8A\bone\cthree^6 L = 0
000226 |     \label{eq:stationarity}
000227 | \end{equation}
000228 | 
000229 | Dividing by $A$ (which is nonzero):
000230 | \begin{equation}
000231 |     \alpha^3 - 2\cthree^3\alpha^2 - 8\bone\cthree^6 L = 0
000232 |     \label{eq:CFE-appendix}
000233 | \end{equation}
000234 | 
000235 | This is the CFE under the declared policy. The combinatorial factors are motivated by
000236 | the following diagrammatic bookkeeping:

000260 | \textbf{(ii) Factor 8 and $\cthree^6$ in $8\bone\cthree^6 L$:}
000261 | 
000262 | The log-dependent term has two components:
000263 | 
000264 | \textbf{Origin of $\cthree^6$:} The factor $\cthree^6$ arises from the product structure 
000265 | of the effective action. With $A = 2\cthree^3$ as the overall amplitude:
000266 | \begin{equation}
000267 |     8\bone\cthree^6 L = 8\bone \cdot \cthree^3 \cdot \cthree^3 \cdot L
000268 |     = 4 \cdot A \cdot \cthree^3 \cdot \bone L
000269 | \end{equation}
000270 | The factorization proceeds as follows:
000271 | \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, topsep=2pt, itemsep=1pt]
000272 |     \item One factor of $\cthree^3$ comes from three $\cthree\, aF\tilde{F}$ vertices 
000273 |         in the anomaly chain (triangle diagram).
000274 |     \item The second factor of $\cthree^3$ comes from the amplitude normalization $A/2 = \cthree^3$.
000275 |     \item The coefficient 8 decomposes as $8 = 4 \times 2$, where 4 is from $|D_2|^2$ 
000276 |         (two $\mathbb{Z}_2$ reflections) and 2 is from the double cover sheets.
000277 | \end{itemize}
000278 | \textbf{Consistency check:} $8\cthree^6 = 8 \times (8\pi)^{-6} = 8/(8\pi)^6$, which matches 
000279 | the numerical coefficient in the CFE.

000300 | \textbf{(iii) Factor 2/3 in potential:}
000301 | 
000302 | We choose the coefficient $2/3$ in the cubic term of \eqref{eq:potential} so that
000303 | differentiation produces the factor 2 in \eqref{eq:stationarity}:
000304 | \begin{equation}
000305 |     \frac{d}{d\alpha}\left(-\frac{2}{3}A\cthree^3\alpha^3\right) = -2A\cthree^3\alpha^2
000306 | \end{equation}
000307 | This choice is consistent with the integral representation used in the suite
000308 | bookkeeping: the cubic term is motivated by a triangle subgraph.

000310 | \textbf{Why no $\alpha^2$ term?}
000311 | 
000312 | The absence of an $\alpha^2$ term in the CFE is enforced by a declared renormalization
000313 | condition: the quadratic coefficient is absorbed into a counterterm fixed at the reference
000314 | point of the chosen scheme. The toy one-loop calculation shows that an $\alpha^2$ term is
000315 | generic, so the condition must be stated explicitly rather than implied by Ward identities.
000316 | The toy calculation is a structure check only (the toy $\beta$-prefactor mismatch is flagged),
000317 | not a Standard Model one-loop derivation. The resulting precision requirement is strong
000318 | ($|\epsilon_2|\lesssim 1.58\times 10^{-10}$ for 1 ppm), and is treated as a formal constraint
000319 | of the kernel EFT policy (see \texttt{alpha2\_term\_robustness\_scan}).
