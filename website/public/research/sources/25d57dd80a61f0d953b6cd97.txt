000005 | 1. Inputs and exact consistency condition
000006 | 
000007 | There are N named interfaces, with finite nonempty input/output sets I_k,O_k,

000012 | For M independent copies, w acts coordinatewise. At interface k allow EVERY
000013 | joint total deterministic map F_k:I_k^M -> O_k^M, including encoders,
000014 | decoders and constant-output coordinates. Different interfaces cannot jointly
000015 | act, but each can jointly manipulate its own wires across copies.
000016 | This full-control and replication assumption is a NEW explicit model premise.
000017 | It is not entailed by one-shot consistency or by associative composition.

000022 | part of this definition; it will follow. We classify logical processes, not
000023 | prove that such arbitrary controls are physically achievable in a closed world.

000055 | 3. Any directed cycle fails under a finite-copy experiment
000056 | 
000057 | Let the cycle be k_1->k_2->...->k_m->k_1 (indices modulo m).
000058 | For each edge r:k_r->k_(r+1), dependence supplies:

000062 | Choose a total decoder d_r:I_(k_(r+1))->{0,1} with d_r(u_r^b)=b.
000063 | Its values on other symbols are arbitrary and cannot affect a fixed point.

000066 | outputs except at k_r are fixed by the local maps to the context q_r.

000068 |  a_r^[ d_(r-1)(x_(k_r)^(r-1)) XOR epsilon_r ].
