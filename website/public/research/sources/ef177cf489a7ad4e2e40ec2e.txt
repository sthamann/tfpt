000001 | INDEPENDENT REVIEW OF THE OLD DEFECT REWEIGHTING LEMMA
000002 | ======================================================
000003 | 
000004 | 25 October 2026. Narrow source and algebra review. No Atlas, index, paper or
000005 | archived source was changed. This note does not identify the reweighting
000006 | parameter with heat time, one-particle energy, charge or occupation number.
000007 | 
000008 | Verdict
000009 | -------
000010 | 
000011 | The old formula
000012 | 
000013 |   q(alpha)=q(0) exp(-alpha d s),                         (1)
000014 | 
000015 | is exact as a RELATIVE action statement if all of the following are part of
000016 | the sector contract:
000017 | 
000018 |   * the topological insertion d s is constant on the defect sector;
000019 |   * it is absent from the trivial sector;
000020 |   * after removing this insertion, the defect-to-trivial partition ratio is
000021 |     independent of alpha, including the measure and normalization factors.
000022 | 
000023 | The archived lemma states the first two ingredients in its intended
000024 | spectral-flow normalization, but its displayed hypotheses do not state the
000025 | third one. Indeed it writes both Z_triv(alpha) and Z_def(alpha), then infers
000026 | their ratio from an additive term in S_def alone. As written, that inference
000027 | is incomplete. It is valid if "defect sector reweighting" means that alpha
000028 | changes only the displayed relative topological action, or that every other
000029 | alpha dependence is common and cancels in the ratio.
000030 | 
000031 | The old choices
000032 | 
000033 |   d=2,       s=SF=1                                    (2)
000034 | 
000035 | are retained here as the existing original normalization contract. This
000036 | review neither derives nor rejects them and does not require identifying s
000037 | with energy, charge or a number operator. Its only question is what follows
000038 | from that contract for the complete sector ratio.
000039 | 
000040 | 1. The exact complete-source identity
000041 | ---------------------------------------
000042 | 
000043 | Let X_s and X_0 be the defect and trivial configuration spaces. After putting
000044 | both sectors over fixed reference descriptions, suppose
000045 | 
000046 |   S_s[x;alpha]=S_s^0[x;alpha]+alpha d s,                (3)
000047 |   S_0[x;alpha]=S_0^0[x;alpha],                          (4)
000048 | 
000049 | where d s is independent of x. Allow the residual measures to depend on
000050 | alpha. Define
000051 | 
000052 |   Z_s^0(alpha)=integral_(X_s) exp(-S_s^0[x;alpha])
000053 |                               d mu_(s,alpha)(x),
000054 |   Z_0^0(alpha)=integral_(X_0) exp(-S_0^0[x;alpha])
000055 |                               d mu_(0,alpha)(x),
000056 |   R(alpha)=Z_s^0(alpha)/Z_0^0(alpha).                   (5)
000057 | 
000058 | Then direct factorization gives
000059 | 
000060 |   Z_s(alpha)=exp(-alpha d s) Z_s^0(alpha),
000061 |   Z_0(alpha)=Z_0^0(alpha),
000062 | 
000063 | and therefore
000064 | 
000065 |   q(alpha):=Z_s(alpha)/Z_0(alpha)
000066 |            =exp(-alpha d s) R(alpha).                  (6)
000067 | 
000068 | At alpha=0 one has q(0)=R(0). Consequently the archived form (1) is exact on
000069 | an interval precisely when
000070 | 
000071 |   R(alpha)=R(0)                                         (7)
000072 | 
000073 | throughout that interval. If R is positive and differentiable, (7) is
000074 | equivalent to the relative Ward identity
000075 | 
000076 |   partial_alpha log R(alpha)=0                          (8)
000077 | 
000078 | throughout the interval. At only one value of alpha, equation (8) proves
000079 | first-order stationarity there, not global constancy.
000080 | 
000081 | Equivalently,
000082 | 
000083 |   partial_alpha log q(alpha)
000084 |      =-d s+partial_alpha log R(alpha).                  (9)
000085 | 
000086 | Thus -d s is the complete logarithmic slope exactly when the residual sector
000087 | ratio contributes no slope.
000088 | 
000089 | If the supposed insertion is instead a field-dependent functional J[x], the
000090 | factorization in (6) generally fails already within the defect sector:
000091 | 
000092 |   Z_s(alpha)=Z_s^0(alpha)
000093 |       <exp(-alpha J)>_(s,alpha;0).                      (10)
000094 | 
000095 | Its logarithm contains the cumulants of J. The old single exponential is
000096 | therefore exact only when the identified spectral-flow insertion is
000097 | sectorwise constant, or when an equivalent zero-fluctuation theorem is
000098 | provided. The intended identification J=d SF treats it as the constant
000099 | topological label and satisfies this requirement.
000100 | 
000101 | 2. Fixed-measure Ward test
000102 | --------------------------
000103 | 
000104 | Suppose first that mu_s and mu_0 do not depend on alpha and differentiation
000105 | under both integrals is justified. Normalize expectations with the residual
000106 | weights exp(-S_s^0) and exp(-S_0^0). Then
000107 | 
000108 |   partial_alpha log Z_s^0
000109 |      =-<partial_alpha S_s^0>_s,
000110 |   partial_alpha log Z_0^0
000111 |      =-<partial_alpha S_0^0>_0.                         (11)
000112 | 
000113 | Hence
000114 | 
000115 |   partial_alpha log R
000116 |      =-<partial_alpha S_s^0>_s
000117 |       +<partial_alpha S_0^0>_0.                         (12)
000118 | 
000119 | The old reweighting follows exactly if and only if these two residual response
000120 | expectations agree for every alpha in the claimed interval. A sufficient
000121 | special case is that the residual actions are alpha independent. Another is
000122 | a common alpha deformation whose normalized expectations agree in the two
000123 | sectors. Neither conclusion follows merely from additivity of alpha d s in
000124 | the defect action.
000125 | 
000126 | 3. Alpha-dependent measures
000127 | ----------------------------
000128 | 
000129 | If the measures vary with alpha, first transport each sector to a fixed
000130 | reference space and write
000131 | 
000132 |   d mu_(r,alpha)=w_r(alpha,x) d mu_(r,*),       r=s,0.
000133 | 
000134 | Let
000135 | 
000136 |   sigma_r(alpha,x)=partial_alpha log w_r(alpha,x)       (13)
000137 | 
000138 | be the measure score. Under the usual differentiation and integrability
000139 | hypotheses,
000140 | 
000141 |   partial_alpha log R
000142 |     =-<partial_alpha S_s^0>_s+<sigma_s>_s
000143 |      +<partial_alpha S_0^0>_0-<sigma_0>_0.              (14)
000144 | 
000145 | Equation (14), rather than (12), is the complete Ward test. Moving domains,
000146 | gauge-fixing Jacobians and determinant normalizations must be included in the
000147 | scores after pullback. The archived exponential remains exact if the right
000148 | side of (14) vanishes. Measure compatibility alone means the ratio is
000149 | well-defined; cancellation still has to be stated or proved.
000150 | 
000151 | 4. Smallest decisive countercheck of the omitted condition
000152 | -----------------------------------------------------------
000153 | 
000154 | Take one configuration in each sector, with unit counting measure, and set
000155 | 
000156 |   Z_s(alpha)=exp(-2 alpha),
000157 |   Z_0(alpha)=exp(-alpha).                               (15)
000158 | 
000159 | The defect action has the exact additive term 2 alpha. Nevertheless
000160 | 
000161 |   q(alpha)=Z_s(alpha)/Z_0(alpha)=exp(-alpha),            (16)
000162 | 
000163 | not exp(-2 alpha). In the notation above,
000164 | 
000165 |   d s=2,
000166 |   Z_s^0(alpha)=1,
000167 |   Z_0^0(alpha)=exp(-alpha),
000168 |   R(alpha)=exp(alpha).                                  (17)
000169 | 
000170 | Thus (6) gives exp(-2 alpha)exp(alpha)=exp(-alpha), as it
000171 | must. This is only an algebraic diagnostic of the missing hypothesis. It is
000172 | not a second P1 source, a physical countermodel or a refutation of the
000173 | intended relative normalization.
000174 | 
000175 | If instead both sectors carry a common baseline alpha and the defect sector
000176 | has the additional topological shift 2 alpha,
000177 | 
000178 |   Z_0(alpha)=exp(-alpha),
000179 |   Z_s(alpha)=exp(-3 alpha),                             (18)
000180 | 
000181 | then their ratio is exactly exp(-2 alpha). Equation (18) is the intended
000182 | relative-action situation in its smallest form.
