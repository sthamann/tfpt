000137 | \begin{theorem}[Block 2: Uniqueness of the CP-even admissible vacuum from the primitive positive transfer]
000138 | \label{thm:cp-even-vacuum-uniqueness}
000139 | Fix finite volume or a declared cutoff. Let $\mathcal K_{+,\mathrm{adm}}^{(V)}$ be the cone of
000140 | CP-even admissible vectors in the retained basis, and let $T_V$ be the blocked admissible
000141 | transfer operator obtained from the local finite $C_6$ kernels
000142 | $\mathcal Y_y^{(\varepsilon)}$, the diagonal admissibility weights, and the two-stage selector
000143 | $\Padm$. Then $T_V$ is primitive on $\mathcal K_{+,\mathrm{adm}}^{(V)}$. Hence $T_V$ has a unique
000144 | strictly positive Perron vector $v_V^\ast$, the finite-volume CP-even admissible vacuum
000145 | $\rho^\ast_V$ of \Cref{thm:admissible-vacuum-finite-volume} is unique, and the gap above that
000146 | vacuum is strictly positive.
000147 | \end{theorem}

000149 | \begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
000150 | The primitivity lemma stated immediately above turns the transport positivity of
000151 | \TFPTxref{prop:strict-positive-c6-green} and the admissible word connectivity into an explicit
000152 | graph-theoretic criterion for $T_V$. Perron--Frobenius / Krein--Rutman then gives a unique
000153 | strictly positive Perron vector $v_V^\ast$ in the cone
000154 | $\mathcal K_{+,\mathrm{adm}}^{(V)}$, which yields the unique CP-even finite-volume vacuum. The
000155 | simplicity of the Perron root is precisely the finite-volume gap statement.
