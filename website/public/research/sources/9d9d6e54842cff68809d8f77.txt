000062 | 1. WHAT A FULL SPIN SUM DOES
000063 | 
000064 | Let the four fermionic torus blocks be written, in one standard convention, as
000065 | 
000066 |   A = Z_NS^NS = chi_0 + chi_v,
000067 |   B = Z_NS^R  = chi_0 - chi_v,
000068 |   C = Z_R^NS  = chi_s + chi_c,
000069 |   D = Z_R^R   = chi_s - chi_c.
000070 | 
000071 | Then
000072 | 
000073 |   (A+B+C+D)/2 = chi_0 + chi_s,
000074 |   (A+B+C-D)/2 = chi_0 + chi_c.
000075 | 
000076 | These are the two labelled E8 extensions of D8. The sign is the Arf or spin
000077 | SPT choice, equivalently the labelled s versus c chirality after all field
000078 | labels are retained. The unrefined q series cannot distinguish chi_s and
000079 | chi_c.

000081 | This formula contains two logically separate operations:
000082 | 
000083 |   i. projection in an untwisted sector, for example (A+B)/2=chi_0;
000084 |   ii. addition of the twisted Ramond sector with a specified relative phase.
000085 | 
000086 | The first operation alone produces the parity invariant observable algebra.
000087 | It does not produce chi_0+chi_s or chi_0+chi_c.
000088 | 
000089 | This distinction is explicit in the primary literature. Smith, Lin,
000090 | Tachikawa, and Zheng state that a bosonic theory is obtained by summing spin
000091 | structures, T_B=T_F/(-1)^F, and that the inverse fermionization carries an
000092 | Arf quadratic refinement. Their torus formula (5.1) displays all four defect
000093 | blocks and the independent Arf sign. In their E8 example the untwisted blocks
000094 | are chi_0 plus or minus chi_s and the twisted blocks are chi_c plus or minus
000095 | chi_v. Thus the existence or parity of four sectors is necessary input. It is
000096 | not itself the gauging map.
