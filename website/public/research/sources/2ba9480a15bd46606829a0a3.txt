000079 |  K(k)=A(k)S P_\TT(p)S^TA(k)^T

000138 | \begin{theorem}[Free covariant curvature field]
000139 | Equation~\eqref{eq:toe-r7-covariant-W}, with Wick's rule for higher vacuum correlations,
000140 | defines a positive free bosonic operator-valued tempered distribution on
000141 | a symmetric Fock space. Its commutators vanish at spacelike separation.
000142 | There is a strongly continuous unitary representation of the proper
000143 | orthochronous Poincare group, with invariant cyclic Fock vacuum, spectrum
000144 | in the closed forward cone, and covariant rank-four curvature transformation.
000145 | Its only one-particle helicities are $+2$ and $-2$.
000146 | \end{theorem}
000147 | \begin{proof}
000148 | For Schwartz test tensors $f$, factorization~\eqref{eq:toe-r7-covariant-positive} gives
000149 | \begin{equation}
000150 |  \|f\|_1^2=\int\frac{d^3p}{(2\pi)^3\,2r}
000151 |  \widehat f(k)^*K(k)\widehat f(k)
000152 |  =\int\frac{d^3p}{(2\pi)^3\,2r}
000153 |  \|L(k)^T\widehat f(k)\|^2\geq0.
000154 |  \label{eq:toe-r7-covariant-norm}
000155 | \end{equation}
000156 | Quotient the zero-norm tensors and complete to $\cH_1$.
000157 | Write $u_f$ for the one-particle image of the test tensor. With $a(u)$
000158 | antilinear in $u$, set $R(f)=a^\dagger(u_f)+a(u_{\bar f})$ on the
000159 | finite-particle domain of $\Gamma_s(\cH_1)$, using the Fourier convention
000160 | implicit in~\eqref{eq:toe-r7-covariant-W}. This is complex linear in $f$ and obeys
000161 | $R(f)^*=R(\bar f)$ on that domain. It gives~\eqref{eq:toe-r7-covariant-W}. The usual creation
000162 | estimate $\|a^\dagger(u)\psi_n\|\leq\sqrt{n+1}\|u\|\|\psi_n\|$
000163 | and continuity of~\eqref{eq:toe-r7-covariant-norm} make this an operator-valued tempered
000164 | distribution. Wick positivity follows from this explicit Fock
000165 | construction, not from assuming $\Pi$ is positive.
