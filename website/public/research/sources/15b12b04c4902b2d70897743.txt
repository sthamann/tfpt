000019 | 1. General complex family matrix
000020 | 
000021 | Let M be any complex 3 by 3 matrix, lifted identically to every actual
000022 | particle and antiparticle carrier slot, and put
000023 | 
000024 |   B=M P_gamma,+.

000026 | For either marked weak exterior creator X=X_wa the already checked Clifford

000029 |   A=[X,B]=M X gamma_F.                                   (1)

000031 | The actual finite real structure is J_F=K_swap followed by coefficient
000032 | conjugation.  Since K_swap X K_swap=X^dagger and
000033 | K_swap gamma_F K_swap=-gamma_F,
000034 | 
000035 |   A^dagger             =-M^dagger X^dagger gamma_F,
000036 |   J_F A J_F^-1         =-bar(M) X^dagger gamma_F,
000037 |   J_F A^dagger J_F^-1  = M^T X gamma_F.                 (2)
000038 | 
000039 | The v254 KO6 prescription has epsilon'=+1 and no factor one half.  Hence
000040 | 
000041 |   D_KO=A+A^dagger+J_F(A+A^dagger)J_F^-1
000042 |       =S X gamma_F-S^dagger X^dagger gamma_F,
000043 |   S=M+M^T.                                               (3)
000044 | 
000045 | Thus the completed coefficient is the complex symmetric part S.  The
000046 | antisymmetric part of M cancels.  There is no universal factor two: a
000047 | symmetric input doubles, while a single directed E_ij produces the two
000048 | directions E_ij+E_ji with their original coefficient.  Adding a half would
000049 | be a different normalization from the stated v254 formula.

000052 | M that D_KO is selfadjoint, gamma_F-odd and J_F-real.  This directly rules
