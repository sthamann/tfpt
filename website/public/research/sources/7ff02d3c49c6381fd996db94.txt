000106 | ## 3. Pure mapping class quotient of the original unmarked stratum
000107 | 
000108 | This section adds a different equivalence to the original four meridian model.
000109 | It does not quotient fixed reflection markings by incompatible base changes.
000110 | The standard pure mapping class generators are the two adjacent Dehn twists,
000111 | represented by squared Hurwitz moves b0^2,b1^2. The two twist generation of
000112 | PMod(S0,4) is recalled in Section2 of Ferihe Atalan, MPIM24-26:
000113 | https://archive.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/5138/1/mpim-preprint_2024-26.pdf
000114 | 
000115 | Use b_i(x,y)=(x*y*x^-1,x). With N=I+A, one multiplication gives
000116 | 
000117 | ```
000118 | b_i(ci,cj) = (N*ci+A*cj, ci), j=i+1;
000119 | b_i^2: ci -> ci+N*(ci+cj), cj -> cj+N*(ci+cj).
000120 | ```
000121 | 
000122 | N^2=0 proves these squared moves are involutions and commute on the character
000123 | tuples. The meridian product is preserved. On the product1 space b2^2=b0^2
000124 | modulo uniform character gauge: N*(c0+c1)=N*(c2+c3), so their difference is
000125 | the same N*(c0+c1) in all four positions. They preserve each peripheral
000126 | Gamma conjugacy class. Hence the full pure mapping class image on the original
000127 | Gamma moduli is C2 x C2, generated by P0=b0^2 and P1=b1^2.
