000021 | 2. The geometric reflection is not the Calderon polarization
000022 | 
000023 | Let C be the ORTHOGONAL projection onto the Cauchy data of a symmetric Dirac operator,
000024 | and let J_n be its normal Clifford action, J_n*=-J_n and J_n^2=-I. Under the usual
000025 | Dirac hypotheses the Cauchy space is Lagrangian for <J_n u,v>. Consequently
000026 | J_n C J_n*=I-C. For F=2C-I this gives J_n F=-F J_n.
000027 | The algebraic operator R_alg=J_n F is a selfadjoint involution, satisfies
000028 | R_alg F R_alg=-F and R_alg F=J_n. This is a reconstructed, generally nonlocal
000029 | operator; it is not automatically the local geometric lift of a deck reflection.
000030 | If R and F are anticommuting involutions then (RF)^2=-I, whereas R=F would give
000031 | (RF)^2=I. The algebraic quarter turn is not a derivation of charged holonomy i.
000032 | 
000033 | An explicit Dirac calculation: on a circle let A_alpha=-i partial_theta+alpha,
000034 | alpha=1/4, in the periodic frame. On TWO GRADED COMPONENTS OF THE SAME DOMAIN put
000035 | B=diag(A_alpha,-A_alpha), J_n=[[0,-I],[I,0]], and R=[[0,I],[I,0]]. Then
000036 | J_n^2=-I, R^2=I, R J_n R=-J_n, R B R=-B. The actual differential operator
000037 | D=J_n(partial_r+B) commutes with the geometric reflection psi(r)->R psi(-r).
000038 | On the half-infinite cylinder C_half=1_(0,infinity)(B), and
000039 | R C_half R=I-C_half, not C_half. Thus its Cauchy range is not the +1 eigenspace of R.
000040 | 
000041 | There is also a compact version; the conclusion is not an artifact of an infinite
000042 | collar. Double [0,ell]xS^1 to the r-circle of length 2ell with constant D as above.
000043 | The involution r->-r fixes r=0 and r=ell, and has the local lift R. The Cauchy
000044 | projection of the original finite cylinder is G_ell in section 3. On its two
000045 | boundary components the deck lift is diag(R,R). On any nonzero B mode it conjugates
000046 | G_ell=[[A,Cross],[Cross,I-A]] to [[I-A,Cross],[Cross,A]], which differs from G_ell.
000047 | In particular it cannot equal 2G_ell-I, an operator that commutes with G_ell.
000048 | Do not confuse this deck operation, which fixes each boundary, with reflection
000049 | WITHIN the original cylinder: (u,v)->(Rv,Ru); the latter preserves graph(exp(-ell B)).
000050 | 
000051 | More generally, a lift over the fixed boundary is pointwise in the bundle, while
000052 | the principal symbol of 2C-I depends on tangential cotangent direction for an
000053 | ordinary Dirac boundary. These cannot be identified just from reflection regularity.
000054 | If R_tau is instead DEFINED as 2C-I, the algebraic statement is true by definition,
000055 | but its identification with a geometric reflection has become an extra hypothesis.
000056 | This corrects the stated geometric inference; it is not a no-go for all TFPT seams.
