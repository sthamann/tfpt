000039 | There are two halves, each carrying a sign s,t in {-1,+1}. Reflection exchanges

000041 | An unnormalized symmetric joint measure is the matrix K_st. Its reflected form
000042 | is sum_st conjugate(F(s)) K_st F(t), divided by sum_st K_st for normalization.
000043 | Thus reflection positivity is exactly K positive semidefinite.

000046 | are positive (19,1), and the associated normalized transfer is primitive. For
