000039 | 2. Same operator skeleton, different insertion algebras

000041 | Fix a compact connected geometric spin collar M with a selfadjoint Dirac
000042 | realization D0, boundary B0, grading Gamma0 where applicable, real structure

000051 | diagnostic operator skeleton, not a proposed fundamental source or chosen
000052 | physical coupling. Use a common selfadjoint domain induced from D0, with a
000053 | local selfadjoint elliptic geometric boundary condition stable under smooth
000054 | scalar multiplication. Its product with I on the finite factor is also stable
