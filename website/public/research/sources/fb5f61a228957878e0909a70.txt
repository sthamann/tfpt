000284 | 7. THE DIRECT PUSHFORWARD IS NOT THE OLD RANK THREE ANCHOR
000285 | ==========================================================
000286 | 
000287 | The direct pushforward supplies a useful exclusion without choosing a new
000288 | bundle. Let E=pi_*S_0. Since pi is finite,

000290 |   rank E=3,
000291 |   chi(E)=chi(S_0)=deg(S_0)+1-g(C)=1+1-2=0.                (23)
000292 | 
000293 | On P1 this gives deg E=-3. The selected even theta characteristic is

000295 | 
000296 |   H0(P1,E)=H0(C,S_0)=0.                                  (24)
000297 | 
000298 | Every Birkhoff-Grothendieck summand therefore has degree at most -1. Their
000299 | sum is -3, hence
000300 | 
000301 |   pi_*S_0 isomorphic O(-1) direct-sum O(-1) direct-sum O(-1).              (25)
000302 | 
000303 | The original v117/P2 anchor O(-2) direct-sum O(-1)^2 is therefore not the
