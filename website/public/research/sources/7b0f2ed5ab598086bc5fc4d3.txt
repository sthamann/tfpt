000185 | Let $\phi_N=(\phi_N^1,\ldots,\phi_N^8)$ be the bosonic current obtained
000186 | from the sixteen Majorana copies by edge-projected collar bosonization,
000187 | with zero mode fixed by the $\Z_4$ charge sector.  Each complex-fermion
000188 | pair gives one boson.  The longitudinal derivative uses the frozen
000189 | lattice symbol
000190 | \[
000191 |  \omega_N(m)=\frac{N}{\pi}\left|\sin\frac{\pi m}{N}\right|,
000192 |  \qquad 0<|m|\le\frac N2.
000193 | \tag{2.9}\label{eq:latticesymbol}
000194 | \]
000195 | The edge projection is the analytic top (or bottom) eigenbranch through
000196 | the zero-energy crossing; massive bulk-complement modes are not included
000197 | in $\phi_N$.  For a fixed normalized
000198 | two-cocycle $\varepsilon_\lambda$, define on the finite-energy vectors
000199 | \[
000200 |  \Psi_{\lambda,N}(f)
000201 |  =\sum_{m}\widehat f_m\,
000202 |    \varepsilon_\lambda
000203 |    :\!\exp(i\lambda\cdot\phi_N)\!:_m .
000204 |  \tag{2.10}\label{eq:chargedfield}
000205 | \]
000206 | Normal ordering is with respect to $C_N^{(0)}$.  Formula
000207 | \eqref{eq:chargedfield} is meaningful at every finite $N$.  The theorem
000208 | does not follow merely from that fact: the normal-ordering constant and
000209 | the graph norm of the exponential can grow with $N$.
