000010 | \int_{\mathcal H_{\mathrm{adm}}}\mathcal D\varphi\,

000030 | \begin{theorem}[Exact admissible renormalization-group flow]
000031 | \label{thm:exact-admissible-rg-flow}
000032 | The admissible effective average action satisfies the exact functional flow equation
000033 | \[
000034 | \partial_t \Gamma_k[\phi]
000035 | =
000036 | \frac12\,
000037 | \operatorname{STr}_{\mathcal H_{\mathrm{adm}}}
000038 | \Bigl[
000039 | \bigl(\Gamma_k^{(2)}[\phi]+R_k\bigr)^{-1}
000040 | \partial_t R_k
000041 | \Bigr],
000042 | \qquad
000043 | t:=\log k.
000044 | \]
000045 | Thus the running couplings of the admissible theory are generated dynamically on $\Gamma_k$, not by
000046 | the static projector $\Padm$.
000047 | \end{theorem}

000049 | \begin{proof}
000050 | Differentiate the Legendre transform defining $\Gamma_k$ at fixed mean field $\phi$. Using
000051 | \[
000052 | \frac{\delta^2 W_k}{\delta J\,\delta J}
000053 | =
000054 | \bigl(\Gamma_k^{(2)}+R_k\bigr)^{-1},
000055 | \]
000056 | one obtains
000057 | \[
000058 | \partial_t \Gamma_k
000059 | =
000060 | \frac12 \operatorname{STr}
000061 | \bigl[
000062 | \bigl(\Gamma_k^{(2)}+R_k\bigr)^{-1}\partial_t R_k
000063 | \bigr].
000064 | \]
000065 | The supertrace is taken on the admissible physical sector because the regulated functional integral
000066 | itself is defined there.

000115 | \begin{theorem}[Admissible 1PI graph expansion]
000116 | \label{thm:admissible-1pi-graph-expansion}
000117 | Around any admissible background field $\phi$, the effective action admits the loop expansion
000118 | \[
000119 | \Gamma_k[\phi]
000120 | =
000121 | S_{\mathrm{adm}}[\phi]
000122 | +\frac{\hbar}{2}\operatorname{STr}\log\!\bigl(S_{\mathrm{adm}}^{(2)}[\phi]+R_k\bigr)
000123 | +\sum_{\ell\ge 2}\hbar^\ell\Gamma_{\ell,k}^{\mathrm{1PI}}[\phi].
000124 | \]
000125 | Each coefficient $\Gamma_{\ell,k}^{\mathrm{1PI}}$ is the sum of admissible one-particle-irreducible
000126 | Feynman graphs with propagator
000127 | \[
000128 | G_{k,\phi}:=\bigl(S_{\mathrm{adm}}^{(2)}[\phi]+R_k\bigr)^{-1}
000129 | \]
000130 | and vertices given by the higher functional derivatives $S_{\mathrm{adm}}^{(n)}[\phi]$.
000131 | \end{theorem}
000132 | 
000133 | \begin{proof}
000134 | Set $\varphi=\phi+\sqrt{\hbar}\eta$ and expand
000135 | \[
000136 | S_{\mathrm{adm}}[\phi+\sqrt{\hbar}\eta]
000137 | =
000138 | S_{\mathrm{adm}}[\phi]
000139 | +\frac{\hbar}{2}\langle \eta,S_{\mathrm{adm}}^{(2)}[\phi]\eta\rangle
000140 | +\sum_{n\ge 3}\frac{\hbar^{n/2}}{n!}S_{\mathrm{adm}}^{(n)}[\phi](\eta^n).
000141 | \]
000142 | The regulated functional integral therefore becomes a Gaussian measure with covariance
000143 | $G_{k,\phi}$ dressed by higher interaction vertices. Taking the logarithm produces the connected
000144 | diagram expansion, and the Legendre transform removes one-particle-reducible pieces. The resulting
000145 | coefficients are exactly the admissible 1PI graph sums recorded above.
000146 | \end{proof}
