000037 | \begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
000038 | The primitive reconstruction yields one elementary relative seam class and therefore one primitive
000039 | generator of the seam $K^1$ lattice. Along a single positive seam circuit the spectral flow changes
000040 | by one, so the associated loop has class $[u_\Sigma]=1$ and spectral flow $SF(U_\Seam)=1$. The
000041 | primitive circuit closes after one full turn, giving $\Delta_\Seam=2\pi$ and hence
000042 | $\cthree=\Delta_\Seam/(16\pi^2)=1/(8\pi)$. The carrier theorem gives
000043 | $\gamma=\tr_EY^2=5/6$, so the seam-even opening is the normalized complement of the carrier
000044 | quadratic norm rather than a new independent numerical input.
