000176 | 4. ODD FIELDS REQUIRE INTERTWINERS, NOT ONLY PARTITION FUNCTIONS
000177 | 
000178 | The existence of four traces alone would not fix odd-field correlation
000179 | functions. We retain the original Gamma vertex intertwiners, their
000180 | oscillator action, adjunction and cocycle. For an odd integral x they
000181 | shift cosets by f:
000182 |  M_0 <-> M_f,        M_h <-> M_c.                     (5)
000183 | They act within the reconstructed NS space M_0+M_f and within its R
000184 | space M_h+M_c. Relative to the bosonic L theory they connect untwisted
000185 | and twisted spaces; they are defect endpoints, not ordinary local L
000186 | fields. The last terminology describes this conditional field-theory
000187 | construction; a microscopic source for these endpoints remains missing.
000188 | 
000189 | On Gamma one explicit cocycle is
000190 |  epsilon(x,y)=(-1)^(sum_{i>j} x_i y_j).
000191 | Its commutator obeys
000192 |  epsilon(x,y)/epsilon(y,x)=(-1)^(B(x,y)+p(x)p(y)),
000193 |  p(x)=B(x,x) mod2.
000194 | Together with the vertex exchange phase this gives the inherited graded
000195 | locality. The Ramond module and the defect junctions must use compatible
000196 | intertwiners. They are retained from the original lattice quantization;
000197 | we do not purport to derive their normalization from the four numbers
000198 | A,B0,C,D or from an abstract finite discriminant group.

000200 | For example, with 0<tau<beta and a properly smeared or regulated field,
000201 | define the starting-sector two-point trace
000202 |  C_d(tau)=Tr_{M_d}[
000203 |   exp(-(beta-tau)H_d) V_{-x} exp(-tau H_{d+f}) V_x].  (6)

000205 |  (C_0+C_f)/(A+C), with W=exp(-beta H) in A,C.
000206 | The four bosonic line-insertion numerators are respectively
000207 |  C_0+C_h, C_0-C_h, C_f+C_c, C_c-C_f.

000209 | intermediate Hamiltonian H_{d+f}, hence its energies and time dependence;
000210 | it is stronger than merely reproducing a Hilbert-space dimension.
000211 | Higher closed strings of odd insertions can be treated by the same
000212 | sector bookkeeping with their inherited ordered cocycles. No claim of
000213 | a source-selected full interacting continuum measure is made here.

000218 | two-point response is supplied by P V_{-x} Q exp(-tau H_Q) Q V_x P.
