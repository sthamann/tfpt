000112 | 3. The associator obstruction and why it vanishes on H
000113 | 
000114 | First note that every multiplication channel in an object of Ext_H(U) is
000115 | nonzero. The invariant Frobenius pairing of a connected unitary extension is

000118 | multiplication by M_(-b) would give zero in the bracketing (M_a M_b)M_(-b),
000119 | whereas the bracketing M_a(M_b M_(-b)) contains the nonzero unit action on
000120 | M_a. This is a contradiction. Hence each one-dimensional fusion space below
000121 | is used by a nonzero intertwiner.
