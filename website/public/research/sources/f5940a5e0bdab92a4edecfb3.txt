000483 | 
000484 | The implication at01:397-405 from an H1 graph estimate to an arbitrarily small
000485 | relative bound is invalid. A bounded-coefficient first-order perturbation need
000486 | not be infinitesimally bounded relative to a first-order anchor.

000489 | eps<|a|. For P=-A, the sum restricted to Dom(A) is the zero operator on that
000490 | proper dense domain. It is not closed or selfadjoint, though its closure is the
000491 | bounded zero operator on the whole Hilbert space; compact resolvent is lost.

000495 |   A=sigma_x(-i partial_theta)+sigma_y(-i partial_r),
000496 |   P=a I(-i partial_r).
000497 | In periodic theta coordinates k=n+1/4. Reflection r->-r with fiber lift sigma_x

000499 |   ||P psi_m||/||A psi_m||=|a|m/sqrt(m^2+1/16)->|a|.
