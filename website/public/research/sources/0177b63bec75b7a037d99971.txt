000171 | 3. Hilbert spaces, domains, and unitary longitudinal decomposition

000184 | Define maps W_0,W_1:K -> H_cov by
000185 | 
000186 |   (W_0 g)(theta)=exp(i theta/2)  g(2 theta),
000187 |   (W_1 g)(theta)=exp(3 i theta/2)g(2 theta).             (10)

000189 | They obey the NS boundary condition because both phases change sign after
000190 | theta -> theta+2 pi. Their Fourier images are respectively
000191 | 
000192 |   W_0 exp(i m x) = exp(i(2m+1/2)theta),
000193 |   W_1 exp(i m x) = exp(i(2m+3/2)theta).                 (11)

000195 | These are the even-n and odd-n parts of the complete NS basis
000196 | exp(i(n+1/2)theta). They are orthogonal and their union is complete.

000199 | 
000200 |   integral_0^2pi |g(2theta)|^2 dtheta/(2pi)
000201 |     = integral_0^2pi |g(x)|^2 dx/(2pi),                 (12)

000203 | because x=2theta traverses the base circle twice and the factor from
000204 | dx=2dtheta cancels that multiplicity. Hence

000210 | (F1) gives the full Fourier and Sobolev decomposition just described.
