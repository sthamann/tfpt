000009 | Does the signed Petersen radial transfer from v808 admit a natural marked

000013 | Answer
000014 | ------
000015 | 
000016 | Yes for the positive transfer, and no for the literal v487 deck marking.
000017 | 
000018 | The reversible Petersen operator has a canonical polar decomposition
000019 | 
000020 |       P = J_P |P|.
000021 | 
000022 | There is an explicit isometry W with
000023 | 
000024 |       W |P| W^{-1} = M_lazy.
000025 | 
000026 | It maps the stationary shell mark to the family vacuum, the hypercharge mode
000027 | to the 2/3 even family mode, and the third v808 mode to the 1/3 odd family
000028 | mode.  But under this same forced spectral mapping,
000029 | 
000030 |       W J_P W^{-1} = K = Z Delta,
000031 | 
000032 | where Delta is the v487 deck swap and
000033 | 
000034 |       Z = diag(1,-1,-1)
000035 | 
000036 | is the canonical reflection across the Perron line.  Consequently no static
000037 | or time dependent isometric intertwiner can carry both the positive transfer
000038 | and the literal deck marking Delta.  The obstruction already occurs on the
000039 | one dimensional 2/3 eigenspace.
000040 | 
000041 | There are two exact surviving constructions:
000042 | 
000043 | 1. a signed static correspondence
000044 | 
000045 |       W P W^{-1} = K M_lazy;
000046 | 
000047 | 2. a period two correspondence to the positive lazy walk,
000048 | 
000049 |       W_n = K^n W,
000050 |       W_{n+1} P W_n^{-1} = M_lazy.
000051 | 
000052 | The period two frame transports the hypercharge sign rather than discarding
000053 | it.  Equivalently, the canonical bipartite double cover turns P into the
000054 | positive lazy walk by a fixed six dimensional isometry.  Its deck exchange is
000055 | transported to a K twisted exchange, not to the plain v487 deck.
000056 | 
000057 | This is stronger than the old equality after six powers.  The two transfers
000058 | are exactly equivalent at every even time.  Their odd steps differ by K.
000059 | The transported shell multiplication operator is not conserved: its exact
000060 | one step defect has rank two.  Also, Z has an older source candidate: v409
000061 | sets the same matrix as the seam sheet involution.  What remains absent is a
000062 | source intertwiner proving that this involution and the v487 deck use the same

000090 | 2. Reversible radial Hilbert space
000091 | ----------------------------------
000092 | 
000093 | The shell sizes are 1, 3 and 6.  The radial Markov operator is
000094 | 
000095 |           [ 0    1    0  ]
000096 |       P = [1/3   0   2/3 ],
000097 |           [ 0   1/3  2/3 ]
000098 | 
000099 | with stationary measure
000100 | 
000101 |       pi = (1,3,6)/10.
000102 | 
000103 | Detailed balance holds entry by entry:
000104 | 
000105 |       pi_i P_ij = pi_j P_ji.
000106 | 
000107 | Thus P is selfadjoint on H_pi = R^3 with scalar product
000108 | 
000109 |       <f,g>_pi = sum_i pi_i f_i g_i.
000110 | 
000111 | The three source marked eigenfunctions from v808 are
000112 | 
000113 |       f0 = (1,1,1),                 eigenvalue  1,
000114 |       q  = (6,-4,1),                eigenvalue -2/3,
000115 |       r  = (3,1,-1),                eigenvalue  1/3.
000116 | 
000117 | The second vector is literally the shell hypercharge observable evaluated on
000118 | the charge pure classes e^c, u^c and Q.  Their squared H_pi norms are 1, 9 and
000119 | 9/5 respectively.  Hence
000120 | 
000121 |       b0 = f0,
000122 |       bY = q/3,
000123 |       br = sqrt(5) r/3
000124 | 
000125 | is an orthonormal marked basis.
000126 | 
000127 | Conjugating by D^(1/2), D = diag(pi), gives the ordinary symmetric matrix
000128 | 
000129 |                [ 0       1/sqrt(3)       0       ]

000131 |                [ 0        sqrt(2)/3       2/3     ]

000135 | 3. Canonical polar decomposition
000136 | --------------------------------
000137 | 
000138 | Since P is invertible and selfadjoint on H_pi, its polar factors are unique.
000139 | Interpolation on the three simple eigenvalues gives coordinate free formulas
000140 | 
000141 |       |P| = (4/5)P^2 - (1/15)P + (4/15)I,
000142 |       J_P = -(6/5)P^2 + (8/5)P + (3/5)I.
000143 | 
000144 | In the original shell coordinates these are
000145 | 
000146 |             [ 8/15  -1/15   8/15]
000147 |       |P| = [-1/45  32/45  14/45],
000148 |             [ 4/45   7/45  34/45]
000149 | 
000150 | and
000151 | 
000152 |             [ 1/5    8/5   -4/5]
000153 |       J_P = [ 8/15  -1/15   8/15].
000154 |             [-2/15   4/15  13/15]
000155 | 
000156 | Direct multiplication gives
000157 | 
000158 |       J_P^2 = I,       J_P |P| = |P| J_P,       P = J_P |P|.
000159 | 
000160 | Both factors are selfadjoint for the pi weighted scalar product.  Their action
000161 | on the marked basis is
000162 | 
000163 |              b0    bY    br
000164 |       |P|     1    2/3   1/3,
000165 |       J_P     1     -1    1.
000166 | 
000167 | The negative sign is therefore attached canonically to the hypercharge mode.
