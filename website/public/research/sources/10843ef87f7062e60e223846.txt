000069 | 4. Exact diagnostic and full continuous scalar-symbol classification
000070 | 
000071 | Use periodic trivialization H=L2(S1), B_alpha=N+alpha, N=-i d/dtheta,
000072 | alpha=1/4 or 3/4, and C e_n=e_n for n>=0, zero for n<0. This is equivalent
000073 | to the usual alpha-twisted boundary domain. The quarter transition is
000074 | exp(2pi i B_alpha)=exp(2pi i alpha) I and is kept throughout.
000075 | 
000076 | For |a|<1 write b_a(z)=(z-a)/(1-conj(a)z). A finite rational unimodular
000077 | symbol is a phase times a quotient of finite products of these factors.
000078 | Cancel common factors first. Its winding is numerator degree minus
000079 | denominator degree. For S=C M_u C, zero leakage implies M_u 1=u belongs to
000080 | H2. Conversely analyticity and boundedness of u preserve H2 and, since
000081 | |u|=1, multiplication is an isometry there. A noncancelled denominator
000082 | factor has a pole in the disk. Hence in this rational class:
000083 | 
000084 |  zero leakage <=> no denominator after cancellation.
000085 | 
000086 | Together with winding one this forces exactly ONE Blaschke factor, times
000087 | a constant phase. This classifies the full finite-rational-symbol family,
000088 | not just a finite root grid. Real rational roots in the executable are
000089 | exact diagnostics; the argument also covers complex roots in the disk.
000090 | 
000091 | In fact this classification extends to EVERY continuous scalar unimodular
000092 | symbol u on the circle. Zero leakage implies that its negative Fourier
000093 | coefficients vanish. Analytic Fejer sums then converge uniformly to u,
000094 | so u extends to the disk algebra A(D). Since |u|=1 on the boundary,
000095 | continuity gives a zero-free collar of that boundary; all zeros are in a
000096 | compact interior subset and there are finitely many. Divide out their
000097 | Blaschke factors. The quotient v and 1/v are both in A(D), with unit
000098 | boundary modulus. The maximum principle applied to each forces |v|=1
000099 | inside; hence v is constant. By the argument principle, winding one gives
000100 | one Blaschke factor. This is an analytic theorem about the whole scalar
000101 | Hardy class; a finite executable grid is only its diagnostic check.

000106 | 2-(1+a)/(1-a). At a=1/4 that bound is 1/3.
000107 | For m>=1 its negative Laurent coefficient is
000108 | 
000109 |  [z^(-m)]u_a=(1-a^2) a^(m+1).
