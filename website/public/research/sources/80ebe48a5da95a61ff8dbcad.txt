000069 | 2. Small exact counterexample
000070 | -----------------------------
000071 | 
000072 | Let X be a centered real Gaussian variable smeared at positive Euclidean time,
000073 | and write Y = Theta X for its reflected copy.  Choose the covariance
000074 | 
000075 |        E[X^2] = E[Y^2] = a > 0,
000076 |        E[YX] = c > 0,
000077 | 
000078 | with 0 < c <= a, so that the elementary covariance matrix
000079 | 
000080 |        [ a  c ]
000081 |        [ c  a ]
000082 | 
000083 | is positive semidefinite.  Such a centered Gaussian field is reflection
000084 | positive on the one-dimensional positive-time test space.  Explicitly, put
000085 | rho=c/a and expand a polynomial F in Hermite polynomials for the normalized
000086 | variable X/sqrt(a): F=sum_n f_n H_n.  The bivariate Gaussian identity gives
000087 | 
000088 |        E[(Theta F)F] = sum_n n! rho^n |f_n|^2 >= 0.
000089 | 
000090 | Thus positivity holds on the whole polynomial algebra, not merely at degree
000091 | one.
000092 | 
000093 | Use the positive-time polynomial basis
000094 | 
000095 |        F_0 = 1,
000096 |        F_1 = X^2.
000097 | 
000098 | The genuine OS Gram matrix is
000099 | 
000100 |        G_ij = E[(Theta F_i) F_j].
000101 | 
000102 | Wick's rule gives
000103 | 
000104 |        E[X^2]       = a,
000105 |        E[Y^2 X^2]   = E[Y^2]E[X^2] + 2E[YX]^2
000106 |                      = a^2 + 2c^2.
000107 | 
000108 | Hence
000109 | 
000110 |        G_full = [ 1       a          ],
000111 |                 [ a   a^2 + 2c^2     ]
000112 | 
000113 | and
000114 | 
000115 |        det G_full = 2c^2 > 0.
000116 | 
000117 | Thus the full moment functional is positive on span{1,X^2}.
000118 | 
000119 | Now make exactly the erroneous replacement of a full four-point entry by its
000120 | connected four-point cumulant.  For a centered Gaussian,
000121 | 
000122 |        kappa_4(Y,Y,X,X) = 0,

000124 | while the two-point cumulant is kappa_2(X,X)=a.  The resulting cumulant
000125 | substitution is

000127 |        G_trunc = [ 1   a ],
