000294 | \begin{theorem}[Renormalized observable hierarchy from the admissible 1PI action]
000295 | \label{thm:operational-completeness}
000296 | There exist functors
000297 | \[
000298 | \Rren:\mathbf{TFPT}^{\mathrm{rig}}\to\GrenTFPT,
000299 | \qquad
000300 | \Mphys:\GrenTFPT\to\OphysTFPT,
000301 | \qquad
000302 | \Mscheme:\OphysTFPT\to\OschemeTFPT/\SchGrp
000303 | \]
000304 | with the following properties:
000305 | \begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{(O\arabic*)}, leftmargin=2em, topsep=3pt, itemsep=3pt]
000306 |     \item \textbf{Reality first.}
000307 |     Main-text observables factor through $\Mphys\!\circ\!\Rren$ and therefore belong to
000308 |     $\OphysTFPT$ before any scheme choice is made.
000309 |     \item \textbf{Unique factorization through the basis.}
000310 |     Every physical readout row and every appendix scheme row factors through exactly one component
000311 |     package of $\Bind(\Tstar)$ via a declared physical or scheme map.
000312 |     \item \textbf{Scheme covariance.}
000313 |     For every scheme morphism $\psi:s\to s'$ one has
000314 |     \[
000315 |     \psi\!\left(\Mscheme^{(s)}(\Mphys(\Rren(\Tstar)))\right)
000316 |     =
000317 |     \Mscheme^{(s')}(\Mphys(\Rren(\Tstar))).
000318 |     \]
000319 |     \item \textbf{No hidden continuous fit parameter.}
000320 |     Any continuous freedom in a readout row is either a declared scheme degree of freedom in
000321 |     $\SchGrp$ or already a theorem-level branch parameter fixed in $\Tstar$.
000322 |     \item \textbf{Complete kill surface.}
000323 |     Every admissible empirical kill test of the manuscript is the pullback of a component test on
000324 |     $\OphysTFPT$ and only afterwards, if needed, of a scheme representative in $\OschemeTFPT$.
000325 | \end{enumerate}
000326 | \end{theorem}

000328 | \begin{proof}
000329 | Define $\Rren$ by sending the rigid object $\Tstar$ to the renormalized 1PI package extracted from
000330 | the exact admissible flow of \Cref{thm:exact-admissible-rg-flow}. Its infrared endpoint is
000331 | \[
000332 | \Gamma_{\mathrm{TFPT}}^{\mathrm{ren}}[\Phi_c]
000333 | =
000334 | \lim_{k\to0}\Gamma_k[\Phi_c],
000335 | \]
000336 | equivalently the Legendre transform of the retained admissible generating functional after removing
000337 | the infrared regulator. By \Cref{thm:admissible-1pi-graph-expansion}, the same object carries the
000338 | admissible 1PI graph expansion around each admissible background. Reflection positivity, lower
000339 | boundedness, and coercivity on the admissible sector ensure the convexity needed for this Legendre
000340 | construction in a neighborhood of the closed branch. Hence the dressed inverse propagators,
000341 | determinant-response kernels, and cosmology saddle system belong to one and the same renormalized
000342 | package $\GrenTFPT$.
000343 | 
000344 | Next define $\Mphys$ by reading physical observables from that 1PI package before any scheme choice:
000345 | pole masses are the zeros of the dressed inverse propagators, mixing data are the branch invariants
000346 | of the corresponding residue matrices, determinant-response observables are read from the exact
000347 | response kernels, and cosmology rows factor through the same FRW / reheating / Boltzmann interface
000348 | block. Final late-time numerical representatives are attached only after the declared comparison
000349 | map. This proves \textbf{(O1)}.
000350 | 
000351 | For \textbf{(O2)}, the factorization through the minimal basis package $\Bind(\Tstar)$ is exactly
000352 | the theorem-level content of the rewritten sector split. The flavor rows factor through
000353 | $F_{\mathrm{fl}}$, neutrino rows through $N_\nu$, determinant-response rows through
000354 | $R_{\mathrm{CS}}$, strong-CP rows through $C_{\mathrm{CP}}$, cosmology rows through
000355 | $C_{\mathrm{cos}}$, and the retained seed $\phiz$ survives only in the UV kernel package
000356 | $K_{\mathrm{UV}}$. No row uses two basis packages at once.
000357 | 
000358 | For \textbf{(O3)}, every scheme morphism in $\SchGrp$ acts only after $\Mphys$ has produced a
000359 | physical observable package. Therefore threshold transfer, finite renormalization, and snapshot
000360 | change act on scheme representatives without altering the underlying physical point, exactly as in
000361 | the displayed covariance identity.
000362 | 
000363 | For \textbf{(O4)}, every continuous parameter entering a readout row is already fixed either by the
000364 | closed branch $\Tstar$ or by the declared scheme object $s\in\SchGrp$. Since $\Rren$ and $\Mphys$
000365 | are defined from the fixed admissible measure and its 1PI action, no extra continuous fit parameter
000366 | can appear in between.
000367 | 
000368 | Finally, \textbf{(O5)} follows because every empirical kill criterion in the manuscript is attached
000369 | to one of the physical rows in $\OphysTFPT$ and only afterwards, if needed, represented by a scheme
000370 | object in $\OschemeTFPT/\SchGrp$. Thus the whole observable hierarchy factors through the same
000371 | admissible 1PI action and then through the declared physical / scheme split.
000372 | \end{proof}
