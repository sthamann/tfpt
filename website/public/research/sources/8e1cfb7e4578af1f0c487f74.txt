000032 | \paragraph{Assumptions and preregistered obligations.}

000034 | Let a periodic cubic lattice have $V=L^3$ cells and spacing $a>0$.  The six
000035 | orthonormal symmetric-tensor coordinates are ordered as
000036 | $(11,22,33,23,13,12)$, with the off-diagonal basis tensors normalized by
000037 | $1/\sqrt2$.  Component $A$ lives at $x+a m_A/2$, where
000038 | \[
000039 |  m_A=(000,000,000,011,101,110).
000040 | \]
000041 | We use the physical-position Fourier phase
000042 | $\exp[-\ii k\cdot(x+a m_A/2)]$.  The staggered derivative symbol is
000043 | \[
000044 |  \ii\kappa_i^{(a)}(k),\qquad
000045 |  \kappa_i^{(a)}(k)=\frac{2}{a}\sin\frac{ak_i}{2},\qquad
000046 |  r_a(k)=|\kappa^{(a)}(k)|.
000047 | \]
000048 | It vanishes only at the homogeneous momentum in the first Brillouin zone.
000049 | The complete homogeneous canonical block is removed as an additional
000050 | zero-mean prescription.  It is not declared gauge.

000094 | \paragraph{The exact spin-two commutator projector.}
000095 | 
000096 | For $\kappa\ne0$, let $P=I_3-\kappa\kappa^T/r^2$.  The orthogonal
000097 | spin-two projector on symmetric tensors is
000098 | \[
000099 |  (P_{\TT}h)_{ij}=P_{ia}P_{jb}h_{ab}
000100 |  -\frac12P_{ij}P_{ab}h_{ab}.                       \tag{4}
000101 | \]
000102 | It has rank two, $P_{\TT}^2=P_{\TT}=P_{\TT}^T$, and its range is
000103 | $\{h:h\kappa=0,\ \Tr h=0\}$.
000104 | 
000105 | \begin{theorem}[Polynomial curvature projector]
000106 | For every $\kappa\ne0$,
000107 | \[
000108 |  C:=K_qK_pK_q=r^4P_{\TT}.                          \tag{5}
000109 | \]
000110 | Consequently
000111 | \[
000112 |  C^T=C\ge0,\quad C^2=r^4C,\quad \Tr C=2r^4,
000113 | \quad \operatorname{rank}C=2,
000114 | \quad CB^T=0,\quad Ct=0.                           \tag{6}
000115 | \]
000116 | Although (4) has inverse powers of $r$, $C$ itself is a homogeneous
000117 | degree-four polynomial in $\kappa$.
000118 | \end{theorem}
000119 | 
000120 | \begin{proof}
000121 | The TT space is $\Ker B\cap t^\perp$.  If $h$ is TT, (1) gives
000122 | $K_qh=r^2h$ and $K_ph=h$, hence $Ch=r^4h$.  Equations (3) give
000123 | $CB^T=0$.  They also give
000124 | $Ct=K_qK_ps=K_qB^T\kappa=0$.  The scalar and momentum constraints are
000125 | independent at $r>0$, so
000126 | $\Ran B^T+\operatorname{span}\{t\}$ is four-dimensional and is the
000127 | orthogonal complement of the two-dimensional TT space.  Thus $C$ is $r^4$
000128 | on TT and zero on its orthogonal complement, proving (5).  The properties in
000129 | (6) follow immediately.  Finally, $C=K_qK_pK_q$ is polynomial; equivalently
000130 | the two factors of $r^{-2}$ implicit in (4) cancel against $r^4$.  A direct
000131 | component multiplication of (4) is included in the executable checker.
