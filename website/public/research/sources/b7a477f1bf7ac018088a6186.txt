000120 | 2. Direct algebraic fixed-point and infinity census
000121 | ====================================================
000122 | 
000123 | A fixed point of rho_(a,b) can be written
000124 | 
000125 |   x=e^(i b pi/4)t,       y=i^a v,       t,v real.

000127 | The curve equation becomes
000128 | 
000129 |   v^2=(-1)^a[(-1)^b t^4-1].                              (8)
000130 | 
000131 | For (a,b)=(1,1) or (1,3), this is
000132 | 
000133 |   v^2=t^4+1.

000135 | Its positive and negative graphs close separately at the two points at
000136 | infinity, giving two ovals.  For completeness, if
000137 | 
000138 |   u=1/x,       eta=y/x^2,

000148 | infinity points, so these real forms are genuinely fixed-point free.
