000018 | 1. Independent Kahler-zero check
000019 | --------------------------------
000020 | On a finite original regulator, let h=h* and ell>0. Then R=exp(-ell h)
000021 | is positive selfadjoint. Any differentiable real physical variation of
000022 | h within this chart has a selfadjoint transfer tangent X=delta R. Indeed
000023 |  delta R=-int_0^ell exp[-(ell-s)h] delta h exp[-s h] ds;
000024 | taking its adjoint and changing s->ell-s gives the same operator.
000025 | The statement also holds for a real ell variation.
000026 | 
000027 | For the Grassmannian graph chart, use the actual standard potential
000028 |  K(R)=log det(I+R*R).
000029 | The ambient form on real tangent vectors is, up to the chosen overall
000030 | positive normalization,
000031 |  omega_R(X,Y)=2 Im Tr[(I+R*R)^-1 X* (I+RR*)^-1 Y].
000032 | For R=R* and X=X*,Y=Y*, both inverse factors equal A=(I+R^2)^-1, so
000033 |  omega_R(X,Y)=2 Im Tr(A X A Y)=0.                        (1)
000034 | Proof: conjugating the trace gives Tr(Y A X A), which by cyclicity is
000035 | Tr(A X A Y). Thus the trace is real. No commutation between X,Y,A is
000036 | assumed. The displayed zero proof is correct.
000037 | 
000038 | Important scope:
000039 | * The induced metric is generally nonzero: Tr(A X A X)=
000040 |   Tr[(A^(1/2) X A^(1/2))^2]>=0, strictly positive for nonzero finite X.
000041 |   Zero symplectic pullback does not mean a constant potential or zero
000042 |   Riemannian volume.
000043 | * The original map R(H,Hbar) is not holomorphic merely because H uses
000044 |   complex coordinates. Computing partial_H partial_Hbar K(R(H,Hbar))
000045 |   is not the pullback of the ambient Kahler form without the required
000046 |   holomorphic-map hypothesis. These two operations cannot be substituted.
000047 | * The given original h has both spectral signs in its unbounded circle
000048 |   version. R=exp(-ell h) may then be unbounded, and logdet(I+R*R) need
000049 |   not exist. The finite proof extends only with actual domains, traces
000050 |   and cyclicity justified. The bounded Cauchy graph remains meaningful
000051 |   in the original source; that fact alone does not justify this logdet.
000052 | * A genuinely normal-dependent ordered transfer need not be selfadjoint
000053 |   even when each instantaneous h is. Equation(1) is not asserted for
000054 |   that broader original dynamical-boson source.
