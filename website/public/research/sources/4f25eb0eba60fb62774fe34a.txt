000120 | 2. Full Pin-minus monodromy and the longitudinal reduction
000121 | ------------------------------------------------------------
000122 | 
000123 | For the flat Moebius band, write the one-step deck transformation as
000124 | 
000125 |   (s,w) -> (s+1,-w).
000126 | 
000127 | On transverse pinor data let
000128 | 
000129 |   P phi(w)=phi(-w),
000130 |   J=sigma_1 P,
000131 |   K_j=ker(J-jI),                 j in {+1,-1}.          (F1)
000132 | 
000133 | For Pin-minus the two lifts have c=+i or c=-i, and an equivariant section
000134 | satisfies
000135 | 
000136 |   Psi(s+1,.)=c J Psi(s,.),
000137 |   Psi(s+2,.)=-Psi(s,.).                               (F2)
000138 | 
000139 | Thus the orientable double is NS. On K_j the one-step scalar phase is cj.
000140 | For c=+i this gives shift 1/4 on K_+ and shift 3/4 on K_-; for c=-i the two
000141 | assignments are exchanged. Hilbert-valued Fourier expansion and Parseval
000142 | give the orthogonal and complete decomposition. The same decomposition
000143 | holds on the geometric Sobolev domains because weak longitudinal and
000144 | transverse derivatives respect the two-sector coefficient characterization.
000145 | 
000146 | This is precisely the full version of the scalar coefficient model below.
000147 | Equations (10)--(16) suppress K_+ and K_- after their orthogonal projections;
000148 | they do not infer the phases from the fibre matrix sigma_1 alone. A fibre
000149 | eigenvalue determines a phase only after the transverse reflection parity is
000150 | included in J.
