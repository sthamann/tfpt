000034 | exactly as proved in ELLIPTIC-GREEN-RP.txt. The checkerboard union Omega is

000053 | 1. The checkerboard is not a Schottky half
000054 | ==========================================
000055 | 
000056 | Use rho=hS and recenter the first coordinate by
000057 | 
000058 |   tau=t-1/4.
000059 | 
000060 | Then
000061 | 
000062 |   rho(tau,s)=(-tau,s),
000063 | 
000064 | and its fixed circles are tau=0 and tau=1/2 modulo one. Its two Schottky
000065 | halves are the annuli
000066 | 
000067 |   A_+={0<tau<1/2},
000068 |   A_-={-1/2<tau<0}.
000069 | 
000070 | The four edges of Fix(S) cut each annulus into two rectangular disks. Since
000071 | rho covers x -> 1/bar x, it sends Omega to p^(-1)({|x|>1}). The open disk
000072 | {|x|<1} contains no branch value, so its inverse image has two components
000073 | exchanged by the deck map h. In the centered coordinates h sends tau to
000074 | -tau and hence exchanges A_+ with A_-. Therefore the two components of
000075 | Omega are opposite checkerboard disks, one contained in A_+ and one
000076 | contained in A_-. In particular Omega is not a subset of A_+ or A_-.

000079 | the vertical and horizontal presentations. This is the exact geometric
000080 | difference from the previous annulus theorem: that theorem chose one whole
000081 | component of E\Fix(rho), whereas the actual graph side takes one disk from
000082 | each component.
