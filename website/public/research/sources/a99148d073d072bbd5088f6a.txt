000018 |  [sA(x)sB(y)+sB(x)sA(y)]/[2(x-y)] * sqrt(dx/s(x))*sqrt(dy/s(y)).
000019 | The unwanted opposite-sheet pole cancels. The diagonal residue is one.

000021 | ones. This is established primary mathematics, not a new kernel formula.
