000008 | Let the normal double be an antiperiodic circle of length 2 ell, let the

000011 |   T=h^2+mu^2 >=0,  E=sqrt(T).
000012 | 
000013 | For the scalar second order operator -partial_n^2+T, with all finite internal
000014 | multiplicities retained in Tr, zeta functional calculus gives, up to a
000015 | T-independent normalization,
000016 | 
000017 |   F_AP(T)=log det_zeta,AP(-partial_n^2+T)
000018 |          =Tr[2 ell E+2 log(1+exp(-2 ell E))]
000019 |          =Tr[2 log(2 cosh(ell E))].                    (1)
000020 | 
000021 | For an arbitrary selfadjoint matrix direction Q=dot(T), including
000022 | [T,Q]!=0, trace cyclicity and the Frechet trace identity give
000023 | 
000024 |   dot(F_AP)=ell Tr[E^-1 tanh(ell E) Q].                 (2)
000025 | 
000026 | The split in C59 is therefore algebraically correct:
000027 | 
000028 |   dot(F_bulk)=ell Tr(E^-1 Q),
000029 | 
000030 |   dot(F_glue)
000031 |     =-2 ell Tr[E^-1 exp(-ell E) X_E Q],                (3)
000032 | 
000033 |   X_E=(2 cosh(ell E))^-1.

000035 | No commuting approximation is used in (2) or (3). Operator level derivatives
000036 | still require divided differences; only after the trace may they be written

000038 | continuous limit at E=0. The bulk and gluing pieces separately contain E^-1
000039 | and require a gap or a common limiting prescription; their singular parts
000040 | cancel in the total AP determinant derivative.
