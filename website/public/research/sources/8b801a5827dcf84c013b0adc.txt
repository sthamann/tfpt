000274 | \begin{theorem}[Stratified master functional and continuous closure]
000275 | \label{thm:continuous-closure-from-master-functional}
000276 | On the retained admissible branch, the projected modular free energy factorizes as

000278 | \mathcal M_{d^\star_{\mathrm{disc}}}(\xi)
000279 | =
000280 | \Gamma_{U(1)}(\alpha)
000281 | +
000282 | \Gamma_{\mathrm{grav}}(\chigeo)

000285 | +
000286 | \Gamma_{\mathrm{vac}}(\rho_{\mathrm{vac}})

000289 | Its admissible critical points are therefore exactly the solutions of
000290 | \[
000291 | \partial_\alpha\Gamma_{U(1)}(\alpha)=0,
000292 | \qquad
000293 | \partial_{\chigeo}\Gamma_{\mathrm{grav}}(\chigeo)=0,
000294 | \qquad
000295 | \partial_\delta\Gamma_{\mathrm{tr}}(\delta_{\mathrm{ph}})=0,
000296 | \qquad
000297 | \delta_\rho\Gamma_{\mathrm{vac}}(\rho_{\mathrm{vac}})=0.
000298 | \]
000299 | Equivalently, the continuous closure laws are
000300 | \[
000301 | F_{U(1)}(\alpha)=0,
000302 | \qquad
000303 | \mathcal R_{\mathrm{grav}}=0,
000304 | \qquad
000305 | P_{\mathrm{tr}}(\delta_{\mathrm{ph}})=0,
000306 | \qquad
000307 | \delta_\rho\Gamma_{\mathrm{vac}}(\rho_{\mathrm{vac}})=0.
000308 | \]
000309 | In particular the continuous closure equations are derived from one primitive variational object
000310 | rather than imposed as independent sector primitives. Equivalently, the stationary points of the
000311 | stratified master functional $\mathbb M$ are exactly the stationary points of
000312 | $\mathcal M_{d^\star_{\mathrm{disc}}}$ on the minimal admissible stratum.
000313 | \end{theorem}

000315 | \begin{proof}
000316 | By construction,
000317 | \[
000318 | \mathbb M(\mathfrak s)=+\infty
000319 | \]
000320 | off the unitary-equivalence class of the minimal admissible bordism $\mathcal B_{\min}$. Hence every
000321 | stationary point of $\mathbb M$ must lie on that minimal stratum, where the barrier vanishes and
000322 | \[
000323 | \mathbb M(\mathfrak s)=\mathcal M_{d^\star_{\mathrm{disc}}}(\xi).
000324 | \]
000325 | 
000326 | Now apply \Cref{cor:derived-post-carrier-kernel}: all continuous terms are evaluated on one and the
000327 | same retained sector $\operatorname{Ran}(\Padm)$. On that branch the family, determinant, transport,
000328 | and vacuum projectors commute, and the physical Hilbert space splits into orthogonal direct summands
000329 | corresponding to the electromagnetic, geometric, transport, and vacuum blocks. For a block-diagonal
000330 | operator, the relative partition function factorizes, the zeta determinant factorizes, and the eta
000331 | invariant is additive. Therefore the logarithms split $\mathcal M_{d^\star_{\mathrm{disc}}}$ into a
000332 | sum of four sector functionals plus a constant.
000333 | 
000334 | The $U(1)$ block is exactly the already-defined electromagnetic effective action, so
000335 | \[
000336 | \partial_\alpha\Gamma_{U(1)}(\alpha)=F_{U(1)}(\alpha).
000337 | \]
000338 | The geometric block is the reduced spectral Einstein functional, hence
000339 | \[
000340 | \partial_{\chigeo}\Gamma_{\mathrm{grav}}(\chigeo)=\mathcal R_{\mathrm{grav}}.
000341 | \]
000342 | The transport block is the logarithmic resolvent determinant of the exact $C_6$ transfer
000343 | denominator, so
000344 | \[
000345 | \partial_\delta\Gamma_{\mathrm{tr}}(\delta_{\mathrm{ph}})
000346 | =
000347 | P_{\mathrm{tr}}(\delta_{\mathrm{ph}}).
000348 | \]
000349 | Finally the vacuum block is by definition the admissible vacuum effective action, so
000350 | \[
000351 | \delta_\rho\Gamma_{\mathrm{vac}}(\rho_{\mathrm{vac}})=0
000352 | \]
000353 | is exactly its Euler--Lagrange equation. Thus the four continuous closure equations are the
000354 | Euler--Lagrange equations of one projected modular free energy.
000355 | \end{proof}
