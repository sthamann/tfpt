000226 | \begin{lemma}[Primitive trace-balanced carrier]
000227 | \label{lem:primitive-trace-balanced-carrier}
000228 | Let $Y$ be an endomorphism of a finite-dimensional carrier $E$ such that
000229 | \[
000230 | 6Y^2-Y-\mathbf 1=0,
000231 | \qquad
000232 | \tr_EY=0,
000233 | \]
000234 | and assume the realization is primitive in the sense that $E$ is not a nontrivial direct sum of
000235 | identical trace-balanced $Y$-modules. Then
000236 | \[
000237 | \operatorname{Spec}(Y)=\left\{-\frac13,\frac12\right\},
000238 | \qquad
000239 | \dim\ker\!\left(Y+\frac13\mathbf 1\right)=3,
000240 | \qquad
000241 | \dim\ker\!\left(Y-\frac12\mathbf 1\right)=2.
000242 | \]
000243 | Equivalently,
000244 | \[
000245 | \rank E=5,
000246 | \qquad
000247 | E=E_3\oplus E_2,
000248 | \qquad
000249 | Y=-\frac13P_3+\frac12P_2.
000250 | \]
000251 | \end{lemma}

000254 | The quadratic polynomial factors as
000255 | \[
000256 | (2Y-\mathbf 1)(3Y+\mathbf 1)=0,
000257 | \]
000258 | so the only eigenvalues are
000259 | \[
000260 | y_+=\frac12,
000261 | \qquad
000262 | y_-=-\frac13.
000263 | \]
000264 | Write
000265 | \[
000266 | m_+:=\dim\ker(Y-y_+\mathbf 1),
000267 | \qquad
000268 | m_-:=\dim\ker(Y-y_-\mathbf 1).

000272 | 0=\tr_EY=\frac12\,m_+-\frac13\,m_-,

000283 | the displayed spectral form of $Y$.
