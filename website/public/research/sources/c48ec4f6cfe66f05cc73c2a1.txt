000201 | ================================
000202 | 
000203 | The geometric clock Q:x->ix permutes the four branch marks and transports
000204 | their local kernels. It does not turn a small branch meridian into the old
000205 | longitudinal circle generator. Therefore the following statements have
000206 | different scopes:

000220 | 5. Consequence for the holomorphic source kernel
000221 | ================================================
000222 | 
000223 | The rank-two kernel (8) is obtained by an exact pushforward of the already
000224 | given elliptic Szego half-form, including its square-root Jacobian. Therefore
000225 | the holomorphic two-point kernel, and algebraically the determinant Wick
000226 | expressions built from that kernel, transport to the quasipermutation,
000227 | ramified spin-trace chart on the punctured base with the cover-induced domain
000228 | (4). The central lift and its phase are inherited from the original cover
000229 | spinor rather than selected independently.
000230 | 
000231 | This local half-form calculation does not by itself prove a positive
000232 | equal-time CAR covariance on a base Hilbert space. Such a claim additionally
000233 | requires a specified real slice and one-particle inner product, a closed trace
000234 | operator T across all branch vertices, and the operator statements
000235 | 
000236 |   C_base=T C_E T^*,       C_base=C_base^*,       0<=C_base<=I,
000237 | 
000238 | with the relevant isometry or coisometry property of T. None of those
000239 | Hilbert-space statements follows merely from the bilinear holomorphic formula
000240 | (7a). In particular, a square-root Jacobian gives the correct tensorial
000241 | transformation law but is not on its own a proof of unitarity or positivity.
000242 | 
000243 | The strongest positive statement proved here is therefore narrower:
000244 | 
000245 |   the same elliptic Szego half-form has the old quarter Cauchy principal part
000246 |   at every marked branch point, including its central spin phase.

000251 | Without that equation and the Hilbert-space conditions above, local quarter
