000143 | \subsection{Normalized quotient form}
000144 | 
000145 | The relative Euclidean theory used in the main closure section is not interpreted as a difference of measures. Instead it is organized as the normalized quotient
000146 | \[
000147 | Z_{\mathrm{rel}}[J,\eta,\bar\eta]
000148 | =
000149 | \frac{Z_{\mathrm{adm}}[J,\eta,\bar\eta]}{Z_{\mathrm{ref}}[0,0,0]}.
000150 | \]
000151 | Because the denominator is source independent on the admissible sector, the reference theory acts only as a fixed normalizer and does not interfere with reflection positivity.
