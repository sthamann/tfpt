VERDICT

The 24 directions are not merely “spinor-like.”  They all lie in one odd
Ramond discriminant class.  In the corrected v148 convention, the 20
directions in W20 and the 4 in W4 all have class (3,3); their adjoints all
have class (1,1).  None is an integral D8 root, hence none can be a current
bilinear of the NS 16-Majorana observable net.  The .20 current construction
therefore depends exactly on v973's uncovered N2 leg, followed by N1:

  raw seam source
    -> source-derived rank-8 / 16-Majorana even net       [still open]
    -> localized Ramond spinor sector (3,3)+(1,1)        [N2]
    -> selected local simple-current extension           [N1]
    -> the 24 fields + adjoints inside the E8_1 currents.

There are two ordered obstructions, not one.

1. The absolute first source obstruction remains QGEO.KERNEL.01 / the local
   current map.  The literal raw P1 seam Calderon operator has not been shown
   to be unitarily the proposed mu4-equivariant free c=8 contraction.  The
   actual one-copy QWZ edge supplies one complex chiral CAR channel; its eight
   transverse rows do not supply eight independent currents.  Thus the
   D8_1 even net itself is not yet derived from the original raw seam.

2. Conditional on that D8_1 net, the first obstruction for these particular
   24 fields is N2.  NS vacuum correlations and fermion bilinears remain in
   the even classes.  A localized Ramond sector or charged intersector field
   must be constructed before any of the 24 can be a source operator.  N1's
   crossed-product theorem then identifies the endpoint, but does not create
   the missing source operator.

THE SMALLEST EXACT CHARGE TEST

The checker spin_source_origin_check.py reconstructs the .20 sign patterns
without floating arithmetic:

  W20: first five signs have exactly one minus; last three have odd minus
       count.  Census 5 x 4 = 20.
  W4:  first five signs are all minus; last three have odd minus count.
       Census 1 x 4 = 4.

All doubled sign vectors have true norm two after division by two.  The first
block has odd minus parity and the second block has odd minus parity, so every
source direction is class (3,3).  Negation reverses both parities because the
block lengths 5 and 3 are odd, hence every adjoint is class (1,1).

The same check compares the two Lagrangian glues already present in v148:

  L_a = {(0,0),(1,1),(2,2),(3,3)}
  L_b = {(0,0),(1,3),(2,2),(3,1)}.

Their restrictions to the NS classes are identical:

  L_a ∩ NS = L_b ∩ NS = {(0,0),(2,2)}.

Their Ramond parts differ:

  L_a ∩ R = {(1,1),(3,3)},
  L_b ∩ R = {(1,3),(3,1)}.

This is the decisive selection fact.  Scalar quasi-free vacuum correlators,
all Wick/Pfaffian data, and the Z4 action restricted to the NS observable net
cannot distinguish the two extensions.  The source24 fields select L_a only
when a nonzero (3,3) source sector response is supplied.  A bare statement
that the source has a Z4 clock or an index-4 conditional expectation is not
that response.

THE NEXT DECISIVE OPERATOR MATRIX ELEMENT

Do not test another root census or another NS Gram.  Construct joint source
charge projectors for the two marked charge characters (equivalently the
source realizations of G and the independent A/T character) and one localized
operator sequence Phi_N(f).  The first acceptance test is

  liminf_N || P_(3,3)^N Phi_N(f) P_(0,0)^N Omega_N || > 0,
  lim_N    || P_(3,1)^N Phi_N(f) P_(0,0)^N Omega_N || = 0,

with the adjoint satisfying

  (Phi_N(f))* : (3,3) -> (0,0), equivalently carrying conjugate class (1,1)
  in the opposite orientation.

A single Z4 projector is insufficient: it cannot distinguish (3,3) from
(3,1).  The second marked character is load-bearing.  This projector sandwich
is the smallest test that simultaneously decides the Ramond sheet and prevents
the target glue label from being inserted by hand.

For this matrix element to become the constructive N2 -> N1 bridge, the same
sequence must also satisfy:

  * localization in a fixed interval up to a vanishing collar and graded
    locality with the sourced D8 observable net;
  * a nonzero finite-energy limit for Phi_N and its actual adjoint;
  * weight one / rotation covariance and the source clock action with no
    separately appended charge register;
  * the normalized OPE or mode relations with the sourced D8 currents,
    including the level-one central term, not only a matching two-point norm;
  * source-algebra generation/exhaustion, so the resulting crossed product is
    the scaling net rather than one available representation among several.

Once these hold for one normalized (3,3) generator and its adjoint, the
existing D8 action and simple-current theorem generate the spinor orbit and
identify the local extension as E8_1.  The 24 .20 currents then inherit a
source operator meaning.  Before that test, they are exact target currents
with an open origin map.
