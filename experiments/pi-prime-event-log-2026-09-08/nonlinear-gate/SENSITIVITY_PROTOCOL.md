# Post-outcome sensitivity protocol: remove extrapolated log(p) slope

This amendment is explicitly written AFTER the frozen primary nonlinear run. Its results are diagnostic, not a new independent confirmation. The primary protocol, code, predictions and null results remain unchanged.

Observed issue: the baseline's final-test MSE is 0.836410 versus 0.756319 for the training mean. The log(p) feature lies 30.4–33.3 training standard deviations beyond its training mean. Its coefficient contributes -0.32835 to the average prediction, while the observed target mean shifts only +0.00940 from training. Holding every other fitted component fixed and subtracting just this term reduces final-test MSE; the exact ablation is in baseline_diagnostic.json. The issue is extrapolation of a fitted local slope, not integer-index corruption or label leakage.

Single change: omit the raw log(p) baseline feature and refit all remaining baseline features on the same training range. Keep the normalized log-gap target and previous-gap features, residue features, penalty 100, all categorical predictors, smoothing 50, search order, train/selection/test ranges and no-refit rule unchanged. Repeat the entire observed/positive/null experiment with the exact same 999 stream seeds. No further choice of baseline or selector after this sensitivity result.
