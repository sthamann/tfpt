## What changed: charges can now move locally under the Hamiltonian

[The local transport construction](local-charge-transport-round20/PROOF.md)
retains the full E8 lattice, Gram cross terms, cocycle and finite-frame gauge
quotient. It removes one exact condition: every site's charge no longer has
to equal every other site's charge. There is now one full charge module per
site, with 8N integer coordinates, not one global eight-coordinate module.
Finite gradient stiffness may penalize differences; it is physical energy,
not an exact synchronization constraint.

On-site charged fields T_x,p now have faithful local representatives. The
positive nearest-neighbor transfer term is

    U_xy,p=T_y,p T_x,p*,
    L_J=J sum_(xy,p)(2-U_xy,p-U_xy,p*),  J>0.

It moves p from x to y and preserves all eight total charges. Its actual
neighboring matrix element is -J for L>=3, and -2J for L=2 because that
periodic positive-edge inventory counts two coincident directed channels.
The corresponding leading probabilities are J^2 t^2 and 4J^2 t^2, in the
time generated by the physical Hamiltonian. Local occupations are NOT
conserved. The all-zero charge profile is no longer a stationary basis
state: neutral dipoles can be created without changing total charge.

The former synchronized code cannot simply be kept. Compressing L_J into
it gives only 48JN times identity, while the full hopping leaks out of that
code. Exact total-charge conservation also forbids every uniform n->m
transition for n!=m at every order, not just at first order. Thus the
increase from 8 to 8N physical coordinates is a substantive model change.

## Actual charge and energy balances

[The charge-flow proof](charge-propagation-round20/PROOF.md) gives the
bounded component current I_xy^a=iJ sum_p p^a(U-U*) and exact incoming-minus-
outgoing continuity. For any region S,

    ||Q_S^a(t)-Q_S^a|| <= 2J |boundary S| |t|.

This is a genuine bounded difference on the unbounded charge domain, with
actual periodic edge multiplicities. The full system has exact total-charge
conservation. An all-order integer-flow/Dyson argument also bounds distant
charge-configuration transitions, but its constants grow with volume; it
is not a relativistic or uniform Lieb--Robinson cone.

For the square-root clock, a separate positive-gap resolvent proof bounds
the charge response by an additional sqrt(3/e_m) factor. Its current is
not local. The base-time distance/Taylor-order argument does not transfer
to sqrt(X), whose matrix kernel can already connect distance-two sites.

[The scalar/charge energy proof](scalar-charge-energy-round20/PROOF.md)
derives the actual new transfer term

    d rho_sc(x)/dt + div j_m(x)
       =(lambda/N)phi_x^2 i[L_J,e(n_x)]

for the scalar-charge input. It cancels against the corresponding change
of diagonal charge and hopping energies. The full physical parent has a
local energy-node commutator balance, including its gravitational/auxiliary
terms and the noncommuting neighboring hopping bonds caused by odd Gram
pairings. This is an exact common-core identity with a qualified weak
evolution on Dom(X+E_fast+1)^(3/2), not a claim that every individual
unbounded energy current is bounded on all finite-energy states.

The inhomogeneous scalar momentum force remains. Full energy conservation
is NOT yet a complete symmetric relativistic energy-momentum tensor, nor
does the bounded-readout theorem establish an unbounded energy-current limit.
