Charged fields, relative time and the origin of the Maxwell coefficient

VERDICT: PARTIAL. Exact finite reconstruction obstruction under stated data;
conditional static completion and an explicit sufficient state datum.
No physical TFPT gate is closed. No complete TFPT solution is claimed.

PURPOSE AND ORIGINAL INPUT

The requested result is a common source-derived theory, not merely a neutral
E8 boundary. The preceding Maxwell contract gives a conditional dynamical
quotient of an already stipulated 9R+1L theory. The present question is whether
restoring Clifford matrices already used in source construction supplies the
missing charged fields and uniquely fixes their time and the gauge dynamics.

Native source native_source.py builds six 8x8 Clifford matrices inside
_gamma_pairs(3), but exports only their fifteen even products restricted to
the four-dimensional even chirality. The prior family_intertwiner proof
establishes P+ gamma_a P+=0 and the nonzero covariant maps P- gamma_a P+.
Those maps are not executed local fields of its 64-CAR/60-CCR Hamiltonian.
The already existing source-clifford-grade contract also distinguishes
spinor STATES in the full finite source from spinor FIELD OPERATORS under
conjugation. That exclusion is not a new result here.

The full TFPT architecture, including P1/P2, compiler, E8 glue, clocks,
alpha, flavor, spacetime and gravity, is not replaced by the example below.
In particular we do NOT claim every member of the counterfamily satisfies
all P1/P2 constraints or a hypothetical future common clock implementation.

1. WHAT CAN BE RECOVERED STATICALLY

Write S+=C4 for the retained family chirality. Its matrix algebra is A+=M4.
The candidate full complex Clifford algebra is

 B=Cl6(C)=M8,
 B_even=End(S+) direct_sum End(S-)=M4 direct_sum M4,
 B_odd=Hom(S+,S-) direct_sum Hom(S-,S+).

If a six-dimensional Euclidean vector representation V and an irreducible
graded Clifford representation c are required, with

 c(v)*=c(v), {c(v),c(w)}=2<v,w>I,

then this static completion is unique up to equivalence. The two chiral
Spin(6) modules are 4 and bar4. The vector intertwiner is unique up to phase,
with Clifford normalization fixing its magnitude; a rephasing removes the
remaining phase. The native precompression construction already realizes it.

This is conditional: the supplied M4 block alone does not require odd
Clifford generators or retain the other central summand. Every unital
*-endomorphism M4->M4 is injective by simplicity and onto by finite dimension,
and is inner. This finite algebra cannot on its own supply a nontrivial
localized charged-sector category.

The chirality epsilon=P+-P- is an ALGEBRAIC grading. Identifying it with
physical fermion parity and realizing its generators as localized fields
requires a further field-theoretic map. Matrix oddness is insufficient.

Doplicher--Roberts reconstruction concerns a LOCAL OBSERVABLE NET and its
superselection structure, not this isolated finite matrix block. Its primary
abstract states the net/locality and sector setting explicitly [L1]. No DR
theorem is applied to the chiral 1+1D candidate here; its statistics and
massless-sector issues would need their own treatment.

2. EXACT LOSS OF RELATIVE SECTOR TIME

Even grant the static completion. Let H+ and H- be self-adjoint finite
Hamiltonians on the two blocks and let Delta be real. Define

 H_Delta=H+ direct_sum (H-+Delta I).

For every a=a+ direct_sum a- in B_even,

 exp(it H_Delta) a exp(-it H_Delta)
 = exp(it H+) a+ exp(-it H+)
   direct_sum exp(it H-) a- exp(-it H-),                 (1)

independently of Delta. For T=P- T P+,

 alpha_t^Delta(T)=exp(it Delta) exp(it H-) T exp(-it H+). (2)

Thus even the FULL even algebra and its time evolution do not determine
the relative field energy. This is stronger than merely losing one block
by compression. Adjunction holds exactly for every Delta. If the Spin
action preserves chirality, the scalar sector shift preserves covariance.

A particularly explicit state-preserving example is

 H_Delta=Delta P-, Delta>0, rho=P+/4,
 T=P- gamma_1 P+, T* T=P+, T T*=P-.

Every even multi-time correlation is identical for all Delta, and rho is
the same Spin-invariant ground-state density matrix. Nevertheless, for t>=0,

 Tr(rho T* exp(-t H_Delta) T)=exp(-Delta t).             (3)

Delta=1 and Delta=2 give different field gaps with the same retained state
and all even dynamics. Both Hamiltonians are positive. Euclidean positivity
here is finite quantum-mechanical positivity: the reflection kernel is a
Gram kernel built from exp(-tH_Delta) A rho^(1/2). This is not a construction
of a relativistic local net or a proof of physical charged-field locality.

For fixed baseline blocks, (1)-(2) hold at any Delta. The ground-state
example and general covariance statement have the stated restricted scope.
They do not prove a universal impossibility theorem for TFPT.

3. THE PRECISE EXTRA DATA THAT WOULD REMOVE THIS AMBIGUITY

Suppose beta>0, both baseline H+,H- and their common normalization are known,
and a FULL normalized Gibbs state on M8 is supplied. Set
 Z+=Tr exp(-beta H+), Z-=Tr exp(-beta H-).
Its central weights satisfy

 w+=Z+/(Z+ + exp(-beta Delta) Z-),
 w-=exp(-beta Delta) Z-/(Z+ + exp(-beta Delta) Z-).

If both weights are nonzero, this gives exactly

 Delta=beta^(-1) log[(Z- w+)/(Z+ w-)].                  (4)

The normalized plus-corner state is exp(-beta H+)/Z+ for every Delta.
In the simple example the full Gibbs weights are
 w+=1/(1+exp(-beta Delta)), w-=1-w+.
The KMS CONDITION on the even direct-sum algebra alone leaves its central
mixture free: convex mixtures of component KMS states remain KMS. One must
not confuse that condition with specifying the full Gibbs density on M8.
At zero temperature the state in (3) cannot determine Delta.

A sourced symmetry C with C P+ C^-1=P- and C H C^-1=H would also relate the
two blocks. Such a symmetry could exclude an independent Delta P-. A clock
acting only by block-diagonal phases cannot do so. The native BAR tensor
operation has not been exhibited as that common block-exchanging symmetry
with physical time. Likewise a known term -Q/4 does not in itself exclude
an additional sector offset; a full sourced H=L0-Q/4 on both sectors would
be stronger data and is not contradicted by this theorem.

Consequently this contract identifies a recoverable missing datum, rather
than asserting that no future source principle can select it. Equation (4)
fixes only this scalar offset after the other data are supplied, not the
whole charged field theory, localization, couplings or cosmological state.

4. THE MAXWELL ORIGIN IS A SEPARATE SELECTION PROBLEM

In the already declared 9R+1L candidate let a be the compact connection,
normalized so integer charges couple to a and its holonomies have period
2pi. At fixed charge lattice, choose e>0 and add

 S_Maxwell=(1/(4e^2)) integral f_mu_nu f_mu_nu
          =(1/(2e^2)) integral f_01^2                 (Euclidean 2D).

Changing e preserves compactness and the anomaly cancellation 9-9=0.
It also preserves the neutral E8 quotient but changes the previously
derived Schwinger mass m^2=9e^2/pi. At fixed source length L, mL changes;
this is not removed by a change of units or a rescaling of a that preserves
its specified period and unit charge. Quantized charges and the continuous
gauge coefficient are distinct data in the primary construction [L2].

Even an induced quadratic response does not identify these two data.
For the local nonzero-momentum charged boson Phi of the granted candidate,
in a fixed zero-mode sector and with matched Euclidean conventions,

 S[Phi,f]=1/2 <Phi,(-partial^2)Phi> + i c <Phi,f>,
 c=3/sqrt(pi).

Gaussian completion at fixed background f gives

 S_ind[f]=c^2/2 <f,(-partial^2)^(-1)f>
         =9/(2pi) <f,(-partial^2)^(-1)f>.              (5)

Its kernel is nonlocal. It is not the bare local Maxwell term. Including
the latter yields, for p!=0, the quadratic coefficient

 1/2 [1/e^2+9/(pi p^2)] |f(p)|^2.                    (6)

Equations (5)-(6) are Gaussian identities within the supplied charged-boson
action. They are not a native derivation of that action, an integral over
all compact bundles, or a proof about its zero modes. A separately derived
microscopic source and regulator could fix a local coefficient; the present
Clifford completion and neutral quotient do not do so. The original free
QWZ source and the separate rotor parent must not be silently identified
with this ten-channel action. The existing rotor has its own specified
link term and electric coefficient and different matter charges.

5. CONSEQUENCE FOR THE REQUESTED COMPLETE SOLUTION

The attempted repair was concrete: restore the original precompression
Clifford maps, reconstruct charged fields, and use their response to select
the Maxwell bridge. Static completion works conditionally. The inference
from that completion and retained dynamics to a unique charged-field time
fails by (1)-(3). The needed scalar state input is precisely exposed by (4).
The neutral E8 structure and anomaly conditions also do not select e; (5)
shows why the induced response alone is not the missing Maxwell input.

The first missing native map must provide ALL of:
 - a surviving charged representation and physically correct grading;
 - one sourced time/state on both retained and charged fields;
 - the compact connection, its measure and electric coefficient;
 - compatible source clocks and the controlled local limit.

These are prerequisites for using the Maxwell candidate as TFPT physics.
They are not sufficient for three chiral families, a 3+1D local theory,
gravity, the alpha-functional origin or absolute vacuum/initial-state data.
Those remain separate common-source requirements. No values for the missing
data have been chosen here merely to label the result complete.
