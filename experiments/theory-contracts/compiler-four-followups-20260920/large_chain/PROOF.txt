NATIVE PERIODIC PACKET GRAMMAR AND ITS CRITICAL COMPRESSION
20 September 2026 | follow-up 4 | exact compression, not a full-model EFT

1. THE CHANGE OF QUESTION

The earlier one-wall core was not invariant and its folded linear crossing
lay above a quadratic band minimum. This calculation does not reuse that
identification. It includes every assignment of matter registers to incident
sources, so native local moves can create and annihilate pairs of walls as
well as transport them. The geometry is still chosen: an even simple ring of
N>=4 matter registers and N sources. Couplings remain the same J,kappa,mu.

2. ACTUAL STATES AND THE PHASE DICTIONARY

Let sigma_v=+1 mean that matter v belongs to source edge v, and sigma_v=-1
mean that it belongs to edge v-1 (indices modulo N). Edge e receives

  n_e=1+(sigma_e-sigma_(e+1))/2 in {0,1,2}

registers. Put its source and these assigned registers in the native packet

  |P_n>=240^(-1/2) sum_alpha |alpha> |psi_alpha>^(tensor n).

For n=0,1,2 these are exactly s,Phi,Omega. Every matter register belongs to
one packet, so their product |Psi_sigma> is a valid normalized full-space
vector. No middle register is copied. All assignments have total source
quarter-phase K=N mod4. Unlike completing a single open-chain vacancy with
Phi, pair creation on the ring does not change the conserved total charge.

On the ring n0=n2=(N-n1)/2. Alternating sigma gives Omega,s,Omega,s,... .
Equal neighbouring sigma gives a Phi source: a defect of the alternating
packet order. Such defects occur in pairs for even N.

3. THE BASIS IS ALMOST, BUT NOT EXACTLY, ORTHONORMAL

Distinct individual phase strings are orthogonal. The only different
assignments with the same phase string are sigma=all+ and sigma=all-, each
with n_e=1 on every edge. Their overlap is a directed loop of N independent
root overlaps. The native first moment E|psi><psi|=I4/4 gives

  s_N=<Psi_all+|Psi_all->=4^(1-N).

Thus the exact Gram matrix is

  G=I+s_N F,
  F=|all+><all-|+|all-><all+|.

All eigenvalues are positive. The actual orthonormal compression is therefore
G^(-1/2) K G^(-1/2), not K alone, where K_(sigma,tau)=<Psi_sigma|H|Psi_tau>.

4. MATRIX ELEMENTS OF THE ORIGINAL HAMILTONIAN

The native moments give the following diagonal energies per source:

  packet         kappa L       J H_E
  Omega              0            0
  Phi           kappa/2         3J/2
  s bridge      3kappa/4         3J/4

For Phi, simultaneous source/bound-register reflection fixes the invariant
pair and the other register sees mean r=I/2. For an s bridge, the endpoints
belong to different packets and have independent I4/4 marginals; the native
reflection average is (I+Swap)/5. The event expectation follows because a
bound register has r eigenvalue -1, while an independent marginal has mean
r expectation 1/2. Every V contributes diagonal energy mu.

The diagonal sum is

  E_ref + delta n1,
  E_ref=3N(kappa+J)/8+mu N,  delta=(kappa+9J)/8.

L and H_E preserve each source phase. Their only additional off-diagonal
matrix element is between all+ and all-. Every L acts as 1/2 on these
all-Phi states, so its sum has cross element kappa N s_N/2. For H_E this
cross element is zero: at the two incident registers the bra has one bound
root and the ket has the other; the two reflection factors each contribute
-1 to the corresponding overlap, reproducing the identity term exactly.

The nonconstant part of V transfers one unit of phase between adjacent
sources. Reassigning one matter register gives a two-edge overlap loop:

  <Psi_flipped|mu V|Psi_sigma>=-mu/(2*4)=-mu/8.

This applies equally to wall transport and wall-pair creation/annihilation.
There is one finite-ring correction. Reassigning N-1 registers all in the
same direction closes a loop of length N with one V overlap. Its amplitude
is -mu s_N/2. The only such links join a uniform assignment to the N states
with one register still assigned as before. Let C be the symmetric adjacency
matrix of these 2N unordered links. No other matrix elements survive.

Consequently, with computational spin operators X_v,Z_v,

  H_I=E_ref I+delta sum_e(1+Z_e Z_(e+1))/2-(mu/8)sum_v X_v,

  K=H_I+s_N[(kappa N/2+mu N)F-(mu/2)C].                 (1)

This is an exact native matrix-element identity for every even N>=4, not a
Hamiltonian term chosen to obtain Ising behaviour. The checker independently
contracts all overlaps for all pairs of assignments at N=4,6,8. Quarter-phase
balance removes unbalanced graphs; each surviving graph is a directed loop
evaluated with the native first moment. The all-N statement follows from
classifying the changed-register intervals as just described.

5. CONTROLLED RING CORRECTION; UNCONTROLLED COMPLEMENT

The Gram correction and exceptional links vanish in operator norm. Using
||F||=1, ||C||<=N, ||G^(-1/2)||=(1-s_N)^(-1/2), one obtains

  ||G^(-1/2)KG^(-1/2)-H_I||
    <= N s_N/(1-s_N) [kappa+3J/2+21mu/8].                (2)

For example ||H_I||<=N[kappa/2+3J/2+9mu/8]; multiplying this by
s_N/(1-s_N) bounds the Gram contribution. The remaining term is bounded by
N s_N[kappa/2+3mu/2]/(1-s_N). Thus (2) holds at fixed couplings and tends
to zero exponentially with N. It controls the COMPLETE packet compression,
including its low eigenvalues, but says nothing yet about the orthogonal
complement in the microscopic Hilbert space.

That distinction is substantial. Consider a Neel assignment, with N/2 Omega
packets and N/2 s bridges. Project source modules onto precisely these Omega
and uniform modules. H_E on an s source has the same matter operator as L,
(4I-Swap)/5, and it annihilates each Omega packet. In this module sector
H0+J H_E therefore acts through bridges with coefficient kappa+J.

Each bridge has swap mean1/4 and variance15/16, giving energy variance3/80.
For N>=6 distinct bridges have zero covariance: if they meet one Omega pair,
their other endpoints trace to independent I4/4; if disjoint this is immediate.
N=4 is a separate two-packet loop with a small additional covariance.

The entire packet span in this particular phase string contains only the
original Neel vector. V changes phase strings and cannot cancel the residual
just found. If P denotes the orthogonal projector onto the whole packet span,

  ||(I-P)H|Psi_Neel>||^2 >= 3N(kappa+J)^2/160, N>=6.    (3)

The exact N=4 coefficient is39/500 instead of3N/160. The permutation-trace
checker evaluates the actual product density (I+Swap)/20 on each Omega pair
for N=4,6,8,10,12, independently reproducing (3).

Equation (3) refutes invariance of the bare assignment space. It does not
exclude a dressed low-energy space or an Ising universality class. A norm of
an extensive residual alone also cannot determine errors in intensive
observables. What is missing is a separated complementary spectrum and a
uniform elimination/control theorem for energies and responses near the
candidate point. Neither (1) nor (2) supplies it.

6. A GROUND-CRITICAL CANDIDATE, NOT THE OLD MIDBAND CROSSING

After a staggered spin rotation, H_I is the standard ferromagnetic
transverse-field Ising chain with

  K_I=(kappa+9J)/16,   h=mu/8.

The known exact Ising solution gives bulk excitation dispersion

  epsilon(q)=2 sqrt(K_I^2+h^2-2 K_I h cos q).

At h=K_I, equivalently

  2mu=kappa+9J,                                         (4)

the compression has gapless linear excitations epsilon(q)~2K_I|q| about its
own thermodynamic ground state. Equation (2) shows that the native packet
compression has the same limiting spectrum as this Ising family. This is
different from folding a massive one-wall band and calling its middle a
vacuum. The native operator sum_e(-1)^e(n_e-1)/N is represented exactly by
sum_e(-1)^e Z_e/N: it detects the alternating packet order.

The previous ray J=mu cannot satisfy (4) for kappa>0 and mu>=0. Even this
restricted variational description therefore points away from that ray.
An explicit positive test point is kappa=1, J=1/100, mu=109/200. Numerical
diagonalization of the full packet compression at N=4,6,8,10,12 controls its
Gram corrections and finite-size gap. These are labelled compression
eigenvalues, never full-source eigenvalues. The exact Ising finite-ring gap
at criticality is2K_I tan(pi/(4N)); N times the gap tends to pi K_I/2.

The linear branch belongs at present to the compression only. No full-model
critical point, full vacuum, physical speed of light, chirality, fermion
statistics, three-dimensional space or gravitational sector follows. These
coefficients use only native first/second moments shared by isotropic sources,
so they also do not yet distinguish the special higher E8 structure.

7. DECISION FOR THE FOUR FOLLOW-UPS

There is now a concrete all-size variational grammar in which changing
bindings naturally gives pair creation and an Ising critical candidate.
The full native complement matters at order kappa+J and remains uneliminated.
The finite four-source proof, the common-clock/current bridge, and the need
to preserve distinct local observable algebras are therefore relevant to
this candidate, not separate decorative checks. The next decisive step is
to control the dressed packet family over the actual same-H vacuum and test
its local phase response at fixed couplings near (4). Without that result,
calling the Ising critical line the universal solution would be false.
