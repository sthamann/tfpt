RESULT
======

There is a concrete conditional compiler rule, and its exact missing premise
can now be named.

If the raw process has already been mapped, on the same state space, to the
marked affine E8 currents J^alpha_n, and if continuous time acts diagonally on
those currents while preserving their brackets and adjoints, then its most
general phase rule is

  [H,J^alpha_n] = (alpha(h) - a n) J^alpha_n .                 (1)

The alpha(h) term is an internal Cartan rotation. The actual TFPT C clock has
order 30 and characteristic polynomial Phi_30. It has no fixed Cartan vector.
Requiring the same evolution to be covariant under a bracket-compatible lift
of that actual C clock therefore forces h=0. On the affine vacuum module,

  H = a L0 + c I .                                             (2)

Vacuum normalization H Omega=0 fixes c=0. Positive nontrivial energy fixes
a>0, but its numerical value remains the choice of time unit. Thus the
continuous common rule is unique up to time calibration once the named affine
same-space premise is supplied.

This is the first bridge result. P1/P2 and the finite E8 clocks do not by
themselves supply that premise. In particular they do not yet construct the
raw-source map to affine modes or prove that raw time acts as a continuous
current-algebra derivation which leaves the linear current-mode space invariant
with state-independent scalar frequencies. Every Hamiltonian commutator is of
course a Leibniz derivation on the full operator algebra; that general fact is
not the selector used here. Without the stronger invariant-current premise, a
physically different family survives all established finite data below.

The picture is simple. The E8 brackets are the teeth of a gear: they force all
root phases to mesh. The order-30 clock then removes the remaining internal
tilt. What is still missing is the axle that identifies raw source time with
the affine mode number n.

2. EXACT ZERO-MODE CLASSIFICATION
=================================

Let D be a derivation of the current algebra which leaves each E8 root-current
line invariant,

  D(E_alpha)=lambda_alpha E_alpha,

and require D to be a derivation. Whenever alpha+beta is a root, the nonzero
bracket [E_alpha,E_beta]=N_(alpha,beta) E_(alpha+beta) gives

  lambda_(alpha+beta)=lambda_alpha+lambda_beta.                (3)

The opposite-root bracket gives lambda_(-alpha)=-lambda_alpha. Choose eight
simple roots. Equation (3) then determines lambda on every root by its integral
simple-root coordinates, so

  lambda_alpha = alpha(h)                                     (4)

for one h in the Cartan. Conversely every h satisfies all bracket equations.
Therefore the full diagonal zero-mode freedom has dimension eight; it is not
240 independent root rates.

This converse is checked constructively. The checker enumerates all 240 roots
and all 13,440 ordered nonzero root-sum brackets. Starting with the sixteen
positive and negative simple roots, repeated addition by a signed simple root
reaches all 240 roots. No numerical rank tolerance is used.


3. THE ACTUAL CLOCK REMOVES THE EIGHT INTERNAL PARAMETERS
=========================================================

For the actual matrix C the checker proves exactly

  C^15=-I,   C^30=I,
  charpoly(C)=Phi_30
              =x^8+x^7-x^5-x^4-x^3+x+1.                      (5)

Hence 1 is not an eigenvalue. Clock covariance of (4) says

  alpha(h)=(C alpha)(h) for every root alpha,

equivalently C^T h=h. Equation (5) forces h=0. The actual J and compiler-c
clocks also have zero fixed Cartan dimension; their joint fixed space is zero.
C alone is already decisive.

This is where the actual clock carries more information than an arbitrary
root-permutation Hamiltonian. It eliminates every bracket-compatible internal
root phase. It does not determine the affine mode slope a.


4. AFFINE EXTENSION AND THE FORCED RULE
=======================================

Now impose the stated additional premise: the raw evolution has been mapped
to the same affine current algebra and acts phase-diagonally on its modes.
Write D(J^alpha_n)=omega(alpha,n)J^alpha_n. Applying D to

  [J^alpha_m,J^beta_n]

for every nonzero root bracket gives additivity in both the root and mode
arguments. The central opposite-root bracket fixes the adjoint sign. The
general solution is

  omega(alpha,n)=alpha(h)+q n.                                (6)

After Section 3, h=0. With q=-a and the standard positive-energy convention,
equation (6) is [H,J^alpha_n]=-a n J^alpha_n. On every cyclic negative-mode
word this agrees with aL0. Any difference commutes with every current and is a
scalar on the irreducible vacuum representation, giving (2).

This is a direct bracket argument within the phase-diagonal affine ansatz. It
does not rely on equal dimensions. Sugawara supplies the same L0 in the chosen
level-one vacuum representation. The earlier
universalraum-selection-vs-identity result already proved uniqueness after
postulating geometric current rotation. The new content here is the preceding
classification and the use of the actual Phi_30 clock: all possible internal
Cartan terms are removed, leaving only the common mode slope.


5. WHY A POSITIVE 240-ROOT HAMILTONIAN IS NOT THIS GENERATOR
===========================================================

Suppose positive numbers r_alpha assigned to root-register kets are also
claimed to be the diagonal current-derivation weights. Current adjunction and
the opposite-root bracket require

  r_(-alpha)=-r_alpha.                                        (7)

If both r_alpha and r_(-alpha) are nonnegative, (7) forces both to zero.
Therefore a nontrivial positive 240-root Laplacian or permutation Hamiltonian
can be a separate register dynamics, but it cannot simultaneously be the
phase-diagonal generator of E8 current automorphisms.

This sharpens the earlier A2 sign witness. The earlier pure permutation failed
already at one nonzero bracket because it swapped alpha and beta while fixing
alpha+beta. Adding correct cocycle phases may repair a discrete automorphism,
but positivity of root-label energies still does not turn that register
Hamiltonian into the phase-diagonal invariant-current generator (1).


6. EXACT REMAINING FAMILY WITHOUT THE CONTINUOUS BRIDGE
=======================================================

Keep the same affine E8 vacuum Hilbert space, the same marked currents, the
same internal grade-preserving C/J/c clock operations, positivity, the same
vacuum, the same grade-one energy and the same discrete quarter-step. For any
integer b>=0 set

  H_b = L0 + 4 b L0(L0-1).                                   (8)

On grade n its energy is f_b(n)=n+4b n(n-1). It is nonnegative, f_b(0)=0,
f_b(1)=1, and f_b(n)=n modulo 4. Hence

  exp(-i pi H_b/2)=exp(-i pi L0/2)                            (9)

on every integer grade. Because H_b is a function of L0, it commutes with all
internal grade-preserving E8 operations, including the actual clocks.

The family is physically inequivalent. At grade two, H_0 has energy 2 and H_1
has energy 10. At t=pi/8 their return phases differ by -1 while grade one is
unchanged, so this is not a common rescaling of time.

Exactly what (8) fails is also explicit:

  f_b(n+m)-f_b(n)-f_b(m)=8 b n m.                             (10)

For clarity, ad(H_b)=[H_b,.] is still a Leibniz derivation on the full operator
algebra, and unitary conjugation by exp(-itH_b) preserves products and
commutators. What fails is the stronger condition that the linear affine
current-mode space be invariant with state-independent scalar frequencies.
From [L0,J_n]=-nJ_n, functional calculus gives the exact operator identity

  [f(L0),J_n] = J_n (f(L0-n)-f(L0)).                           (11)

For f=f_b the right factor is

  -n - 8 b n L0 + 4 b n(n+1).                                (12)

It depends on L0 when b!=0. Thus J_n is not a scalar-frequency eigenoperator
on all grades, even though the full adjoint evolution is a perfectly valid
operator-algebra automorphism. Equation (10) is precisely the nonadditivity of
the grade energies; it is not a violation of the operator Leibniz rule.
Consequently H_b is not the geometric local conformal current flow. It is a
witness against selection by the finite clocks and vacuum, not a competing
local E8 theory.


7. THE PRECISE FIRST BRIDGE GATE
================================

A source proposal now has one decisive early gate. It must provide a map Phi
on one common GNS/state space such that, on a common invariant core,

  (i)   Phi preserves the marked E8 current brackets, cocycle phases and adjoint;
  (ii)  Phi maps the source vacuum/state to the affine cyclic vacuum;
  (iii) the source clocks map to bracket-compatible C/J/c lifts;
  (iv)  the linear span of Phi(J^alpha_n) is invariant under ad(H_source),
        with [H_source,Phi(J^alpha_n)]=-a n Phi(J^alpha_n) for one a>0;
  (v)   the same a composes for multi-time products.

Conditions (i)-(iii) alone still admit (8). Condition (iv) is the first broken
causal edge and reduces the generator to aL0. Condition (v) prevents a fit to
isolated one-step data. A smallest necessary early witness is therefore not
another root spectrum: it is one independently constructed source current with
two different nonzero mode numbers, together with one nonzero current bracket.
Their commutator frequencies must have one common additive slope before any
larger continuum optimization is justified. Passing this finite witness is not
sufficient to derive all affine modes, the common invariant core, locality or
multi-time composition; the full conditions (i)-(v) remain the acceptance gate.

What remains after success is honest and small: the time calibration a and the
proof that the affine field/mode dictionary is generated by the raw P1/P2 seam.
Neither a target 3D graph nor a connectivity penalty enters this result.
