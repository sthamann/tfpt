Unitary D4 versus OS reflection
===============================

These are not the same structure in the original sources.

* The marked-sphere D4 is holomorphic/linear: rho:z->iz and sigma:z->1/z.
  It acts by permutation/unitary pullback on residues, with sigma*w1=w3 and
  sigma*w2=w2.  See verification/v177_seam_marking_kernel.py:19-34,69-112
  and tfpt_1_architecture_e8.tex:5173-5196.

* The OS reflection is anti-linear/antiunitary: the seam-circle family D is
  z->mu conjugate(z), and on Fock space Theta=U_r K, Theta^2=+1, inverting the
  clock.  It is explicitly distinguished from the deck involution.  See
  tfpt_research_contracts.tex:12148-12171,12173-12182,12234-12255 and
  12323-12337,12364-12375.

On the unit circle sigma(z)=1/z and conjugate(z) have the same point action for
mu=1, but their action on complex fields/differentials differs by conjugation.
An intertwining real structure must be proved; pointwise coincidence is not an
operator identification.

Does the primitive kernel already split the three families?
===========================================================

The residue representation is canonically
  U = B_alt (+) E_plane,       dimensions 1+2,
where B_alt is spanned by (1,-1,1,-1) and E_plane by
(1,0,-1,0),(0,1,0,-1).  A D4-equivariant Hermitian operator on U therefore has
the form lambda_B P_B + lambda_E P_E.

A genuine non-scalar candidate is already present: the geometric Green matrix
G_ij=-log|p_i-p_j| on the four marks.  With any common diagonal regularization
d its eigenvalues on U are
  lambda_B=d,              lambda_E=d+log 2 (multiplicity 2),
so lambda_B-lambda_E=-log 2 independently of d.  The paper records this
regularization-independent D4 anisotropy explicitly:
tfpt_1_architecture_e8.tex:1928-1959 and 5365-5374.

But this does not yet answer the source question.  The text uses the canonical
residue pairing, not G, for the E8 A3 metric, and does not identify G with the
raw P1 Calderon operator.  QGEO.KERNEL.01 still asks for the raw operator
identity, not a shared spectrum (verification/status_ledger.csv:257;
verification/v177_seam_marking_kernel.py:35-42,130-136).  QGEO.STATE.01 proves
only character-block diagonality/clock conservation; the bounded sub-principal
symbol remains open (status_ledger.csv:272;
verification/v199_seam_state_invariance.py:13-30,75-94).  Hence there is no
proved source-prescribed anisotropic family insertion yet—only a very specific
existing candidate with a one-scalar unresolved identification.
