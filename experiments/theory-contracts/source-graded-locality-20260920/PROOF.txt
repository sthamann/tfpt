At the designated free-fermion reconstruction point the previously given

4. The existing block-preserving deformation cannot activate a competing
   neutral null cosine, even far beyond the earlier small interval

Keep the E8 kinetic block separate and deform the pair by the declared
positive Lorentz-boost metric. Write eta in R. For a vector (r,A,B),

 Delta_eta=[r²+cosh(2eta)(A²+B²)+2sinh(2eta)AB]/2.

For a self-null vector r²+A²-B²=0, the pair contribution is at least
|B²-A²|/2. Hence

 Delta_eta >= r² for all eta.                       (6)

Let Q=q(r)=sum r_i, an even integer on E8. Neutrality under the original
source number and selected hypercharge gives respectively
 Q-A+B=0, Y8.r-A+B=0. Thus Y8.r=Q and B=A-Q.
For r!=0, Q cannot be zero; self-nullness would otherwise give r²=0.
Since 1_8 and Y8 are orthogonal with squared norms8 and5/6,

 r² >= Q²(1/8+6/5) =53 Q²/40.                       (7)

If r²<8, evenness and (7) leave only r²=6 and Q=+/-2. But nullness and
neutrality would require A=(Q²-r²)/(2Q)=+/-1/2, not an integer. Therefore
every primitive doubly-neutral self-null vector other than +/-n satisfies

 Delta_eta >= r² >=8 for every eta.                 (8)

When r=0, neutrality forces A=B; primitivity leaves +/-n. Its dimension
is exp(2eta). The bound (8) is sharp in the stated doubly-neutral class.
For z, its W_aux coordinates have r²=8, Q=2, A=-1,B=-3 and

 Delta_eta(z)=4+5cosh(2eta)+3sinh(2eta)>=8,

with equality at eta=-log(2)/2. The given glue also preserves z.

There is a further uniqueness statement at this lower norm. If r²=8,
(7) forces Q=+/-2. Take Q=2, so A=-1,B=-3. Write c,w,f for the sums of
the three colour, two weak and three family entries of r. The oriented
glue of F_aux(r) is 2(c+w) modulo4; the pair adds -3A+11B=-30. Thus glue
invariance requires 2(c+w)-30=0 modulo4. Half-integral E8 vectors have
c half-integral and w integral, so 2(c+w) is odd and cannot satisfy this.
Hence r is integral. Hypercharge and source number give
 -2c+3w=12, c+w+f=2.
With integer t these imply c=3t,w=4+2t,f=-2-5t. The block Cauchy bound is
 r² >= c²/3+w²/2+f²/3=(40t²+44t+28)/3.
Requiring this at most8 gives 10t²+11t+1<=0, whose roots are -1,-1/10.
The only integer t is -1. Equality requires all entries constant in each
block: r=(-1,-1,-1,1,1,1,1,1)=-a. This reconstructs z. Q=-2 gives -z.
Thus z is the UNIQUE competing primitive null direction, up to sign,
attaining the minimum E8 squared norm8 under q,Y and the fixed glue.
This is a minimal algebraic competitor, not a dynamical selection of its

These are scaling-dimension theorems for self-null local cosines in the

5. The same neutral plane identifies a conditional Ising route, with its
   locality and selection conditions kept explicit

Define u=(n+z)/2 and v=(n-z)/2. Then B(u,u)=1, B(v,v)=-1, B(u,v)=0.

integral vector of Gamma. In this plane, a u+b v is an original local

There is an explicit positive COMPETITION METRIC

with eigenvalues1 (multiplicity8),7+4sqrt(3),7-4sqrt(3). It gives

At this point the two existing cosines restricted to the neutral plane
are the standard dimension-one dual sine-Gordon pair. Its familiar
refermionized quadratic equations have Majorana masses proportional to
g_n+g_z and g_n-g_z. Equal appropriately normalized real couplings produce

new microscopic source-spectrum proof. See Lecheminant/Gogolin/Nersesyan,

The putative self-duality is not a symmetry of the microscopic lattice.
The rational reflection S=I+2 v v^T K preserves K and interchanges n,z,

characteristic-vector property. n is characteristic and z is not, so NO

rule out a separately derived dynamical selection or noninvertible duality.

Cano et al., arXiv:1505.07825, analyze externally arranged interfaces

Their result supports a specific conditional interface mechanism; it does

6. What this resolves and what must come from the full source

The complement proposal now has an explicit whole-lattice realization:
it restores mutual locality between the vector and spinor projections.
The two familiar field descriptions share a D8 core but select different
local extensions. This is stronger than comparing dimensions or matching
isolated eigenvalues, and it preserves charge and local statistics.

It also decides the first missing implication of the previous Flavor test:
the pure E8-plus-gapped-pair phase does NOT supply its assumed light odd
mass legs. Fitting b,c inside that identification would solve a different
problem. A correct reconstruction has to retain or dynamically expose the
appropriate charged/defect sectors before discussing physical Yukawas.

The existing competing null direction makes that requirement concrete,
but its relevance requires leaving the block-preserving family in (8),
or another mechanism with explicitly changed premises. The microscopic
source must determine the full positive energy/operator, the allowed
couplings and their state/phase, not only the even root lattice or a
polarization/sign projector. The actual I8 elementary fermions form the
real SO16 vector (10,1)+(1,6) in the D5+D3 dictionary, not automatically
the complex Spin10 half-spinor of Standard Model matter. The neutral
effective Ising fermion is not that charged multiplet either.

No unique UV Hamiltonian, spontaneous domain pattern, phase locking,
physical CP phase, full determinant-line Ward identity, chiral3+1D parent
or gravitational sector has been derived here. This is a precise new
operator/locality bridge and a scoped exclusion of the assumed mass-map
route, not a complete TFPT solution or a promotion of T1--T8.
