FERMION ORIGIN INVENTORY — 2026-09-20

Scope
-----
Read-only inventory of existing native fermionic/graded operator maps that
could connect the original affine E8 cubic
    C_(A,i)(B,j)(C,k) = d_ABC epsilon_ijk
to physical Yukawa slots. This preserves the existing kills: dimension
matching is not an operator map; the direct same-field Weyl Yukawa contraction
vanishes; and the one-copy QWZ edge has rank-one leading current Gram. No new
model or contract is introduced here.

Result in one sentence
----------------------
The repository contains a real microscopic CAR field map, sectorwise
half-shift/Fock embeddings, and a conditional E8-plus-massive-pair lattice
map. None is yet a source-derived graded map from the E8 current cubic to
elementary 3+1D Yukawa fields. Before this follow-up, the W_aux construction proved that selected E8
currents can be pure low-energy operators, but the upstream inventory had not
yet proved the stronger parity statement that every microscopic odd vertex
necessarily carries the massive pair and is annihilated by a source-derived
low-energy projection. This follow-up proves that statement only conditionally
for the declared grading, W_aux map, and free-Dirac light-sector projection;
it still does not establish native P1/P2 source origin.

1. Existing actual field/operator maps
---------------------------------------

1.1 Microscopic charged CAR edge field — strongest native field map

Primary source:
  experiments/theory-contracts/microscopic-charged-car-limit/README.md
  lines 3–22, 24–67, 119–226, 250–272.

The map is explicit on the original QWZ cylinder (width 8, mass 1, r=1):
R_N g is a top-row local smearing and Psi_N(g)=a_N(R_N g) is the actual
microscopic annihilator. It has graded CAR locality, the actual filled sea,
the actual adjoint, integer source charge [Q_N,Psi_N]=-Psi_N, and controlled
finite-time generator convergence on a common finite-energy core. The
particle/hole Fock embedding is obtained from the source spectral projection;
no auxiliary charge Hilbert space is appended and no massive occupied source
modes are silently removed.

What it supplies:
  * an honest elementary odd CAR field and its adjoint;
  * source vacuum/state/time/domain control;
  * an unbounded integer charge ladder from actual source excitations.

What it does not supply:
  * E8 root or half-charge assignments;
  * eight selected chiral species;
  * the D5+A3 lambda vertex;
  * a 3+1D field map or physical Yukawa coupling.

The source states that the field is not the E8 half-charge vertex and that
the next gate is an inter-representation half-charge intertwiner in the same
microscopic CAR algebra. It also leaves open whether TFPT selects only the
even observable algebra; if so, odd charged fields live in an extension
(README.md lines 250–272).

----------------------------------------

Primary source:

  lines 1–20, 37–63, 68–120.

For the actual one-copy width-eight QWZ source, the low-energy inventory is one

leading-current Gram of rank one; transverse row count does not produce eight

The same proof records the correct representation alternative: a real CAR

an equivariant real 16-dimensional elementary Majorana carrier. This is the
precise reason a finite 16=16 identification cannot be used as the Yukawa
field map.

2.2 Raw carrier origin gate — pure polarization cannot select the carrier split

Primary source:
  experiments/theory-contracts/raw-carrier-origin-gate-20260920/PROOF.txt
  lines 1–33, 35–72, 76–112, 114–152.

For the actual pure self-dual CAR covariance C=(I+iA)/2, post-projection
restriction of the same involution leaves only the + eigenspace. On a
self-dual J-stable space the +/- ranks are equal, so the same CAR involution
cannot give a J-stable (3,2) split. The pure polarization has U(5) stabilizer;
its natural complex-linear operators are scalar, and no canonical 3+2 split
exists. An independent internal carrier operator can be introduced on a
coefficient space, but it must have a separately derived source factor/map.

The strongest conditional Yukawa statement is the invariant factor
Lambda^3 E_- tensor Lambda^2 E_+ -> C for S(U(3)xU(2)); its nonzero invariant
space is one-dimensional. The degrees (3,2), representations, volume form,
and nonzero amplitude are assumptions. The BoundaryYukawaKernel interface
leaves the raw-source arrow open. This is the exact origin boundary, not a
native Yukawa realization.

2.4 E8 coset projection — useful warning, but not microscopic fermion parity

Primary source:
  experiments/theory-contracts/charged-sector-memory-gate-20260920/PROOF.txt
  lines 11–36, 39–64, 66–100, 129–158.

In the abstract E8_1 lattice theory, theta grades D and D+s; P=(1+theta)/2
and Q=(1-theta)/2 commute with L0. Therefore H=aL0+c has QHP=0, while a
spinor current has QSP nonzero and can return through a commutator. This
proves that an available odd/off-diagonal current response is not autonomous
Hamiltonian mixing. It also explicitly says that this theta grading is the
E8 extension grading, NOT microscopic fermion parity: both lattice summands are
bosonic here.

This report cannot be cited as proof that microscopic odd Yukawa legs are
projected out. It supplies the correct acceptance condition for a future
source map: separately establish a physical/readout P, a nonzero field corner
QSP, and a source-derived Hamiltonian corner QHP (or a controlled Liouvillian
mixing limit). These are three distinct obligations.

3. What the native cubic already supplies
-------------------------------------------

Primary source:
  experiments/theory-contracts/source-flavor-origin-20260920/PROOF.txt
  lines 1–24, 97–149, 153–205, 207–232.

The original cubic is read from the pinned source certificate, not guessed:
  C_(A,i)(B,j)(C,k)=d_ABC epsilon_ijk.
Its 45 squarefree monomials contain actual charged slots, including nonzero
e^c L H_d and Q d^c H_d entries, with the same normalized source coefficient.
So the current cubic does contain the internal representation slots needed for
down and lepton channels.

But with one neutral Higgs-family background h, the directed family matrix
  A(h)_ij=epsilon_ijk h_k
has rank two and determinant zero. The direct single-Higgs-family reading
therefore cannot yield three nonzero unequal masses. More basically, the
local zero-derivative same-field Weyl contraction is symmetric in family
labels, while d times epsilon is antisymmetric under the combined exchange;
the proposed direct Yukawa coefficient vanishes. This does not kill the
original current cubic, E6 invariants, or nonlocal/dressed interactions.

The minimal repair is a full chiral mass block
  M_full=[[A(h), b],[c^T,M]],   M_eff=A(h)-b c^T/M.
Rank restoration requires both source overlaps h^T b and c^T h to be nonzero.
Here b,c,M are explicitly marked as SOURCE MATRIX ELEMENTS still to derive.
The finite spectral-triple implementation accepts typed complex Yukawa matrices
but supplies no source family map F_d(theta),F_e(theta). The required result
remains a source-derived graded field map with nonzero mixing operators,
compatible with state, real Hamiltonian, clocks and local observables.

5. Decisive acceptance gate for the parent task
------------------------------------------------

The strongest next test is now narrow and operator-level:

1. Freeze the conditional W_aux decomposition and define the microscopic
   grading/parity and the actual candidate odd elementary vertices before the
   E8 reconstruction.
2. Transport each candidate through W_aux and explicitly compute its pair
   coordinates and pair-sector matrix elements.
3. At the free Dirac point, define the light-sector projection P_light and
   prove (or refute) P_light O_odd P_light=0 with a declared spectral gap and
   domains. Do not use theta as a synonym for microscopic parity.
4. Independently test the E8 cubic's candidate Yukawa legs through the full
   mass block: derive b,c from the same source and check h^T b != 0 and
   c^T h != 0. If the surviving low-energy operator is necessarily the
   bosonic quartic/current b_aux, record that it cannot serve as an elementary
   Weyl leg without an additional source-derived transmutation.
5. For a physical flavor phase, require a flavor-dependent complement response;
   a common massive determinant factor cancels from normalized pure-E8
   correlators and cannot generate a relative Down/lepton phase.

Until this gate is done, the defensible classification is:
  * native elementary CAR field map: EXISTS for one QWZ edge;
  * native E8/Yukawa field map: OPEN;
  * conditional E8 current map: EXISTS under the chosen W_aux/bosonization
    assumptions;
  * proof that all microscopic odd light legs are removed: OPEN;
  * nonzero source-derived Yukawa mixing b,c: OPEN;
  * physical 3+1D chirality/spin-statistics/time: OPEN.

This inventory deliberately does not upgrade any conditional boundary
construction or abstract E8 sector theorem into a native TFPT origin result.
