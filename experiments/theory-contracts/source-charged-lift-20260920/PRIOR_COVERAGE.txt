Scope
-----

This is a source inventory for the newly exposed charged-lift ambiguity. It
does not add a theorem or modify any repository contract. The prior coverage
already proves the concrete T-versus-F ambiguity and its charged OPE effect;
the broader classification of all integral isometric sections remains the
candidate nonduplicate theorem.

Candidate theorem status
------------------------

The existing sources prove the concrete D8 ambiguity needed for a review:

  T(r)=F(r)+(a.r/2)n,
  a.r/2 in Z for D8 roots,
  same neutral quotient class, different charged action.

The proposed stronger statement that every integral isometric section has

  F_lambda(r)=F(r)+(lambda.r)n,  lambda in E8,

is not stated as a general classification in these four contracts. The
specific D8 restriction T would require lambda=a/2. Since a/2 has five
negative half-signs, it is not an E8 half-root (the E8 half-coset requires an
even number of negative signs). Therefore the extension obstruction may be a
new theorem, provided it is derived as a classification and kept separate
from the already-proved concrete OPE counterexample.
