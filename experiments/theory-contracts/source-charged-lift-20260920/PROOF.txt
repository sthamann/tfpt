UR.SOURCE.CHARGED_LIFT.01
20 September 2026

QUESTION AND SCOPE

The neutral E8 quotient of UR.SOURCE.FLUX_INDEX.01 does not yet specify
the action on charged fields. UR.SOURCE.CLIFFORD_GRADE.01 exhibits this
with two representatives F(r),T(r) and the charged seed e9. Here we ask
whether ANY integral additive lift of the same quotient can retain the
common charged-spinor action, and what minimal local lattice change would
make the full T(E8) lift possible.

The theorem is about additive lattice-vertex lifts with fixed quotient
root labels and bilinear locality. It is not a classification of arbitrary
interacting QFTs, non-vertex currents, nonlocal dressings or twisted sectors.
No new Hamiltonian, vacuum or gauge measure is chosen. E8 is an INTERNAL
lattice here, not a spacetime dimension or a derived physical gauge group.

INPUTS AND PRIOR RESULTS

Gamma=Z^10, K=diag(1^9,-1), B(x,y)=x^T K y,
a=(1,1,1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1), n=(a,-1,3), z=e9-e10, g=K n.
Thus B(n,n)=B(z,z)=0, B(n,z)=2, B(n,x)=g.x.
The original q=(1,...,1) and
Y=(-1/3,-1/3,-1/3,1/2,1/2,0,0,0,1,1) annihilate n and z.

Write E8=D8 union (s0+D8), with
D8={d in Z^8: sum(d) even} and s0=(1/2,...,1/2).
Let
  F(r)=(r-(a.r)a/2,0,-a.r),
  T(r)=(r,-a.r/2,a.r/2).
The prior contracts prove
  ker(g.)=F(E8) direct-sum Z n,
  B(F(r),F(s))=r.s,
  T(r)=F(r)+(a.r/2)n,
  Gamma=F(E8) direct-sum Z e_R direct-sum Z m,
  e_R=-e9, m=n+e9, B(e_R,e_R)=1, B(m,m)=-1,
with F(E8) orthogonal to that pair. The full decomposition is unimodular.

The existing charged seed e9 has g-charge -1. Under the common T(D8)
currents its Cartan weight is p0=-a/2, the opposite D8 spinor class c.
Its previously constructed 128-weight orbit and critical energy Vc are
prior conditional results, not derived again here.

1. COMPLETE CLASSIFICATION OF INTEGRAL ADDITIVE SECTIONS

Let pi:ker(g.) -> E8 be the fixed quotient map, pi(F(r)+j n)=r.
Every additive section S with pi S=id differs from F by
  S(r)-F(r)=ell(r)n,
where ell:E8->Z is a homomorphism. E8 is integral unimodular, so every
such homomorphism has a unique representation ell(r)=lambda.r with
lambda in E8. Conversely every lambda in E8 gives a section

  S_lambda(r)=F(r)+(lambda.r)n.                         (1)

It is automatically isometric because n is null and orthogonal to ker(g.).
There is no finite-search cutoff in this classification.

For any original local vector x=F(r_x)+A e_R+B m in Gamma, the weight
read by these lifted Cartan directions is

  B(S_lambda(r),x)=r.(r_x+(A-B)lambda).                (2)

In particular the weight belongs to E8 for EVERY integral x and every
section. For x=e9 it is -lambda. This is stronger than comparing F and T
at a single root: changing the integral charged representative does not
produce a Cartan weight outside E8 under one of these full E8 lifts.

2. THE COMMON CHARGED-SPINOR ACTION CANNOT EXTEND THIS WAY

For e9, the required common action is
  B(T(r),e9)=-(a.r)/2.
Matching this on a spanning set of D8 roots, or just the rank-eight
D5+D3 product-root system, forces lambda=a/2 in (1).

But a/2=s0+(0,0,0,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1); the latter integral shift has odd
sum. Therefore a/2 is NOT in E8. Equivalently it has five negative
half-signs rather than the even parity of the E8 half-coset.
Thus no integral additive section of the full neutral E8 quotient
preserves the stated common charged action. More generally (2) excludes
all opposite-spinor c weights under such a section, since that entire
coset is disjoint from E8. This conclusion fixes the common Cartan/root
identification; it is not a universal ban on physical fermions or other
E8 embeddings, and it does not require that E8 be a physical gauge group.

The maximal domain of T that remains inside the original Gamma is exactly
D8. Indeed a.d is even for d in D8. On s0+D8, a.s0=-1 and a.d is even,
so (a.r)/2 is half-integral. Consequently T has 112 integral D8 root
vectors but all 128 half-root vectors lie outside Gamma. [E8:D8]=2.

There is also a locality witness for EVERY original odd field, not only e9.
For an E8 half-root r and any x in Gamma,

  B(T(r),x)=B(F(r),x)+(a.r/2)g.x,
  exp(2 pi i B(T(r),x))=(-1)^(g.x).                   (3)

All components of g are odd, so g.x = B(x,x) mod2. Equation (3) is -1
on every odd local Gamma vertex. An even spinor current cannot have this
nontrivial full monodromy with a mutually local field. A cocycle phase
can implement ordinary exchange signs but cannot change this monodromy.
This statement uses the standard lattice-vertex locality dictionary.

3. q AND Y DO NOT SELECT A LIFT BY THEMSELVES

Because q.n=Y.n=0, every S_lambda in (1) has the same q and Y markings.
To see that the freedom is compatible even with the whole integral
lattice, set t=F(lambda), and define the usual null shear

  L_lambda x=x-(g.x)t+B(x,t)n-(lambda^2/2)(g.x)n.      (4)

For lambda in E8, its squared norm is even. All coefficients in (4) are
integral on Gamma. Direct bilinear expansion gives B(Lx,Ly)=B(x,y),
L_lambda n=n, and L_-lambda=L_lambda^-1. Hence this is an integral
unimodular isometry, and L_lambda F(r)=S_lambda(r).

It preserves the q and Y covectors on ALL Gamma whenever q.t=Y.t=0.
For example lambda=e1-e2 and every integer multiple satisfy both
conditions. Thus the existing two global markings do not distinguish
these lifts. If fields, states and energy are all transformed together,
(4) is a coordinate change, not a new physical theory. The unresolved
choice concerns their identification with the fixed source fields and
time. We do not count infinitely many coordinate choices as infinitely
many experimentally distinct theories.

4. MINIMAL LOCAL REPAIR: AN EVEN NEIGHBOR, NOT THE SAME FIELD ALGEBRA

Put h=n/2 and
  Gamma_0={x in Gamma:g.x even}.
This is also the even-norm sublattice of Gamma and has index2. Since
  B(h,x)=g.x/2,
Gamma_0 is exactly the part of the original lattice mutually integral
with h. The lattice

  L_h=Gamma_0+Z h                                    (5)

is integral and even: h^2=0, Gamma_0 has even norm, and
(x+jh)^2=x^2+j g.x is even for x in Gamma_0. It contains Gamma_0 with
index2. Because |det Gamma_0|=4, |det L_h|=1; the signature is (9,1).

It has the following explicit orthogonal decomposition:

  L_h=T(E8) direct-sum Z h direct-sum Z z,
  Gram(h,z)=[[0,1],[1,0]]=U.                          (6)

For a direct proof of equality, first T(r)=F(r)+(a.r)h belongs to L_h
for all r in E8; a.r is integral, and F(r) is in Gamma_0. Also z is in
Gamma_0. These generators have block Gram diag(G_E8,U), determinant-1,
so their full-rank sublattice of the unimodular L_h has index1. Thus
L_h is the even unimodular lattice E8+U, usually denoted II_(9,1).

The repair is minimal after fixing the retained Gamma_0 and full T(E8):
one half-root r has odd a.r, so T(r)-F(r) is an odd multiple of h.
Since n=2h lies in Gamma_0, these data already generate h and therefore
(5). There is no proper intermediate integral lattice containing both.

Every original odd field is absent from L_h: L_h intersect Gamma=Gamma_0.
Its nontrivial monodromy with the added currents was already seen in (3).
For example e9 is not in L_h. Consequently all the original charged
c-spinor fields of charge -1 are not local fields of this repaired
bosonic lattice. It is legitimate to investigate them as twisted fields
or as line endpoints in a larger theory, but that extra field-sector
construction and its dynamics are not supplied by (5).

This explains the distinction between two operations:
  (i) choosing an integral representative of a neutral quotient class;
  (ii) changing the local field lattice by retaining an even sublattice
       and adjoining a different sector.
Operation (ii) is not an ordinary basis change on the original Gamma.

5. BOTH EVEN NEIGHBORS AND THE CHARGE-NORMALIZATION CHANGE

There is no unconditional unique-sector choice. The dual quotient
Gamma_0^*/Gamma_0 has four classes, represented by
  0, f=e9, h=n/2, h+f.
Their quadratic values Q(x)=B(x,x)/2 mod1 are respectively
  0, 1/2, 0, 0,
and B(h,f)=-1/2 mod1. Thus there are two even unimodular neighbors:
  L_h=Gamma_0+Zh,
  L_c=Gamma_0+Z(h+f).
The original odd lattice is Gamma_0+Zf. Requiring specifically T(E8)
selects the h neighbor, since its half-root class is h rather than h+f.
This is conditional selection by the desired current embedding, not an
origin theorem selecting a projection or a twisted sector.

The old q and Y extend to L_h unchanged because they vanish on h,z.
The old gauge covector g, however, has image 2Z on L_h: it vanishes on
T(E8),h and gives g.z=2. Relative to the NEW local lattice, g/2 is the
primitive covector. It is inconsistent to say simultaneously that (5)
keeps the old charge-one fields as local operators and that the original
primitive-charge normalization was unchanged.

Although h=n/2 is half the OLD index vector, adding its vertex is not a
derivation of a physical half-flux event. The previous integer-flux
index theorem assumed the old charged channels and their bundle. A
different sector or gauge normalization requires a new source/bundle
and operator map; the native P1 winding does not provide it automatically.

6. PHYSICAL INTERPRETATION AND LITERATURE BOUNDARY

The exact algebra clarifies why two successful partial constructions
cannot simply be superimposed: the common D8 spinor field algebra and
the desired local T(E8) current extension have incompatible mutual
locality on the original odd fields. A consistent even-lattice repair
exists and has been written explicitly, but it changes which fields
are local. It is not a source-derived gauge process or a solution of
the native 64-CAR/60-CCR to boundary intertwining problem.

Projection plus twisted-sector reconstruction and fermionization of
the E8 theory are established structures. A primary comparison is
Burbano--Kulp--Neuser, arXiv:2112.14323, sections3.5.2--3.5.3. Their
field-theory spin-structure/GSO discussion does not identify the finite
TFPT Clifford matrices with an infinite chiral field algebra. Our
rank-ten statements above are proved directly from the given lattice;
no novelty is claimed for bosonization, GSO projections or even neighbors.

The next source obligation is an actual choice and realization of local
observables AND charged/twisted sectors with a common time, rather than
another quotient-root count. If only the neutral E8 sector is intended,
one need not force the particular c fields into its local algebra.
If those fields are the intended matter, their additional sector and
its correlations must be retained. E8 remains a compiler/current
structure here, not a mandatory spacetime gauge group.
