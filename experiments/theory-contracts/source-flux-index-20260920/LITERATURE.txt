1. R. Narayanan and H. Neuberger
   Massless Composite Fermions in Two Dimensions and the Overlap.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-lat/9609031
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-lat/9609031

Read: pp.2-5, the continuum 11112 example, its derivative-dressed
zero-mode-saturating insertion and the warning about unjustified
exponentiation into a dilute gas. The source distinguishes the correlator
from an automatic local effective action. This is the methodological
precedent used in PROOF.txt section3. Its particular composite spectrum
and amplitudes are not transferred to our unequal-chirality 9+1 system.

2. Chong Wang
   Bound states of three fermions forming symmetry-protected topological
   phases. Phys. Rev. B 91, 245124 (2015).
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.91.245124
   Accepted author paper:
   https://link.aps.org/accepted/10.1103/PhysRevB.91.245124

Read: sectionI p.2 and especially sectionIII pp.3-4. The latter explicitly
starts with nine charge-one fermion Chern insulators and one oppositely
chiral charge-three cluston Chern insulator. After gauging U(1), its chosen
gapped bulk construction yields the bosonic E8 phase. Vanishing charge
Hall response and chiral central charge8 explain the relevant pattern.

This is direct prior art for the 9+1 charged-fermion route to E8. We make
no novelty claim for that mechanism. Matching its charge/chirality data
does not establish an equivalence of microscopic Hamiltonians. The paper
chooses bands, a 2+1d bulk and gauge dynamics. It does not derive them from
TFPT P1/P2, the native 64-CAR/60-CCR source, a rotor clock or this boundary
energy metric. We do not export a blanket confinement statement to
gapless matter or arbitrary compact U(1) theories. The two-dimensional
index calculation in PROOF.txt and Wang's 2+1d bulk construction are
distinct arguments with distinct premises.
