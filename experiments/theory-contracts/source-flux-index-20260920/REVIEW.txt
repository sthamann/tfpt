1. Noncircular conditional selection of g
------------------------------------------

The previous proof first classified every integral covector neutral on the
full transported E8 lattice:

  g(v,u)=(v a,u,-3v),                         u,v in Z.           (1)

It then selected u=-v by demanding that the already desired n be neutral.
That route is a consistency check but cannot be advertised as an origin of
n: using n to choose g and then using g to recover n would be circular.

There is, however, a noncircular conditional selection using symmetries that
were present before the new gauging.  For (1), with K^-1=K,

  A_gg = g^T K g = u^2-v^2,                                      (2)

while the mixed anomaly with the original source number q=(1,...,1) is

  A_qg = q^T K g = u+v.                                          (3)

The same result holds for the declared hypercharge Y because
Y_(1:8).a=-2 and Y_9=Y_10=1:

  A_Yg = Y^T K g = u+v.                                          (4)

Consequently either of the following independent requirements selects the
same branch:

  * full F(E8)-neutrality plus preservation of q and pure gauge anomaly
    freedom; or
  * full F(E8)-neutrality plus preservation of Y and pure gauge anomaly
    freedom.

Equations (3) or (4) force u=-v.  Primitive nonzero normalization then
forces |v|=1, hence

  g=+/-K n.                                                       (5)

The alternative pure-anomaly-free branch u=+v violates both mixed
conditions by 2v.  Thus (5) can be selected without assuming n-neutrality,
provided preservation of at least one of q or Y under the proposed gauging
is an actual premise.  This is still conditional on the previously chosen
ten-channel source and E8 lift F; it is not an axiomatic TFPT derivation.


2. Unit flux fixes the signed vertex n
--------------------------------------

For a two-dimensional Weyl fermion of integral charge g_i, the Dirac index
in a U(1) sector of flux Q is the signed multiplicity g_i Q.  Chirality
reverses which Grassmann field or conjugate saturates the zero modes.  With
the present K convention, the complete signed insertion vector is therefore

  ell(Q)=Q K g.                                                   (6)

Using (5), a unit flux gives

  ell(+1)=n,       ell(-1)=-n.                                   (7)

This also checks gauge invariance directly:

  g.ell(+1)=g^T K g=0.                                           (8)

The original q and Y charges vanish for the same reason as the mixed
anomalies.  Hence, once the compact gauging of exactly g is assumed, n is
not an arbitrary twelve-body charge pattern: it is the unique signed
zero-mode saturation rule of the minimal nonzero flux sector.

The word "selection rule" is essential.  Equation (7) states which
correlator can saturate the Grassmann zero modes in that topological sector.
It does not by itself insert a nonzero local Hamiltonian term.


3. Local flat-patch representative and free weights
----------------------------------------------------

The first nine entries of n have magnitude one and belong to nine distinct
right-moving channels.  They therefore contribute nine distinct fermion or
conjugate-fermion insertions, with no Pauli collision.  Their free conformal
weight is

  h=9*(1/2)=9/2.                                                  (9)

The last entry has magnitude three in one left-moving channel.  Three
identical Grassmann fields cannot be multiplied at one point.  Gauge-covariant
point splitting, followed by coincidence, produces the Vandermonde
descendant

  chi (D_bar chi) (D_bar^2 chi)                                  (10)

up to normalization, orientation/conjugation and lower connection terms.
In a flat free-field gauge it is represented by chi dchi d^2chi.  Its weight
is

  hbar=1/2+3/2+5/2=9/2.                                          (11)

Thus the minimal local representative contains twelve elementary fermion
factors and three derivatives, with

  (h,hbar)=(9/2,9/2),  Delta=9,  spin=0.                          (12)

This independently reproduces the free scaling dimension of the vertex n.
The derivative statement concerns the coincident local representative; the
individual zero modes on a compact surface are global sections and depend
on the gauge background and spin structure.

Narayanan and Neuberger's exactly solved 11112 model gives the directly
analogous charge-two formula

  chi_1 chi_2 chi_3 chi_4 psi (d psi)

and explicitly identifies it as the unit-instanton 't Hooft vertex.  The
same paper warns that a dilute-gas exponentiation into an effective local
Lagrangian is not justified merely from this vertex:

  R. Narayanan and H. Neuberger, "Massless Composite Fermions in Two
  Dimensions and the Overlap", arXiv:hep-lat/9609031, especially the
  discussion around its equations for V and <V>.
  https://arxiv.org/html/hep-lat/9609031v1


4. What the index does not determine
------------------------------------

The unit-flux result does not determine any of the following:

  * that the Q=+/-1 sectors exponentiate to a local term
    lambda O_n + conjugate;
  * that lambda is nonzero in the chosen volume, regulator, spin structure
    and topological-sector sum;
  * the magnitude or phase of lambda (a theta angle changes the relative
    phase of the two flux orientations);
  * that the resulting shorthand is literally cos(n) with the normalization
    used in the earlier boundary Hamiltonian;
  * relevance, pinning, a selected vacuum, domain formation or an Ising
    critical point.

On a compact Euclidean surface, flux sectors and their zero modes are exact.
Calling them a dilute gas of localized instantons on the infinite plane is
an additional approximation.  The safe conclusion is the sector-wise
selection rule (7), not a generated cosine coefficient.


5. Equal-velocity local bosonization
------------------------------------

The optional Schwinger statement is correct at the local quadratic level
under explicit extra assumptions: the ten free chiral channels have equal
velocity, the only new interaction is a minimally coupled compact U(1)
gauge field with Maxwell normalization (1/4e^2)F^2, and no additional
Thirring/current-current term is inserted.

Let g_R be the first nine entries of g.  Since |g_R|^2=9, define the
canonically normalized charged right boson

  Phi_c=(g_R.phi_R)/3.                                           (13)

An orthogonal rotation of the equal-velocity right kinetic term leaves
eight right-moving bosons in g_R^perp and one Phi_c.  Under the gauge
transformation, Phi_c has charge magnitude three.  Together with the
charge-magnitude-three left channel it forms the quadratic charged
Schwinger sector.  The remaining eight oscillator directions are neutral.

The anomaly-free one-loop/bosonized polarization gives

  m_S^2 = (e^2/(2 pi)) (|g_R|^2+g_10^2)
        = (e^2/(2 pi)) (9+9)
        = 9 e^2/pi.                                              (14)

This agrees with the standard charge-q Schwinger value q^2 e^2/pi for
q=3.  The coefficient assumes the stated Maxwell convention; moving e into
the covariant derivative changes notation, not the physical formula.

"Charge-three pair" in this paragraph describes the quadratic current
sector.  The right collective vertex g_R/3 is not an integral vector of the
original microscopic lattice and is not, by itself, a new local elementary
fermion.  The compactification lattice, cocycles and large-gauge sectors
remain glued to the neutral directions.


6. Exact E8 quotient and the remaining global step
---------------------------------------------------

There is an exact algebraic result stronger than dimension counting.  Since
g=K n,

  L=ker(g.)=n^perp with respect to B(x,y)=x^T K y.                 (15)

The radical of L is Z n.  The vector n is primitive, null and
characteristic in the odd unimodular lattice I_(9,1).  Therefore

  L/Z n                                                          (16)

is positive definite, even, unimodular and rank eight.  It is consequently
isometric to the E8 lattice.

The roots can be displayed without invoking uniqueness.  Flip particle and
antiparticle conventions so the nine right charges are all +1 and the left
charge is +3.  The quotient contains

  * 72 A8 roots e_i-e_j; and
  * 168 roots represented by +/-[(e_i+e_j+e_k)-e_10], one for each
    unordered triple among nine right channels.

There are 2*binomial(9,3)=168 representatives of the second kind.  All are
gauge neutral and have induced norm two.  Adding one such order-three
coset to A8 gives an index-three even unimodular extension, hence the 240
roots of E8.

This proves the neutral quotient lattice and identifies the candidate
weight-one root system.  It does not alone prove that the full infrared
operator algebra is the E8 level-one lattice VOA.  Operators differing by
n are distinct ultraviolet representatives before the charged/topological
sector is treated.  Promoting the quotient to the physical IR algebra needs
at least:

  * a consistent compact gauge regulator or bulk inflow for the surviving
    gravitational anomaly c_R-c_L=8;
  * the large-gauge, holonomy, spin-structure and theta-sector projection;
  * a vacuum/topological-sector calculation showing which n-dressed
    representatives have nonzero correlators and are identified in the IR;
  * preservation of the full lattice cocycles/glue and absence or control
    of allowed marginal current-current deformations.

The nearby cluston construction is useful corroboration of the charge
pattern: nine charge-one IQH edges together with one oppositely chiral
charge-three cluston edge represent a neutral E8 state.  That result uses a
2+1-dimensional topological bulk and cannot be imported as the missing
standalone 1+1-dimensional TFPT derivation:

  S.-J. Huang and M. Hermele, "Surface field theories of point group
  symmetry protected topological phases", Phys. Rev. B 97, 075145 (2018),
  especially the charge-one/charge-three construction around eqs. (58)-(67).
  https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.97.075145


7. Smallest positive bridge and final verdict
---------------------------------------------

The strongest currently justified statement is:

  CONDITIONAL THEOREM.  Assume the already chosen ten-channel I_(9,1)
  source and E8 lift F are coupled to a compact U(1); demand full F(E8)
  neutrality, pure gauge anomaly cancellation, primitive charge and
  preservation of the pre-existing q or Y symmetry.  Then the gauge charge
  is uniquely g=+/-K n.  A minimal nonzero Euclidean flux has signed fermion
  zero-mode insertion +/-n.  Its flat free local representative contains
  twelve fermion factors and three derivatives and has
  (h,hbar)=(9/2,9/2).  The neutral quotient lattice is E8.

This is genuine progress over treating n as a completely free twelve-body
charge pattern: within this explicitly gauged source, topology fixes its
selection rule.  It is not yet an origin of a nonzero local cos(n) coupling
or of its phase and vacuum.

The optional equal-velocity bosonization gives eight neutral chiral
oscillator modes plus one massive charge-three Schwinger sector with
m_S^2=9e^2/pi.  Calling the complete infrared theory E8 still requires the
global gauge/glue/vacuum analysis listed above.

Research verdict: PASS for the conditional charge-and-flux selection;
PARTIAL for the proposed origin bridge; NOT ESTABLISHED for a native TFPT
gauging, a generated cosine, the whole IR E8 algebra, flavor/Yukawa physics,
gravity cancellation or a TOE claim.  The old two-species rotor is a
different microscopic system and supplies none of these missing premises.

  Chong Wang, "Bound states of three fermions forming symmetry-protected

In Abschnitt III startet die Konstruktion fuer den zweidimensionalen
E8-Zustand mit neun freien Fermion-Chern-Isolatoren und einem
entgegengesetzt chiralen Ladung-3-Cluston-Chern-Isolator. Anschliessend wird
die U(1)-Symmetrie kompakt geeicht. Weil die Hall-Leitfaehigkeit der
Gesamtkonstruktion verschwindet, entsteht kein Chern-Simons-Term; die dort
angenommene 2+1-dimensionale kompakte U(1)-Dynamik konfiniert und hinterlaesst
einen unfraktionierten bosonischen Zustand mit chiraler Kantenladung8. Die

1xLadung3-Mechanismus als Huang--Hermele 2018. Fuer diesen allgemeinen

konkreten kompakten Eich-/Konfinierungsdynamik. Daraus folgen weder die
