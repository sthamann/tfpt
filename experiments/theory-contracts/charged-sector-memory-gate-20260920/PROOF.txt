1. Why this is the decisive test of the new proposal

The proposed common Q could carry (i) missing current commutators,
(ii) Feshbach self-energy and (iii) temporal memory. The generic projection
identities are correct, but (i) uses QA P for current operators A, whereas
(ii)/(iii) require the independently different corner QH P of the physical
Hamiltonian. These corners must be checked on the SAME representation.
We use the intended E8_1 theory itself, not another auxiliary world.

The pre-existing A3->D3 isometry, lambda->s dictionary, D8+spinor closure
and conditional C/J velocity selection are retained. They do not by
themselves establish QHP!=0 or a microscopic source.

2. The exact grading in the target

Let D={p in Z^8:sum p_i even}, s=(1/2)^8, and L=D union (D+s)=E8.
The lattice Hilbert space is the orthogonal direct sum
  H_E8=(M(1) tensor C[D]) direct_sum (M(1) tensor C[D+s]),
where M(1) is the eight-current oscillator Fock space. Define theta to be
+1 on the first summand and -1 on the second. It is the Z2 character of
L/D and an automorphism of the bosonic E8 algebra. This is the extension
grading, NOT microscopic fermion parity; both summands are bosonic here.
Set P=(1+theta)/2, Q=(1-theta)/2.

The common Sugawara operator is
  L0=p^2/2 + sum_(n>0) a_-n . a_n.
It preserves each lattice charge and therefore each coset. In particular
P strongly commutes with L0 and its spectral projections. For the very
time proposed in the pasted text, H=aL0+c, this gives
  QHP=PHQ=0                                             (1)
on the common finite-energy core, with the corresponding closed reducing
decomposition on D(H). This also holds for the quadratic Cartan family H_V
of source-rg-clock-bridge, which preserves every lattice charge.
No assumption about a finite energy truncation is required.

3. The charged current really does use Q

Take the norm-two spinor root beta=s and n>0. A spinor current changes
the extension grading, so PS_beta,m P=QS_beta,m Q=0. In the unitary affine
vacuum representation choose phases so that
  B=S_beta,-n, A=B*=S_-beta,n.
Then A Omega=0, B Omega is in QH, and the level-one relation gives
  ||B Omega||^2=n.
Consequently
  P[A,B]P Omega = P A Q B P Omega = n Omega,             (2)
although PAP=PBP=0. The other complement term vanishes on Omega.
All these statements concern the invariant finite-energy core; the
unbounded modes have not been declared bounded on the full Hilbert space.

The explicit mixed path [J_D8,S] in the user text is different: J_D8 is
block diagonal, S is block off diagonal, so P[J_D8,S]P=0. Its full-space
commutator is nonzero but is not itself the neutral Q-return path in (2).
In the D8 vacuum theory alone Q is not already a hidden orthogonal subspace;
the spinor module and the common Hilbert projector appear after the field
extension. This must not be confused with an unexamined microscopic Q.

Thus the complement supplies a nonzero observable current response exactly
as suggested in the new text. But (1) and (2) coexist in the same theory.
Availability of a transition operator is not autonomous transition by H.

4. The Feshbach and memory consequences for this SAME P

Write A0=PHP, D0=QHQ, B0=QHP. For bounded H, or for an unbounded block
operator with the required common domains/resolvents, the Schur complement is
  P(z-H)^-1P = [z-A0-B0*(z-D0)^-1 B0]^-1.
The time equations for p(t)=P psi(t), q(t)=Q psi(t) are
  i p'=A0 p+B0* q,
  q(t)=exp(-itD0)q(0)-i integral_0^t exp(-i(t-u)D0)B0 p(u)du.
Substitution gives the kernel
  K(t)=B0* exp(-itD0) B0
and a separate initial-state driving term B0* exp(-itD0)q(0).

For (1), all these terms are identically zero:
  Sigma(z)=0, K(t)=0, initial Q driving=0.               (3)
The P dynamics is simply generated by H restricted to PH. There is no
vacuum lowering from this eliminated sector and no temporal memory arising
from this sector elimination. This does not exclude memory in another
coarse graining, a partial trace, measurement dynamics, or a different H.

The two sectors already have overlapping energy a+c at grade one (120 vs
128 states). The spinor complement is not a uniformly high-energy space.
At its spectral points the displayed Q resolvent is not defined; use
nonreal z for the exact identity. A hypothetical coupling between those
states requires degenerate mixing, not ordinary gapped adiabatic elimination.

Smallest decisive test for a proposed microscopic completion:
  K(0)=B0*B0,  <psi,K(0)psi>=||QHP psi||^2.             (4)
If B0 is bounded, K(t) is strongly continuous and K identically zero iff
B0=0. For unbounded B0 use (4) as a quadratic-form statement on its specified
domain; existence of the full time kernel still requires a domain theorem.
Likewise, for real z with D0-z>=delta I>0, the self-energy obeys
  -Sigma(z)=B0*(D0-z)^-1 B0 >=0.
Any state with B0 psi!=0 has a strictly positive quadratic form on the
right (when defined). Thus memory and below-complement dressing are two
views of the SAME Hamiltonian coupling, but current response (2) does not
establish that coupling. Small energy denominators require separate control.

5. Clock covariance further distinguishes a sector from a full readout

On grade one, PH has the eight Cartan states and 112 integer D8 roots;
QH has 128 spinor roots. The actual Coxeter isometry preserves the Cartan
space and sends 56 of the integer roots to spinor roots. Hence
  rank(Q U_C P | grade1)=56,
  dim(P grade1 intersect U_C P grade1)=8+56=64.
P is therefore NOT invariant under this clock, even though P commutes with
H and U_C commutes with H in the abstract target. A clock-induced change
of sector is not a time-induced change of sector.

The cumulative C-orbit of the retained integer roots has counts
  112,168,200,220,228,234,236,238,240.
After eight powers, the linear span together with the Cartan is the entire
248-dimensional grade-one space. The distinct D8 root-subset orbit has
period 15. These counts use the pinned original matrix, not an arbitrary
Coxeter conjugacy class. Cocycles can multiply root images by phases but
cannot change these intersection/rank counts.

Positive consequence: A retained grade-one subspace containing all D8
currents and invariant under the actual C must already contain ALL 248
E8 currents. The D8 truncation cannot be a clock-covariant complete current
readout. This is compatible with using it as an intentionally incomplete
subalgebra, provided that incompleteness is retained in future responses.

6. What a viable continuation must supply

The extension exists mathematically; this proof does not refute it. Nor is
the spinor sector 'forbidden by superselection' in the full E8 algebra:
spinor fields are precisely permitted intertwiners. What is absent is the
off-diagonal H coupling for the proposed sector projection and H=aL0+c.

There are two logically different possibilities to investigate in an actual
source, not to assume as repairs:
  (a) P is a physical local/readout projection, rather than the full D8
      coset projection. H may preserve a total charge of the full source
      while not preserving this local/readout P, allowing QHP!=0;
      e.g. a local sector can exchange a compensating charge with another
      part of the same source. The choice of P and that coupling must be
      derived and must preserve the claimed clock/charge identifications.
  (b) Microscopic H has a genuine cross-sector corner relative to this P,
      while H=aL0+c is only an effective limit. Then show whether the corner
      and its appropriately rescaled response survive that limit. Exact
      affine covariance at every scale cannot simply be assumed as well.

In continuum AQFT, taking P to project onto the closure of A(O)Omega is
not an automatic local reduction: Reeh--Schlieder can make that closure all
of H. An operator-level conditional expectation E, if it exists with the
needed state/modular compatibility, has instead the Liouvillian mixing
condition (1-E) L E !=0, with E realized as the corresponding orthogonal
projection in GNS-L2 and with the Liouvillian domain preserved. Existence
and the source-derived choice of such E
are additional obligations. A dynamically covariant E gives no memory
from that elimination either.

Neither follows by labelling an arbitrary Q 'spinor'. A nonzero field
corner QSP, a nonzero Hamiltonian corner QHP, and a source-to-record
instrument are three separate obligations. A field insertion performed
by an external probe is not an autonomous event law.

The proposed alternative of selecting a minimal bosonic invertible chiral
phase is a conditional route to E8, not a new source derivation here.
One must first establish bosonic locality, invertibility, a gravitational
anomaly and the meaning of primitivity in that phase classification from
the original P1 data. P1 unit seam winding cannot silently be identified
with the generator of the c=8 invertible phase group.

6a. Positive conditional selection: why 16, and what would derive it

For N=2n free chiral real Majoranas, the even algebra is SO(2n)_1. Its
spinor weight s_n=(1/2,...,1/2) has
  h_s=s_n^2/2=n/8=N/16.
Thus demanding that the SOURCE spinor itself realize the fixed TFPT
weight-one glue field WITH the same conformal stress normalization implies
N=16 in this class. Merely demanding a
bosonic local spinor extension requires h_s integral, hence N in 16Z.
The smallest positive member is again N=16.

Existence here is stronger than just a spin-statistics count. For the
standard D_n lattice and n even, 2s_n is in D_n, s_n . D_n is integral,
and the index-two extension D_n union (D_n+s_n) is even precisely when
s_n^2=n/4 is an even integer, i.e. n in 8Z. Its determinant is 4/2^2=1.
At n=8 this is the E8 even unimodular lattice, so the extension genuinely
exists and supplies the 128 weight-one spinor currents. At n=16 the spinor
weight is 2 instead, illustrating why integer spin alone does not select
the current content. Odd-N spinors are not the same simple-current setup.

This is a conditional selection theorem, not a derivation of the microscopic
CAR source. It assumes the free chiral Majorana class, the physical spinor
interpretation, and the demand for its bosonic local extension (or its
identification with the weight-one TFPT glue). Those are precisely the
identifications that must be obtained from P1, not fitted afterward.

A broader version starts with an ACTUAL bosonic, spin-independent,
invertible 2+1D gapped phase with no protecting symmetry. Its chiral
gravitational index is c_-/8 in Z. If it is nontrivial and primitive under
stacking, orientation fixes c_-=+8 or -8. To conclude the E8_1 edge one
also needs a fully chiral conformal limit with the regularity/holomorphy
hypotheses of the classification theorem (e.g. strongly rational holomorphic
VOA of CFT type at c=8, Dong--Mason). Invertible bulk alone does not select
physical edge velocities or prove this continuum limit.

The missing raw map is a normalized anomaly transgression tau from the
P1 seam class to that bosonic invertible-phase index, including tau(1)=+/-1.
A group homomorphism Z->Z need not preserve primitive generators. P1's
unit winding is not this normalized map. The existing v489 c=8 diagnostic
starts with 16 decoupled layers and does not supply it.

The test must determine the anomaly's magnitude, not just a modular phase.
For a holomorphic theory the character multiplier is
  T_00=exp(-2*pi*i*c/24).
E8 (c=8) and E8^4 (c=32) both have one module, S=(1), and the SAME T_00.
Thus ordinary S,T data or a single Dehn-twist phase cannot prove primitive
index one. Thermal Hall response relative to a specified trivial reference
can distinguish these: kappa_xy/T=(pi^2 k_B^2/(3h))*c_- under its bulk/edge
and low-temperature assumptions. No such raw P1 response was derived here.
The modular commutator alone is not a substitute: Gass--Levin (2024) give
c_-=0 systems with spurious nonzero values absent further hypotheses.
