QUESTION AND SMALLEST TEST

Let V be the E8 adjoint one-particle space and choose normalized root vectors
E_alpha.  Consider

    Q |alpha> = E_alpha tensor conjugate(E_alpha),
    D_root = span{Q|alpha> : alpha an E8 root}.

For a unitary root-monomial lift

    S_g E_alpha = eta_g(alpha) E_{g alpha},  |eta_g(alpha)|=1,

one has exactly

    (S_g tensor conjugate(S_g)) Q|alpha>
      = eta_g(alpha) conjugate(eta_g(alpha)) Q|g alpha>
      = Q R_g |alpha>.

This remains true for arbitrary U(1) root phases.  A diagonal character left
by a nontrivial lift word also cancels pointwise against its conjugate.  Hence
the .18 lift signs, including negative fixed-root signs and the recorded word
characters, disappear on this paired root-label representation.  Every
root-diagonal Hamiltonian is transported tautologically, and every finite
root-monomial permutation is intertwined.

The decisive issue is closure under the actual current algebra, not another
lift census.  Identify V tensor conjugate(V) with End(V).  Then Q|alpha> is
the rank-one root projector P_alpha, and the diagonal infinitesimal current
action is

    delta_X(T) = [ad_X, T].

Every E8 root supplies an sl2 triple (E_alpha,H_alpha,E_minus_alpha).  Apply
the single zero mode X=E_alpha to P_minus_alpha.  On E_minus_alpha,

    [ad_E_alpha,P_minus_alpha] E_minus_alpha
      = ad_E_alpha E_minus_alpha
        - P_minus_alpha(ad_E_alpha E_minus_alpha)
      = H_alpha.

But every element of D_root maps E_minus_alpha to a scalar multiple of
E_minus_alpha, never to H_alpha.  Therefore

    delta_E_alpha(P_minus_alpha) not in D_root.

The failure is already exact in one mandatory root sl2 subalgebra; no large
Hilbert-space model is needed.  The accompanying integer checker records the
nonzero matrix entry H_alpha <- E_minus_alpha as 1 in both normal and -OO
runs:

    neutral_current_bridge_check.py
    neutral_current_bridge_check.json

The full adjoint/dual invariant is instead the Killing/identity tensor

    Omega_K = sum_i H_i tensor H^i + sum_alpha E_alpha tensor E^alpha.

Because the E8 adjoint is irreducible, the invariant subspace in
adjoint tensor dual(adjoint) is one-dimensional (Schur).  The eight Cartan
terms are essential.  Omega_K is one singlet, not a 240-dimensional encoded
root register.  Keeping the whole 248 x 248 tensor product would restore a
representation, but would be a much larger construction whose physical
source and locality still have to be derived.
