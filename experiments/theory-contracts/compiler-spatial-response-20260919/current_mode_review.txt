with the compact adjoint and positive current metric

Then, at k=1,

  <i_n(a),i_n(b)> = kappa(a^dagger,b).

term n kappa(a^dagger,b), which the two square roots remove.  Therefore i_n is

  [L_0,J_-n(a)] = n J_-n(a),
