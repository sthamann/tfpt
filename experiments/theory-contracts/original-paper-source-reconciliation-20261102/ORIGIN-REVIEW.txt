# Origin and architecture corpus reaudit

2 November 2026. Read-only audit of the 17 document views assigned in
`work/corpus_reaudit/assignments.json`, group `origin`. No new theory,
scientific checker, model, proof run, canonical edit or Atlas mutation.
The old openness diagnosis was treated as a claim to test.

## Outcome for the parent's claims

The broad description that the original P1 consists only of the number c3,
with its entire source still to be constructed from that number, must be
corrected. The original architecture explicitly declares a primitive,
reflection-positive kernel on one oriented seam with unit winding. It
separately declares P2 as its five-slot 3+2 readout interface and proves its
algebraic consequences. Several constructions we approached again already
exist at precisely that relative compiler scope. Requiring their internal
clocks to be actively preparable physical gates would change their target.

This does not turn the compiler papers into a zero-input or complete physical
TOE derivation. The same original papers explicitly separate their structural
completion from the raw-seam realization and the strict T1--T8 program.
Their later updates must be followed rather than reviving each earlier gap
as an independent present obligation.
