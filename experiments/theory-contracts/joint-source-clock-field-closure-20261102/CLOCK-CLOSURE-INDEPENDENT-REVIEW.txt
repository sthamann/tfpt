Analytic proof without E8 classification
Write D8={q in Z8:sum q even}. Its standard simple-root basis is
  e0-e1,...,e6-e7,e6+e7,
with covolume2 and Gram determinant4.

The computed R is orthogonal of order3. Its columns fall into two sets
A={0,1,4,5}, B={2,3,6,7}. A columns have support in rows0,1,2,3;
B columns in rows4,5,6,7. Each supported four-vector has entries +/-1/2
and an ODD number of minus signs. The four entries of every column sum
to +1 or -1. These are identities of the explicit R, not root-count data.

Every D8 root is +/-e_i +/-e_j. For i,j in the same column set the
image is integral, has even coordinate sum and norm2, hence belongs to
D8. There are 2*binom(4,2)*4=48 such roots. For i,j in different column
sets the image has eight entries +/-1/2. Each four-entry block still
has odd sign parity, since multiplying a block by -1 flips four signs.
The total sign parity is therefore even. All these 4*4*4=64 images
lie in the SAME coset D8+s. In particular p is one such image, and
p-s=(0,0,-1,0,-1,0,0,0) belongs to D8.

D8 roots generate D8. Consequently R(D8) is contained in D8+Zs.
Conversely p belongs to R(D8), so D8+Zp=D8+Zs is contained in L.
This proves equality L=D8+Zs, without using W(D8) orbit saturation.

For q in D8, q*s=sum(q)/2 is integral, s*s=2 and 2s belongs to D8.
The lattice D8+Zs is therefore integral and even. The new coset has
order2, so its Gram determinant is det(D8)/2^2=4/4=1. Its norm2
half vectors are exactly the 128 choices of signs with even parity.
These facts identify the conventional D8+s even-unimodular lattice,
without needing the classification of rank8 unimodular lattices.
