Result
The fixed original spectral-action family does not introduce a spin-changing
endpoint variable. This is a statement about its literal variables, NOT an
all-P1 no-go and NOT a proof that charged coordinates require a new physical
postulate. On a supplied quotient even-CAR net, the existing localized bosonic
DHR automorphism already gives a local crossed-product extension with positive
state through the canonical expectation. Its normalized charged generator has
nonzero norm automatically. The unresolved original identification is the joint
map of local support, state, reflection, physical time, admissible restriction,
source charges and physical carrier actions. Neither absence from the original
vacuum observable algebra nor absence of a defect symbol in S_prim settles it.

3. What eta gluing does and does not supply

JMP35(1994)5155-5194, https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9405012,
author full text https://web.math.ucsb.edu/~dai/paper/dfJMP.pdf.
Section1 begins with a definite metric, orientation and spin structure;
Proposition2.15 places the exponentiated eta in the inverse determinant
line; Theorem2.20 glues by contraction of determinant lines. Equations

Thus eta is appropriate to transport the phase of an ACTUALLY SUPPLIED
operator/bundle/domain family. These results do not themselves select a
spin-changing defect family, its local charged module, or its physical

The ordinary J,eta,bar_eta field-source derivatives only insert fields
already specified in the source algebra. This identifies the absent
functional input; it does not require this particular construction if the
charged extension is instead used only as canonical DHR coordinates.

4. Nonzero DHR charged norm is already conditional mathematics
Let A be the given quotient even-CAR net and rho_s its existing localized,
transportable dimension-one bosonic spinor automorphism, with the fixed q
chart from HALF-WINDING-DHR-SOURCE-ADDENDUM.txt. Under the standard local
extension hypotheses let B=A crossed_product_rho_s Z2, with normalized
charged generator V and canonical expectation E:B->A. For the actual
given state omega_A set omega_B=omega_A composed with E. Then
  omega_B(V*V)=omega_A(1)=1.
Positivity follows from positivity of E and omega_A. This is the smallest
analytic test: a new nonzero reflected point-field calculation is not
needed merely to prove algebraic occurrence of a charged DHR coordinate.
The point-field UV limit, original OS identification and actual source
region/time correspondence are distinct claims.

Known fixed-class exclusion is retained only in its exact scope: source
CAR words, currents, W_ext and its functional calculus preserve the NS/R
sum. They cannot manufacture a Ramond-changing operator INSIDE that
unchanged vacuum algebra. Crossed-product reconstruction adds charged
coordinates and is not excluded by this statement. Likewise, the already
established 124>120 current obstruction concerns a same-conformal U plus
its complete marked h-module INSIDE V_D8, not B or all P1 realizations.
