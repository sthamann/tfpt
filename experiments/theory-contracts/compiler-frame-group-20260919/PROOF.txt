Question
--------
For one fixed outer mark q*, the correlated-event construction has 40 native
Bell10 reflections that fix q*.  Do these events generate the native carrier
Weyl group W(D5), rather than merely another group of order 1920?

Answer
------
No.  The adjoint/projective frame group has order 1920, kernel C2^4 and
quotient S5, and the extension splits.  Nevertheless its S5-module on C2^4
is not the even-sign module of W(D5).  The two groups are not isomorphic.

Exact enumeration
-----------------
The checker reconstructs the 60 Bell10 monomial reflection matrices from the
pinned native source and selects the 40 whose outer permutation fixes q*=0.
Matrices are encoded exactly by a permutation of ten Bell rays and ten mu4
phase exponents.  Breadth-first closure gives:

  phase-faithful group H40:       |H40| = 3840,
  center and scalar subgroup:     Z(H40) = {+I,-I},
  kernel over outer S5:           C2^5, order 32,
  outer image:                    S5, order 120.

Four explicit involutive lifts of adjacent S5 transpositions satisfy the
complete A4 Coxeter relations and generate a complement of order 120.  Thus
H40 is a split C2^5 semidirect S5 for the concrete five-dimensional binary
module found by the source.  The full S6 non-splitting proved in contract .09
is unaffected: only this fixed-mark S5 restriction splits.

Quotienting the scalar center gives the group seen by conjugation on arbitrary
Bell10 observables:

  P40 = H40/{+I,-I},              |P40| = 1920,
  O2(P40) = C2^4,                 |O2| = 16,
  P40/O2(P40) = S5,
  P40 = C2^4 semidirect S5.

Positive identification of this module
--------------------------------------
The same exact matrices identify which split C2^4:S5 this is.  In a computed
F2 basis of O2(P40), the centralizer of the even A5 subgroup in Mat4(F2) is
exactly

  {0, I, J, J^2},       J^2 + J + I = 0.

This is a copy of F4.  It turns O2(P40) into the additive group of F4^2, and
the even subgroup is F4-linear.  Its order is 60 and its determinant is
trivial (A5 has no nontrivial abelian quotient), hence it is SL2(4).  An odd
S5 Coxeter lift sends J to J^2, exactly the nontrivial Galois automorphism of
F4/F2.  Therefore

  P40 = F4^2 semidirect (SL2(4) semidirect Gal(F4/F2))
      = ASL2(4):2.

The certificate records one exact J in the generated kernel basis.  This
positive name does not change the obstruction below: this semilinear affine
group is not the even signed-permutation Weyl group W(D5).

Why this is not W(D5)
---------------------
Order and quotient are insufficient.  The decisive invariant is the
conjugation action of S5 on the characteristic normal 2-core O2.

For P40 the action is transitive on all fifteen nonzero kernel vectors:

  orbit signature on O2(P40)-{0}:  15.

For W(D5), O2 is the even-sign subspace of F2^5.  S5 preserves Hamming
weight, so its fifteen nonzero vectors split into weights two and four:

  orbit signature on O2(W(D5))-{0}:  10 + 5.

This comparison is isomorphism-invariant.  Any normal 2-subgroup of P40 maps
to a normal 2-subgroup of S5; since O2(S5)=1, it lies inside the displayed
C2^4.  Hence that kernel is the full characteristic O2 and cannot be replaced
by another kernel under a hypothetical group isomorphism.

The complete element-order census independently disagrees:

  P40:   1^1, 2^115, 3^320, 4^540, 5^384, 6^320, 8^240.
  W(D5): 1^1, 2^155, 3^80, 4^500, 5^384, 6^400, 8^240, 12^160.

In particular W(D5) has 160 elements of order 12; P40 has none.

Frame information
-----------------
The kernel of the H40 conjugation action on the full Bell10 operator algebra
is exactly {+I,-I}.  Therefore the exact co-moving frame for arbitrary Bell10
observables has 1920 distinguishable states.  It can be recorded as one of
16 kernel states together with one of 120 S5 states.

If only classical Bell labels are retained, all phase/kernel information is
lost and the image has 120 states, namely S5.  That smaller record is
insufficient for the coherence-sensitive stopped channel.

Under the standard Nielsen-Chuang deterministic exact programming premise,
correcting all 1920 inequivalent frame unitaries on arbitrary independent
Bell10 data requires 1920 mutually orthogonal program states.  This is a
conditional perfect-control bound, not a universal lower bound for
state-specific correlated relational encodings.  Each single background
reflection has Tr(U)=4, so its normalized Choi overlap with the identity
program is 4/10=2/5; an unflagged native Choi state is not by itself a perfect
deterministic frame program.
Primary theorem: Nielsen and Chuang, Programmable quantum gate arrays,
https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9703032 (exact deterministic setting).

Representation and physics boundary
-----------------------------------
The abstract P40 group is not W(D5), and its Bell10 representation was already
known not to equal the native carrier Lambda2(W5) representation: the native
Pauli witness has Bell character 2 but carrier-edge character 10.  The present
group audit strengthens that boundary from representation inequivalence to an
abstract group obstruction.

The result supplies an exact finite reference-frame algebra only.  It does not
derive a physical observer, the outer mark, a memory that records every event,
continuous interpolation, a local current, physical time, or any TOE gate.
