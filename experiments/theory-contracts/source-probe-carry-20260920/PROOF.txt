1. Original-source recurrence, sea and the finite-energy convention

Use TX=sigma_x/(2i)-sigma_z/2, TY=sigma_y/(2i)-sigma_z/2, mass1,
seam i^r, and k=2pi(n-r/4)/N. The strip symbol in the lower-edge convention
has diagonal -sin(k)sigma_x+rho sigma_z, rho=1-cos k, and upper/lower
transverse blocks TY* and TY. With u_-=(1,-1)/sqrt2 and u_+=(1,1)/sqrt2,
the normalized b_y=NW rho^y u_- satisfies exactly

  (h_W(k)-sin k)b=NW rho^W |W-1>u_+,
  NW^2=1/(sum_(j=0)^(W-1)rho^(2j)).

For rho<1, the residual is at most rho^W, and 1-NW^2<=rho^2.
In the sigma_x basis, h^2 has blocks sin^2(k)I+BB* and sin^2(k)I+B*B,
with B=rho I-S for a nilpotent shift S. Thus |h|>=|sin k|.
At even N, r=2 (a chosen AP background), no momentum has sin k=0.
The grand-canonical free Fock ground state is the unique filled-negative sea.
Its gap satisfies

  sin(pi/N)<=Delta_N<=sin(pi/N)+(1-cos(pi/N))^W.

The upper bound is the quasimode spectral-distance bound at k=pi/N.
At fixed particle number, a particle-hole gap involves BOTH excitation
energies; the displayed bound is not that gap. The r=0 zero-mode ambiguity
persists. Choosing r=2 does not derive it as the physical P1 background.

For sin k!=0 the wrong spectral subspace is separated from the approximate

  <b,P_wrong b><=NW^2 rho^(2W)/(4 sin^2 k).

The supplied weaker bound is valid; at W8 the error is O(k^30).

3. Completed fixed-width Fourier remainder for the sector energies

Let F_W(k)=-(1/2)Tr|h_W(k)| and E_N(f)=sum_n F_W(2pi(n-f)/N).
For each fixed finite W>=1, uniformly for 0<=f<=1,

  E_N(f)-E_N(1/2)=(2pi/N)(f-1/2)^2+O_W(N^-3).          (1)

For general f use its fractional part. The constant depends on W; no
joint growing-width estimate is asserted.

Proof. The positive energies are sqrt(sin^2 k+mu_j(rho)), with mu_j the
eigenvalues of B*B. At rho0 the sole small eigenvalue is simple; all others
are1. Since det B=rho^W, analytic spectral separation gives
mu_0(rho)=rho^(2W)/q_W(rho), where q_W is analytic and q_W(0)=1. The sum
of the massive energies is analytic near0 despite their degeneracies.
Writing t=2sin(k/2), the small energy is

  e_0(k)=|t| a_W(t^2),
  a_W(u)=sqrt(1-u/4+u^(2W-1)/(2^(2W)q_W(u/2))),
  a_W(0)=1.

Use the globally periodic cusp g(k)=-2|sin(k/2)|. Then
-e_0-g=-|t|(a_W(t^2)-1)=O(|k|^3). Consequently R_W=F_W-g is C^2 on
the circle, and its third derivative is piecewise analytic with finite
jump, hence of bounded variation. Away from0, h is invertible and its
absolute-value trace is analytic; in particular B(2)=2I-S is invertible
at pi. Subtracting -|sin k| globally would introduce a spurious pi cusp.
Three integrations by parts and the BV Fourier estimate imply
Rhat_W(m)=O_W(m^-4). Exactly,

  ghat(0)=-4/pi, ghat(m)=1/[pi(m^2-1/4)] for m!=0,
  Fhat_W(m)=1/(pi m^2)+O_W(m^-4).

Absolute Fourier summability permits exact sampling aliasing:
E_N(f)=N sum_l Fhat_W(lN) exp(-2pi i l f).
The l0 term cancels in the difference, and the remainder is uniformly
bounded by const_W N^-3 sum_(l!=0)|l|^-4. The leading sum is the Bernoulli
identity sum_(l!=0)exp(-2pi i l f)/l^2=2pi^2 B2(f).
Since B2(f)-B2(1/2)=(f-1/2)^2, (1) follows.

The supplied scaled values relative to PERIODIC f0, independently replayed,

4. Carry, cocycle and source charge

The bijection (m,k)->m+ks identifies l2(D8) tensor C2 with l2(E8).
T_s(m,0)=(m,1), T_s(m,1)=(m+2s,0) has
[Q_a,T_s]=s_a T_s, T_s^2=T_(2s). With G(m,k)=(-1)^sum_(a<=5)m_a i^k,
G T_s G^-1=i T_s: the second leg shifts that integer sum by5.
The no-carry flip instead has phase -i on its return leg.

This is the phase-free charge translation. In the inherited lattice
cocycle convention the lift obeys U_s^2=epsilon(s,s)U_(2s), with
epsilon(s,s)=-1. Rephasing can alter this sign, not remove the carry.

The microscopic S_A=D_A^2=X^2 tensor (-1)^N_A is neutral under total
occupation charges N_a: [N_a,S_A]=0. An equivariant field limit with the
same charge action remains neutral. A product-stable unscaled limit of
S_A^2=I retains that relation. Neither implies a charged T_s or vertex.
Scalar renormalization cannot change neutrality. These are conditional
obstructions to that direct identification, not to every renormalized field.

Sector-dependent Wick charges, an opposite-edge compensator, or a genuinely
nonmultiplicative field scaling can change the comparison; each requires
its explicit charge map, products and locality theorem. A finite cyclic
choice of charge origins telescopes to zero over a complete cycle and
cannot produce persistent integer carry. Oriented flux spectral flow
counts crossings but does not by itself define an insertion or prepare the
new ground state at a gapless crossing.

5. Stronger exact result for probe-dependent reduction

Let theta be the actual E8/D8 coset grading, P=(1+theta)/2 the FULL D8
module, Q=1-P the spinor module. This theta is not microscopic fermion
parity. Let H strongly reduce P, including H=L0-delta.Q_charge.
For self-adjoint spinor-odd probes A_a, theta A_a theta=-A_a, so
P A_a P=Q A_a Q=0. Put H_P=PHP, D=QHQ, B_a=Q A_a P.

For bounded probes (or at each fixed finite energy cutoff), real controls
j_a give exactly

  H[j]=[ H_P, sum_a j_a B_a* ; sum_a j_a B_a, D ],
  mu_ab(dE)=B_a* dE_D(E) B_b,
  Sigma(z;j)=sum_ab j_a j_b integral mu_ab(dE)/(z-E),
  P(z-H[j])^-1P=[z-H_P-Sigma(z;j)]^-1.                (2)

Use nonreal z for self-adjoint H[j], or specified common resolvent sets.
The self-energy is EXACTLY quadratic in the sources, because the probes
have no Q-to-Q corner. The inverse in (2) contains arbitrarily many
returns; it is not a truncation to second-order projected response.
This is an application of the standard Schur/Feshbach identity, not a new
general projection method; see arXiv:2105.02058 and arXiv:0704.3244.

For time-dependent real controls define B(t)=sum_a j_a(t)B_a. Eliminating q
from i p'=H_P p+B(t)*q, i q'=Dq+B(t)p gives, for q(0)=0,

  i p'(t)=H_P p(t)-i integral_0^t K_j(t,u)p(u)du,
  K_j(t,u)=sum_ab j_a(t)j_b(u) integral exp(-iE(t-u))mu_ab(dE). (3)

This is the exact projected propagator for all such probe histories.
If q(0)!=0 add B(t)*exp(-itD)q(0). If probes have Q A Q!=0, D becomes
probe-dependent and the unperturbed two-insertion mu no longer suffices.
For unbounded continuum currents, these identities require a common
self-adjoint realization, domains or form-domain control and well-posed
time evolution. A finite-energy cutoff proves the bounded version, not
those analytic prerequisites. The finite-energy lattice matrix element
below is exact on the VOA core without claiming all global control limits.

Crucially mu is OPERATOR-VALUED on the whole retained P space. Its single
vacuum expectation does not specify its off-diagonal matrix elements or
subsequent transitions. No autonomous event law, detector record or quantum
instrument follows from (2)-(3) without further specified operations.
The measure is positive as a matrix of operators:
sum_ab conjugate(c_a)mu_ab c_b>=0. An individual off-diagonal carry entry
is complex, not a separately positive measure. Q-readouts and initial-Q
processes are not recovered just by the compressed propagator assertion.

6. The carry is a nonzero off-diagonal response in that same measure

Let V_s(z)=sum_n V_(s,n) z^(-n-1), s^2=2. On the VOA finite-energy core,

  V_(s,-1)Omega=|s>, V_(s,-1)^2 Omega=0,
  V_(s,-3)V_(s,-1)Omega=epsilon(s,s)|2s>.

Indeed V_s(z)|s>=epsilon(s,s) z^2 exp(sum_(n>0)s_-n z^n/n)|2s>.
These mode indices follow from charge and energy, including the cocycle.
Take Hermitian probes A=V_(s,-1)+(V_(s,-1))*,
B=V_(s,-3)+(V_(s,-3))*; the adjoint pieces annihilate the relevant first
states by their negative output energies. Both are theta-odd. For H=L0,

  <2s|mu_BA(dE)|Omega>=epsilon(s,s) delta_1(dE),
  <2s|Sigma_BA(z)|Omega>=epsilon(s,s)/(z-1),
  <2s|K_BA(t)|Omega>=epsilon(s,s) exp(-it).            (4)

Here Sigma_BA and K_BA mean ORDERED cross components. For a real Hermitian
perturbation j_A A+j_B B, the full mixed term also contains the reverse
component mu_AB, whose <2s|...|Omega> atom is epsilon(s,s) delta_3.
Thus its full mixed self-energy entry is
j_A j_B epsilon(s,s)[1/(z-1)+1/(z-3)] for H=L0.
For H_delta the two intermediate energies are1-delta.s and3-delta.s.

The vacuum scalar <Omega|mu_BA|Omega> cannot detect this charge2s output.
P must retain |2s> (L0 grade4, Z4 glue grade2); replacing P by the vacuum projection destroys
the theta-odd two-block property because Q A Q can then be nonzero.

Conformal grade is not automatically source frequency. For
H_delta=L0-delta.Q_charge the intermediate energy is E_s=1-delta.s,
and the final energy is E_2s=4-2delta.s. Replace1 in (4) by E_s.
For the quarter-holonomy delta=s/2, E_s=0 and E_2s=2. The source-holonomy
shift is not silently subtracted. At a pole use nonreal z; no gapped
elimination is asserted. The separately studied growing-radius local
limit can suppress that offset, but adds its own stated scaling assumption.
Quadratic self-energy does not imply quadratic eigenvalue response:
at E_s=0 the pole can give a splitting proportional to |j|. Furthermore,
the actual Coxeter does not preserve this fixed D8 projector, as proved
