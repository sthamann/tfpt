1. Reduced source-bar4 pair sector
---------------------------------
On matter-left 4 tensor source bar4 tensor matter-right 4, let

  K=(1/60) sum_r r tensor bar(r) tensor r,   L=I-K,

using the pinned sixty reflections.  Exact summation gives

  spec(L)={1/2 x 8, 5/6 x 36, 11/10 x 20}.

The eight-dimensional bottom space is the direct sum of the two Bell families

  Bell(M_left,S) tensor M_right
  plus M_left tensor Bell(S,M_right).

Their cross-Gram matrix is delta_cd/4.  The exact projector and event mean are
recorded in pair_sector_check.json and verified entry by entry.

For an open chain of m edges, the states Psi_(j,c), j=0,...,m and c=1,...,4,
pair every source left or right of the single unpaired matter position.  They
satisfy for every m

  H_m Psi_(j,c)=(kappa m/2) Psi_(j,c),
  <Psi_(j,c),Psi_(k,d)>=delta_cd 4^(-|j-k|).

Each colour Gram determinant is (15/16)^m, so at least 4(m+1) displayed ground
states are independent in this reduced sector.  This is a lower bound on the
ground multiplicity, not an equality claim.

The adjacent local-bottom projectors have squared principal cosines

  1 x 12, 16/225 x 56, 1/225 x 4, 0 x 56.

The maximal nontrivial principal cosine is c=4/15.  The two-projection bound
and the local spectral gap 1/3 therefore give, for every m,

  gap(H_m above kappa m/2) >= (kappa/3)(1-2c)=7 kappa/45.

The all-m theorem is analytic.  The checker additionally replays the sparse
Gram entries through m=6 and Toeplitz determinants through m=10; it does not
construct a full-chain Hamiltonian.

2. Full 240-label variational control
-------------------------------------
The reduced-sector ground statement is kept in its confirmed scope.  In the
full phase-free source space define on a selected edge

  Omega_e=240^(-1/2) sum_alpha
            |alpha>_S |psi_alpha>_Mleft |psi_alpha>_Mright.

Each native event fixes Omega_e and its one-matter marginal is I/4.  Put such
packets on alternating nonoverlapping edges and the uniform source state on
the intervening edges.  Selected edges have zero energy and each intervening
edge contributes 3 kappa/4.  Hence

  <H_m>=(3 kappa/4) floor(m/2) < kappa m/2.

Thus kappa m/2 is the exact reduced bar4-sector ground energy, not the global
ground energy of the full 240-label phase-free Hamiltonian.

3. Mixed 640-dimensional sector
-------------------------------
Let R1=barSym2(C4), dimension 10, and on R1 tensor A tensor B define

  M=(1/60) sum_l barSym2(r_l) tensor r_l tensor r_l,
  L1=I-M.

The exact 160-dimensional local spectra are

  spec(M) ={1 x1, 1/3 x9, 1/5 x60, 1/15 x45, -1/15 x45},
  spec(L1)={0 x1, 2/3 x9, 4/5 x60, 14/15 x45, 16/15 x45}.

The symmetric matter block bar10 tensor Sym2(4), dimension 100, has precisely
the old C60 reflection-channel spectrum.  The 60-dimensional exterior block
was computed separately, rather than inferred by dimension.

Let Omega lie in barSym2(C4)_R tensor Sym2(C4), with Sym2(C4) the symmetric
AB subspace inside C4_A tensor C4_B.  With P the packet range and S=Swap_BC,

  P S P=P/4,

so W=Ran(P)+S Ran(P) has dimension eight.  For H=L1+(4I-S)/5, its coefficient
matrix on (P,SP) is

       [ 4/5  -2/5 ]
  h =  [            ],
       [-1/5   8/5 ]

with eigenvalues

  Eminus=(6-sqrt(6))/5,  Eplus=(6+sqrt(6))/5,

each with multiplicity four.

On W_perp, L1>=2/3 and (4I-S)/5>=3/5, hence H>=19/15.  Also

  Eplus >= 19/15   because 3 sqrt(6)>=1.

Therefore the whole stated 640 sector has ground Eminus with multiplicity four
and

  gap >= 19/15-Eminus=(1+3 sqrt(6))/15.

The value 19/15 is a complement lower bound, not a complete complement
spectrum.

4. Haar control
---------------
The native sixty-ray and complex-projective Haar reflection ensembles share
the exact second moment

  average(r tensor r)=(I+Swap)/5.

That moment and Omega invariance already reproduce the same h and Eplus/minus.
The Haar local moment instead has spectrum

  {1 x1, 1/5 x30, 1/7 x84, -1/15 x45}.

Its complement obeys H>=7/5 and Eplus>=7/5 because sqrt(6)>=1.  Thus the Haar
ground remains Eminus with gap at least (1+sqrt(6))/5.  The source-specific
native content is the differing full local spectrum, including the 1/3 and
1/15 sectors, and the native 19/15 complement bound.  The 19/15 and 7/5 values
are bounds, not complete spectra.

5. Bell10 connection, phase, and physical boundary
--------------------------------------------------
The quadratic-coordinate map from the 240 oriented labels exactly intertwines
the conjugate Sym2(4) action.  Its sixty normalized analysis rays are the tight
frame bar(psi_l^2), and the pinned .21 certificate uses the same ray and basis:

  F10^dagger(q_l tensor psi_l^2)=bar(psi_l^2)/sqrt(2).

This is a source-action and ray identification, not a 10=10 dimension match.
F10 provides a conditional finite state/readout identification with the
grade-two current-product response space.  Its action phase must remain
explicit.  The pinned .18 proof gives

  (R_l tensor U_l) F10 = F10 bar(U_l),

whereas the actual affine source operator is

  S_l=i(R_l tensor U_l),
  S_l F10=i F10 bar(U_l).

Only the compensated finite action C_l=-i S_l matches the mixed-source
bar(U_l) action.  The unmodified affine-current action therefore is not the
mixed-source action.  Neither F10 nor this compensation transports the
pure-permutation Hamiltonian, its state preparation, or its dynamics to
affine-current dynamics.  Pure root-label permutations remain distinct from
the phase-faithful .21 current operators.

----------------------------

(m-1)/8.  Open-chain term counting alone does not prove those identities.
