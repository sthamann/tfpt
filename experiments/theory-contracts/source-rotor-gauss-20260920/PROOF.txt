1. Physical space on any finite connected graph
----------------------------------------------
Let the graph have v vertices and e oriented edges. B is its v by e incidence
matrix, with +1 at the source and -1 at the target. Each site has the original
two complex modes L,H, N_x=n_Lx+n_Hx and rho_x=N_x-1. The Gauss identities
below apply to arbitrary finite connected graphs. Instantiating the original
Round37 hopping operator also retains its allowed ambient degrees at most6;
this is not a claim that P1/P2 select any of these spatial graphs.
Integer electric fluxes
E belong to Z^e; [E_l,U_l]=U_l. No external electric edges are included.
The Gauss constraint is B E=-rho. Summing it forces N_total=v.

Choose a spanning tree. For every allowed occupation mask f with v occupied
modes, there is a unique integer tree flow E_T(rho_f), with chord entries
zero. On a tree edge u->v its value is the sum of rho in the component on
the target side after cutting the edge. The sign follows from summing B E
over that component. Integer neutrality proves existence and uniqueness.

Let b=e-v+1 and let C be the e by b fundamental-cycle matrix, oriented so
that its chord submatrix is I_b. Then every solution, with no flux cutoff, is

       E = E_T(rho_f) + C w,            w in Z^b.                 (1)

Indeed the difference of two solutions is an integer circulation; its chord
values determine w, and a tree has no nonzero circulation. Thus the map

       J: |f> |w> -> |f> |E_T(rho_f)+Cw>

is a unitary from the fixed-v-particle matter space tensor l2(Z^b) onto the
full Gauss-physical space. Its inverse records the matter mask and chord
fluxes. A tree has b=0, hence dimension binomial(2v,v); a graph with loops
retains b independent integer winding coordinates. This finite graph result
does not assert that a spatial patch equals an infinite-volume local limit.

2. The exact electric interaction and its retained complement
------------------------------------------------------------
Write L=B B^T and Lplus for its Moore-Penrose inverse on neutral vectors.
For b>0 define Q=C^T C (positive definite), theta(rho)=Q^-1 C^T E_T(rho).
Orthogonal projection onto real cycle flows gives

 E_parallel=-B^T Lplus rho,
 E_T=E_parallel+C theta,
 |E|^2=rho^T Lplus rho+(w+theta)^T Q(w+theta).                    (2)

For trees the second term is absent. These are identities in all integer
fluxes and all allowed occupations, not a continuum approximation.
Changing the tree solution by Ck, k integral, changes theta to theta+k and
the integer coordinate to w-k. Thus the affine flux lattice and energy do
not depend on this bookkeeping choice. A different fundamental-cycle basis
changes integer coordinates by a unimodular matrix and preserves (1)-(2).

In an open chain, with sites 0,...,v-1 and edges j->j+1,

 E_j=-sum_(x<=j)rho_x,
 H_E=(kappa/2) sum_(j=0)^(v-2) (sum_(x<=j)rho_x)^2.               (3)

This gives an exact spatially extended density interaction. A configuration
with charges +1 and -1 separated by d edges has electric energy kappa*d/2.
This is the energy of that flux/occupation configuration, not the potential
after dynamical screening and not a theorem of confinement for the full H.
The original local formulation remains local; (3) is not instantaneous
signalling. Fourier inversion of the lattice divergence at nonzero momentum
gives H_E,long=(1/2) sum_(q!=0) K_el(q)|rho_q|^2 with
K_el(q)=kappa/(4 sin^2(q/2)). This is the formal real longitudinal kernel;
a periodic integer rotor still retains the affine winding term of (2).
It diagnoses an electric density kernel, not a dispersion relation.
The two-species charge is rho=n_L+n_H-1, so the electric
term alone acts on total density, with no direct relative L-H density term.
Species-changing kinetic terms remain present in the full H.

For an oriented square and rho=(-1,+1,0,0), E_T=(1,0,0,0), C=(1,1,1,1)^T:

       |E|^2 = 3/4 + 4(w+1/4)^2.                                (4)

The integer minimum is 1; the longitudinal real minimum is only 3/4.
Multiplication by kappa/2 gives the already known omitted energy kappa/8.
This is not a full-H ground energy. An L-edge cycle with a unit dipole across
one link instead has |E|^2=(1-1/L)+L(w+1/L)^2. The quarter shift is therefore
not by itself the native mu4 action. It is a coordinate in electric winding
space, not an identified spatial spinor boundary condition.

At fixed rho and positive Euclidean time tau, tracing just the electric
cycle term yields the real positive factor

 exp[-tau*kappa*rho^T Lplus rho/2]
   * sum_(w in Z^b) exp[-tau*kappa*(w+theta)^T Q(w+theta)/2].       (5)

It depends on rho and cannot generally be dropped as constant normalization.
It does not alone generate a complex relative CP/mass phase. Equation (5)
is not the trace of the full hopping Hamiltonian; noncommuting hopping,
fermion signs and real-time phases require their actual common evolution.

3. All original hopping, state and time are carried along
-------------------------------------------------------
A term T=c_t^dagger c_s times a directed product of U links changes matter
charge by delta rho and electric flux by an integer path vector p, with
B p=-delta rho. The original CAR sign and coefficient remain unchanged.
In the coordinates (1), its winding shift is the unique integer ell obeying

       C ell=p+E_T(rho)-E_T(rho+delta rho).                       (6)

It is integral because the right side is an integer circulation; in the
fundamental-cycle convention ell is its chord subvector. Since E_T is
linear, this shift depends on the path, not on the initial occupation.
Tree hops have ell=0; chord and loop-crossing paths can shift w. Formula
(6) applies to the original mixed L/H links, low/low links, their adjoints
and two-step paths. Onsite backtracks must retain the original chosen
ambient degree. Replacing it by the reduced graph degree is a different H.

Define H_red=J^dagger H J with electric term (2) and these exact hopping
rules. The electric operator is self-adjoint on its diagonal square-sum
domain. On a finite graph the remaining hoppings and onsite number terms
are bounded, so the full self-adjoint operator and its domain are carried
by J. Therefore, for any initial density operator and bounded observable,

 J^dagger exp(-it H) J=exp(-it H_red),
 Tr(rho exp(itH) O exp(-itH))
   =Tr(rho_red exp(itH_red) O_red exp(-itH_red)).                  (7)

This is an exact state/time dictionary for this rotor parent, without
adiabatic elimination, a selected vacuum or an extra interaction. It is
not a dictionary to the QWZ CAR source, the native C/J action or the E8 edge.

4. Local observable versus charged field: the decisive type check
---------------------------------------------------------------
Let O have finite support in the original tensor/CAR algebra and commute
with every local gauge transformation exp(i alpha G_x). Choose a finite
region R containing all its matter sites and the endpoints of every link
on which it acts. Sum Gauss generators over R. Internal edge fluxes cancel;
the remaining boundary flux acts on links outside the support of O.
Consequently O commutes with exp(i alpha sum_(x in R) N_x) for every alpha.
At alpha=pi this is matter fermion parity on its support. Hence every such
gauge-invariant finite-support observable is even.

This proof applies to bounded gauge-invariant operators using unitaries;
polynomial commutators are identities on the standard finite-support core.
On a finite closed graph the physical sector already has fixed N=v and
parity (-1)^v, giving the same compressed statement. The local argument is
stronger: it does not rely only on a chosen total number sector.

Bare c_x obeys [G_x,c_x]=-c_x and does not map the Gauss-physical sector to
itself. A finite internal Wilson line has two gauge-charge endpoints. It
can dress a transfer c_y^dagger U_path c_x, but cannot make a single odd
fermion into an invariant finite-support observable. A boundary/infinite
string or an explicitly enlarged charged-sector field algebra is different
and must retain its support, boundary charges and dynamics.

Thus an injective, parity-preserving, bounded-support map of an ungauged
local CAR FIELD algebra into this gauge-invariant OBSERVABLE algebra is
impossible: its odd generator would have to be both odd and even. This
does not exclude charged fermions in gauge theory, nonlocal charged
dressings, charged-sector intertwiners, an even-observable map or emergent
fermionic sectors. In particular, gauging charge is not identical to
opening the massive odd-sector gap in source-graded-locality-20260920.
Those are different mechanisms and neither proves the other.

The positive map should not be understated. For the unique tree path x->y,
let W_xy be the oriented Wilson product. Then exactly

       J^dagger (c_y^dagger W_xy c_x) J = c_y^dagger c_x.         (8)

Onsite densities and L/H coherences are zero-path cases. Products give the
number-preserving observable algebra on the fixed-particle sector. This is
not a map of the entire even CAR algebra: e.g. an even pair creator still
has nonzero U(1) charge. Support in the original model includes the path;
the reduced endpoint notation does not make a long path into a local hop.

6. A constructive converse: the compatible gauge charge is fixed
----------------------------------------------------------------
The failed direct identification does not require inventing arbitrary new
charge weights. Within the already declared ten-channel boundary, one can
classify every integral charge covector g that leaves the full transported
E8 current sector neutral. This new gauge charge is distinct from the global
source charge q=ones; the latter is not redefined.

The existing lift can be written

       F(r)=(r-(a.r)a/2, 0, -a.r),       a.a=8.

Write g=(h,u,w), h in Z^8. Since the E8 roots span R^8, g.F(r)=0 for
every root is equivalent to

       h = (a/2)(a.h+2w).

Thus h=v a, w=-3v. Because the entries of a are +/-1, integrality forces
v to be an integer, while u remains an arbitrary integer. Exactly all
compatible covectors are therefore

       g=(v a,u,-3v),             u,v in Z.                       (11)

Now additionally require that the existing desired reconstruction operator
n remain gauge neutral. Its charge is g.n=-u-v, so u=-v. For a nonzero
primitive charge vector (gcd of entries1), the answer is unique up to sign:

       g=+/-K n
        =+/-(1,1,1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-3).                        (12)

This is a necessary compatible charge assignment selected by the specified
E8 embedding, n-neutrality and primitive normalization. It is not a
microscopic source derivation or an implementation in the old equal-charge
L/H rotor. In particular it requires relative charges of both signs and a
charge-three mode in the original local-field convention.

There are three exact further checks (choose the + sign):

       g^T K^-1 g = n^T K n = 0,
       q^T K^-1 g = q.n = 0,
       Y^T K^-1 g = Y.n = 0.                                    (13)

Here Y is the existing boundary hypercharge covector. In the conditional
1+1-dimensional Abelian edge convention these are the pure gauge and mixed
q/g, Y/g anomaly coefficients. Their vanishing is necessary, not a complete
gauged regulator, a3+1D anomaly calculation or a proof of dynamics. The
signature of K is still9-1=8, so the chiral gravitational anomaly has not
vanished; a full regulated realization must account for the bulk or other
appropriate compensating degrees of freedom.

For the two already identified competing null vertices,

       g.n=0,                  g.z=2,   z=e9-e10.                (14)

Thus a bare cos(n) is symmetry-allowed under this candidate U(1), whereas
the bare competing cos(z) is not gauge invariant. A separately charged
dressing could change the latter statement; none has been inserted here.
Being allowed does not generate cos(n), determine its coefficient, make it
relevant or pin its field. No gauge field is silently added to the source.

In the previously selected W_aux basis, all E8 charges vanish and the two
complementary basis vectors e_R=-e9, m=(a,0,3) have charges +1,-1. This
shows exactly where such a gauge action would live. It does not imply that

All entries of g are odd. Its pi rotation is therefore matter fermion
parity on the original ten local fermion fields. This is compatible with
an even local E8 sector, but does not supply the light odd charged fields
or the native Clock action. It is a selection criterion for the missing

7. Consequence for the source-selected interaction question
----------------------------------------------------------
The rotor has genuine interacting physical dynamics; this was already known.
The exact reduced kernel and residual winding now specify what must be
transported if it is used. They do not freely supply the previously chosen
local generalized metric V_theta, ten chiral channels or the n transfer.
In particular, a Coulomb density kernel is not already the local constant
velocity matrix of that boundary. Screening or a controlled boundary limit
could change this comparison, but has to be derived from the same parent.

Likewise, all local gauge-invariant fields being even is not evidence that
they form E8. Many even algebras are not E8, and the native Clock action,
current products, charged extension and common physical time still require
an explicit intertwiner. The old local Clock lift already fails this test.

What remains useful is a precise positive reduced description with all
physical sectors, and a sharper bridge requirement: construct the charged
field extension and its actual native symmetry/time action, then derive the
required kernel and coherent transfer. Merely detecting a quartic monomial
or retaining a scalar determinant factor does not establish that bridge.
No new parameter, parent, vacuum assumption or T1--T8 closure is introduced.
