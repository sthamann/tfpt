# Rotor/Gauss origin inventory — 2026-09-20

## Result

The existing compact-U(1) rotor parent does contain a real noncentral
electric-matter coupling and a well-defined Gauss-physical open-edge/tree
sector. It therefore supplies more than a formal Gaussian determinant. It
does **not** yet supply a coherent E8 transfer, an effective static
interaction on the E8 channel lattice, or a selected low-energy vacuum/gap
from TFPT/P1/P2. The previously checked site-fixed rotor-independent C6
grade lift is not repeated here; its scope and exclusion remain exactly as
documented in `clock-rotor-joint-charge/README.md:148-169`.

The source-linked facts are:

1. The parent is the unchanged two-species compact-U(1) model

   ```text
   H = kappa/2 sum_l E_l^2
       + dGamma([[A+beta A^2, eta A], [eta A, M I]])
   A_xy = a U_xy,
   a=1/12, beta=1/4, eta=1/2, kappa=1/100, M=4.
   ```

   The ambient cubic low backtrack is `d=beta*a^2*6=1/96`. These are declared
   model parameters, not source-selected TFPT constants.

2. The physical preparation used throughout is one fermion per site, usually
   the bare low mode, with `E_l=0` on every link. Arbitrary local L/H
   coherence, mixtures, and entanglement remain in the same Gauss sector.
   This is a specified reference/preparation family, not a selected vacuum or
   the complete low-energy spectral space.

3. On an open tree Gauss fixes all edge fluxes from occupations. On a two-site
   edge the exact relation is especially simple and gives the requested
   density formula below. A tree has no independent winding rotor; a cycle
   retains one integer winding parameter and is a separate sector.

4. A genuine induced quartic commutator was already proved in the earlier
   provenance contract. It is a noncentral rotor commutator, not an inserted
   free four-fermion term. Its controlled elimination into an effective static
   interaction and its map to E8 remain open.

5. Existing coherent transfer is only local L/H matter transfer with rotor
   transporters. The exact high-mode source equation and finite-time edge/tree
   checks do not construct a coherent E8 transfer or a common E8 field map.

## 1. Parent, filling, and what “low energy” means here

The unrotated parent is recorded in
`local-window-round37/LOCAL_WINDOW.md:12-35` and repeated in
`local-source-round38/LOCAL_SOURCE.md:48-89`:

```text
H = kappa/2 sum_l E_l^2
    + dGamma([[A+beta A^2, eta A], [eta A, M I]])
```

with two fermion modes `L,H` per vertex and one unbounded integer rotor per
oriented link. The reference charge is one per site. The bare reference has
one low fermion per vertex and zero electric flux. The one-site low backtrack
in the cubic ambient parent is `d=1/96`, while the high onsite mass is
`M=4`. The high mode is therefore an excited species in the declared source
equation; the documents do not derive a filled interacting ground state.

The exact high-mode equation is

```text
d/dt tau_t(c_H,x)
  = -i M tau_t(c_H,x)
    -i eta a sum_(y~x) tau_t(U_xy c_L,y),
```

with integrated source `B_xy=U_xy c_L,y`. The `c_H` and `B` operators are
gauge-covariant odd intermediate operators; their squared norm gives the
physical even observable `n_H,x`. They are not a newly selected vacuum or a
gauge-invariant E8 field.

The matter-resummation contract states the same scope explicitly:
initial `E=0` states have exactly one L/H fermion per site, with arbitrary
species coherence on a finite patch and bare-low filling elsewhere; these are
assumed preparations, not selected vacua. See
`matter-resummation-round43/MATTER_RESUMMATION.md:9-40`.

## 2. Exact Gauss reduction on the open two-site edge

The source convention is

```text
G_x = N_L,x + N_H,x - 1 + div_x E,
div E = sum_outgoing E - sum_incoming E.
```

This is recorded in `clock-rotor-joint-charge/README.md:73-100`; the code
implements the same equation in
`local-window-round37/checker.py:222-229`.

Orient the one edge from site 0 to site 1 and call its integer flux `E`. With
`N_i=N_L,i+N_H,i`, the two constraints are

```text
G0 = N0 - 1 + E = 0,
G1 = N1 - 1 - E = 0.
```

Adding and solving gives the exact identities

```text
N0 + N1 = 2,
E = 1 - N0 = N1 - 1,
H_E = kappa/2 E^2 = kappa/2 (N0 - 1)^2.
```

This is a derivation from the declared Gauss law, not a selected flux ansatz.
Each site has two modes, so the two-site fixed-charge sector consists of the
`choose(4,2)=6` Fock masks. The complete benchmark confirms six physical
states and checks `G0=G1=0` on every one, with no flux cutoff; see
`local-source-round38/test_checker.py:173-180` and
`local-source-round38/LOCAL_SOURCE.md:258-271`.

The bare-low reference is the mask with one low fermion on each site:
`N0=N1=1`, `E=0`. The other physical masks include a double occupancy and a
hole, with the compensating flux fixed by the equations above. Thus the
electric density energy is exactly zero for the reference and `kappa/2` for
the `N0=0` or `N0=2` edge configurations.

The same reduction generalizes to a rooted star with edges `0 -> j`. For the
physical one-fermion-per-site sector, the exact Gauss equation is
`N_i+div_i E=b_i` with `b_i=1`; on each leaf,

```text
E_(0->j) = N_j - 1.
```

Enumerating all masks with total particle number equal to the total reference
charge gives the complete tree sector, not an assumed electric truncation.
The six-leaf star has seven sites, seven fermions, 3,432 physical states and
64,416 stored Hamiltonian entries. This is proved in
`matter-resummation-round43/MATTER_RESUMMATION.md:132-183` and independently
reported in `local-source-round38/LOCAL_SOURCE.md:258-271`.

On a cycle, by contrast, Gauss leaves one integer winding `m`; the cycle
formula and its tail bound are in
`matter-resummation-round43/MATTER_RESUMMATION.md:146-179`. That winding is
not present on the open edge/tree and must not be silently carried into the
tree reduction.

## 3. Exact reference gap/phase scale and its limit

The local-source contract defines

```text
Delta = M - d + kappa/2
      = 4 - 1/96 + 1/200
      = 9587/2400.
```

It is the exact phase denominator in the high-mode source equation and the
energy difference for the checked reference transfer. In the two-site test,
start from the bare-low state and move one low fermion to a high mode while
the edge flux changes to `E=-1`. The diagonal original-parent energy change is

```text
Delta = [M*(high occupation) + d*(low occupation)
         + kappa E^2/2]_excited
        - [same expression]_bare,
```

which is verified symbolically in
`local-source-round38/test_checker.py:81-98`. This is the source's exact
onsite/electric transfer scale, and the electric half-step is not omitted.

It should not be called a proven many-body spectral gap of the full
interacting edge or cubic parent. The test compares the diagonal original
onsite parent energies; the hopping terms remain in the full benchmark
Hamiltonian. The contract itself calls the result a phase gap and keeps the
selected-vacuum and low-energy-spectral-reduction questions open; see
`local-source-round38/README.md:33-42,87-98`.

The finite edge and star are complete physical Gauss-sector evolutions at
model time `t=1`. The bare-low high-occupation outputs are approximately
`0.00036097226121...` on the edge and
`0.00219096851702...` on the six-leaf star; equal coherent +/-i edge inputs
produce distinct values near `0.5`. These are exact finite-parent benchmarks,
not evidence for a selected low-energy E8 phase. See
`local-source-round38/LOCAL_SOURCE.md:260-296`.

## 4. Existing induced interaction: real, but not yet an effective E8 term

The earlier `clock-interaction-provenance` audit already found the only
relevant source-native non-Gaussian edge in this inventory. On an existing
link,

```text
[E,U] = U,
H_E = (kappa/2) E^2,
Vhop = a (U c_1^* c_0 + U^* c_0^* c_1),
a=1/12, kappa=1/100.
```

Because the rotor coefficient is noncentral,

```text
[Vhop,[H_E,Vhop]]
 = kappa a^2 [n0+n1-2 n0 n1+2 E(n0-n1)],
```

and the pure quartic component is

```text
-2 kappa a^2 q0 q1 = -(1/7200) q0 q1.
```

The checker tests all 16 four-mode Fock masks, arbitrary integer flux, and
the full original two-site hopping list; the coefficient survives. This is a
genuine induced commutator component, not a free quartic term inserted by
hand. Provenance and exact scope are in
`clock-interaction-provenance/README.md:132-180`.

The same source document states the decisive limitation: the commutator is
not yet a static effective Hamiltonian. Its time ordering, Gauss reduction,
controlled elimination, and coefficient in an actual low-energy theory remain
to be derived. The parent has four L/H complex modes on two sites plus an
infinite rotor; no map to the sixteen Clock Majoranas, the E8 boundary, or an
E8 charge/grading dictionary has been proved. See
`clock-interaction-provenance/README.md:175-187`.

## 5. Existing coherent transfer and its boundary

There is an established coherent local transfer mechanism. The exact source
equation transports a low fermion through `U_xy` into the high mode while
retaining the electric phase

```text
tau_s^0(U_l^sigma)
 = U_l^sigma exp(i kappa s (sigma E_l + 1/2)).
```

For initial `E=0`, shifted-link flux states from different neighbors are
orthogonal. The local source contract obtains the exact free-source norm

```text
q(t)=p_H+(eta a)^2 sum_(y~x) p_L,y
     * 2(1-cos(Delta t))/Delta^2,
```

and encloses the full `n_H,x(t)` by explicit source-defect bounds. These are
coherent L/H matter-transfer statements, not an E8 transfer. See
`local-source-round38/LOCAL_SOURCE.md:131-177,192-249`.

The later matter-resummation contract keeps the full free electric and onsite
phases in the all-order matter-row representation, but explicitly leaves
electric branches in a separate bound and does not evaluate the full cubic
resummed readout. It also proves that the auxiliary row need not be a
canonical full-Fock annihilator: the rotor coefficients do not commute, and
the anticommutator acquires a quartic correction

```text
[t^4] <{P_infty(t),P_infty(t)^*}>_(LL,E0)
  = - kappa g^2 (M-d)/6.
```

This is an obstruction to declaring the auxiliary row a full canonical field,
not an E8 coherent-transfer theorem. See
`matter-resummation-round43/MATTER_RESUMMATION.md:9-40,250-306`.

The electric-propagation contract retains the M, E, MEM and MME branches of
the same parent through fourth order and supplies fifth-order remainders. It
is a controlled finite-time expansion on the named sectors, not a completed
effective transfer or a full 3D gauge solver. See
`electric-propagation-round42/ELECTRIC_PROPAGATION.md:8-60,145-205`.

## 6. Prior charge backreaction and the scope of the cycle reduction

The `common-engine-threeway` contract already contains the relevant
charge-dependent rotor backreaction. On its four-edge open-square cell, the
same native low transport keeps amplitude `1/12`, while a disjoint spectator
dipole changes its diagonal transport cost from `+1/200` to `-1/200`.
Both input states satisfy Gauss. The contract also states explicitly that
the winding variable is integer-valued and must not be replaced by a freely
minimized real number. This is an established prior result, not a new
interaction or a new density-coupling derivation; see
`common-engine-threeway/README.md:11-17,42-72,117-130`.

That source does write an exact Hodge decomposition for the declared
four-edge neutral plaquette. With one cycle vector `z`, its checker verifies

```text
e = e_long + z*lambda,
e.e = q^T Lplus q + (z.z)*lambda^2,
```

while retaining the integer winding chart and checking all integer physical
states. The implementation and returned provenance are in
`common-engine-threeway/cell.py:124-185`.

It does **not** already provide the proposed typed general connected-graph
map in terms of arbitrary tree flux `E_T(rho)`, cycle-complement matrix `C`,
and

```text
theta(rho) = (C^T C)^(-1) C^T E_T(rho),
E = E_T(rho) + C w,
```

for an arbitrary graph and cycle rank. The existing record has one fixed
plaquette cycle vector and a special neutral charge chart. The most useful
extension, if pursued, is therefore a typed general cycle/complement map
that preserves both the Gauss source charges and integer winding; this
inventory does not construct that extension.

## Verdict

The existing rotor parent does establish:

- a genuine noncentral electric coefficient algebra;
- exact Gauss reduction on open trees and complete finite physical edge/star
  sectors;
- the actual bare-low, one-fermion-per-site reference filling;
- the exact two-site identities
  `N0+N1=2`, `E=1-N0`, and `H_E=kappa(N0-1)^2/2`;
- a precise onsite/electric transfer scale `Delta=9587/2400`; and
- local coherent L/H transfer together with an induced quartic commutator.
- prior four-edge charge backreaction with integer-winding preservation, as
  documented by `common-engine-threeway`.

It does not yet establish:

- a selected interacting low-energy vacuum or a many-body spectral gap;
- a controlled static effective quartic interaction after Gauss reduction and
  rotor elimination;
- a coherent transfer acting on the E8 channel lattice or its glue/charges;
- an intertwiner to the sixteen-Majorana Clock; or
- a source-selected E8 phase.

The strongest next source test is therefore a typed, controlled low-energy
reduction of the already existing rotor commutator on the complete physical
edge/tree sector, followed by an explicit charge/grading-preserving map to the
candidate E8 operators. That is an extension of an existing parent, not a new
interaction model. Until the reduction and dictionary are supplied, the
quartic commutator is provenance evidence only.
