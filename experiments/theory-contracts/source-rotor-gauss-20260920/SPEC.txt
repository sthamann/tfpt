UR.SOURCE.ROTOR_GAUSS.01 -- 2026-09-20
Research status: PARTIAL, experiments only.

Question
--------
What does the existing dynamical compact-U(1) rotor provide after an exact
Gauss reduction on an arbitrary finite connected graph, and can its physical
local operator algebra be identified with the conditional E8 current source
used by the existing boundary contracts?

Fixed input
-----------
Use the unchanged local-window-round37 parent with its two complex L/H modes
per vertex, integer electric rotors, all retained original hopping terms,
two-step paths and declared ambient onsite backtracks. Keep the declared
parameters a=1/12, eta=1/2, beta=1/4, kappa=1/100, M=4 and Vmag=0. No new
parent, coupling, vacuum rule, continuum limit or interaction is introduced.

The conditional ten-channel I_(9,1) boundary dictionary, its source number q,
hypercharge, glue and E8 lifts are inherited from the existing

The earlier clock-interaction-provenance Gaussian-closure audit, the

are prior provenance, not new findings of this contract.

Decisive tests
-------------
1. Choose a spanning tree on a finite connected graph with v vertices and e
   oriented edges. For rho=N-1 satisfying sum rho=0, construct the unique
   integer tree flow E_T(rho). With b=e-v+1 and an integral fundamental-cycle
   matrix C, prove the complete no-cutoff physical parametrization

       E=E_T(rho)+Cw,  w in Z^b.

   The map from matter masks and winding coordinates to physical rotor states
   must be bijective. It must reduce to the full fixed-v-particle matter
   sector on a tree and retain every integer winding sector on a graph with
   cycles.

2. With L=BB^T and Lplus the neutral Moore-Penrose inverse, prove the exact
   electric identity

       |E|^2 = rho^T Lplus rho
              +(w+theta(rho))^T(C^T C)(w+theta(rho)),
       theta=(C^T C)^(-1) C^T E_T(rho).

   Preserve the affine integer lattice. A real minimizer of the cycle term is
   not an allowed replacement for the integer winding coordinate.

3. Transport every original matter hopping and its CAR sign through the
   reduction. For a path flux increment p and matter change delta rho, the
   winding shift must be the integral circulation determined by

       C ell = p + E_T(rho) - E_T(rho+delta rho).

   Carry the onsite terms, state preparation, self-adjoint electric domain and
   the same real-time evolution. State the exact unitary/time dictionary and
   its finite-graph scope.

4. Separate charged fields from gauge-invariant observables. A bounded
   finite-support observable commuting with the local Gauss transformations
   is even under matter parity on its support. A bare odd fermion therefore
   does not define such an observable; a Wilson-line transfer has endpoint
   charges and must retain its support.

5. Apply the existing conditional E8 source-charge dictionary without
   assigning a new gauge charge. For q(x)=sum_i x_i, count the transported
   E8 roots at charges -4,-2,0,2,4 as

       1, 56, 126, 56, 1.

   The eight Cartan directions are neutral. Under the additional hypothesis
   that this q is the rotor's gauged total matter charge and all images are
   finite-support Gauss-invariant observables, the 114 charged current
   operators cannot have nonzero injective images. Record this as a
   conditional direct-map gate, not as a no-go for charged sectors, boundary
   dressings, other global symmetries, fermions in gauge theory, or E8 in an
   extended algebra.

6. Classify the converse integer gauge-charge covectors within the same
   declared ten-channel E8 embedding. Requiring every lifted E8 root F(r) to
   be neutral gives

       g=(v a,u,-3v),  u,v in Z.

   Requiring the existing n-transfer to be neutral and g to be nonzero
   primitive gives the unique assignment up to sign,

       g=+/- K n=+/-(1,1,1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-3).

   Record the exact bilinear checks g^T K^-1 g=0, q^T K^-1 g=0 and
   Y^T K^-1 g=0, while retaining the uncancelled chiral gravitational
   anomaly from signature 9-1=8. Check g.n=0 and g.z=2: the bare cos(n) is
   allowed by this necessary candidate charge, whereas bare cos(z) is not.
   State explicitly that this is not realized by the old equal-charge L/H
   rotor and does not introduce a gauge field, coupling, cosine term or gap.

Success
-------
An exact finite-connected-graph physical-space, electric-energy, hopping and
time reduction is written with all integer cycle sectors retained. The older
edge/square mechanism is explicitly credited rather than repackaged as new.
The source-charge census, conditional 114-current gate and constructive
candidate-charge converse are stated with their extra identification
assumptions. The result cleanly identifies the remaining field/charge/Clock/
time intertwiner problem.

Stop criterion
--------------
Stop the direct E8 bridge when the charge identification forces the 114
charged currents to vanish, or when a claimed field map drops support,
boundary charge, CAR grading, integer winding or common time. Do not replace
the failed map by a dimension count, a new generalized metric, an arbitrary
interaction, a selected vacuum, or a continuous-symmetry corollary.

Scope and non-claims
--------------------
The verdict is PARTIAL. No P1/P2 origin, QWZ-to-rotor source map, native
Clock action, E8 phase selection, selected low-energy vacuum, many-body gap,
physical 3+1D continuum, T1-T8 closure or promotion is claimed. The limited
onsite-class local-U(1) corollary does not classify general symmetries or
repair the charge map. The positive reduction is a coordinate theorem for
the declared finite parent; it is not a full theory of infinite volume or a
new fundamental force law. The candidate charge is a necessary assignment,
not a realized gauge-field source or a coupling/gauging/gap construction.
