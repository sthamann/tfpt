# What selects the three-factor Bell chain, and what does not?

12 September 2026. NON-RH independent finite refinement of
`interaction/COMPOSITION.md`. Reproduction: `commutant.py`.

## Outcome

**Full mixed-unitary symmetry and reflection do not by themselves select
the equal-weight Bell chain.** At d=4, the complete invariant algebra has
dimension six, whereas the algebra generated by the two Bell projectors
has dimension five. Swapping the two outer factors supplies the missing
independent invariant. It is an extremely simple competing operation.

**There is nevertheless a precise positive selection theorem:** on the
FIXED open graph 1--2--3, insist that the Hamiltonian contain only
nearest-neighbor two-factor terms, impose full active U tensor bar(U)
tensor U symmetry and reflection, and require the individual nontrivial
edge interactions to favor their unique Bell ground. Then the total
Hamiltonian is exactly the equal-weight Bell chain, up to a positive scale
and scalar offset. This is a theorem under explicit hypotheses, not a
derivation of those hypotheses from TFPT.

## 2. The complete invariant algebra has dimension six for d>=3

Middle-factor partial transpose converts the mixed invariance condition
into ordinary U^tensor3 invariance. Ordinary Schur-Weyl duality says that
the latter commutant is spanned by the six permutations, independently
when d>=3. Taking their middle partial transposes gives the full basis

    I, F, P, Q, P Q, Q P.

This use of standard mixed Schur-Weyl theory is not a new TFPT theorem;
see Theorems 3.1 and 3.8 of
[Linear Programming with Unitary-Equivariant Constraints](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00220-024-05108-1).
The checker explicitly reconstructs all six partially transposed
permutations, checks their rank and common span, and verifies invariance
against all sixteen actual d=4 Hermitian source words.

The full Hermitian space therefore has six real coordinates:

    H = a I + b P + c Q + d1(PQ+QP) + d2 i(PQ-QP) + e F,

with all coefficients real. The symbol d1 here is a coefficient, not the
Hilbert-space dimension d.

## 3. Two Bell generators miss one invariant direction

The exact relations

    P^2=P, Q^2=Q, P Q P=P/d^2, Q P Q=Q/d^2

reduce every word in P,Q to span{I,P,Q,PQ,QP}. These five matrices are
independent for d>=2. Thus this is the full unital associative algebra
generated by the Bell interactions. Repeated products, commutators and
finite-dimensional continuous evolutions made only from these generators
cannot generate an independent F at d>=3.

This also explains the missing direction operationally. Let S project on
Ran(P)+Ran(Q), which has dimension 2d. Directly,

    S=(P+Q-PQ-QP)/(1-1/d^2),
    R=I-S,
    R_plus=R(I+F)/2, R_minus=R(I-F)/2.

Both complementary projectors are nonzero for d>=3, and

    rank R_plus=d(d-1)(d+2)/2,
    rank R_minus=d(d+1)(d-2)/2.

At d=4 these ranks are 36 and 20. Every nonconstant Bell word vanishes
on both, so its generated algebra sees only ONE common scalar there.
F acts as +I on R_plus and -I on R_minus, separating the two sectors.
On S the Bell algebra is M2 on a multiplicity space, repeated d times.
Consequently

    Alg(I,P,Q) = M2(C) direct-sum C,
    End_R(U)   = M2(C) direct-sum C direct-sum C.

The full representation has a d-dimensional fundamental occurring twice,
plus the two residual irreducible representations of the displayed ranks.
At d=4 no fully invariant Hamiltonian on this fixed three-factor space can
have a one-dimensional ground state: its smallest possible eigenspace
dimension is four. A unique ground on each edge does not evade this fact.

### Low-dimensional exception

At d=2, R_minus has rank zero and the full commutant has dimension five.
Indeed

    F=I-2(P+Q)+4(PQ+QP).

This identity is also exactly checked. The independent-sixth-direction
claim is restricted to d>=3; the TFPT-inspired conditional model uses d=4.

## 4. Reflection and positivity alone leave simple alternatives

Reflection is implemented by F, with FPF=Q. Imposing FHF=H leaves the
four-dimensional Hermitian span

    I, P+Q, PQ+QP, F.

In particular (I+F)/2 and (I-F)/2 are positive invariant reflection-symmetric
projectors, independent of the whole Bell-generated algebra. At d=4 their
spectra are respectively 0 (24),1 (40) and 0 (40),1 (24).
Their ground states differ drastically from the Bell chain's fourfold
ground. Counting terms or calling an operator simple cannot exclude them.

Even prohibiting F by restricting to the Bell-generated algebra still
leaves the reflection-invariant directions I,P+Q,PQ+QP. The last term is
not merely a rescaled equal-weight Hamiltonian: it has genuine three-factor
support and independently changes the energies of the two fundamental
multiplicity sectors relative to the remainder.

## 5. Strict locality on the chosen graph gives a stronger theorem

Specify the graph to be OPEN 1--2--3, and define strict nearest-neighbor
Hamiltonian locality as

    H = h12 tensor I3 + I1 tensor h23.

Arbitrary on-site terms and scalar offsets can be included in these terms.
This excludes a direct 1--3 term and genuine three-factor terms. It is
stronger than membership in the associative algebra of local observables.

If such an H is invariant under R(U), Haar-average its two local terms.
The average preserves their supports and leaves H unchanged. Each averaged
pair term belongs to the two-factor mixed commutant span{I,P_Bell}. Hence

    H = alpha I + beta P + gamma Q.

This argument does not assume the originally supplied local terms are
separately invariant: an invariant decomposition is obtained by averaging.
Reflection then forces beta=gamma, because P,Q,I are independent:

    H = alpha I + beta(P+Q).

The exact d=4 checker also verifies this without constructing a Haar
integral. Let E12,E23,E2 denote normalized partial-trace conditional
expectations onto the corresponding support algebras. Then

    Pi_NN=E12+E23-E2

is the orthogonal projector onto their sum. On the complete six-element
invariant Hermitian basis, H-Pi_NN(H) has rank three, leaving precisely
I,P,Q. Adding the reflection equations gives rank four, leaving precisely
I,P+Q. Both F and PQ+QP fail this nearest-neighbor support test.

### The sign still needs a physical criterion

Global positivity does not force beta<0: P+Q is positive, as is 2I-P-Q.
Their lowest sectors are different; for d=4, P+Q has a 56-dimensional
kernel. Scalar shifts can make any Hermitian choice nonnegative.

Requiring each edge's nonconstant invariant pair term to have its unique
Bell state as ground forces its Bell-projector coefficient negative.
Together with reflection this gives, after a scalar shift,

    H = J[(I-P)+(I-Q)],   J>0.

Thus the equal-weight Bell chain IS selected up to positive scale and
energy offset within this fixed local family. If a sign/ground criterion
is omitted, both attractive and repulsive Bell directions remain allowed;
if the coupling is allowed to vanish, no motion remains another option.

## 6. What remains external and how to use this result

The conditional theorem does not choose:

- why the physical register consists of three or many alternating dual factors;
- why full active U(4), rather than only the actual source markings, is a symmetry;
- why the physical interaction graph is an open nearest-neighbor chain;
- why a direct outer exchange or a higher-body invariant is absent;
- why an isolated edge favors its Bell ground;
- the energy scale J, time unit, state preparation, or a common continuum.

These are distinct assumptions. A physical derivation of locality might
exclude F13 and PQ+QP without arbitrarily deleting algebra elements, but
the algebraic dimension calculation does not provide that derivation.
Conversely, F13 is not a counterexample to the conditional nearest-neighbor
theorem because it explicitly violates its fixed graph support.

The previous composition result remains valid for the selected conditional
chain, including its fourfold block ground, cloning-channel identity and
nonzero bare-embedding leakage. The present refinement clarifies exactly
which assumptions select that chain and prevents full symmetry alone from
being credited with selecting it. No T1-T8, RH or complexity claim is made.
