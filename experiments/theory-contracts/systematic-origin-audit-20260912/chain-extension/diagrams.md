# All odd lengths: an exact planar Bell-chain module

12 September 2026. NON-RH. Companion executable: `diagrams.py`.
The model remains conditional: alternating fundamental/conjugate factors,
one-dimensional nearest-neighbor geometry, Bell links and their physical
interpretation are premises, not outputs of the original TFPT source.

## Outcome in simple language

The five-pairing repair extends to every finite odd chain length. The same
local reconnection rule acts within a precisely defined space of planar
pairing patterns, and those patterns are linearly independent at every
integer local dimension d>=2. The all-length proof is analytic, not an
inference from testing five chain sizes.

For lengths1,3,5,7,9 the number of patterns is1,2,5,14,42. At d=4 their
retained physical dimensions are4,8,20,56,168. Thus the model has an exact
finite family of closed Bell interactions, but the retained description
does not remain a single four-state block as the chain grows.

This is a planar Temperley-Lieb module. It is not claimed to be the full
fundamental isotypic component of U(d) for d>2, nor the smallest space
generated by repeated action of a particular Hamiltonian on an initial
vector. The n=5 cyclicity theorem remains separate.

## 1. Precise admissibility and Catalan counting

Let n=2m+1 be odd. Sites in odd positions carry U on C^d; even positions
carry conjugate(U). A diagram has m noncrossing pair-arcs and one uncovered
site, whose index carries the logical input. **No arc may enclose the
uncovered site.** Equivalently its through-line can reach the exterior
without crossing another arc.

Adjoin one final endpoint at position n+1 and connect it to the uncovered
site. This is a bijection with noncrossing perfect matchings of2m+2 ordered
points. Conversely removing that last endpoint produces an admissible
one-defect diagram. The number of diagrams is therefore

    C_(m+1) = binomial(2m+2,m+1)/(m+2).

Every arc connects opposite parities, and the uncovered site has odd
position. Thus every pair is between a fundamental and a conjugate factor,
and the remaining logical factor is fundamental. The exterior-defect
condition matters: unrestricted partial matchings have a different count
and are not the module defined here.

## 2. Actual tensor maps and exact Gram

For an admissible diagram D, define its unnormalized map by

    (D psi)_(i1,...,in)
       = product_(a,b in arcs(D)) delta_(ia,ib) * psi_(i_free(D)).

These are actual tensor maps, not abstract labels. Let Dcal be the joined
map with one d-dimensional logical block per diagram. Contracting two
diagrams joins their logical endpoints along one path; every closed
remaining component gives one free index sum. Therefore

    Dcal*Dcal = G_n(d) tensor I_d,
    G_n(d)_(D,E)=d^(number of closed components after contraction).

In particular the diagonal entries are d^m. The generator construction
uses unnormalized diagrams throughout; factors1/d only enter through
normalized Bell projectors.

## 3. All-length linear independence, with proof

This proof is independent of finite Gram tests.

**Step1: close and vectorize.** Vectorize each diagram map by joining its
logical input to the extra last endpoint. Vectorization is injective and
gives the product of Bell-pair tensors associated with the completed
noncrossing perfect matching.

**Step2: prove independence at d=2.** Apply the same invertible matrix

    epsilon = [[0,1],[-1,0]]

to every even-positioned tensor factor, including the new last factor.
Every Bell pair becomes an antisymmetric singlet, up to a nonzero sign
depending on the pair orientation. This works simultaneously because each
arc joins opposite parities. The resulting matching tensor corresponds to
the multilinear polynomial

    f_M = product_(i,j in M, i<j) (x_i y_j-y_i x_j).

Order the variables lexicographically as

    x1 > y1 > x2 > y2 > ... > x_(n+1) > y_(n+1).

The leading monomial of f_M selects x_i from each left arc endpoint and
y_j from each right endpoint. Different noncrossing perfect matchings have
different left-endpoint sets: their opening/closing sequence is a Dyck
word, and stack matching uniquely reconstructs the noncrossing arcs.
Thus all f_M have distinct leading monomials. In any nonzero linear
combination the greatest leading monomial cannot cancel, proving linear
independence. Invertible local epsilon changes and vectorization preserve
that independence.

**Step3: lift to every integer d>=2.** Project each vectorized tensor
factor onto the same fixed two-dimensional coordinate subspace. Every
unnormalized d-dimensional Bell pair becomes the corresponding
two-dimensional pair. A linear relation at dimension d would therefore
give exactly the same relation at d=2, already excluded.

Consequently G_n(d) is strictly positive for all finite odd n and integer
d>=2, and

    rank(Dcal) = d C_((n+1)/2).

The positivity follows because the Hilbert-Schmidt Gram of the independent
maps is d G_n(d); together with Dcal*Dcal=G_n(d) tensor I_d, this proves
the full retained-space rank, not just independence of diagram labels.

This fits established valence-bond/Temperley-Lieb mathematics, rather than
being a new TFPT-specific combinatorial principle. In their original
research paper, Nachtergaele, Spitzer and Starr use noncrossing arcs that
do not enclose unpaired sites and discuss the corresponding independent
Hulthen-bracket basis and nonorthogonal Hamiltonian coordinates, especially
section7, pages17–19. Their ferromagnetic spectral results are not imported
as claims about this differently normalized conditional model.
[Ferromagnetic Ordering of Energy Levels](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0308006)

## 4. All-length exact local dynamics

For an adjacent pair set e_j=d P_(j,j+1), with P the normalized Bell
projector. On any diagram:

- If j and j+1 are already paired, e_j multiplies the diagram by d.
- Otherwise it pairs j with j+1 and reconnects their former partners.
- If one endpoint was the defect, its former partner becomes the defect.

The result is again a single admissible planar one-defect diagram. This
is local surgery on adjacent strands: it cannot introduce a crossing or
an arc spanning the exterior through-line. The rule therefore proves
invariance for every finite odd n, not merely for a list of enumerated n.

The actual Bell projectors give the relations

    e_j^2=d e_j,
    e_j e_(j+1) e_j=e_j,
    e_(j+1) e_j e_(j+1)=e_(j+1),
    [e_j,e_k]=0 for |j-k|>1.

The first relation is idempotence of P. The adjacent relation is the
three-factor contraction P_j P_(j+1) P_j=P_j/d^2. Distant projectors act
on disjoint factors. Hence these identities hold for arbitrary length,
with the same loop parameter d as in the Gram.

If E_j are their diagram coefficient matrices, then

    G E_j=E_j*G,
    h_n=(n-1)I-(1/d)sum_j E_j,
    H_n Dcal=Dcal(h_n tensor I_d),
    G h_n=h_n* G.

The same invariant space supports arbitrary selected edge weights and
polynomials in these Bell projectors; selecting weights as physical data
is an additional issue. Because G>0, a Cholesky factor G=C*C gives the
exact finite isometric encoding J=Dcal(C^-1 tensor I_d) and Hermitian
effective parent C h_n C^-1, just as in the five-site proof.

## 6. What remains outside this theorem

- This planar module is not asserted to exhaust all U(d)-fundamental
  multiplicities for d>2; nonplanar invariant contractions are not included.
- No minimal H-Krylov hull is claimed beyond the separately proved n=5
  result. Invariance of a candidate span is not minimality from one state.
- Unrestricted marked local compiler operations are not retained. The
  previous actual-g1 leakage witness remains valid at n=5.
- Finite positive Gram matrices do not give a uniform infinite-chain
  norm bound, a continuum limit, or compatible interlevel embeddings.
- Neither a unique physical graph, a selected clock, a common 3+1D
  origin, nor a TFPT-to-this-chain map has been derived.

The theorem is exact all-length **finite Bell/Temperley-Lieb closure**
under explicit premises. It does not complete any T1-T8 problem or any
arithmetical or computational complexity conjecture.
