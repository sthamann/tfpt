## 1. Main result: the fixed trimer encoding is uniformly suboptimal

This is stronger than a failed weak-coupling estimate. Let L=3M physical
registers form a **uniform periodic** chain, with even M>=4. Let W be the
tensor product of the previously constructed exact three-site ground
isometries G and conjugate(G), alternated around the chain. No restriction
is imposed on the entanglement of the M logical registers.

Write e0=4-sqrt(6). The exact boundary map gives

    W* H_L W = M(e0+5/4) I + (3/8) H_M,logical.

Every overlapping logical three-site window satisfies

    h_(j,j+1)+h_(j+1,j+2) >= e0 I.

Summing these M inequalities counts each logical edge twice, hence

    H_M,logical >= M e0/2 I.

For every normalized state in the range of W, therefore,

    <H_L>/L >= [(19/16)e0+5/4]/3
             = (96-19sqrt(6))/48 = 1+delta,
    delta=(48-19sqrt(6))/48 > 0.

Positivity is exact: 48^2=2304 > 19^2*6=2166. On the other hand, a product
of original Bell pairs on alternate physical bonds has energy exactly L.
Occupied pair bonds have energy zero; all remaining bonds see I16/16 and
have mean energy two. The variational principle gives

    E_ground(H_L) <= L,
    <H_L>_encoded - E_ground(H_L) >= delta L.

Thus **no ground vector can lie entirely in this fixed trimer subspace**,
and its energy-density error cannot vanish as these lengths increase.
This does not assert zero overlap with the ground state, nor exclude a
dressed, state-adapted, or nonlocal encoding. The proof is for L=12,18,24,...
with the specified periodic boundaries; it is not a claim about all boundary
conditions. The previous weak-link expansion remains valid in its own regime.

`trimer_variational.py` independently reconstructs G from the pinned source,
checks its exact spectrum and boundary response, and verifies the constants
and dimer expectations. The all-length step is the displayed operator
inequality and edge-counting argument, not finite-size extrapolation.

    U H_source U* = 2(L-1)I + (1/2) sum_(j,a) gamma_a(j) gamma_a(j+1)

## 3. Why the four-dimensional logical factor keeps appearing

This algebraic statement concerns the algebra generated by **all individual
primitive bond operators**, not the commutant of their Hamiltonian sum.
Consider an open chain with L sites. Ignore scalar Pauli phases and encode
each local Clifford word by four binary bits. Their nondegenerate local
commutation form is M4=J4+I4 over F2, where J4 is the all-ones matrix.
Indeed M4^2=I4 in characteristic two.

The 4(L-1) independent bond words place the same primitive bit at adjacent
sites. Independence follows by removing the leftmost endpoint successively:
a vanishing word combination must have zero coefficient on its first edge,
then on its second, and so on. Their commutation matrix is

    K = A_path(L-1) tensor M4.

For an m-vertex path the kernel equations force the even components to zero
and make all odd components equal. The final endpoint sets that common value
to zero when m is even. Thus rank(A_path(m)) is m for even m and m-1 for odd m.
Consequently rank(K) is 4(L-1) for odd L and 4(L-2) for even L.

The full Pauli-word space has dimension 4L, so the symplectic orthogonal
complement of the bond-word span has dimension four. It is generated by the
four global products of the same primitive gamma on every site. Such global
products commute with every bond. Two distinct products anticommute when L
is odd and commute when L is even. Since Pauli words form a matrix basis,
these sixteen commuting-with-bonds words span the entire bond-algebra commutant.

- Odd L: the commutant is M4. The bond algebra acts as
  M_(4^(L-1)) tensor I4, giving a **protected factor-four degeneracy**.
- Even L: the commutant is C^16. There are sixteen joint parity sectors,
  each of dimension 4^(L-2), with a full matrix bond algebra within each.

The odd-chain logical factor is therefore not special to length three and
does not itself certify a renormalization fixed point, a low-energy gap,
or a spatial dimension. The remaining nontrivial factor is still exponentially
large. `bond_algebra.py` checks the rank formulas at lengths2 through17;
the endpoint and Pauli-basis arguments above establish their all-length scope.

## 4. A stronger free-fermion obstruction from the exact spectrum

The full L=3 Hamiltonian has ground energy 4-sqrt(6), with multiplicity four.
Its first two distinct positive excitation gaps are

    d1=sqrt(6)-2,          multiplicity4;
    d2=sqrt(6)-sqrt(2),    multiplicity12.

Suppose the full 64-dimensional spectrum came from an unconstrained
quadratic six-mode fermion Hamiltonian, after any unitary change of basis.
Diagonalizing that quadratic Hamiltonian gives nonnegative independent
mode excitation energies. Its ground degeneracy four means two zero modes.
The first excitation multiplicity equals the ground multiplicity, so exactly
one positive mode has energy d1. The next level cannot be a multiple
occupation of that same mode. With no other level below d2, it must contain
distinct modes of energy d2. Occupying one of them together with the d1 mode
would produce d1+d2. But

    (4-sqrt(6))+d1+d2 = 2+sqrt(6)-sqrt(2)

is absent from the exact spectrum. Contradiction.

This excludes a full unconstrained quadratic Fock-space solution at that
length, not a parity-constrained or sector-dependent construction, interacting
integrability, or free subsectors. `bond_algebra.py` recomputes the exact
spectrum and includes a genuine free-model positive control. The direct
Jordan-Wigner transformation independently gives quartic interactions.

## 5. An exactly solvable dimer comparison, without moving the target

Define Omega_ij=sum_a gamma_a(i)gamma_a(j). Independent exact source algebra
gives the three-site triangle spectrum

    T=Omega12+Omega23+Omega13,
    spec(T)={-4:12, -2:24, 0:8, 4:12, 6:8}.

Hence T+4I is positive. The transformed original Bell singlet d obeys
(gamma_a tensor I+I tensor gamma_a)d=0 for every a. Both d12 tensor V3
and V1 tensor d23 lie in the kernel of T+4I.

For even periodic L>=6 and J>=0, take the **different** Hamiltonian

    H_D=J sum_j Omega_(j,j+1)+(J/2) sum_j Omega_(j,j+2)
       =(J/2) sum_j T_j.

Each consecutive triple contains a complete dimer in either alternating
covering. Therefore both coverings attain the operator lower bound
E0=-2JL. This proves ground-state existence and energy at all these lengths.
It does not prove here that their span is the entire ground space, or prove
a uniform gap. In source units J=1/2, this means adding (1/4)sum N13 to
the periodic primitive chain, whose ground energy including its constant
is then L. That coupling is an additional choice, not a derived source rule.

The generated ten-word correction prevents an easy identification with this
comparison model. On a six-site periodic dimer state, let the three operator
sums be A=sum Omega_nn, B=sum Omega_nnn, D=sum H10_nn. Subtract their means
to obtain leakage vectors. Exact source evaluation gives their Gram matrix

    [[12, -24, 6],
     [-24, 48, -12],
     [6, -12, 15/2]],

with kernel span{(2,1,0)}. Thus this bare dimer product is an eigenstate
of alpha A+beta B+u D only if alpha=2beta and u=0. Changing only the two
vector couplings cannot repair a nonzero H10 perturbation. This is a finite
counterexample to a universal exact-product claim, not a phase-transition
or dimer-order exclusion. `dimer_parent.py` verifies the positive triangle
and the Gram exactly without constructing a full 4096x4096 matrix.

## 6. A constructive one-parameter improvement for the original chain

The Hamiltonian need not be changed to improve on isolated dimers. Start with
the original Bell-pair covering and apply disjoint local filters

    F(t)=I-t h4

on the intervening bonds. This is a trial-state construction, not a proposed
physical time law. It is an exact matrix-product state with a two-site unit
cell and virtual dimension4. In the source basis its matrices are

    A^(s,t)_(a,b)=F_(st,ab)/2.

Write p=1-4t+5t^2 and b=1-4t+(7/2)t^2. The transfer map E has
E(I)=E*(I)=pI. For 0<=t<1/4, F is invertible, so its sixteen reshaped rows
span all4x4 matrices. The transfer map is strictly positive: for every
nonzero positive X and nonzero vector v, at least one Kraus term has
nonzero expectation in v. This gives a unique dominant transfer fixed point.
At t=1/8 its entire spectrum is checked directly:

    spec(E)={37/64:1, 3/32:4, 1/128:6, 0:5}.

Contraction with the exact fixed points gives the two alternating bond
energies and their mean per physical site:

    e_filtered=(2-10t+14t^2)/p,
    e_between=2-2(b/p)^2,
    e(t)=(2-10t+14t^2)/(2p)+1-(b/p)^2.

In particular e(0)=1, e'(0)=-1, and the simple rational parameter t=1/8 gives

    e(1/8)=5023/5476 < 1.

This is an exact **thermodynamic variational upper bound** for the original
uniform nearest-neighbor chain, approximately0.917275. The ground-energy
density therefore obeys

    (4-sqrt(6))/2 <= e_ground <= 5023/5476.

The lower bound is the earlier sum of overlapping trimer inequalities.
The limit of ground energy per site exists by bounded boundary interactions;
open and periodic ground energies differ by at most one bond norm. The
filtered state supplies the upper bound, not an equality or a proof of its
being the ground state. Its input pair pattern does not prove spontaneous
dimer order, and the rational t is a variational choice, not a natural constant.

Finite rings must not use the limiting normalization as an exact substitute.
For M cells at t=1/8 their norm is

    Tr(E^M)=(37/64)^M+4(3/32)^M+6(1/128)^M,

not merely p^M. The simple displayed e(t) is the infinite-cell limit. The
subleading spectral ratio6/37 controls the transfer convergence for this
trial state; it is not a many-body Hamiltonian gap or a physical mass.
See [dimer_filter.md](dimer_filter.md) and its exact checker.

## 7. Source choice and the next research gate

[hierarchy_source.md](hierarchy_source.md) supplies a complementary all-length
countermodel: the same actual primitive/family covariance allows arbitrary
positive edge weights on a bipartite graph. Uniform and trimerized weights
remain spectrally distinct even after equalizing their mean energies.
The internal family cycle is not a spatial three-site translation.

The filtered state supplies the first minimal **correlation-adapted** ansatz,
with a two-site unit cell allowed but not imposed as a property of nature.
Further work must retain local deformations and distinguish these gates:

1. Recover the exact dimer comparison energy and the nonzero H10 leakage.
   This catches an ansatz that silently drops the generated interaction.
2. Improve on the new upper bound5023/5476, tighten the lower bound, and check
   convergence as retained correlations increase. The first improvement
   below1 is now established, but a variational improvement is not yet a
   rigorous exact ground solution.
3. Distinguish spontaneous dimer correlations from boundaries or an input
   alternation. Establish a gap or long-distance statement with the relevant
   error control before using it as a continuum argument.
4. Separately derive graph, energy rule and time/update interpretation from
   the compiler. Solving a conditional one-dimensional spin model would
   not by itself close that physical-source obligation.

No additional physical input is silently supplied. T1-T8 remain open;
no RH, factorization, P-versus-NP or Hylæan capability claim is made.
