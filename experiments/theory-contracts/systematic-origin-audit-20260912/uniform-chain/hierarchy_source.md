# Can the original compiler select weak links and spatial blocks?

NON-RH original-source audit and all-length conditional countermodel. No graph,
physical dynamics, or T1–T8 completion is inferred from the constructions below.

## Result

The reviewed original onsite compiler data do **not** force spatial trimer
tiling, weak interblock links, or a hierarchy of their strengths. The same
onsite source symmetry is compatible with uniform chains, trimerized chains,
and other finite bipartite graphs, using the actual four-generator primitive
comparison H4 throughout. The source family three-cycle acts inside
each register; it is not a permutation of spatial sites.

This does not exclude emergent translation breaking or a source mechanism not
yet identified. It identifies the first missing implication in this route:
**an operational relation mapping internal compiler operations to a spatial
adjacency and coupling/update rule**. A chosen blocking of an already supplied
uniform chain is not evidence that weak physical bonds exist at its boundaries.

## An all-length countermodel with identical onsite source

Let G=(A∪B,E) be any finite simple bipartite graph. Put V=C4 on A and bar(V)
on B. For the actual original four skew-Hermitian generators gi define

    Qi=gi⊗bar(gi),
    h4=Σ_(i=1,...,4) (I−Qi)/2.

Put this primitive comparison on each edge, oriented from A to B. For positive
edge weights J_e define

    H_G(J)=Σ_(e∈E) J_e h4,e.

This is a positive Hermitian operator. For every U in the group generated by
the actual gi and the family lift w, the global action

    R_G(U)=tensor_(v∈A) U ⊗ tensor_(v∈B) bar(U)

preserves each primitive edge comparison separately. The dual Qi are commuting
involutions, and w cycles the first three while fixing the fourth. Therefore

    [H_G(J),R_G(U)]=0

for **every** assignment of positive J_e, every graph size, and every such
graph. This proof is by the exact two-site covariance and tensoring identities;
it is not an extrapolation from finite chain tests. No positive frustration-
free or unique-ground-state claim is needed or made for the many-edge sum.

Thus actual primitive/family covariance cannot distinguish a path from a
bipartite star or grid, nor strong from weak edges. All of those geometries
and interpretations remain additions to the onsite source.

For an explicit pair on the *same* open path, use N=3m sites with m≥2:

    uniform:       J_i=1,                    i=1,...,N−1;
    trimerized:    J_i=1 if i mod3≠0,
                       lambda if i mod3=0,   0<lambda<1.

Both chains are connected, positive and invariant under the same onsite source
operations. As lambda→0, the second chain separates into m disjoint triples;
this hierarchy was put into its edge weights. The three possible offsets of
the weak-link pattern are further spatial choices. Internal family cycling
preserves every h4,i, so it neither chooses an offset nor makes lambda small.

## These coupling choices are observable, not merely different notation

Write ai=i gi, so ai are traceless Hermitian4×4 matrices with
Tr(ai aj)/4=delta_ij. For the actual local dimension d=4, put

    E_e=h4,e−2I=−(1/2)Σ_i ai⊗bar(ai)

on each oriented edge, and normalized trace τ(X)=Tr(X)/4^N. Then

    τ(E_e)=0,
    τ(E_e E_f)=0 for e≠f,
    τ(E_e²)=1.

The proof covers overlapping edges: a distinct edge has an endpoint not shared
with e, and each ai has zero trace. Its normalized partial trace onto e
therefore vanishes. The same-edge norm is1 by the orthonormality of the four
endpoint matrices. Hence partial trace isolates each centered bond coefficient. In particular
the bond operators are linearly independent modulo the identity for any finite
simple graph. The energy variance is exactly

    τ(H)=2Σ_e J_e,      Var_τ H=Σ_e J_e².

The trimer example has r=2m strong bonds, s=m−1 weak bonds and M=r+s bonds.
Rescale its weights by c=M/(r+s lambda), so its mean energy equals that of the
uniform chain. Its variance still exceeds the uniform variance by

    M r s (1−lambda)²/(r+s lambda)² > 0.

Consequently these same-mean finite Hamiltonians are not unitarily equivalent:
their second spectral moments differ. There is **no15/256 prefactor** for the
primitive H4 model; its local normalized variance is1. This conclusion holds
for every m≥2 and every0<lambda<1, analytically. It
does not assert different continuum phases or prove any physical model true.

## Translation does constrain weights — only when it is independently imposed

On a periodic chain with N divisible by6, the cyclic site permutation T sends
h4,i to h4,i+1. In the actual source chart h4 is also invariant under exchanging
its two sites, as checked exactly. Linear independence of centered bonds proves

    [T,H(J)]=0  iff  J_i=J_(i+1) for all i.

Thus an *added* one-site translation-invariance condition forces uniform
nearest-neighbor weights. It does not explain an input period-three hierarchy.

There is a typing qualification: on the alternating fundamental/conjugate
register, a one-site shift exchanges the two site types. Calling it a symmetry
of the entire marked source requires specifying that identification. A two-site
shift preserves the types without that issue. It already excludes the
nontrivial period-three weight pattern, since simultaneous periods2 and3 force
period1. Two-site translation alone permits alternating weights; it does not
force all weights equal without another condition. A period-three weighted
Hamiltonian has a three-site matrix-translation symmetry, but its type-preserving
period is6. None of these spatial translation rules comes from the internal
order-three matrix w merely having that order projectively.

It remains possible for an independently supplied uniform model to develop a
translation-breaking state or an effective scale hierarchy. Demonstrating that
would require the actual thermodynamic/state/flow argument; inserting a tiled
weak-link Hamiltonian assumes its conclusion instead. Exact block elimination
on a uniform chain is a separate useful investigation, but its truncation error
cannot be controlled by declaring an input weak-link ratio that is actually1.

## Keep three missing choices separate

- **Graph:** which physical registers exist and which may interact?
- **Couplings/blocking:** what chooses edge strengths, the initial scale ratio,
  a tiling or a state that could break translation symmetry?
- **Time/update:** is the operator a Hamiltonian, a Euclidean filter, or a
  generator in a Liouville representation, and what fixes its rate/clock?

Global onsite covariance answers none of these three by itself. A decisive
next source mechanism must generate one common adjacency-and-weight object or
select a state/flow that produces it, and distinguish the uniform and tiled
countermodels without presupposing the desired hierarchy.

## Optional stronger-symmetry illustration: a different, flat model

Replacing h4 by I−P_Bell gives a separate counterexample with full global
U4 covariance, because every Bell edge is fixed by U⊗bar(U). That parent is
not the same interaction as h4 and is not used to establish the primary result
above. Its local mean is15/16 and variance15/256, rather than2 and1. For that
separate flattened model the variance-excess formula carries the15/256 factor.
Both illustrate source underdetermination; they must not be silently identified.
