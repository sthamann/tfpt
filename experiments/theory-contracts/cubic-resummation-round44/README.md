## What makes the calculation controlled

Two auxiliary columns are evolved on **46874** particle/current/species
states generated by elementary walks of length at most six. Distinct
loop currents remain distinct even at the same particle position. The
original hopping monomials, low backtracks and full electric energies
are retained. There is no replacement by a spatial tree or zero-loop model.

Every transition out of that set is enumerated: **652522 outside rows**
feed a Duhamel source bound. Positive hopping powers give an evaluated
all-exit certificate independent of the unbounded outside electric energy.
Further propagation, independent loops and arbitrarily high later winding
are bounded through the full propagator, not silently omitted. The total
auxiliary reconstruction error at t=1 is below **1.315e-7**. The remaining
electric bound is about **3.464e-5** and is added separately.

Degree-60 matrix and scalar exponentials use exact rational arithmetic
with outward Taylor tails. Four times share the same computed matrix
powers. A second polynomial evaluator, original-parent columns, all cubic
matter jets through time order three and different configuration sizes
provide independent checks. The finite configuration approximation is
NOT claimed to reproduce every fourth-order source coefficient exactly;
its additional error is explicitly included.

See [CUBIC_RESUMMATION.md](CUBIC_RESUMMATION.md) for the derivation, all four
bare-state intervals, scope of the initial-class error bound, counterchecks
and resource accounting. The outside rows and exact-integer arithmetic
are real costs; no total speedup is claimed.
