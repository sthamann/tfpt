---------------------

the observable responses of a localized spinor sector, before constructing
an unbounded microscopic half-charge field?

and the even observable net. A contractive cell-average embedding extends

known localized CAR automorphism, including its nonsmooth test functions.

Stop criterion: introducing a target Hamiltonian or flux register, calling
the word prescription a microscopic homomorphism, or claiming FE-GEN from

2. A contractive source map removes the smoothness obstruction
-------------------------------------------------------------
Use x in [0,1), a=r/4, cells I_(N,x)=[x/N,(x+1)/N), and the SAME top-row
spinor chi_+=(1,1)/sqrt(2). For a periodic complex test function g define

 (Q_N^a g)(x,y) = sqrt(N) integral_(I_(N,x))
                    exp(-2 pi i a t) g(t) dt delta_(y,7) chi_+.

No new source state, energy, coupling, or degree of freedom appears.
Cauchy-Schwarz in each cell proves ||Q_N^a g|| <= ||g||_L2, for all L2 g.
The embedded step function converges to exp(-2 pi i a t)g(t) in L2;
therefore inner products converge to the continuum inner products.
Supports enlarge by at most one lattice cell.

For C1 g, let F(t)=exp(-2 pi i a t)g(t). Compared with the original
point-sampled R_N^a, the error at a site is bounded by
||F'||_infinity/(2 N^(3/2)). Consequently

 ||Q_N^a g-R_N^a g|| <= ||F'||_infinity/(2N).

The source CAR norm bound gives the same operator-norm error for a(Qg)
and its adjoint. Thus the known smooth field/adjoint limits also hold for Q.

To pass to any L2 g, first approximate g by a fixed smooth g_k. The error
of the source field is <=||g-g_k||_2, uniformly in N; the limit field has
the identical bound. Take N to infinity first, then k to infinity.
This proves strong field and joint-adjoint convergence on the known source
comparison maps. No N-dependent test-function approximation is presumed.

For products, use the telescoping product identity and the uniform factor
bounds. Every fixed finite word, including words involving adjoints, has
the correct complex vacuum expectation in the ORIGINAL filled sea.
Positivity follows as the limit of omega_N(X_N* X_N)>=0; the limiting CAR
relations follow from convergence of the test-function Gram matrices.

At finite N this is a lift of words, NOT a homomorphism from the complete
infinite-dimensional CAR algebra: Q_N*Q_N is not I. This qualification
prevents the finite-rank approximation from being called a net isomorphism.

Time evolution is also controlled for arbitrary fixed L2 tests at finite
times. Both source and limit one-particle evolutions are unitary. Smooth
approximation gives uniform norm error independent of time, and the known
smooth Duhamel estimate is uniform on compact time intervals. The same
two-limit argument and product telescope prove finite-time word convergence.
This does not imply an energy-domain bound for a nonsmooth charged field.

4. The bounded spinor operation and its source lift
---------------------------------------------------
Primary input: J. Boeckenhauer, Rev. Math. Phys. 8 (1996), Sections 5–7,
https://arxiv.org/html/hep-th/9507047 . Equations 23–28 give a real
Bogoliubov unitary V for an even number of Majorana components. Theorem
5.4 identifies its restriction to the even vacuum net with a spinor
sector. This established sector theorem is cited, not re-proved here.

For reproducibility, the operation inside its localization interval can
be specified pairwise. Write o,e for an odd/even Majorana pair, f_n for
the interval integer Fourier basis and f_r for its half-integer basis:

 V f_0^o = i(f_(1/2)^o-f_(-1/2)^o)/sqrt(2),
 V f_0^e = -(f_(1/2)^o+f_(-1/2)^o)/sqrt(2),
 V f_n^o = i sign(n) f_(n+sign(n)/2)^o,             n!=0,
 V f_n^e = -i sign(n) f_(n-sign(n)/2)^e,            n!=0.

The two zero-mode columns are orthonormal and fill the two missing odd
half modes. All other columns biject onto the remaining half modes.
This proves unitarity on the entire interval Hilbert space. Conjugating
coefficients and reversing frequency proves V Gamma=Gamma V. Eight such
pairs use the stated sixteen Majoranas, without an auxiliary flux register.

Outside the localization interval I, V is +I on one component of the
punctured complement and -I on the other. Hence rho_V(B(f))=B(Vf) fixes
every EVEN local word contained in either disjoint complement interval.

approximants stay there up to one cell). An interval intersecting but not
containing I can spread through I. These are
the required local-net statements. It need not fix an odd-left times
odd-right bilocal product; that product receives a minus sign. Treating
the two separated components as one local interval would be a false
strengthening of the localization claim.

Each factor is uniformly bounded by ||f_i||. This includes nonsmooth Vf_i,

5. What the construction buys, and what it does not buy
-------------------------------------------------------
The charged sector is a representation of existing observables. It is not
an operator taking the microscopic vacuum to a new charged vector. The
prescription transforms the observables queried in the same sea. It does
not prove that the full continuum automorphism has a finite, local source
implementer, nor that its state is obtainable by a physical preparation.

Known D8 sector facts at sixteen Majoranas give conformal spinor weight one
and order-two sector fusion. The fusion statement is up to equivalence of
representations. It does not identify V^2 with I, erase integer lattice
charge carry, or contradict U_s^2=-U_(2s) in the inherited charge convention.

There is also a time distinction: alpha_t(rho_V(A)) and rho_V(alpha_t(A))
need not agree. Pulling source evolution back through rho_V conjugates its
generator; the sector's conventional conformal Hamiltonian uses its own
covariance implementers. The word limit alone does not identify these
with one physical clock or supply the determinant phases of a charged field.

The construction gives per-word content of a specified top-edge subnet.
It does not establish a Buchholz-Verch liminf inclusion for B_N^gen or
identify that subnet with the actual microscopic generated algebra.
In particular it does not give the UPPER inclusion needed by ALG-EXH: additional
bounded microscopic sequences may survive in a scaling limit. Nor does it
show that a required gauge/code projection keeps this sector accessible.

Thus it is now legitimate to investigate a bounded local-extension route:
derive the physically allowed simple-current extension of the sourced
observable net, then establish its source realization and exhaustion.
It is not legitimate to declare that extension selected because the
spinor sector exists. The D8 observable net and its E8 extension remain
different local theories; the marked charge dictionary and original
P1/P2 assumptions must decide which is realized.
For sector dynamics, a compatible DHR covariance cocycle/charged implementer
and its microscopic convergence remain to be supplied; the bounded word
limit by itself does not construct them.
