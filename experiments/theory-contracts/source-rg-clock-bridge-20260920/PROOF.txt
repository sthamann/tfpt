1. Exact inventory of the original microscopic source

Use the hopping blocks of verification/v1033_charged_disorder.py, inherited
from v988, with m=1, W=8 (the following argument works for fixed W>=2),
sector r=1, and the actual filled negative spectral sea. Set
  p_j=2*pi*(j-1/4)/N,  D_N=N*h_N/(2*pi),
  H_N=dGamma(D_N)-Tr(1_(D_N<0)D_N).
Choose the N unaliased labels with principal p_j in (-pi,pi].
This is the raw Hamiltonian, not the Hsharp approximation.

The existing microscopic-neutral-limit proof gives, in the sigma_x basis,
  h(p)=[-sin(p)I, B*; B, sin(p)I],
  B=rho I-S_W, rho=1-cos(p),
where S_W is the lower unilateral shift. Let
  T=(1+rho^2)I-rho(S_W+S_W*).
Then B*B=T-|e_last><e_last|. With eigenvalues in INCREASING order,
negative rank-one interlacing gives
  lambda_2(B*B)>=lambda_1(T)
    =1+rho^2-2rho cos(pi/(W+1)) >=(1-rho)^2.
The last inequality uses rho>=0. Squaring h cancels its crossblocks:
  h^2=diag(sin^2(p)I+B*B, sin^2(p)I+BB*).
Its eigenvalues come in +/- pairs for each singular value of B, so all
2W-2 bulk eigenvalues obey
  |e_bulk(p)| >= sqrt(sin^2(p)+(1-rho)^2)=1.             (1)
This is uniform in p, N and W; no fitted numerical gap is used.

If rho>=1, ||Bx||>=rho||x||-||S_W x||>=(rho-1)||x||.
Thus ALL eigenvalues, including the two remaining ones, satisfy |e|>=1
whenever |p|>=pi/2. Any |e|<1 therefore belongs to at most two branches
inside |p|<pi/2, where
  |e|>=|sin p|>=2|p|/pi.                                (2)

2. Full fixed-energy Fock exhaustion

Diagonalizing the ACTUAL sea-normal-ordered Hamiltonian gives exactly
  H_N=sum_(d_a>0) d_a n_a + sum_(d_a<0) |d_a| n_a^hole.
All summands are nonnegative; particle/hole cancellation cannot lower a
bulk excitation. Fix E<infinity and take N>2*pi*E. By (1), every vector
in 1_[0,E](H_N) belongs to the Fock space generated by the two small edge
modes at labels in the N-independent finite set
  J_E={j in Z: |j-1/4|<=pi*E/2}.                         (3)
Equation (3) follows from (2) after multiplying by N/(2*pi).
There are no other momentum windows and no bulk excitations in this cutoff.

For each fixed j, the existing normalized top/bottom quasimodes are
  q_top(y) proportional rho^(W-1-y) chi_plus,
  q_bottom(y) proportional rho^y chi_minus.
Their residuals at energies -sin(p_j), +sin(p_j) are <=rho^W.
Because p_j!=0 and the bulk has the uniform gap (1), the two exact small
eigenvectors can be phase-aligned with these quasimodes. Their errors tend
to zero, bounded for large N by a constant times rho^W/|sin(p_j)|.
Their excitation energies converge to |j-1/4| with error at most
  N/(2*pi) * (rho^W+|p_j|^3/6)
    =O(N^(1-2W))+O(N^-2).                              (4)
For fixed W>=2 this tends to zero. Distinct j are exactly orthogonal.

Map one limiting particle/hole oscillator per edge per j to the appropriate
EXACT microscopic eigenmode, sending the excitation vacuum to the filled
sea. Second quantization gives an exact isometry on every finite label
window. On J_E there are only finitely many occupation configurations, and
their energies converge uniformly by (4). Define H_infinity to have TWO
fermionic excitation oscillators of cost |j-1/4| for each integer j.
If E is not in its discrete spectrum, the finite set has a positive gap
from E. For all sufficiently large N the membership of every configuration
in the cutoff agrees. The second-quantized map is consequently UNITARY ONTO
the entire microscopic cutoff 1_[0,E](H_N), not merely into it.
At a spectral threshold, replace equality by the usual E-epsilon / E+epsilon
inclusions. Small N need not have the limiting cutoff rank.

This is Hilbert-space exhaustion in the fixed r=1 filled-sea Fock
representation, INCLUDING all particle/hole charge sectors, and fixed time
scaling. It does not prove Buchholz--Verch local scaling-algebra exhaustion,
all operator-product limits, or equivalence for W(N), new flux sectors,

3. What the result excludes about E8, and what it does not

It excludes seven additional independent low-energy complex channels on
the top edge of the unchanged source; there is exactly one per chirality.
Every finite-energy state of that representation is already in the edge
Fock completion. A missing E8 sector cannot be explained here by overlooked
orthogonal bounded-energy bulk states.

A same-stress conformal extension of the complex CAR edge has c=1; it
cannot become E8_1, whose Sugawara c=248/(1+30)=8. Conversely a compatible
unitary conformal subnet E8_1 inside a c=1 net would require a commuting
coset with c=-7. On a conformal vacuum this would give
||L^coset_-2 Omega||^2=c_coset/2=-7/2. The raw quarter-twisted sea must not
automatically be identified with that conformal vacuum. More generally a
unitary positive-energy highest-weight coset of weight h cannot have c<0:
||L_-n |h>||^2=2*n*h+c*n*(n^2-1)/12 is negative for large n.
These are STRESS-COMPATIBLE assertions. Existence of a c=1 subnet inside a
larger c=8 source in general is perfectly possible; section 2 is what rules
out extra finite-energy source states in this particular representation.

There is also a small, explicit spectral obstruction at the declared energy
normalization. Below E=1.1, H_infinity has rank 10: vacuum, four single
excitations of costs 1/4,1/4,3/4,3/4, and five allowed pairs. In contrast,
the E8 vacuum plus grade-one space has dimension 1+248=249. Asymptotically
isometric maps of these 249 states into the same source cutoff are impossible.
This witness assumes that the target grade-one energy is 1 in the source
units. It cannot be used against an arbitrarily changed energy calibration.
The raw QWZ quarter holonomy is retained, not silently replaced by NS L0.

Without stress, localization and energy compatibility, separable Hilbert
spaces can carry abstract E8 representations; no contradiction follows.
Nor does this proof exclude a nonconformal reorganization within edge Fock.
It excludes the proposed SAME-SOURCE, SAME-STRESS physical repair.

4. Why the fixed-point shortcut does not select the dynamics

v158_fixed_point_stable.py correctly counts a POINT-defect perturbation
integral d tau O using coupling dimension 1-h. It then calls the h=2
quartic irrelevant and the chiral c=8 fixed point isolated against all
interactions. For an EXTENDED 1+1-dimensional edge, the perturbation is
integral d tau dx O and the coupling dimension is 2-(h+hbar). A chiral
quartic of (h,hbar)=(2,0) is marginal by power counting. Power counting
alone says nothing about its beta function, but exactly marginal examples
exist: constant positive current-density couplings in a chiral boson theory.

This is not an objection to the correct threshold for a point defect, or
to classifications restricted in advance to Lorentz-scalar deformations.
It invalidates using that restricted count to derive unique extended-edge
kinetics BEFORE a common Lorentz/conformal geometry has been established.
The affected inference is GATE.METRIC.15's all-interaction rigidity and its
use as 'no drift v158' in the QGEO.KERNEL.01 reduction. The latter remains
open independently; this experiment does not alter its ledger marker.

Primary comparisons:

  positive non-universal velocity matrix and the E8 lattice example.

No claim that every chiral quartic is exactly marginal is needed here.
