2. Composition and amplitudes

For two operations A and B the next operation is BA. For all 3,600 pairs
of the 60 chosen representatives, exactly:

  3,168 products = c C with |c|^2=1,
    216 products = c C with |c|^2=2,
    216 products = 0,

where C is another original representative. The full complex c is used
in the check; it is not replaced by a classical transition probability.
Inductively every finite sequential word is zero or a scalar times one
of the same 60 rays. This is a finite projective semigroup, not a selection
of which word nature executes.

The zero cases come from rank-one composition:

  |s><t| |u><v| = <t|u> |s><v|.

They are exactly the six orthogonal intermediate state pairs, multiplied
by six choices for each free endpoint: 6*6*6=216. Equal intermediate rays
give the second 216 cases with |c|^2=2; other rank-one overlaps and all

These are amplitudes, not automatically probabilities. A unitary root is
