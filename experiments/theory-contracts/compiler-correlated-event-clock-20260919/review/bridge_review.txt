BRIDGE REVIEW: Bell10 versus quartic carrier, and the phase-count boundary
2026-09-19 — bounded read-only countercheck

Verdict
=======

1. The new Bell store Sym^2(C^4) is not linearly equivariantly identifiable
   with Lambda^2 W5 for the native source action.  The stated Pauli-kernel
   element g=Z tensor I is an exact character/rank witness.

2. The CP channels and the stopped channel are exactly blind to the scalar
   lift U -> tilde U=-i U.  N mod 8 can be retained as a classical event-count
   record, but Phi(z)^6 does not by itself create a coherent phase-sensitive
   observable.  Such an observable requires a coherent reference branch or a
   source-derived charged/off-diagonal sector on which the phase is relative.

I. Direct .09/.12 representation check
======================================

The .09 original basis formula in
outputs/komposition-und-auswahl/core.py constructs V subset (C^4)^tensor4 by
the five exponent patterns

  (4,0,0,0) and permutations,
  (2,2,0,0) paired in the three displayed ways,
  (1,1,1,1).

Take

  g = Z tensor I = diag(1,1,-1,-1),       det g = 1.

Every monomial occurring in all five V columns has an even total exponent in
the last two coordinates: 0, 2, or 4.  Therefore the native quartic action is

  R4(g)=g^tensor4|W5 = I5,
  Lambda^2 R4(g)=I10,
  tr(Lambda^2 R4(g))=10.

This was also evaluated directly from the exact .09 V matrix: g^tensor4 V=V
entry by entry and the recovered 5x5 matrix is I5.  The .09 checker separately
places all sixteen two-qubit Paulis, including g, in the actual quotient
kernel, so the witness belongs to the reconstructed source group.

On the Bell store, the action is the ordinary symmetric-square action.  Since
g has eigenvalues (1,1,-1,-1),

  tr Sym^2(g) = ((tr g)^2 + tr(g^2))/2 = (0+4)/2 = 2,

with eigenvalue multiplicities +1 x6 and -1 x4.  Thus

  tr Sym^2(g)=2 != 10=tr Lambda^2 R4(g).

No invertible/full-rank equivariant linear map between these two 10-dimensional
representations can exist.  More sharply, if T intertwines them, equivariance
at g gives Sym^2(g)T=T, so im(T) lies in the six-dimensional +1 eigenspace and
rank(T)<=6.  This witness does not claim Hom_G=0; it excludes the linear
equivariant identification required to equate the two carriers.

There is no determinant-normalisation ambiguity in this test.  The .12 formula
uses h=lambda g with lambda^4 det(g)=1.  Here det(g)=1 and the native element g
already lies in SU(4), so no -i correction is forced.  Choosing an additional
determinant winding would be a different lifted path, not the action tested
above.  Even multiplying g by a mu4 scalar would only multiply Sym^2(g) by
lambda^2 while leaving R4(g) unchanged; its trace would be +/-2, still not 10.

The Bell10-to-duad permutation dictionary remains valid as a ray/permutation
statement.  It must not be upgraded to a linear representation equivalence
with the separate Lambda^2 W5 carrier.

II. Exact CP-channel blindness
==============================

For every density operator rho,

  Ad_{-iU}(rho)=(-iU)rho(-iU)^dagger=U rho U^dagger=Ad_U(rho).

For a history h of N paired Bell events,

  tilde K_h=(-i)^N K_h,
  tilde K_h rho tilde K_h^dagger=K_h rho K_h^dagger.

Therefore C20, its stay/jump branch maps S and J, the stopped CPTP map

  Phi=J(I-S)^(-1),

and every count-resolved CP map are identical for U and tilde U=-iU.  Their
diamond distance is zero.  The fundamental C4 word lift carries
exp(-i*pi*N/4); its paired Bell action carries (-i)^N, but both are global
history phases after passage to neutral conjugation channels.

III. What Phi(z)^6 does and does not retain
===========================================

Let E_N be the CP, trace-nonincreasing map for histories in which six accepted
jumps occur at total primitive-event count N.  Then

  Phi(z)^6 = sum_{N>=6} z^N E_N.

At eighth roots omega^k, inverse discrete Fourier transform recovers

  E_[r] = sum_{N congruent r mod 8} E_N.

Hence an experiment that records the binary instrument outcomes can retain N
mod 8 classically.  This is sufficient to attach the bookkeeping value
exp(-i*pi*N/4) to every record.  The fourth-root reduction similarly tracks
the paired factor (-i)^N.

For non-real z, Phi(z)^6 is generally not a CP channel; it is the Fourier or
characteristic generating function of the count-resolved CP instrument.
Neither it nor the inverse-Fourier classical sectors contain off-diagonal
coherence between different N histories.  Multiplying each history Kraus
operator by its scalar lift phase leaves every E_N unchanged.  Thus the phase
cannot be inferred as a coherent observable from the stopped neutral channel.

To make the lift phase observable one must add source-derived structure that
turns it into a relative phase: for example a coherent control/reference path
that does not acquire the phase, or charged sectors H_q with off-diagonal
operators Hom(H_q,H_q') carrying different mu4 characters.  No such reference
or charged observable is supplied by the Bell10 CP instrument or by the
formal variable z.  The physical interpretation of the lift phase therefore
remains open even though its N mod 8 bookkeeping is exact.
