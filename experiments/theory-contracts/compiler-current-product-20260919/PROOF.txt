The new connection
==================

The actual product of the existing 24 currents connects the quartic 1+3
source of .19/.20 with the quartic 2+2 Bell source of .18. The result is
stronger than matching charge dimensions or a two-point Gram matrix:
the product is evaluated with the original Chevalley cocycle, compact
adjoint and level-one null relations. Its uniformly counted native response
has exactly the earlier ten-dimensional source image as its highest
eigenspace.

Write F20 for the unitary quartic 1+3 map, F10 for the isometry of the
quartic 2+2 map, and psi_l for the 60 original normalized Gaussian rays.
For every l there is a unit vector chi_l in the five-dimensional carrier
such that

  F20 conjugate(psi_l^3) = chi_l tensor psi_l.

The normalized current-product vectors r_l are, under the explicit
grade-two isometry constructed below,

  r_l = chi_l tensor psi_l^2 in bar5 tensor Sym2(4),
  F10^dagger r_l = conjugate(psi_l^2)/sqrt(2).

The exact full response operator is

  G = sum_(l=1..60) |r_l><r_l|
    = (3/4) I50 + (9/4) P10,
  P10 = F10 F10^dagger = (4G-3I50)/9.

Consequently its spectrum is 3 with multiplicity 10 and 3/4 with
multiplicity 40. The old Bell source is the full, uniquely determined
highest-response subspace of these products. No extra interaction or
adjustable coefficient has been added for this identity. G is a response
frame, NOT a Hamiltonian or a rule dynamically preparing that subspace.
The equal counting of 60 labels is inherited from the specified source
frame; it is not a derived distribution of physical event times.

==============================

The construction is inside the affine E8 level-one vacuum already used
in .06/.20. The raw P1 seam-to-current operator map remains an additional
physical obligation. We use the original roots from v1_e8_glue and the
Chevalley/Affine implementation from v498, transported through the pinned
integral-triality basis. In this convention

  [e_alpha,e_-alpha] = -H_alpha,
  kappa(e_alpha,e_-alpha) = -1,
  e_alpha^dagger = -e_-alpha.

Let f_a be the four half-spin weights with odd minus count in the last
three coordinates. The roots of C_a (W4) and X_ia (W20) have doubled
coordinates

  c_a = (-1,-1,-1,-1,-1; f_a),
  x_ia = (+1,...,-1 at i,...,+1; f_a).

Their Chevalley phases are fixed by actual SU5 and A3 root transport
from C_0=X_00=+e, then checked on all matrix units. This gives
W4 = 1 tensor 4 and W20 = bar5 tensor 4; the bar5 is equivalently
Lambda4(C5). The use of a real orthogonal W5 frame for the finite

The actual mixed bracket is

  [C_a,X_ib] = Y_i,(a wedge b),

with zero at a=b, the exterior sign on reversal and 30 distinct D8 root
outputs. Adjoint currents supply the conjugate sector. This is a source
operator dictionary, not an assignment made from equal dimensions.

2. Level-one composition and the grade-two product
=================================================

For compact-adjoint currents the original affine relation gives

 <0|J_1(a^dagger) J_1(b^dagger) J_-1(c) J_-1(d)|0>
   = kappa(b^dagger,c) kappa(a^dagger,d)
   + kappa(b^dagger,d) kappa(a^dagger,c)
   + kappa(a^dagger, [[b^dagger,c],d]).

The last term cannot be removed. For one root the first two terms sum
to 2 while the last is -2: the squared creation mode is null at level
one. A commuting bracket alone does not imply a Gaussian four-point law.
For example C_0 and C_1 commute but their distinct roots have inner
product +1, and their grade-two word has zero norm. In contrast the
X_01,C_0 test gives 2=1+0+1; the original Affine class independently
reproduces it. A root-orthogonal commuting control gives 1=1+0+0.

Define a symmetric product map T from bar5 tensor Sym2(4) by

 T(i;aa) = J_-1(X_ia) J_-1(C_a) Omega,
 T(i;ab) = [J_-1(X_ia)J_-1(C_b)
             +J_-1(X_ib)J_-1(C_a)] Omega/sqrt(2), a<b,

where the input symmetric off-diagonal basis is normalized. The zero-mode
Leibniz identity and the already checked X,C actions make T equivariant
for SU5 x A3. The highest-weight product has norm one: its two roots
are orthogonal and their sum has norm squared four. Irreducibility and
the compact invariant inner product therefore give T^dagger T=I50.
The finite checker also evaluates the product Gram directly, including
the level-one quotient. Thus the product realizes an actual grade-two
bar5 tensor 10, with normalization and action fixed by the source.

It is the same type of sector used in .16/.18, inside 10_D5 tensor 10_A3.
The other D5 five-dimensional component is not included in this T.
Within the fixed glue-(2,2), D5-ground/A3-descendant corner this component
occurs once. The 20 diagonal-family weights have lattice norm squared
four; the 30 off-diagonal weights have lattice norm squared two but
remain affine grade two. These latter states are not grade-one currents.
Adjoints give 5 tensor bar10, not the other 5 tensor 10 copy of .18.
An overall phase of the irreducible intertwiner is conventional; the
relative phases used in the products and Bell conversion are fixed.
The identification is an explicit representation/intertwiner statement;
it does not declare a new species or a 50-dimensional physical space.

For all 60 rays the exact quartic contraction factorizes:

  w20_l = chi_l tensor psi_l,
  w4_l = psi_l.

Consequently [w20_l,w4_l]=0 by psi_l wedge psi_l=0. Their individual
mode squares annihilate the vacuum. In the completed direction

  w_(l,s) = sqrt(5/6) w20_l + s sqrt(1/6) w4_l,

the cross term survives:

  J_-1(w_(l,s))^2 Omega
    = s (sqrt(5)/3) J_-1(w20_l)J_-1(w4_l) Omega
    = s (sqrt(5)/3) T r_l,

  ||J_-1(w_(l,s))^2 Omega||^2 = 5/9.

This is an insertion amplitude in the specified affine vacuum, not an
event probability or a new Pauli-statistics derivation. Both individual
old squares have norm zero; the additional source component makes this
particular two-current response nonzero. The sign s remains in the
amplitude even though it cancels in the response projector.

3. The earlier Bell source emerges as a response subspace
=======================================================

The five original quartic tensors V_A have squared norms
n_A=(4,12,12,12,24). Use orthonormal symmetric-power bases throughout.
The two maps used previously are

  (F20)_(A,a),j = 2 (V_A)_(a;j)/sqrt(n_A),
  (F10)_(A,b),c = sqrt(2/n_A) (K_A)_(b,c),

where K_A is the 2+2 splitting into Sym2(4) tensor Sym2(4).
They satisfy F20^dagger F20=I20 and F10^dagger F10=I10.

No phase information is dropped when comparing them: the checker gives
the exact unitary U between the symmetric monomial basis and the original
Pauli Bell basis, and verifies

  F_Bell conjugate(U) = (I5 tensor U) F10.

For every original ray, direct exact contraction proves

  F10^dagger r_l = conjugate(v_l)/sqrt(2),
  v_l = symmetric coordinates of psi_l tensor psi_l.

Hence

  P10 r_l = F10 conjugate(v_l)/sqrt(2),
  ||P10 r_l||^2 = 1/2.

The sum of all 60 product projectors is checked entry by entry and equals
G=(3/4)I50+(9/4)P10. This is a finite exact identity, not a fit of numerical
eigenvalues. It both identifies the ten-dimensional subspace from the
product response and recovers the old .18 readout vectors after normalized
projection. A label-dependent projection probability has not been hidden:
the value is 1/2 for every normalized product input r_l.
For the unnormalized actual squared current the Bell-projected norm
squared is therefore (5/9)(1/2)=5/18. Applying the explicitly specified
projective filter rho -> P10 rho P10 to each labeled normalized product
state gives one half of the old Bell source preparation map; conditioned
on success its output equals the old ray exactly. This equality is for
these labeled preparations and this filter, not for every possible
instrument having the same one-step effects.

The normalized projected vectors u_l=F10 conjugate(v_l) satisfy

  sum_l |u_l><u_l|=6 P10.

Thus the old effects |u_l><u_l|/6 are recovered on this space. The states
in the complementary 40-dimensional response band have not disappeared;
discarding them would be an additional conditioning step. The response
spectral gap is 9/4, dimensionless in the normalized frame convention.
It is not an energy gap. G/60 is the corresponding uniformly averaged

For the unnormalized actual squared modes q_(l,s)=J_-1(w_(l,s))^2 Omega,
their response on im(T) is

It has the same distinguished ten-dimensional image, with response

4. What becomes operationally determined
========================================

The source24 effects of .20 admit an actual current realization. On the
fixed one-current grade i_n(W24), i_n(a)=J_-n(a)Omega/sqrt(n), put

  K_x = P_Omega J_n(w_x^dagger) P_(n,24)/sqrt(5n).

Then K_x i_n(b)=<w_x,b>Omega/sqrt(5), and the 120 effects K_x^dagger K_x
sum to the identity on this domain. These absorption maps are fixed up
to outcome phase if one imposes one Kraus operator and vacuum output.
Forgetting the sign recovers the old source20 absorption instrument for
every input. These are compressed positive-frequency mode operators;
no spacetime-local detector implementation follows merely from them.

The same effects also have a square-root instrument that leaves the
excitation in the one-current grade. The difference is exact:

  absorption: a second positive-mode event has probability zero before
              any creation or refresh;
  square root: p(y|x)=|<w_y,w_x>|^2/5, total 1, p(x|x)=1/5.

The product bridge proves compatibility of earlier readout data with
actual source composition. It does not select either detector process,
prepare its incident state, schedule events, or derive the .13 C60
reflection/record/refresh protocol. Non-Gaussian current insertions also
do not by themselves prove an interacting 3+1-dimensional scattering
theory; they are already present in affine current theory.

5. Origin test retained at the physical boundary
===============================================

All 24 source currents are half-roots in Ramond class (3,3) in the
marked D5+A3 convention. Their adjoints are in (1,1); none is an NS
D8 fermion bilinear. The two candidate glues

  L_a={(0,0),(1,1),(2,2),(3,3)},
  L_b={(0,0),(1,3),(2,2),(3,1)}

This is not a claim that every marked P1/P2 datum is identical: the

  (D5)_1 x (A3)_1 --index2--> D8_1 --index2 spinor--> E8_1.

Conditional on a sourced D8 net and the selected local spinor sector,

not supply eight chiral channels; .07 already established this. The newer
bounded localized-spinor construction supplies sector responses only
conditional on eight CAR copies and the even subnet. Neither is silently
promoted to a P1 raw-seam realization here.

The next source test must carry both marked charges: a localized source
field with nonzero vacuum response in (3,3), zero response in the competing
(3,1), and its actual adjoint, while preserving the source time and local
current products. A single clock character or another NS two-point test

QGEO.KERNEL.01 remains logically before this test.
