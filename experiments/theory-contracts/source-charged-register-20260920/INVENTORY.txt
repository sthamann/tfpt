What v1033 actually supplies
-----------------------------
The source defines the four-sector one-body Hamiltonian

  H_R = I tensor h_base + Z tensor h_forward
        + Z^dagger tensor h_forward^dagger,
  Z = diag(1,i,-1,-i),

equivalently `sum_r |r><r| tensor h_r`, with
`h_r = h_base + i^r a + i^(-r) a^dagger`. This is the exact source form in
`v1033_charged_disorder.py:298-321`, also recorded in
`clock-interaction-provenance/README.md:115-126`.

The source's explicit disorder insertion is not the bare register shift.
At `v1033_charged_disorder.py:379-417` it constructs

  full_disorder = kron(shift, u),

where `shift = roll(I_4, 1, axis=0)` and `u` is the sharp matter phase string
from `sharp_arc_phase`. The commutator with the direct-sum source Hamiltonian
is reduced to the far-endpoint residual. Thus the supplied operator is the
one-body/register product `S tensor u`; on Fock space its number-preserving
lift would be `S tensor Gamma(u)`. It is not the field operator `S tensor c_i`.

The v1033 symbolic section does verify a finite Weyl relation and that the
disorder string is even under canonical matter parity
(`v1033_charged_disorder.py:191-229`). That parity statement concerns the
existing phase-string insertion; it does not derive a full-CAR charged field.

Status of the proposed J_s, C, and F_i construction
----------------------------------------------------
For the conditional tensor-product lift `C4 tensor Fock(V)`, let

  F_i = S tensor c_i,
  C = Z tensor i^N,
  J_s |psi_N> = |s-N mod 4> tensor |psi_N>.

The algebraic claims in the proposal are internally consistent under this
explicit lift:

* Since S is unitary, the F_i obey the full CAR:
  `{F_i,F_j}=0` and `{F_i,F_j^dagger}=delta_ij`.
* With `Z S Z^dagger = i S` and
  `i^N c_i i^(-N) = -i c_i`, C commutes with every F_i.
* H_R preserves N and is diagonal in the register basis, so C commutes with
  H_R as well.
* The encoded subspace selected by J_s is stable under F_i and F_i^dagger:
  lowering N shifts the register by +1 and raising N shifts it by -1.
* Direct compression gives

    J_s^dagger H_R J_s
      = dGamma(h_{s-N mod 4})
      = dGamma(b + i^(s-N) a + i^(-s+N) a^dagger).

  The apparent higher dependence on N is therefore a number-dependent
  boundary holonomy of the encoding, while each fixed-N block remains free.

This exact algebraic encoding was not found as a derivation in the inspected
contracts or in the targeted graph/text search. It is consequently a new
conditional representation choice, not an already documented source result.
It also does not change H_R: J_s changes the embedding of the matter Fock
space into register tensor Fock space.

Why this does not identify the original disorder field
-------------------------------------------------------
The original `full_disorder` contains `Gamma(u)`, which acts on one-body
orbitals and has support along the sharp string. Bare S alone changes only
the register label. Replacing `S tensor Gamma(u)` by `S tensor c_i` changes
both particle-number degree and source support. The source records the former
as an even, number-preserving disorder observable; it does not supply the
latter charged field.

The endpoint-product question is therefore still open at the source level:
one must specify how the matter string, its adjoint, and the register shift
compose on the same Fock domain. The prior source-probe result explicitly
warns that the microscopic `S_A=D_A^2` insertion is neutral under occupation
charges (`source-probe-carry-20260920/PROOF.txt:124-137`) and that scalar or
unscaled limits do not turn it into a charged vertex. A sector-dependent
charge map or nonmultiplicative field scaling is listed there only as a
further assumption.
