Question and changed premise
============================
The static-register contract classified a product preparation followed by
an inclusive register trace. It explicitly left correlated charged-sector
encodings open. We now construct one on the SAME assumed Fock lift, with
the SAME Hamiltonian. The test is full CAR, charge, isometry and time
transport, followed by an independent locality test. No additional
Hamiltonian, auxiliary mode, parameter fit or continuum phase is introduced.

Source and first extra choice
=============================
The original v1033_charged_disorder.py defines a one-particle register
Hamiltonian and an endpoint disorder operator. Let z=i, Pi_r=|r><r|,
Z|r>=z^r|r>, S|r>=|r+1 mod4>. Its source blocks have the form

 h_r=b+z^r a+z^(-r) a^dagger,   b=b^dagger.

Here a is the original forward seam hopping; no coefficient is changed.
As in the preceding contract, the common lift

 H_R=sum_r Pi_r tensor dGamma(h_r)
 on C4 tensor Fock(V)

is CONDITIONAL. The original one-particle computation does not select this
lift, its preparation or the physical observable algebra. This is distinct
from Fock(C4 tensor V). N denotes total matter occupation; a fixed filled-
sea subtraction changes neither [N,c_j]=-c_j nor the proof and only relabels
the central sector modulo four. No filled sea or vacuum is selected here.

1. Full charged CAR without discarding the register
==================================================
Define

 F_j=S tensor c_j,   C=Z tensor z^N.

Since S is unitary, {F_j,F_k}=0 and {F_j,F_k^dagger}=delta_jk I. Also
[N,F_j]=-F_j and F_j is odd under the original matter parity. Since
ZS=zSZ and z^N c_j=z^(-1)c_j z^N, [C,F_j]=[C,F_j^dagger]=0.
Moreover [C,H_R]=0, because every source block preserves N.

For each s in Z4 define the isometry on all occupation sectors by

 J_s |psi_N> = |s-N mod4> tensor |psi_N>.

Its image is exactly the C=z^s eigenspace. The four images are orthogonal
and sum to the full source Hilbert space. Direct calculation gives

 F_j J_s=J_s c_j,    F_j^dagger J_s=J_s c_j^dagger,
 H_R J_s=J_s H_s,
 H_s=B+z^(s-N) A+z^(-s+N) A^dagger,
 B=dGamma(b), A=dGamma(a).                              (1)

The phases commute with A because [N,A]=0. Equation (1) is a genuine
same-time intertwiner, not just an equality of compressed expectation
values. It implies exp(-it H_R)J_s=J_s exp(-it H_s) for every real time.
All finite-time charged products and adjoints are transported. The sum
over the four J_s is a unitary identification of the full source with four
copies of this charged Fock representation.

No unique s is selected by the dynamics. Keeping all four copies loses
nothing; choosing one representation as a physical sector is extra state/
observable input. The C4 register has not become four physical families.
The word central refers to the constructed CAR algebra, not to the entire
original source algebra: the original disorder D changes s, as below.

2. Connection to the actual endpoint disorder, including its phase factor
========================================================================
The original source uses the sharp arc

 u=diag(z^q_j), q_j in {0,1}, u^4=I,
 D=S tensor Gamma(u)

Here q_j labels whether orbital j belongs to the phase arc; it is not an
electric charge assignment. The charged-field statement uses [N,F_j]=-F_j.

after the conditional Fock lift. D preserves matter number and is even.
It is not by itself a charged fermion. Simply setting d_j=D(I tensor c_j)
fails CAR for sites on opposite sides of the arc. In fact

 {d_j,d_k}=(z^q_j-z^q_k) S^2 tensor Gamma(u)^2 c_j c_k.   (2)

This is nonzero for q_j=1,q_k=0 and j!=k. Equal dimensions or multiplying
an endpoint by an odd field does not establish the desired field algebra.

All ingredients for an exact canonical completion are already in the
declared source algebra. Define the unitary

 T=sum_r Pi_r tensor Gamma(u)^r.

Then

 F_j^u=T F_j T^dagger
      =S Z^(-q_j) tensor Gamma(u)c_j
      =D (Z^(-q_j) tensor c_j).                        (3)

The factor Z^(-q_j) is forced by this common conjugation, not fitted to a
correlation. The r=3 to r=0 step is included because Gamma(u)^4=I.
Conjugation proves both CAR relations on the full space, including mixed
adjoints. T commutes with C, N and matter parity.

Set J_s^u=TJ_s. Then F_j^u J_s^u=J_s^u c_j and the adjoint relation holds.
The SAME original H_R gives

 H_R J_s^u=J_s^u H_s^u,
 H_s^u restricted to N = dGamma(u^(-r)h_r u^r),
 r=s-N mod4.                                          (4)

Thus (3) supplies an exact charged field extension of the supplied
disorder operator, with charge, normalization and time carried together.
It does not identify these fields with the 64 native Spin10 x SU4 modes
or the continuum E8 spinor fields. Those group dictionaries are different
and have separate proved obstructions.

The original disorder itself is retained by the four-sector dictionary:

 D J_s^u = J_(s+1 mod4)^u,       C D = z D C.          (4a)

Indeed on the N block D changes |r> Gamma(u)^r|psi_N> to
|r+1> Gamma(u)^(r+1)|psi_N>; Gamma(u)^4=I handles the wrap.
C is conserved by H_R and central relative to the F_j^u, but it is NOT
central relative to D. The s labels are therefore not superselection
sectors of the full original observable algebra containing D. Retaining
only one s would lose this supplied observable, even though H_R preserves
that state subspace. It is not justified as a gauge reduction here.

Its unchanged time response is explicitly

 [H_R,D]J_s^u = J_(s+1)^u (H_(s+1)^u-H_s^u).         (4b)

Using (5), the difference in (4b) is
(z-1) z^(s-N) A' + (z^(-1)-1) z^(-s+N) A'^dagger.
This recovers the original endpoint response together with the charged
field dictionary; D was neither discarded nor inserted as a new term in H.

3. What energy (4) actually induces
==================================
For the source sharp arc from x=0 through its stated endpoint, conjugation
u^(-r)h_r u^r cancels the phase on the old closing seam. The phase appears
instead on the forward bond leaving that endpoint. All original other
hoppings and onsite terms remain unchanged. Write that exact matrix as

 h'_r=b'+z^r a'+z^(-r)a'^dagger.

Then

 H_s^u=B'+z^(s-N)A'+z^(-s+N)A'^dagger.                 (5)

Here the phase depends on TOTAL matter number. On every fixed N sector,
(5) is still the exterior-power evolution of one fixed one-particle
Hamiltonian h'_(s-N). In particular every N-particle Slater determinant
stays a Slater determinant. No collision-generated bound state or local
many-particle force follows from (5).

Nevertheless (5) is generally not one quadratic Hamiltonian on the FULL
CAR space across all occupations. Vacuum and N=1 fix a putative quadratic
number-preserving operator uniquely as dGamma(h'_(s-1)); the actual N=2
block uses h'_(s-2), which differs. Pairing terms cannot fix this mismatch
while preserving N as the actual source does.

For an offdiagonal cut hop a'_(ab)c_a^dagger c_b one may display the exact
finite normal-ordered occupation expansion:

 z^(s-N)c_a^dagger c_b
 = z^(s-1)c_a^dagger c_b
   product_(j!=a,b)[1+(z^(-1)-1)n_j].                  (6)

The N=1 coefficient is z^(s-1); a spectator changes it to z^(s-2).
Thus four-fermion and higher terms really occur in these coordinates.
Their coefficients are fixed by the original holonomy and the chosen
encoding; they are not an independently added interaction. This is a
number-dependent boundary condition, not evidence of an independent
massive mediator or a newly derived bulk scattering theory.

The finite checker uses the actual source with nx=3, ny=1, arc endpoint0.
For s=0, the forward cut coefficient a'_(2,0)=-1/2 gives matrix elements

 <2|H_0^u|0>=i/2,
 <2,4|H_0^u|0,4>=1/2.

Here |0> in the first expression means the ONE-PARTICLE orbital labelled0,
not the vacuum. Ordered two-particle states use c_a^dagger c_b^dagger with
a<b. The difference (1-i)/2 is a same-source, exact nonquadratic witness.
Neither the test size nor s=0 is claimed as a selected physical sector.

4. The charged locality test
===========================
Let H_cut=z^(s-N)A'+z^(-s+N)A'^dagger. For any mode c_j outside the cut,
[A',c_j]=[A'^dagger,c_j]=0. Directly from the number commutator,

 [H_cut,c_j]
  =(z-1)c_j z^(s-N)A'
   +(z^(-1)-1)c_j z^(-s+N)A'^dagger.                  (7)

This expression generally contains the cut hopping although j is outside
its support. A further commutator with the occupation of a cut endpoint
is nonzero. The original local b' contribution cannot cancel this at
arbitrarily separated j and endpoint, because its range is fixed. The
coefficient in (7) has no suppression by their spatial distance.

The small-ring check isolates H_cut; it does NOT call the three-site ring
a test of large spatial separation. It verifies the nonzero double
commutator and, as a distinguishing control,

 [H_cut,c_j^dagger c_k]=0

when both modes are outside the cut. This control follows for every such
neutral bilinear because it commutes with N, A' and A'^dagger. Equation
(7) proves the general support statement without a size extrapolation.

The source of the nonlocality is visible: F_j^u in (3) itself contains the
global register and arc string. The J_s^u dictionary preserves algebra
and time, but does NOT preserve the assignment of ordinary local charged
CAR algebras to each physical site. Charge-neutral and charged locality
tests therefore give different answers. No violation of locality by the
original source net is inferred from a nonlocal change of variables.

There is also a positive locality statement. Let O_X and O_Y be bounded
local NUMBER-NEUTRAL observables, so both commute with N. On each N sector
their evolution is exactly that of dGamma(h'_(s-N)). Their full commutator
is block diagonal in N and its norm is the maximum of those block norms.
Any common finite-range propagation bound for the four original local
Hamiltonians therefore applies to the neutral observables under H_s^u.
For the finite-degree QWZ lattice the four link phases have equal modulus;
the usual Lieb-Robinson constants can be chosen uniformly in r, N and
system size. This is inheritance of the standard local bound, not a
new derivation of relativistic light speed. It holds for neutral local
polynomials, not merely the single bilinear checked in the example.
Precisely, this is a bound on neutral-observable commutators. Across all
N sectors, the evolved expression can still contain the central global
factors z^N. A commutator bound alone does not establish norm approximation
by ordinary finite-support neutral polynomials without those factors, or
a thermodynamic observable-net construction.

Nonlocal charged fields are not by themselves a physical contradiction:
in gauge theories charged operators may need strings. What fails here
is the stronger identification with the ordinary site-local charged CAR
net. The single conserved global C is not a local Gauss constraint, and
this construction has not supplied local gauge fields or an interacting
observable net beyond the fixed-number free sectors. Neutral locality
does not by itself fill that remaining physical origin requirement.

4a. A bulk limit that can actually be decided
============================================
There is a stronger consequence for the original finite-range cylinders
of FIXED bounded width, as the circumference grows. Put K_r=dGamma(h'_r)
and choose the untwisted K_0 as reference. W_r=K_r-K_0 is supported on the
marked cut Y, with a norm bounded independently of the circumference.
For every number-neutral local O_X, the exact block formula implies

 ||tau_t^(H_s^u)(O_X)-tau_t^(K_0)(O_X)||
 <= max_r ||tau_t^(K_r)(O_X)-tau_t^(K_0)(O_X)||.       (8)

This is an operator-norm bound on the finite Fock space, not just a
statewise expectation comparison: both sides on the left are block
diagonal in N and each block is a restriction of one right-hand term.
The Duhamel identity and a uniform finite-range bound give

 ||tau_t^(K_r)(O_X)-tau_t^(K_0)(O_X)||
 <= integral_0^|t| ||[W_r,tau_u^(K_0)(O_X)]|| du
 <= C_X (exp(v |t|)-1) exp(-a dist(X,Y)),              (9)

with positive constants independent of circumference and N. v here is the
rate in the exponential estimate; no identification with light speed is
made. Fixed width bounds the number and norm of cut terms. Increasing
width would instead require the corresponding summable boundary estimate.

Consequently, for fixed observation times and regions whose distance from
the cut tends to infinity, the neutral local dynamics converges in norm
to that of the UNTWISTED FREE SOURCE. In this precise bulk limit the
number-dependent boundary phase cannot supply a new local interaction.
This does not assert that arbitrary initial states become Gaussian:
pre-existing state correlations and winding/cut observables are separate.
It also says nothing about time scales growing enough to reach the cut,
or about changing the original Hamiltonian or observable dictionary.

Equations (8)-(9) are an analytic all-size argument under stated locality
assumptions. The nx=3 certificate is not presented as a numerical test of
this limit. This conclusion is a useful stopping criterion for the proposed
interaction mechanism: enlarging the same free source while moving its
cut away cannot turn the encoded boundary phase into a local bulk force.
