================================================

The calculation uses Gaussian rationals and nx=3, ny=1, with six CAR modes,
64 matter states and 256 register-matter states. AST-selected original
v1033 Hamiltonian definitions are executed and compared with the exact
reconstruction, rather than merely hashing a similarly shaped helper model.

- matter number charge and original fermion parity;
- controlled unitary, original D and C Weyl relation;

The certificate counts dynamically registered mathematical conditions;
low-level matrix shape checks and source/manifest guards are additional.
Category names group assertions for navigation, not independent discoveries.
All failures raise exceptions and remain active with Python -OO.

the actual source dressing, and the neutral propagation argument. The
parent's subsequent original-D transport formula and the fixed-width bulk
Duhamel corollary are written out in PROOF.txt. The original-D formula is
also checked finitely. The bulk corollary is an analytic argument; it is
not a numerical inference from this small periodic lattice.

The inventory was made before the actual D dressing was completed. Its
then-open algebraic subproblem is addressed by this contract, while its
physical-origin caveats remain. Neither inventory nor internal review is
an external peer review or proof-assistant formalization.

Final installed replays use the neighboring source_manifest.json. Normal
and optimized outputs are stored as certificate.json and
certificate.optimized.json and must be byte-identical. No prior ledger,
physical gate, empirical scorecard, or verification module is modified.
