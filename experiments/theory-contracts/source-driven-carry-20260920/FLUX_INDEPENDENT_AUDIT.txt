INDEPENDENT AUDIT — FLUX_PROOF_REVIEW
Date: 2026-09-20

Scope and verdict
-----------------
I checked the analytic review against the actual 16x16 W=8 block and the
source-edge-charge-transport proof/checker. I found no counterexample to the
conditional spectral-flow conclusion. The result is valid under the stated
external uniform Peierls drive and unscaled hopping-time convention. Two
proof obligations should nevertheless be made explicit before calling it a
fully closed analytic certificate: (i) the global construction of the
projectorwise commutator solver X, including its patching through all possible
bulk degeneracies, and (ii) the exact target-variance lemma for the rank-2
edge defect. Both are repairable; neither changes the first open physical
arrow (source selection of the drive/intersector field).

1. Actual 16x16 block and zero crossing
-----------------------------------------
The checked source block is the exact SX-basis matrix

    h(p) = [[-sin(p) I, D(rho)], [D(rho)*, sin(p) I]],
    D(rho)=rho I-S, rho=1-cos(p), W=8.

The characteristic identity from the source proof is det D=rho^8. Thus the
only zero of the one-particle spectrum is p=0 modulo 2pi. At rho=0 the
massive singular values are seven copies of one and one singular value is
zero, so the negative massive cluster is an isolated rank-7 projector. This
handles internal degeneracies correctly: no basis inside the rank-7 cluster
is needed, and all commutator equations only use P-to-(1-P) matrix elements.

The only potentially small cross-P gap is the top/bottom pair. The source
residual gives the signed edge coupling d(rho)=rho^8 a(rho), with a(0)>0;
therefore d(p)=O(p^16). The geometrically marked edge projector has mixing
theta(p)=1/2 atan(d(p)/sin p)=O(p^15), theta'(p)=O(p^14). Consequently the
singular pair in X is O(p^13), and its p derivative is O(p^12). This is the
right mechanism for crossing the gapless point without inventing a minimum
gap.

Adversarial point: the review says that A,B,D are finite on the compact
momentum circle, but it does not spell out a finite patching argument. The
needed construction is: use the marked edge chart only on a fixed
neighbourhood of p=0, use the rank-8 negative spectral projector on the
complement, and use rank-projector resolvent calculus on every compact region
where the P/(1-P) gap is positive. Internal massive degeneracies then remain
irrelevant. With this patching stated, the claimed N-independent A,B,D follow.
Without it, “compact circle” alone is a proof shortcut rather than a complete
uniform-constant derivation.

2. Kato estimate and N,T powers
-------------------------------
With v=dp/ds=-2pi/N, X_s=v X_p and d_s X_s=v^2 d_p X_p. The displayed
one-integration-by-parts bound gives per block

    e_N <= 8pi A/(N T_N) + 8pi^2(B+D A)/(N^2 T_N).

Rank at most 16 gives trace norm at most 16 e_N per block; orthogonal block
addition gives Hilbert-Schmidt norm at most 4 sqrt(N) e_N globally. Hence

    ||Gamma-P_drive||_1 = O(T_N^-1),
    ||Gamma-P_drive||_2 = O((T_N sqrt(N))^-1),

and T_N=sqrt(N) yields O(N^-1/2) and O(N^-1), respectively. The powers and
the trace-rate arithmetic are consistent. A usual bulk degeneracy does not
spoil this because the rank-7 cluster is treated as one projector; only
cross-cluster denominators occur.

The estimate is conditional on the stated Kato Hamiltonian and on the
externally prescribed f(s). It does not prove a physical adiabatic theorem
for a self-selected source.

3. +1/2 and +1 full-sea charge
-------------------------------
The source-edge proof supplies the static finite-sea identity

    Tr[(P_(3/4)-P_(1/4)) T_cut] = 1/2 + O(N^-1)

with all seven massive occupied modes retained. The drive estimate transfers
this to the actually evolved covariance with an additional O(N^-1/2) trace
error. Thus the first half-transport is +1/2+O(N^-1/2).

For the full cycle, p_j(5/4)=p_(j-1)(1/4). Since T_cut is circumference
translation-invariant, the static sea contribution cancels exactly at finite
N. The only defect is the tracked j=1 top-particle/bottom-hole pair at the
end point. The source edge profile gives top weight 1-O(N^-16) and bottom
weight O(N^-16) for cut=4, so the static defect is 1+O(N^-16), and the drive
adds O(N^-1/2). The claimed +1 limiting full-sea charge is therefore
correct under the external drive. Total charge remains exactly fixed because
the covariance rank remains 8N.

4. Variance Lipschitz lemma
---------------------------
For Hermitian contractions 0<=A<=I and positive contractions C,P, define

    Var_C(A)=Tr(C A^2) - (Tr(C A))^2
             =Tr(C A (I-C) A).

Writing x=Tr(CA), y=Tr(PA),

    |Tr((C-P)A^2)| <= ||C-P||_1,
    |x^2-y^2| <= |x-y| |x+y| <= 2||C-P||_1.

Therefore

    |Var_C(A)-Var_P(A)| <= 3 ||C-P||_1.

This is valid for the Gaussian filtered regional operator because it is a
convex time average of the projection T_cut, hence a positive contraction.
It applies directly with C=Gamma_N(1) and P=P_drive,N(1), giving a vanishing
variance correction O(N^-1/2). The same argument applies at the half point.

5. Rank-2 edge target variance
------------------------------
Let P_f be the final negative-sea projector, u the tracked positive top edge
mode and v the tracked negative bottom edge mode, and

    P_* = P_f + |u><u| - |v><v|.

For the static regional projection T_cut, the edge estimates imply

    ||(I-T_cut)u||^2 = 1-<u,T_cut u> = O(N^-16),
    ||T_cut v||^2 = <v,T_cut v> = O(N^-16).

Since the filtered operator A_f is the Gaussian dephasing of T_cut in the
final energy basis, each eigenmode variance is no larger than the static
one: for an eigenvector w,

    Var_w(A_f)=sum_z |<z,T_cut w>|^2 exp(-(E_z-E_w)^2/delta^2)
                - <w,T_cut w>^2
              <= Var_w(T_cut).

Decomposing the Slater variance into occupied-to-unoccupied matrix elements,
the rank-2 replacement changes the already controlled sea variance only by
the two edge rows/columns above; removal of the v-row can only remove a
nonnegative contribution. Thus

    Var_{P_*}(A_f) <= Var_{P_f}(A_f) + O(N^-16).

This is the precise meaning of “static bound + O(N^-16)”: it is a filtered
target bound with the existing full-sea filtered variance as the base. The
unfiltered full-sea variance is instead O(N) in the source checker and must
not be called O(N^-16). Finally,

    Var_{Gamma_N(1)}(A_f)
      <= Var_{P_*}(A_f) + 3||Gamma_N(1)-P_*||_1

vanishes, because the proof gives ||Gamma-P_drive||_1=O(N^-1/2) and the
target defect is the same P_drive projector. The rank-2 edge contribution is
therefore O(N^-16) on top of the filtered sea bound, not a replacement for
the sea estimate.

6. Remaining boundary
----------------------
The proof closes a source-native, globally driven spectral-flow statement.
It does not derive the Peierls path, its speed, or the common physical time
from P1/P2. It also does not construct a local intersector field, its adjoint,
or the microscopic C/J and carry realization. The first open arrow remains
the source-derived local charged operator; the spectral-flow result is a
conditional input to that construction.
