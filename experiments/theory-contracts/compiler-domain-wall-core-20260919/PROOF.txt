0. Main result and scope
========================

There is a concrete correction of the proposed wall in the unchanged model.
The incomplete Omega/s wall leaves a free matter register. Binding that
register to a neighbouring source with the already defined Phi packet yields
orthonormal wall-core trial vectors. The native overlap energy connects
neighbouring cores with the exact matrix element -mu/8. For m=2r sources,

  P_core H P_core = E_core I_m - (mu/8) A_path_m,
  E_core = (3r-1)kappa/4 + 3(r+1)J/4 + mu(m-1).               (0.1)

The same family is not invariant for m>=4: its H_0 leakage is already of
order kappa. Thus (0.1) is a genuine operator compression, not a controlled
low-energy effective Hamiltonian. Its homogeneous formal continuation has
a quadratic band minimum. The two-component linear crossing obtained by
doubling its unit cell lies in the middle of that band.

A separate rigorous comparison rules out the entire pair of bare alternating
source-representation sectors at m=4 as the full ground sector at weak J.
At J=mu=kappa/200 their combined weight in ANY full ground vector is at most
0.0608648. This conclusion does not rely on the user's reported but unreplayed
full two-/three-source ground theorems.

Nothing here changes the original status of the source model: the spatial
chain, orthogonal source register and Hamiltonian are an explicitly chosen
continuation. Their derivation from P1/P2, and the full physical theory, remain
separate tasks. The present calculation addresses the requested wall route.

1. Fixed source, packets, and phase conventions
================================================

Use the same 240 full roots, normalized complex vectors psi_alpha in C^4,
and 60 reflections r_l=I-2|psi_l><psi_l| as in .17/.23/.24. On an open
m-edge chain with m+1 matter registers,

  H = H_0 + J sum_e H_E,e + mu sum_e V_e,e+1,
  H_0 = kappa sum_e L_e,
  L_e = I-(1/60)sum_l R_l^(e) tensor r_l tensor r_l,
  H_E,e = sum_alpha |alpha><alpha| tensor (I-r_alpha tensor r_alpha),
  V(alpha,beta) = 1-Re<psi_alpha|psi_beta>.

All these local contributions are positive; ||H_E,e||<=2 and ||V||<=2.
Native identities reconstructed in the checks are

  mean_l r_l = I/2,
  mean_l r_l tensor r_l = (I+Swap)/5,
  mean_alpha |psi_alpha><psi_alpha| = I/4.

Define normalized packets

  s_R = 240^(-1/2)sum_alpha |alpha>_R,
  Phi_(R,A) = 240^(-1/2)sum_alpha |alpha>_R psi_alpha,A,
  Omega_(R,AB) = 240^(-1/2)sum_alpha |alpha>_R psi_alpha,A psi_alpha,B.

The source quarter turn T|alpha>=|i alpha> has eigenvalue i^(-k).
The source charges of s, Phi and Omega are respectively k=0,1,2.
Their source Schmidt modules are 1, bar4 and barSym^2(4), respectively.
Phi and Omega are invariant under the joint source/bound-matter reflection.
Every bound single matter marginal is I4/4.

All L and H_E terms preserve every individual source charge. A nonconstant
V term changes two neighbouring charges by (+1,-1) or (-1,+1). Therefore
only the total charge remains conserved when mu is nonzero.

2. What the proposed Omega/s walls actually are
================================================

Two adjacent pure Omega packets cannot coexist: their rank-one local packet
projectors have ||Q_e Q_(e+1)||=1/4<1. The common matter register is not a
second tensor factor. Hence the symbolic splice Omega Omega does not define
the proposed product of packets.

A valid Omega covering is a matching of edges. The admissible change of
alternating orientation has an ss pair and one uncovered matter vertex.
For m=2r, take the vacancy at vertex v=2j, j=0,...,r. Put Omega on even
zero-based edges below v and on odd edges above v; put s elsewhere. A basis
vector c in the free C^4 register completes the state. Distinct j have
distinct source phase strings, so these 4(r+1) vectors are orthonormal.

Their exact Hamiltonian compression is a scalar:

  P_bare H P_bare = [3r(kappa+J)/4 + mu(m-1)]P_bare.          (2.1)

Indeed all Omega terms vanish, each s edge has expectation 3(kappa+J)/4,
and P_even V_e,e+1 P_even=P_even for charges restricted to {0,2}. There is
no first-order bare-wall hopping. At mu=0 there is no change between definite
source phase patterns at any time, even after relaxation inside a pattern.

This does not mean that the bare walls are frozen for nonzero mu. On a local
two-edge segment, let

  |L_c> = Omega_(R1,AB) s_R2 |c>_C,
  |R_d> = s_R1 |d>_A Omega_(R2,BC).

Exact full-root contraction gives

  <R_d|L_c>=0,  <R_d|V|L_c>=0,
  <R_d|V^2|L_c>=delta_dc/160.                                  (2.2)

Charge selection eliminates all other terms in the cross element of H^2,
so <R_d|exp(-itH)|L_c>=-mu^2 t^2 delta_dc/320+O(t^3). This is a short-time
transition through intermediate charge sectors, not a resolvent coefficient.
The number1/160 is the generic complex-projective-2-design contraction
1/[2d^2(d+1)] at d=4; it is not a special higher E8 fingerprint.

For even m, the two globally shifted alternating phase strings require at
least m factors of the nonconstant V: every source charge must change by2,
and each factor supplies only two unit increments. Applying two transfers
on each disjoint neighbouring pair attains this charge-count lower bound.
It is a statement about allowed perturbative order, not a tunnel amplitude.
For odd m, the two maximal bare orientations have different total charges.
In particular at m=5 they cannot mix at all in this Hamiltonian family.
They also contain different packet/free-register counts. Open-boundary
even/odd effects are therefore not evidence for two bulk broken-symmetry
vacua.

3. Completing the wall with the existing Phi packet
====================================================

At the uncovered vertex, replace one adjacent s source by Phi bound to the
free register. No new source, register or Hamilton term is introduced.
For m=2r and each zero-based core index a=0,...,m-1, set

  v=2 ceil(a/2).

Use the matching of Section2 at v, put Phi on edge a bound to vertex v,
and put s on all other unoccupied edges. Every matter register now belongs
to exactly one packet. There are r Omega, one Phi and r-1 s factors.

For m=4 the four states are

  Phi Omega s Omega,
  Omega Phi s Omega,
  Omega s Phi Omega,
  Omega s Omega Phi.                                           (3.1)

In the first state Phi binds the left end matter register; in the middle
two it binds the shared middle register; in the last it binds the right end.
Their source charges are1202,2102,2012,2021. All m states have distinct
phase strings and total phase m+1 mod4. Their Gram matrix is exactly I_m.
These completed vectors are scalars under the simultaneous native source/
matter group action; the original free C^4 colour has been absorbed by Phi.
Neither property supplies Lorentz spin, fermionic statistics or chirality.

The local Omega-Phi edge exchange has fixed-root cross kernel z or its
conjugate, where z=<psi_alpha|psi_beta>. The Phi-s exchange at the same
vacancy has the identical kernel. In both cases

  mean z=0,  mean z^2=0,  mean |z|^2=1/4,
  <a+1|V|a>=-mean[(Re z) conjugate(z)]=-1/8.                   (3.2)

One V transfer reaches no other core index. H_0 and H_E preserve individual

The eigenvalues of the finite compressed matrix are exactly

They are Ritz values (variational values), not established full-model

4. Why the compressed path is not yet the low-energy theory
===========================================================

On a uniform-source bridge, L=(4I-Swap)/5. With independent I4/4 endpoints,
<Swap>=1/4 and Swap^2=I. Its residual variance is

  <L^2>-<L>^2 = 3/80.

Distinct uniform bridges have zero connected contraction in each core
product state: if they touch opposite matter factors of the same Omega,
tracing their independent external I4/4 marginals leaves I/4 on each inner
factor, so the product expectation is still1/16. Otherwise factorization
is immediate. Omega contributions annihilate the state; the Phi contribution
is exactly1/2 times the state. Hence

  ||(I-P_core)H_0|a>||^2 = 3(r-1)kappa^2/80.                  (4.1)

Residual vectors from different a have different conserved source phase
strings and are orthogonal. Therefore the corresponding residual Gram
matrix is3(r-1)kappa^2 I_m/80. No superposition removes this leakage.
At fixed weak mu the leakage is already order kappa, larger than the
mu-scale bare core hopping. Simply truncating the Hilbert space is not a
controlled weak-coupling expansion.

The full Schur/Feshbach operator would include, when its resolvent exists,

  H_eff(E)=PHP-PHQ(QHQ-E)^(-1)QHP.                             (4.2)

One must first relax the core together with its background under H_0,
identify a separated low subspace and its relation to the full ground, then
derive the remaining dynamics. Equation (4.2) is not evaluated by replacing
its denominator with a guessed constant. A small local matrix or a direct
hop alone cannot settle that missing spectral question.

5. Full four-source energy exclusion of the bare alternating sectors
=====================================================================

This test excludes more than the two product vectors. Let P_alt be the sum
of the complete source representation sectors

  A=(barSym^2(4),1,barSym^2(4),1),
  B=(1,barSym^2(4),1,barSym^2(4)),

with all five matter registers retained. H_0 preserves both. Contract .23
proves the full640-dimensional mixed two-source sector lower bound

  L_1+L_2 >= E_* I,  E_*=(6-sqrt6)/5.

Its proof constructs the invariant two-family block and excludes its
orthogonal complement by19/15. This is an existing checked sector theorem,
not the reported but unreplayed full three-source theorem.

Split the four local L terms into two consecutive pairs. Each pair obeys
the mixed-sector operator bound; extending by identities and adding is
valid even though the pairs share a matter register. Therefore

  P_alt H_0 P_alt >= 2E_* kappa P_alt.                         (5.1)

The explicit normalized completed core

  Gamma=Omega_(R1,AB) Phi_(R2,C) s_(R3) Omega_(R4,DE)

has the exact expectation

  <Gamma|H|Gamma>=5kappa/4+9J/4+3mu.                          (5.2)

At J=mu=0, 2E_*kappa=1.4202041029...kappa, whereas (5.2) is1.25kappa.
Thus no state in A+B is the full ground state. This is an unconditional
variational exclusion, not a numerical diagonalization.

Since P_alt sum V P_alt=3P_alt and sum H_E is positive,

  P_alt H P_alt >= (2E_*kappa+3mu)P_alt.

Comparison with (5.2) excludes every state confined to P_alt whenever

  J/kappa < (23-8sqrt6)/45 = 0.075646... ,                     (5.3)

for any mu>=0. This does not yet bound the alternating component of a
strongly mixed full state at large mu. At weak couplings such a bound is
also available.

Let Q=I-P_alt. Four event terms give ||Q sum H_E P_alt||<=8. For each of A,B,
the three V links have six distinct two-odd-phase targets, with disjoint
target sets also between A and B. Each nonconstant transfer has norm<=1/2.
Consequently ||Q sum V P_alt||<=sqrt(3/2). The H_E output stays all-even
and is orthogonal to every V output. Thus

  ||QHP_alt||^2 <= b^2=64J^2+3mu^2/2.

For any full ground vector g with energy E0, the variational upper bound
(5.2) and compressed lower bound imply

  PHP-E0 P >= dP,
  d=(23-8sqrt6)kappa/20-9J/4.

The projected eigenvalue equation, if d>0, gives d||Pg||<=b||Qg||, hence

  ||P_alt g||^2 <= b^2/(d^2+b^2).                             (5.4)

On J=mu=epsilon kappa, 0<=epsilon<=1/200, this increasing bound is at most
0.0608648. Thus over93.9 percent of any full ground vector lies outside
these bare alternating representation sectors in that four-source regime.

This does not identify the remaining full ground vector, prove the completed
core trial is that ground state, or exclude every bulk dimerized phase.
In particular, an alternating phase with substantial other source modules
would be a different hypothesis. The naive Omega/s assumption has failed
its prescribed smallest decisive energy test.

6. The proposed Dirac cone: a precise spectral distinction
==========================================================

The user-supplied matrix

  h(k)=[[m,t1+t2 exp(-ik)],[t1+t2 exp(ik),-m]]

has the stated exact eigenvalues. It closes at k=pi for m=0,t1=t2=t>0.
For those parameters h(pi+q) has linear eigenvalues +/-t|q| to leading order.
The algebra is correct.

The completed-core compression supplies a concrete instance after doubling
the cell: t1=t2=mu/8, m=0, plus E_core I (an overall hopping sign is removable
by a unitary). Equivalently the single unfolded band is

  E(p)=E_core-(mu/4)cos p.

Its minimum is at p=0 and its excess energy is mu p^2/8+O(p^4). In the folded
description,

  E_+/- (k)=E_core +/- (mu/4)|cos(k/2)|,

the linear crossing at k=pi is at E_core, above the lower band minimum by
mu/4. Subtracting a scalar reference energy does not change which states
lie below the crossing. The linear crossing is therefore not the lowest
single-wall excitation. No negative-energy branch can simply be dropped.

A filled fermionic band or a justified particle/hole reference can make
such a crossing physically low energy. The present single-core states do
not supply that many-body filling, fermion statistics or vacuum law.
Moreover the compressed band is still subject to Section4's leakage.

In the original SSH literature, the two degenerate dimerized vacua are
translation-related patterns t+delta,t-delta and t-delta,t+delta at nonzero
delta. Their equality does not imply t1=t2. That latter equality is the
undimerized critical point. The electronic Bloch spinor, localized soliton
state and translational collective coordinate of the soliton are distinct.
The original work treats soliton motion with an effective mass.

The sublattice spectral symmetry {sigma_z,h}=0 holds only for m=0; at
nonzero m the anticommutator is2mI (Rice-Mele-type staggering). This is not
3+1 dimensional Weyl chirality. No physical identification follows from
the word 'chiral' or two components alone.
