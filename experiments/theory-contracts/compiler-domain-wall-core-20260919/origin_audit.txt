3. WHAT THE PROPOSED 2x2 MATRIX ACTUALLY PROVES

For

           [ m      t1 + t2 exp(-ik) ]
  h(k) =   [ t1+t2 exp(ik)     -m     ],

the exact eigenvalues are

  E_+(k), E_-(k) = +/- sqrt(m^2+t1^2+t2^2+2 t1 t2 cos k).

For positive hoppings the bulk gap closes at k=pi exactly when

  m=0 and t1=t2.

At t1=t2=t and k=pi+q, the off-diagonal entry is

  t(1-exp(-iq)) = i t q + O(q^2),

so the signed bulk electronic bands cross linearly.  This part is exact.

The matrix is the SSH two-sublattice Bloch form only at m=0.  With the written
diagonal term it is Rice-Mele-type: m is a staggered on-site energy.  For the
sublattice operator Gamma=sigma_z,

  {Gamma,h(k)} = 2 m I.

Thus the relevant "chiral symmetry" is exact only at m=0.  It is the internal
sublattice spectral symmetry that pairs E with -E.  It is not physical
left/right Weyl chirality and does not itself produce a relativistic fermion.

4. TWO VACUA, A DOMAIN WALL, AND A BLOCH ELECTRON ARE DIFFERENT OBJECTS

The original SSH mechanism has two translation-related dimerized ground
states.  Their hopping patterns are schematically

  A: (..., t+delta, t-delta, t+delta, t-delta, ...)
  B: (..., t-delta, t+delta, t-delta, t+delta, ...).

They have equal thermodynamic energy because translation exchanges them.
Their degeneracy does not require t1=t2.  In fact, t1=t2 is delta=0: the
undimerized critical configuration where the two ordered patterns merge and
the electronic bulk gap closes.

A domain wall is a spatial interpolation A -> B of the dimerization order
parameter.  It carries a finite formation energy and, in SSH, a localized
midgap electronic state.  Translating the wall is a collective coordinate.
The original SSH analysis gives it an effective mass; this is separate from
the valence/conduction electron Bloch dispersion.  On a periodic chain walls
also occur with an antiwall unless boundary/asymptotic conditions permit an
isolated wall.

Therefore two global symmetry-broken vacua are not automatically the two
local components of h(k), and their equal energy is not the condition
t1=t2.  A wall-position Hilbert space must be constructed before a wall
dispersion can be claimed.

5. DECISIVE SINGLE-WALL SPECTRAL OBSTRUCTION

Suppose, despite the missing derivation, that the critical matrix is read as
a one-wall hopping Hamiltonian.  A physical wall has a nonnegative creation
energy, so the trace-zero spectrum must be shifted by E_c I.  At m=0 and
t1=t2=t the branches are

  E_wall,+/-(k) = E_c +/- 2 t |cos(k/2)|.

Nonnegativity of the lower branch requires E_c >= 2t.  The linear crossing at
k=pi then lies at energy E_c >= 2t: it is a midband crossing, not the lowest
one-wall excitation.  The lower branch has its minimum at k=0 and

  E_wall,-(k) = E_c - 2t + (t/4) k^2 + O(k^4),

which is quadratic.  Calling the k=pi crossing a low-energy Dirac cone needs
an additional half-filled/particle-hole/second-quantized ground-state
construction.  None is supplied by the single-wall packet proposal.

This does not rule out an SSH-like effective theory.  It rules out the stated
inference from a two-label wall basis to a low-energy Dirac carrier.

6. EXISTING-SOURCE WALL COMPRESSION AND THE REMAINING TEST

No new Hamiltonian is needed.  For an even chain m=2r, the maximal-packet
covering construction now supplies wall-position states made from r Omega
packets, r source singlets, and one free four-state register at site 2j.  The
phase strings for different j are distinct.  Therefore the bare wall family
has exactly

  G = I,

and compression of the existing source Hamiltonian gives

  P H P = [3r(kappa+J)/4 + mu(2r-1)] I.

Thus there is no first-order wall hopping and no first-order SSH band in this
family.  This is stronger and cleaner than the older u,w Gram warning: the
new wall basis is orthonormal, but its direct projected dynamics is flat.

A second, constructive trial family completes the free four-state register
into the already existing charge-one packet Phi, with a neighbouring uniform
source.  For four sources (and five matter registers) its ordered states are

  Phi Omega s Omega,  Omega Phi s Omega,
  Omega s Phi Omega,  Omega s Omega Phi.

Their source/phase strings are distinct, so their Gram matrix is exactly the
identity.  With m=2r positions, the supplied local counting and the two
allowed exchanges Omega Phi <-> Phi Omega and Phi s <-> s Phi give the exact
compression

  P H P = E_core I - (mu/8) A_path,

  E_core = (3r-1)kappa/4 + 3(r+1)J/4 + mu(m-1),

where A_path is the nearest-neighbour path adjacency.  This is a genuine
positive connection to an SSH-like hopping alphabet, but only at the level of
the projected trial matrix.  For m>=4 the trial space is not invariant under
the full Hamiltonian, so its path eigenvalues are Ritz values rather than an
exact wall spectrum; the Q-sector correction still has to be calculated.

The translation-invariant one-band continuation of the interior hopping is

  E(p) = E_core - (mu/4) cos p
       = E_core - mu/4 + (mu/8)p^2 + O(p^4).

It therefore has a quadratic low-energy minimum.  If the same uniform chain
is merely rewritten with a doubled cell, its two hoppings are
t1=t2=mu/8 and the folded bands cross at the middle of the band.  This is
exactly the distinction in Sections 4-5: a Dirac-shaped folded crossing does
not become the low-energy one-wall dispersion without a filling or
particle-hole prescription.  The construction does not yet prove a full
vacuum sector, a wall mass, or the origin carrier x family dictionary.

The remaining test is whether eliminating the complement Q=I-P generates a
controlled effective hopping matrix, and whether that matrix has the claimed
SSH form.  The full acceptance sequence is:

  1. Specify a consistent chain tensor factorization.  Omega consumes a
     source and its adjacent matter registers, whereas s is source-only; the
     packet ordering is not yet a uniform local alphabet without explicit
     edge/register bookkeeping.

  2. Construct the two even-chain backgrounds |A_N>, |B_N>.  Prove that one
     lattice translation exchanges them, their energy-density difference
     tends to zero, their finite-size splitting tends to zero, and an A|B
     interface has finite positive energy.

  3. Use the constructed orthonormal wall-position states |n,a> and compute
     their couplings through the complement, rather than posit them:

       <n,a|H Q|q>,  Q H Q,

     followed by the controlled effective operator on P.  The direct term
     P H P is already the scalar matrix displayed above.

  4. Fourier transform the resulting effective kernel.  If a nontrivial Gram
     matrix occurs after enlarging or dressing the basis, solve

       det(K(k)-E G(k))=0.

  5. Only if the derived K and G reduce to the claimed nearest-neighbour
     off-diagonal form, with a proved sublattice anticommutation, may t1, t2,
     m and the SSH/Dirac wording be promoted from ansatz to result.

  6. For the origin-theory dictionary, additionally extend the two packet
     labels to the fixed-plus-pair family space and exhibit the intertwiner
     for the mu_3/Z2 actions and the spectrum {1,2/3,1/3}.

The first missing object is therefore the controlled complement elimination
for the non-invariant Phi trial space, together with a bound showing that the
chosen wall sector remains valid.  The Phi-completed projection already
supplies the critical trial values t1=t2=mu/8 and m=0.  Until the complement
is controlled, these are not the parameters of an exact low-energy wall
band; the carrier x family transfer dictionary also does not follow.
