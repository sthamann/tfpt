NATIVE FRAME ORIGIN AUDIT — 2026-09-19

Scope
-----
Read-only audit of the native origin required by
UR.COMPILER.CORRELATED_EVENT_CLOCK.13.  Verdict: PARTIAL.  The finite Bell10
construction has an exact spatial history frame, but the inspected seam/CAR/

The required interface
----------------------
Let Hbar=Stab(q*)≅S5 be the label/population quotient.  The forty q*-fixing
source events are the four phase lifts of each of the ten transpositions of the
five remaining points, so their outer label actions generate Hbar.  Keeping
the Pauli phases gives the full adjoint frame group

  Hhat = F4^2 semidirect (SL2(4) semidirect Gal)
       = ASL2(4):2,  |Hhat|=1920,

not merely S5 (exact reconstruction: experiments/theory-contracts/compiler-frame-group-20260919/PROOF.txt).
On Bell10=Sym^2(C^4), contract .13 already has concrete
monomial unitaries B_g and the exact covariance

  B_g^* K_(ell,a) B_g = phase(g,ell,a) K_(g^{-1}ell,a),
  a in {stay,jump}.

Consequently F, updated by F <- B_g F, is spatially implemented on this finite
memory.  What is missing is its transport into the native seam theory.  The
first needed equality is the spatial transport

  j(B_g X B_g^*) = W_g j(X) W_g^*,             (A)
  g in Hhat.

for a source-derived embedding j:End(Bell10)->A_seam, a representation or
compatible projective representation g->W_g, or an equivalent covariant
transport of the marked seam observables.  Physical execution of an event does
not require W_g Omega=Omega.  Only the stronger interpretation of every frame
move as a symmetry of one fixed vacuum would additionally require

  W_g Omega = Omega                              (B)

(or the corresponding covariance relation between transported states).
For the recorded instrument one further needs a native commutative pointer
algebra with orthogonal event projections e_ell and a ready state, such that

  V^*(e_ell tensor X)V = K_ell^* X K_ell       (C)

and sequential composition of these outcomes updates the same W-word F.  An
abstract Stinespring dilation or an arbitrary matrix subalgebra does not prove
(A) and (C), nor the optional symmetry condition (B).

Decisive verdict
----------------
The earliest native structures provide separate pieces:

  * Bell10/.13: exact 1920-element adjoint-frame covariance, with S5 as its
    label/population quotient, and an exact history-frame algebra;
  * raw CAR: a criterion for a state-preserving spatial Bogoliubov action;
  * finite mu4 crossed product: a native invariant state and expectation;
  * context instrument/D4 readout: finite algebraic record rotations and
    projectors.

They do not compose in the current sources.  The first broken edge is (A):
there is no source-derived transport of the full 1920-element Bell10 frame to
the native seam algebra, including the transported clock/mark operators.
Condition (B) is an additional requirement only if this transport is claimed
to be a symmetry of one fixed vacuum.  Even if (A) is supplied, (C), the
persistent event pointer/ready-state composition, remains to be derived.
Aut(C_fin)=C6 excludes only the stronger identification with transformations
that fix the whole marked tuple pointwise; it does not exclude a moving frame.
The deployed mu4 expectation erases its nonzero grades and therefore is not by
itself the Hhat-valued F record.  A covariantly transported marked tuple or a
physically justified coarser seam algebra could supply the missing bridge, but
that transport/quotient and its record map are not explicit in the inspected
sources.

The minimal-positive-log proposal does not repair this origin gap.  It chooses
h_joint only by applying a non-monoidal logarithm after tensoring, whereas the
existing .12 one-body path composes to h_local.  P1 fixes unit boundary winding
and scalar normalisation, not which of these two continuous joint lifts nature
uses.
