3. MINIMAL POSITIVE LOGARITHM: EXACT CONDITIONAL THEOREM

Let a fixed self-adjoint unitary G act in finite dimension, tau>0, and H>=0
be time independent with exp(-i tau H)=G. H commutes with G because G is a
function of H. On the +1 eigenspace its eigenvalues are2pi k/tau with k>=0;
on the -1 eigenspace they are(2k+1)pi/tau with k>=0. Therefore
  H >= H_min=pi(I-G)/(2tau)
in Loewner order, with equality only at H_min. Degeneracy does not spoil the
proof, since H_min is scalar on each G eigenspace. This covers all positive
time-independent logarithms, not just the displayed h_m family.

This theorem does not establish the physical need for the smallest logarithm.
Time-dependent paths, source couplings, energy reference and duration need
their own hypotheses. In particular a conjugation channel fixes G only up
to a scalar. G and-G have the same channel and complementary H_min.
The previously used determinant-cleaned paired lift -iG has minimal generator
(2I-G) at tau=pi/2, differing from h_joint by I only; the tensor-composition
defect below is unchanged by this common scalar.

5. WHY THE SAME-SYSTEM REDUCED CLOCK IS NOT A LINDBLAD SEMIGROUP

On the symmetric memory, the spectral projectors of C60 correspond to
1,1/3,1/5,1/15. For its unnormalized Choi convention (trace10), the fixed
vector |0,1>-|1,0> has Choi-projector eigenvalues
  1/10, -1/10, -1/2, 1/2,
respectively. These are individually verified as exact matrix equations.
Therefore its eigenvalue for C60^s is
  lambda(s)=(1-3^(-s))(1-5^(1-s))/10 <0 for every0<s<1.
This proves the submitted assertion for the entire interval, not a sampled
grid. The principal square root is not completely positive.

The stronger homogeneous-generator exclusion is also sound here. For a
finite-dimensional bounded GKLS generator, the quantum-trajectory expansion
contains an invertible no-jump operator K0(t) and a positive CP remainder.
For t>s>0, concatenation implies that the Kraus space at t contains
K0(t-s) times the Kraus space at s. Choi rank is therefore nondecreasing.
Every Choi entry of exp(tL) is real analytic. If rank rose from r at one
positive time to a larger value later, all(r+1)-minors would vanish on an
earlier open interval by monotonicity; analyticity would make them vanish
identically, a contradiction. Rank is constant for t>0.

Here rank J(C60)=55 and rank J(C60²)=100, independently recomputed exactly.
Thus no finite time-independent Lindblad generator on this same M10 algebra
can produce C60 at a positive time. This does not prohibit non-Markovian
reduced dynamics, larger unitary systems, or all time-dependent embeddings.
Primary context for the channel embedding problem:
  Wolf and Cirac, Dividing Quantum Channels,
  https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0611057
  Wolf et al., Assessing non-Markovian quantum dynamics,
  https://arxiv.org/abs/0711.3172
The rank argument is proved explicitly above, not inferred from a title.
