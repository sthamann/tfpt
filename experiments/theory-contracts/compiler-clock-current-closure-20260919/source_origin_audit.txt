SOURCE-ORIGIN AUDIT: charged clock / local net / physical time
19 September 2026

Scope and discovery
-------------------
Fifteen directly relevant original/checker/contract sources were inspected. No
prior contract, ledger, paper or verification file was changed.

Verdict
-------
The original chain does not yet implement the Coxeter/quarter-clock action as a
local normal vacuum-preserving automorphism of the raw seam net. It implements:

1. finite root/character-space actions;
2. an abstract D5+A3 order-four glue class lambda and its crossed-product grades;
3. a chosen free/QWZ target polarization and a chosen charged vertex operator;
4. an exact charge-changing operator only after adjoining a four-level flux
   register to the QWZ matter Fock space.

These are compatible pieces, but they do not derive the charged field, its
polarization or its physical time from raw P1/P2 seam data. In particular, exact
closure of the 240 target roots under a finite clock is a target E8 statement.
It becomes a source-origin statement only after the same action is implemented
normally on the source local algebras, preserves the source vacuum, and has the
required microscopic time covariance.

What is genuinely fixed
-----------------------
* v993 fixes, conditionally on rank 8, ADE and the cyclic-Z4 lift premise, the
  D5+A3 lattice architecture, lambda^2 = vector/parity, and the finite conditional
  expectation. It explicitly leaves P1/P2 and the operator identification open.
* v1006 and the MMST memorandum prove the formal Z4 crossed-product grades,
  expectation and equivariant direct-limit naturality. A non-equivariant tower
  map fails. This is the strongest positive net compatibility already present.
* compiler-local-current-origin .11 proves a useful conditional rigidity: if
  local fields with the same support/smearing carry vacuum-preserving U_C,U_J,
  then one nonzero two-point response forces a scalar rank-eight Gram. It also
  states that these local implementations and their time commutation are inputs,
  not consequences of the finite clocks.
* source-localized-spinor constructs bounded responses of a known DHR spinor
  sector from chosen QWZ edge words. It does not construct a vacuum-to-charge
  operator. Its actual source generator is H_a=L0+(a-1/2)Q, hence H_1/4=L0-Q/4;
  the conformal charged time still needs a DHR covariance cocycle.

Where the source identification first fails
-------------------------------------------
1. v55 builds the Coxeter element from E8 simple-reflection matrices. v531 builds
   T30 on an eight-dimensional primitive-character space and types the
   identification with the E8 Coxeter clock as conditional. Neither file gives a
   local algebra, vacuum, normal implementer or Hamiltonian.
2. The integral-triality certificate supplies exact representation-level Spin(8)
   lifts of the finite matrices, but expressly chooses the lifts and leaves the
   physical D5+A3 fields, state and Hamiltonian open.
3. v113 chooses A16=I8 tensor [[0,1],[-1,0]] and therefore a standard rank-eight
   complex polarization. v162 then conditions the raw-to-target step on R1:
   “transfer operator = seam clock”. v177 leaves the actual raw Calderon operator
   identity open. Thus the chosen free polarization is not recovered from P1/P2.
4. In the executable QWZ charged route, v988 puts the same scalar phase i^r on
   the periodic seam. v1033 proves exactly that matter-only onsite gauge preserves
   total holonomy. Charge-changing corners appear only in the enlarged direct sum
   after adding a local four-level flux register.
5. That scalar eight-channel quarter twist has q=(1/4)^8 and h=1/4. The required
   D5+A3 lambda has h=1. Every integer dressing in the same scalar-phase class
   which reaches norm squared two has odd fermion parity. The D5 half twist plus
   A3 inverse-quarter description further needs the diagonal-U(1) quotient and a
   cocycle/GSO identification; it is not the same raw scalar collar.

The v1033 source, symbolic and finite-QWZ suite was replayed: 35 passed, 0 failed.
This is an exact obstruction to identifying the existing scalar seam twist or the
finite clock shift with the even local lambda field. It is not a no-go against a
different source-derived, channel-resolved charged action.

Useful positive mapping, with its exact boundary
------------------------------------------------
On the enlarged direct sum of the four QWZ boundary sectors, v1033's

    D_I = X_flux tensor U_arc

is even, has dual Z4 charge one, and intertwines H_r with H_(r+1) away from the
far endpoint: its Hamiltonian commutator is supported exactly at that endpoint.
Combined with the equivariant crossed-product theorem, this gives a constructive
local charged route in the enlarged model. It does not put D_I in the original
matter CAR algebra and it carries the common quarter-twist weight, not yet the
D5+A3 lambda weight.

There is also a sharp algebraic source test for the Coxeter action. If the exact
root census shows that C sends any D8 bilinear root to a half-integral Ramond root,
then the EXISTING target-defined C is not an automorphism of the unextended neutral
D8 current net: it does not preserve that net's weight-one space. Its checked root
closure is therefore evidence about the already extended E8 target and cannot by
itself establish source provenance. This is not a logical no-go against a source
derivation. An independently constructed local normal source automorphism C, on a
source field algebra containing the D8 subnet, which preserves the source vacuum
and actually carries a D8 current into a nonzero Ramond current, would instead be
positive evidence that the source forces a charged completion. The distinction is
whether that local source implementation is constructed independently of the
target E8 root dictionary.

First additional assumption
---------------------------
The first source-specific premise is a support-preserving covariant charged
generator, not another lattice census:

There exists an even, charge-one sequence Phi_N(f) in the actual raw local seam
algebra B_N(I), with no auxiliary flux register, whose clock character is the
factorwise D5+A3 lambda character, whose vacuum-to-charge matrix element is
nonzero, and whose microscopic Heisenberg evolution converges to the charged
sector evolution H_1/4=L0-Q/4 (or to L0 after an independently derived covariance
cocycle).

This is the concrete FE-GEN bridge. ALG-EXH is the later statement that these
generators exhaust exactly the desired local extension.

Smallest decisive calculation
-----------------------------
Do one charge-resolved dynamical two-point test on a raw-algebra candidate. Let
Pi_q,N be the actual clock-character projections and set

    B_N(f) = Pi_1,N Phi_N(f) Pi_0,N,
    K_N(t;f,g) = <Omega_N, B_N(f)^* exp[-it(H_N-E_0,N)]
                           B_N(g) Omega_N>.

Require simultaneously:

(A) raw membership/support: Phi_N(f) is in B_N(I), without the flux register,
    and graded commutators with even observables outside I vanish in the limit;
(B) character: the desired D5+A3 lambda corner is nonzero and the scalar common-
    quarter mutant is rejected by the factorwise character projectors;
(C) nonzeroness: liminf ||B_N(f)Omega_N|| > 0;
(D) charge-calibrated conformal weight: first pin the actual continuous charge
    Q_N of B_N(f)Omega_N to the lambda charge and reconstruct, on the a=1/4
    sector,

      L0_N^(rec) = H_N + Q_N/4.

    The bottom of the JOINT energy/charge spectral measure, evaluated with
    L0_N^(rec), must tend to h=1 rather than the scalar-twist value 1/4. The raw
    H_N frequency alone is not a conformal-weight measurement. If the source
    fixes only the Z4 character and not the continuous Q_N normalization, this
    item remains unresolved rather than being reported as an h=1 test;
(E) time: on the common finite-energy core,

      exp(it H_N) Phi_N(f) exp(-it H_N) - Phi_N(u_t^(1/4) f) -> 0

    uniformly for t in compact sets, with u_t^(1/4) generated by L0-Q/4;
(F) covariance cocycle, if the target convention uses L0: exhibit z_N(t) with
    the cocycle law and show the conjugated evolution tends to the independently
    charge-calibrated L0.

This one kernel separates the four possibilities at the first operator identity:
the neutral finite clock fails (B/C), the scalar seam twist fails (B/D), the
flux-register construction fails raw membership (A), while a genuine raw charged
source passes all six structural checks. Passing this test establishes the charged
generator bridge only; it does not construct the local C,J implementations used
by the rank-eight rigidity lemma. That lemma applies only after separately proving
the full .11 premises: vacuum-preserving local U_C and U_J act with the same
smearing on a nonzero eight-vector field map (and their relation to source time is
checked explicitly). Only then does the scalar-commutant argument force the
rank-eight Gram and permit the conditional current-to-Bell bridge. If the charged
test fails for every raw local candidate by a character or holonomy conservation
theorem, the one-collar source route is excluded and an additional charged degree
of freedom or a different raw action must be derived.
