3. THE FULL FINITE BAND IS NOT EXACTLY AFFINE
=============================================

For

  h=sum_(i,k) epsilon_k a^dag_(i,k)a_(i,k),
  epsilon_k=-2 cos(2 pi(k+1/2)/L),

one has

  [h,Eij_n]
    = sum_k (epsilon_k-epsilon_(k+n))
              a^dag_(i,k)a_(j,k+n).

On the full finite ring the coefficient depends on k.  At L=8 the checker
finds multiple distinct exact algebraic values for each of E12_1, E23_2 and
E13_3.  Hence the uncut finite-band currents are not exact scalar-frequency
eigenoperators.

This result does not contradict v454 and is no longer the main verdict.  The
level-one central term in v454 is a vacuum/scaling witness.  A nonlinear
finite band need not already have exact continuum frequencies at every
momentum on the finite Fock space.


4. CONTROLLED AFFINE LIMIT ON THE ACTUAL v454 FERMI PATCHES
==========================================================

4.1 Actual antiperiodic coordinates and the two signs
-----------------------------------------------------

Let L be divisible by four.  v454 uses

  k_j=2 pi(j+1/2)/L.

Relative to k_F=+pi/2 set

  r=j+1/2-L/4.

Relative to k_F=-pi/2, represented by 3pi/2, set

  r=j+1/2-3L/4.

Thus r belongs to Z+1/2 on both branches, and integer current modes preserve
this grid.  Direct substitution into the actual dispersion gives

  -2 cos(pi/2+2 pi r/L)   = +2 sin(2 pi r/L),
  -2 cos(3pi/2+2 pi r/L) = -2 sin(2 pi r/L).

After division by v(2pi/L), with the v454 Fermi velocity v=2, define

  e_L(r)=L/(2pi) sin(2pi r/L).

The rescaled right-branch energy is +e_L and the left-branch energy is -e_L.
Their limiting slopes are +1 and -1.

v454 directly derives this dispersion for one complex fermion species.  To
place the two D8 kernels on the native eight-slot carrier, this contract makes
the explicit same-dispersion tensor-product choice

  h_8 = I_8 tensor h_v454.

Only under this premise do all eight complex species share the selected
branch slope.  The native eight-slot count does not enforce equal spatial
hopping, and P1/P2 have not selected a common velocity.  This premise is
recorded in results.json as first_additional_choice.

4.2 Exact Taylor bound
----------------------

The elementary remainder estimate

  |sin x-x| <= |x|^3/6

implies, for every real r and every integer q,

  |e_L(r)-r| <= (2pi)^2 |r|^3/(6L^2),

and therefore

  |e_L(r)-e_L(r+q)+q|
    <= (2pi)^2/(6L^2) (|r|^3+|r+q|^3).                 (1)

The checker verifies the corresponding symbolic leading terms:

  lim_(L->infinity) L^2(e_L(r)-r)=-(2pi)^2 r^3/6,

  lim_(L->infinity) L^2(e_L(r)-e_L(r+q)+q)
    =(2pi)^2((r+q)^3-r^3)/6.

For a fixed patch and fixed q, (1) is uniform and tends to zero as L^-2.  The
sample values at L=64,128,256 in results.json are explicitly labelled
non-load-bearing numerical illustrations; the proof is the analytic bound.

4.3 Cut kernels and boundary accounting
---------------------------------------

Fix the concrete half-integer patch

  R={-9/2,-7/2,...,7/2,9/2}.

For q>0 define the non-cyclic cut kernel

  Eij_q^R = sum_(r in R, r+q in R) |i,r><j,r+q|.

There is no modular wrap.  A mode q omits exactly q upper-edge source
momenta and q lower-edge target momenta.  Hence the support counts on this
ten-point patch are

  |supp E12_1^R|=9,  |supp E23_2^R|=8,
  |supp E13_3^R|=7.

Because n=1 and m=2 are positive, membership of r and r+n+m in this interval
automatically includes r+n.  The exact cut-kernel bracket is therefore

  [E12_1^R,E23_2^R]=E13_3^R.                           (2)

No boundary correction is hidden in (2).  It would have to be retained for a
general non-convex patch or modes of mixed sign.

On the right branch,

  [h_L^R,Eij_q^R]+q Eij_q^R

is a weighted partial shift whose coefficient at r is precisely the
left-hand side of (1).  Its operator norm is the maximum absolute coefficient,
since the occupied matrix units have distinct source and target slots.
Consequently (1) proves operator-norm convergence on this finite one-body
patch:

  [h_L^R,E12_1^R] -> -E12_1^R,
  [h_L^R,E23_2^R] -> -2E23_2^R,
  [h_L^R,E13_3^R] -> -3E13_3^R.

On the left branch the signs reverse.  In both cases the limiting frequency of
the bracket is the sum of the limiting frequencies of its two inputs.  The
same proof holds for every fixed finite no-wrap half-integer patch.

This is a controlled finite one-body patch theorem.  The positive-mode pair
on a contiguous interval was chosen so that its intermediate momentum stays
inside the cut.  That boundary control does not establish adjoint closure,
negative-mode closure, filled-sea normal ordering or a central extension on
the patch.  It also does not prove strong convergence on a common GNS core or
control a growing patch.  v454 is the separate positive central-term/scaling
witness.
