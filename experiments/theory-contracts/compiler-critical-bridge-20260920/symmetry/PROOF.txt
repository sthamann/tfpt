5. NEUTRALITY, GAUGE CONSTRAINTS, AND CLAIM BOUNDARY
=====================================================

There is no contradiction in a physical observable space being neutral.  A
gauge constraint may leave only neutral physical states and observables while
charged fields act between superselection sectors in a larger field Hilbert
space.  This audit says precisely that such an enlarged action is required if
the marked affine Cartan currents are to be realized.  It does not assert that
the charged intermediate vectors are themselves gauge-invariant observables.

The result does not rule out charged excitations in the full microscopic H,

clock representation, or a symmetry-changing/nonintertwining embedding.  It
does rule out the natural identification in which the scalar packet
