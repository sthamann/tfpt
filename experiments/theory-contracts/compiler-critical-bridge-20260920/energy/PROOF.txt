FULL-MODEL ENERGY COMPARISON ON THE .28 PACKET CRITICAL LINE
================================================================

Verdict
=======

PASS_EXACT_BARE_PACKET_VACUUM_EXCLUDED.

For every even N>=4, kappa>0, J>=0, at the packet critical coupling

  mu=(kappa+9J)/2,

the minimum of the complete bare packet compression is strictly above the
energy of an explicit state in the unchanged full Hilbert space.  The two
states have the same total quarter-phase K=N mod4 and the same joint native
G31 singlet symmetry.  Therefore symmetry cannot protect the bare packet
compression as the full vacuum.

The packet-orthogonal part of the same full-space state also has Rayleigh
energy below the certified packet lower bound.  This directly refutes the
missing premise that the full orthogonal complement is uniformly high on
the critical line.

This is not a no-go theorem for a dressed packet phase or a dressed Ising
critical point.  It says that the undressed .28 packet subspace is not the
full vacuum subspace of the already defined Hamiltonian.


1. Frozen .28 packet lower bound
=================================

The exact .28 compression is

  G^(-1/2) K G^(-1/2) = H_I + ring correction,

where

  H_I = E_ref + delta n1 -(mu/8) sum_v X_v,
  E_ref = 3N(kappa+J)/8 + mu N,
  delta = (kappa+9J)/8.

At mu=(kappa+9J)/2, n1>=0 and sum X_v<=N give

  H_I >= N(13 kappa+69 J)/16.                         (1.1)

The exact .28 norm correction is

  R_N = N s/(1-s) (kappa+3J/2+21mu/8),
  s=4^(1-N).

For N>=4, s/(1-s)<=1/63.  On the critical line the bracket is

  (37 kappa+213 J)/16.

Consequently every normalized bare packet vector obeys

  E_packet >= 3 kappa N/4
              + N(13 kappa/504+689 J/168).             (1.2)

The checker verifies each coefficient exactly.  Equation (1.2) uses the
full Gram-orthonormalized packet compression, including the exceptional
finite-ring links; it is not the uncorrected Ising matrix alone.


2. Aligned native cat in the full Hilbert space
================================================

Let the sum run over the 240 oriented native roots and put

  |A_N> = 240^(-1/2) sum_alpha
          |alpha>^(tensor N) |psi_alpha>^(tensor N).    (2.1)

The source strings for different alpha are orthogonal, so A_N is normalized.
Every primitive native reflection jointly permutes the source label and its
matter root.  The uniform sum is therefore fixed by every generator and is
a joint G31 singlet.

Under the global quarter-phase relabelling alpha->i alpha, renaming the sum
gives (-i)^N from the N matter roots.  Thus A_N has source Fourier charge
K=N mod4, exactly like every packet assignment, whose local packet charges
sum to N.

For each oriented alpha, exactly15 of the60 native primitive reflections
fix alpha.  A local L term therefore has diagonal expectation

  1-15/60 = 3/4.

A local L changes at most one source label.  For N>=2, all off-root terms in
the aligned cat expectation vanish on an untouched orthogonal source ket.
The conditional event term H_E annihilates A_N because each of its two
matter roots has reflection eigenvalue -1.  The source-distance term V
annihilates it because adjacent source labels are identical.  Hence, in the
unchanged full Hamiltonian,

  <A_N|H|A_N> = 3 kappa N/4.                            (2.2)

Combining (1.2) and (2.2),

  E_packet - <A_N|H|A_N>
    >= N(13 kappa/504+689 J/168) > 0.                  (2.3)

This already excludes the bare packet compression as the full vacuum for
all even N>=4.


3. Exact projection onto the packet span
==========================================

Let P be the orthogonal projector onto the complete 2^N-dimensional packet
span and Q=I-P.  For every assignment sigma,

  <Psi_sigma|A_N> = a,       a=240^((1-N)/2).

The packet Gram matrix differs from the identity only on the two uniform
assignments.  With s=4^(1-N),

  u=G^(-1) 1

has entries1 except on those two assignments, where the entry is

  v=1/(1+s).

Writing t=s/(1+s), D=2^N, the exact packet projection weight is

  w=<A_N|P|A_N>
    =a^2[D-2t].                                          (3.1)

It is strictly below1.  Thus QA_N is a nonzero full-space state orthogonal
to every bare packet assignment.  Since the packet span consists of joint
G31 singlets of total charge N, both PA_N and QA_N retain the same symmetry
and charge as A_N.


4. Exact Q-trial energy
========================

Let n1(sigma) count equal neighboring assignment spins.  The native moments

  mean z=1/2,       mean z^2=2/5

give the exact cross matrix element

  <Psi_sigma|H|A_N>
    = kappa a [3N/10+n1(sigma)/5].                      (4.1)

Indeed, if the packet bra assigns n=0,1,2 neighboring matter registers to a
source, the primitive transfer contains the remaining 2-n aligned-cat matter
overlaps and is therefore z^(2-n).  The corresponding local L contributions
are

  n=0: 1-mean z^2=3/5,
  n=1: 1-mean z  =1/2,
  n=2: 1-mean 1  =0.

On a ring the zero- and two-register source counts are both (N-n1)/2, so their
opposite placement leaves the sum 3N/10+n1/5 and hence (4.1) unchanged.  H_E
and V annihilate A_N and therefore do not enter this cross term.

Define

  L0=D-2t,
  L1=N(D/2-2t).

Then

  <PA_N|H|A_N>
    = a^2 kappa (3N L0/10+L1/5).                        (4.2)

The exact .28 packet matrix gives

  u^T K u =
    E_ref(D-2+2v^2)
    +delta N(D/2-2+2v^2)
    -(mu/8)(ND-4Nt)
    +s[(kappa N/2+mu N)2v^2-2mu Nv].                   (4.3)

The checker independently enumerates the hypercube sums in (4.3) at
N=4,6,8,10, including the exceptional C links.  Therefore

  E_Q = <A_N|QHQ|A_N>/(1-w)
      = [3kappa N/4
         -2a^2 kappa(3N L0/10+L1/5)
         +a^2 u^T K u]/(1-w).                          (4.4)

All quantities in (4.4) are rational at fixed N on the critical line.  The
machine result records the exact kappa and J coefficients and verifies that
both lie below the corresponding coefficients in (1.2) for N=4,6,8,10.


5. Direct all-N bound for the Q trial
======================================

The finite checks are not extrapolated.  Equation (4.4) has a simple uniform
bound.  Put

  x=a^2 D=240/120^N.

For every N>=4,

  x<=1/864000,       s/D<=1/1024,       w<=x.

In (4.4), the cross term is nonnegative and appears with a minus sign.  The
hypercube hopping and exceptional-C pieces of (4.3) are also nonpositive.
Dropping them gives an upper bound.  The remaining E_ref, delta, and positive
ring terms yield

  E_Q - 3kappa N/4
    <= N/(863999) [865 kappa/512+5577 J/1024].           (5.1)

The exact rational inequalities

  865/(512*863999) < 13/504,
  5577/(1024*863999) < 689/168

then combine with (1.2) to prove

  E_Q < E_packet lower bound                            (5.2)

for every even N>=4.  Thus the complement is not merely coupled strongly to
the packet span: it contains a same-symmetry normalized trial state below
the entire certified packet energy range at the proposed critical coupling.


6. Evidence boundary
======================

checker.py pins the native source implementation, the root-source audit, and
the exact .28 packet proof/checker/results.  It verifies:

* all240 root reflection counts and the exact z,z^2 moments;
* the aligned-cat energy and symmetry/charge character;
* every coefficient in the packet lower bound;
* the Gram projection weight and exact Q-energy formula;
* direct N=4,6,8,10 hypercube contractions;
* the coefficientwise all-N inequality (5.1)-(5.2).

No diagonalization, residual estimate, or numerical extrapolation enters the
verdict.  Normal Python and python -OO produce byte-identical stdout and JSON.

The conclusion applies to the bare .28 packet subspace at the specified
critical coupling.  A dressed low-energy family may mix the packet states
with this complement and could still realize a phase transition.  Nothing
here proves or refutes such a dressed construction, a thermodynamic critical
theory, fermion statistics, chirality, a continuum limit, or a TOE.
