2. Exact first-QH block
=======================

Let |p> be a normalized packet vector satisfying

  L|p> = lambda |p>,       eta=<p|H_E|p>.

For 0<eta<2 define its normalized first orthogonal source image

  |q> = (H_E-eta)|p> / sqrt[eta(2-eta)].                    (2)

Equation (1) gives its norm exactly.  Commutation gives L|q>=lambda|q>,
and the polynomial (1) means that H_E maps span{|p>,|q>} to itself.  Thus
this is not a truncated moment fit: the subspace is exactly invariant under
H_loc.  In its orthonormal basis,

       [ kappa lambda+J eta       J sqrt(eta(2-eta)) ]
  H =  [                                                   ]. (3)
       [ J sqrt(eta(2-eta)) kappa lambda+J(2-eta) ]

The two exact eigenvalues are

  kappa lambda,       kappa lambda + 2J.                    (4)

For every J>0 the lower eigenvector has bare-complement weight eta/2.
This equals3/10 in the s-symmetric channel and1/2 in the antisymmetric
channel, independent of how small J is.  The limit is a resonant dressing
of a J=0 degeneracy, not a perturbatively small state correction.

Equivalently, eliminating |q> gives the exact energy-dependent self-energy

  Sigma(E) = J^2 eta(2-eta)
             / [kappa lambda+J(2-eta)-E],                   (5)

with H_eff(E)=PHP-Sigma(E).  Coupling and P-Q diagonal separation both
vanish linearly with J.  Treating (5) as an ordinary J^2 correction over a

4. Consequence for the bare Ising line
=======================================

The coefficient9J in the packet Ising wall cost was calculated from bare
packet expectations: Omega sees zero event energy, Phi sees3J/2, and an s
bridge sees3J/4.  Equations (3)-(4) show that these expectations are
barycentres of native event eigenvalues, not dressed local energies.  The
first exact local source dressing changes them differently.  These local
corrections are not a common scalar shift, so the bare coefficient9J cannot be
carried into the full source model without a new global calculation.

This does not justify replacing9 by another number.  Neighbouring local
dressings share matter registers, and the mu V term couples different source
charges.  Global compensation, including a changed hopping coefficient, has
not been excluded.  The next justified calculation is the overlap-compatible
global isometry followed by the simultaneous compression of H and V.  Until
then, 2mu=kappa+9J is a bare-compression candidate, not an established
full-source critical line.


5. Same isometry for charge, time and local response
=====================================================

The low local dressed vector is obtained with the actual native projector

  P_0 = I-H_E/2,
  W_0|p> = P_0|p>/sqrt(1-eta/2).                             (6)

Formula (6) is a linear isometry on a fixed eta packet channel. Across
channels the linear map is W_0=P_0 P(P P_0 P)^(-1/2), with the inverse
defined on the support of P P_0 P. A state-dependent scalar normalization
alone is not a single linear operator on the direct sum of channels.

This same map preserves the L eigenvalue, the s symmetric/antisymmetric

It does not leave arbitrary observables unchanged.  Every local observable
must be transported as

Whether V preserves the separate dressed local subspaces, equivalently the

proved global isometry.

6. Exact first overlap test
===========================

There is also a concrete obstruction to multiplying the one-edge maps (6).
Fix two neighbouring source roots alpha,beta on three matter registers and
write their zero-event projectors as

  P_alpha,12=(I+r_alpha tensor r_alpha tensor I)/2,
  P_beta,23 =(I+I tensor r_beta tensor r_beta)/2.

Their exact commutator is

  [P_alpha,12,P_beta,23]
    = r_alpha tensor [r_alpha,r_beta] tensor r_beta /4.       (8)

For the explicit native pair alpha=0,beta=1, whose unnormalised root inner
product is2+2i, the Gaussian-integer rank calculation gives

  dim(ker H_E,alpha,12 intersect ker H_E,beta,23)=18,
  rank [P_alpha,12,P_beta,23]=32.

The ordered product P_alpha,12 P_beta,23 is not idempotent and hence is not a
projector. This excludes the naive commuting-projector construction, not
every normalized isometry on a specified subspace. On their common kernel
both projectors act as the identity. Controls give common-kernel dimension24 for an orthogonal
native pair and28 for the parallel pair; both controls commute and their
ordered products are projectors.

This is the smallest actual overlap test needed after the local dressing
theorem.  It proves that generic adjacent low-event maps cannot simply be
multiplied.  It does not solve the compatible global constraint: the test
holds on a fixed source fibre and omits source superposition, L, V and the
chain vacuum.
