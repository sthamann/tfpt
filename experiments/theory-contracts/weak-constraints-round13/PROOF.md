# Weak homogeneous momentum constraints: an actual regular-torus obstruction

2026-09-06. Round13 independent result; NON-RH, locally integrated unpromoted research.

## Result and exact scope

For the **actual** positive reduced Hamiltonian of Round 12, the three
prescribed centered-difference total momenta cannot be smoothly deformed
into a weakly preserved constraint system through order `g^2` on a
neighborhood of the explicit compact regular free null torus below.
Arbitrary smooth structure functions and a genuinely deformed common zero
surface are allowed. In particular, no such deformation exists on a full
neighborhood of the free origin. This is stronger than strong-conservation
failure: the weak-closure terms are eliminated by an explicit zero-surface
graph calculation, not omitted from the homological equation.

The conclusion is **not** a pointwise flowbox obstruction. A neighborhood
of an entire recurrent torus is required. It does not exclude a construction
on a small nonrecurrent patch, constraints involving an added clock, changed
zeroth-order constraints, a changed Hamiltonian, singular structure
functions, or non-smooth dependence on `g`. A different finite translation
symmetry is unaffected. Coisotropy is not needed for the obstruction:
preservation of the proposed zero surface already fails.

## 1. Fixed actual inputs; no vertex or generator replacement

Use exactly [Round 12's Hamiltonian and conventions](../momentum-deformation-round12/PROOF.md):

\[
 H(g)=H_0+gH_1+g^2R,
 \qquad H_1=\langle q_{TT},\sigma(\phi)\rangle,
 \qquad R=\tfrac12\langle\sigma,P_{TT}\ell^{-1}\sigma\rangle,
 \qquad (P_{TT}\ell^{-1})(0)=0.
 \tag{1}
\]

Here `sigma` is the actual R8 gradient stress, with its staggered tensor
placements and orthonormal off-diagonal weights. The free frequencies are
`omega_k=sqrt(mu+ell(k))`, `r_q=sqrt(ell(q))`, `mu=m^2>=0` and
`ell(k)=sum_i(2-2 cos(k_i))`. All homogeneous TT modes are excluded. The
massless uniform scalar, if present, is its original free canonical pair.

The fixed zeroth-order vector is the actual total centered momentum,

\[
 J_{0,i}=-\pi^TD_i^c\phi-p_{TT}^TD_i^cq_{TT}
 =\sum_k\sin k_i\,|a_k|^2
   +\sum_{q,s}\sin q_i\,|b_{q,s}|^2 .
 \tag{2}
\]

The unitary Fourier and travelling-wave convention is

\[
 \widehat\phi_k=\frac{a_k+\overline a_{-k}}{\sqrt{2\omega_k}},
 \quad
 \widehat\pi_k=-i\sqrt{\frac{\omega_k}{2}}
                  (a_k-\overline a_{-k}),
 \quad \{a_k,\overline a_l\}=-i\delta_{kl}.
 \tag{3}
\]

The amplitudes `a_k` and `a_-k` are independent; their genuine complex
conjugates in (3) ensure real fields. The actual Nyquist TT mediator
`q=(pi,0,0)` is one real oscillator per polarization and has zero centered
momentum weight; it is never double-counted.

Round 12 proves that every nonzero cubic vertex is nonresonant at fixed
finite volume, and constructs a real cubic `S1` satisfying
`{H0,S1}=-H1`. Its locally defined canonical pullback gives

\[
 H'=H_0+g^2B+O(g^3),\qquad
 B=R+\tfrac12\{H_1,S_1\}.
 \tag{4}
\]

The finite-volume cubic denominators, first vertex, static counterterm,
and all exchange channels are exactly those of Round 12. A uniform
large-volume normal-form estimate is not asserted. The cubic flow exists
for small `g` on a neighborhood of each compact torus used here.

## 2. What weak closure means here

Allow arbitrary real smooth, time-independent coefficient functions

\[
 C_i(g)=J_{0,i}+gJ_{1,i}+g^2J_{2,i}+O(g^3)
\]

and arbitrary smooth matrix structure functions `M_i^j(g,z)` on an open
neighborhood of the compact torus, jointly smooth for `g` near zero. The
proposed weak condition is the Taylor identity

\[
 \{C_i(g),H(g)\}=M_i{}^j(g)C_j(g)+O(g^3).
 \tag{5}
\]

Remainders and the phase-space derivatives needed for the vector fields
are locally uniform. The same proof applies to formal coefficient
identities through order two. No assumption `M0=0` is made: at `g=0`,
`M0 J0=0` may be a nontrivial constraint syzygy. The transformed constraints
are `C'=J0+gF1+g^2F2+O(g^3)`, and (5) is preserved by the same canonical
pullback that produces (4).

We only consider the ideal generated by these three momenta. Terms
proportional to an additional energy, clock, or other new constraint are
a different closure problem and are not covered by (5).

## 3. A zero-surface graph lemma that retains all weak terms

Suppose an invariant compact set `T` is contained in the regular zero set
of `J0`. Assume a whole neighborhood admits coordinates `(j,v)`, where
`j=J0`, and

\[
 X_{H_0}j=0,\qquad X_{H_0}v=V_0(v).
 \tag{6}
\]

Thus the free vector field is independent of the normal coordinate `j`.
Equivalently, there are commuting normal fields `Y_a=partial/partial j_a`
with `Y_a J0_b=delta_ab` and `[Y_a,X_H0]=0`. This is an important special
property, not an assumption valid for arbitrary integrable systems.

The parameter-dependent implicit-function theorem, uniformly near a
compact subset, expresses the actual deformed zero surface as

\[
 C'(g,j,v)=0 \iff j=\chi_g(v),
 \quad \chi_g=g\chi_1+g^2\chi_2+O(g^3),
\]
\[
 \chi_1=-F_1(0,v),\qquad
 \chi_2=(\partial_jF_1)(0,v)F_1(0,v)-F_2(0,v).
 \tag{7}
\]

The derivative `partial_j C'` is invertible there. Differentiating the
zero-surface identity with respect to `v` gives

\[
 dC'(X_{H'})=(\partial_jC')
 \left[X_{H'}j-(d\chi_g)X_{H'}v\right]
 \quad\hbox{on } j=\chi_g(v).
 \tag{8}
\]

The entire `M C'` term of weak closure is zero on this **shifted** surface,
irrespective of its zeroth-order syzygies or higher structure functions.
Consequently the bracket in square brackets is `O(g^3)`. By (4) and (6),

\[
 X_{H'}j=g^2\{J_0,B\}(0,v)+O(g^3),
\]
\[
 (d\chi_g)X_{H'}v
   =gV_0\chi_1+g^2V_0\chi_2+O(g^3).
\]

Evaluation of the `g^2` forcing at `j=chi_g` instead of zero changes it
only at order three; the order-two tangential perturbation multiplied by
`dchi_g=O(g)` also first contributes at order three. Hence

\[
 V_0\chi_1=0,\qquad
 V_0\chi_2=\{J_0,B\}|_{j=0}.
 \tag{9}
\]

**Necessary condition:** every invariant probability measure on `T` must
annihilate the mean of the vector forcing on the right of (9). Indeed,
the mean of a derivative along the invariant flow of a smooth
single-valued function is zero.

This is not the strong-conservation equation with renamed coefficients.
The actual second-order graph coefficient contains the additional term
`(partial_j F1)F1`. It accounts for the normally displaced constraint
surface and the possible `M1 F1` contribution. A direct jet check is in
the executable. If (6) fails, extra terms can occur at order two and this
lemma cannot be used without further work.

## 4. Explicit regular three-constraint null torus in the actual model

Initially `L=6`, `n=L^3`. Momentum labels are triples modulo six. Occupy
the following six scalar travelling modes, and no others:

\[
 k=(1,1,0),\quad l=(2,5,0),\quad -k=(5,5,0),\quad-l=(4,1,0),
 \quad h=(0,0,1),\quad-h=(0,0,5).
\]

For any `epsilon>0` set

\[
 a_k=a_{-k}=\epsilon e^{-i\theta_1},\quad
 a_l=\epsilon e^{-i\theta_2},\quad
 a_{-l}=i\epsilon e^{-i\theta_2},\quad
 a_h=a_{-h}=\epsilon e^{-i\theta_3},
 \quad \theta\in\mathbb T^3.
 \tag{10}
\]

Set every TT coordinate and momentum to zero. For `mu=0`, fix the
uniform scalar momentum to zero and its coordinate to any fixed value.
This is a compact `H0`-invariant torus with

\[
 V_0|_T=\sqrt{\mu+2}\,\partial_{\theta_1}
       +\sqrt{\mu+4}\,\partial_{\theta_2}
       +\sqrt{\mu+1}\,\partial_{\theta_3}.
 \tag{11}
\]

All three components of `J0` vanish exactly. Unlike the four-mode
Round-12 torus, this torus is regular for **all three** momenta. Put
`s=sqrt(3)/2`; the weights of the three occupied modes `k,l,h` are columns
of

\[
 W=s\begin{pmatrix}1&1&0\\1&-1&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad \det W=-\frac{3\sqrt3}{4}\ne0.
 \tag{12}
\]

All six occupied actions are positive. On a neighborhood use their
action-angle coordinates `a_p=sqrt(I_p) exp(-i theta_p)` and retain
ordinary Cartesian coordinates for unoccupied scalar and TT modes.
Take the three independent action directions

\[
 Y_x=(\partial_{I_k}+\partial_{I_l})/(2s),\quad
 Y_y=(\partial_{I_k}-\partial_{I_l})/(2s),\quad
 Y_z=\partial_{I_h}/s.
 \tag{13}
\]

They satisfy `Y_i J0_j=delta_ij` and commute with the complete free vector
field: every occupied frequency is independent of the actions, and the
remaining free Cartesian vector field is independent of these actions.
They also commute with one another. Using their local flows yields the
product coordinates in (6) over a whole neighborhood of the compact torus.

For clarity, `j` here includes the **total** scalar plus TT momentum.
The nonactive contribution is constant along the free flow. Solving the
three selected scalar actions for this total `j`, with all other variables
fixed, is therefore a smooth coordinate change and does not introduce
`j` dependence into `V0`. The TT origin is merely the torus's base point
in those Cartesian directions; the ambient neighborhood allows nonzero
TT coordinates and momenta. The argument never divides by a TT action
at zero, nor restricts weak closure to the noninvariant interacting slice
`TT=0`.

## 5. The full averaged forcing remains exactly nonzero

Let

\[
 M=a_k a_l\overline a_{-k}\overline a_{-l}.
\]

The actual quartic coefficient in `B` is the Round-12 result

\[
 C(\mu,n)=\frac{9(2\mu+5)}
 {64n(4\mu+7)\sqrt{(\mu+2)(\mu+4)}}>0.
 \tag{14}
\]

It includes both dynamic Nyquist exchange channels as well as `R`:

\[
 C=b^2\left[\frac24
 -\frac1{4-(\omega_k+\omega_l)^2}
 -\frac1{4-(\omega_k-\omega_l)^2}\right],
 \quad b^2=\frac9{32n\omega_k\omega_l}.
 \tag{15}
\]

The third pair partition at `2k,2l` has zero actual TT vertex (and a
zero-frequency source would also cancel between static and exchange
terms). The excluded homogeneous TT mode is not a mediator. The checker
reconstructs the actual staggered projector and these vertices and
independently recomputes the normal-form Poisson bracket.

The added pair `+/-h` does **not** change the averaged forcing, even when
its amplitude is one. This follows from an exhaustive, exact finite
selection rule, not an assumed spectator decoupling:

1. `B` is quartic and invariant under actual finite lattice translations.
2. Terms with any TT factor vanish on the torus. Scalar contractions in
   `{H1,S1}` leave two TT factors and thus also vanish there. The remaining
   pure-scalar exchange contains all actual intermediate TT channels.
3. Haar averaging over the three phases retains only monomials balanced
   separately in the `+/-k`, `+/-l`, and `+/-h` frequency species.
4. Enumerating every degree-four monomial in the twelve amplitude and
   conjugate variables, those with species balance, zero lattice wave
   vector, and **any nonzero** centered momentum charge are precisely
   `M` and `conj(M)`. Their charges are `(4,0,0)` and `(-4,0,0)` in units
   `s`. This enumeration also covers all possible spectator terms.

Thus only the already computed coefficient (14) can contribute to the
mean of `{J0,B}`. Since `M=-i epsilon^4` on (10),

\[
 \int_{\mathbb T^3}\{J_0,B\}\,d\theta/(2\pi)^3
   =\bigl(4\sqrt3\,C(\mu,n)\epsilon^4,0,0\bigr)\ne0.
 \tag{16}
\]

No irrational-frequency or ergodicity hypothesis is needed: Haar measure
is invariant under every constant-frequency flow in (11). In particular,
at `mu=4/7`, `L=6`, `epsilon=1`, all six initial amplitudes have modulus
one, and

\[
 C=\frac{301}{2396160},\qquad
 \int\{J_{0,x},B\}=\frac{301\sqrt3}{599040}.
 \tag{17}
\]

This is an exact reproducible regular witness; no unspecified small
spectator amplitude or continuity bound remains.

## 6. Conclusion and limits

Equations (9) and (16) contradict one another. Therefore (5) has no
solution on any full neighborhood of the torus (10), for any finite
`mu>=0`. Scaling every occupied amplitude by `epsilon` places such a
regular torus arbitrarily close to the free origin, while its obstruction
remains a nonzero constant times `epsilon^4`. Thus smooth weak closure on
a full origin neighborhood is impossible as well. The singularity of
the momentum map at the origin is **not** being used as a regularity
assumption; the contradiction occurs on nearby regular tori.

For each cubic `L` divisible by six, choose the same physical angles.
All selection rules, the rank calculation and vertices are unchanged;
only `n=L^3` in (14) changes. The coefficient remains positive.

This theorem excludes even weak preservation of the prescribed
time-independent three-constraint zero surface by the unchanged actual
Hamiltonian through order two. It consequently excludes a coisotropic or
first-class realization with these same hypotheses, but is **not** a
theorem against every possible gravitational constraint completion.
Clock-dependent Dirac observables, changed constraints involving clock
momenta, or weak closure modulo additional constraints are outside (5).
Likewise a local transverse flowbox need not wrap the recurrent torus;
no pointwise no-go or global normal-form convergence is claimed.
