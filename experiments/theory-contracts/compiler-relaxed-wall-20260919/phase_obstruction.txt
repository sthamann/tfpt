NATIVE CENTRAL CHARACTER OF THE REMAINING K=3 COMPETITORS

The native reflection words X=S01 S12 S23 and
Z=S13(i) S13(1) diag(1,1,-1,1) satisfy XZX*Z*=-i I4.
phase_obstruction.py verifies each factor against the actual sixty native
reflections and computes the exact Fourier-source actions. On source charge
q their commutator is i^q I60. Consequently, with five matter registers and
total source charge K, the simultaneous joint commutator is(-i)^(5-K) I.

Every term of the full Hamiltonian commutes with these simultaneous actions:
the native reflection sum is permuted by conjugation, the conditioned event
projector transforms with its source root, and the relative root metric is
unitarily invariant. This holds at all J,kappa,mu in the specified family.

On K=3 the two joint unitaries anticommute. Within any energy eigenspace,
the second maps each eigenspace of the first at eigenvalue z to that at-z.
These are distinct, and the map is unitary. Every K=3 energy eigenspace
therefore has even dimension. Similarly K=0 and2 multiplicities are divisible
by4; K=1 has no such divisibility obstruction.

This is not an energy bound, and vacuum degeneracy is not prohibited by
assumption. The K=3 states remain legitimate global-ground competitors.
