1. Exact conditional variances

Let E denote normalized partial trace over one, or both, matter registers of
a local L. Its operator variance is E(L^2)-E(L)^2. The supplied checker builds
L=N/240 with N Gaussian integral from the actual sixty reflections. For
trace dimension d=4 or16, it forms

  Var=[d Tr(N^2)-Tr(N)^2]/(d^2 240^2).

After dividing an explicitly computed common integer factor, the numerator
is Hermitian Gaussian integral. Its polynomial is checked using exact integer
arithmetic in binary64: an explicit product of Gaussian row-l1 norms bounds
every intermediate integer below2^53. No rounded eigenvalues are used to
prove the following upper bounds or band containments.

  0      1/80,11/720,7/400,3/80                             3/80
  1      0,2/225,1/75,4/225,2/75                           2/75

For phase0 with both matter registers traced, the uniform source is the

2. Comparison method and covariance

Choose two disjoint Omega packet projectors on nonadjacent B=barSym2
sources; let P be their product, Q=I-P. On Q, at least one outer B local
term costs2/3. All remaining local floors are retained. Let A and C be
lower bounds for PHP and QHQ, and b^2 bound ||QHP||^2. Then

  H >= (A+C-sqrt((A-C)^2+4b^2))/2.

To exceed U=391/320 it is enough to check A>U, C>U,
(A-U)(C-U)>b^2. All stated scalar comparisons are checked algebraically.

There are two DIFFERENT covariance geometries. For central configurations,
the two remaining terms use separate legs from independent outer packets.
Tracing those two independent legs factorizes the conditional product even
when the terms share the remaining middle register C. Their connected cross
covariance vanishes and the variances add.

For boundary/bridge configurations, the two terms instead touch BOTH legs
of the same Omega packet. Their joint marginal is P_sym/10; the covariance
does not generally vanish. For arbitrary operator-valued X,Y acting on the
two legs, after subtracting their normalized trace means, the contraction is

  E_Omega(X_B Y_C)-E_B X E_C Y
    =(1/5) E_4[(X-E_4 X)(Y-E_4 Y)].

The external operator factors act on disjoint spaces. Identifying the two
4-dimensional leg indices on the right gives the usual product in the
normalized partial-trace algebra. Operator Cauchy-Schwarz bounds the norm
by sqrt(v_X v_Y)/5. Tracing the second independent packet leg first can only
reduce the bridge variance by the Kadison inequality. Therefore the valid
boundary bound, INCLUDING both ordered cross terms, is

  b^2 <= v1+v2+(2/5)sqrt(v1 v2).                            (2.1)

For the uniform source bridge, its partially traced operator is exactly3I/4,
so its cross covariance really vanishes. For a nontrivial phase0 bridge,
v2<=29/1200 and v1<=2/75 imply the full bound (2.1) is at most77/1200.
This is why the common77/1200 core bound remains valid, including arbitrary
phase0 source classes. The checker verifies this scalar comparison exactly.
The raw variance bounds must not simply be added for a nonuniform bridge.
