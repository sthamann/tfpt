Question
========

Does the complete q=(2,2) source sector have energy at least 3/5, rather
than only the earlier universal adjacent-packet bound 3/10?

Write the sixty-dimensional q2 source representation as

  B direct-sum X,

where B=barSym2(C4) has dimension10 and contains the unique packet Omega,
and X=B-perp has dimension50.  The four pair blocks are BB, BX, XB and XX.

1. BB is exact
================

For terminal label c, let

  TL_c = Omega_(1,AB) Xi_(2,C;c),
  TR_c = Xi_(1,A;c) Omega_(2,BC).

The native second reflection moment

  mean(bar(r)_free tensor r_B)=(I+4 P_Bell)/5

gives, for each c,

  <TL_c|TR_d>=delta_cd/4,
  H TL_c=(4 TL_c-TR_c)/5,
  H TR_c=(-TL_c+4 TR_c)/5.

Thus their eight-dimensional span is invariant.  Its Gram and coefficient
action are

  G=[[1,1/4],[1/4,1]],
  h=[[4/5,-1/5],[-1/5,4/5]],

with energies 3/5 and1, each fourfold.  The only nonzero principal overlap of
the two packet ranges is the TL/TR channel.  On its orthogonal complement the
packet projectors are mutually orthogonal.  Since the exact B local gap is
2/3,

  H_BB >= (2/3)(2-Q1-Q2) >=2/3

there.  Hence the BB floor is exactly3/5 with multiplicity4.

2. The decisive BX bound
==========================

The actual BX Hamiltonian obeys the lower comparison

  H_BX >= L_X + (2/3)(I-Q_Omega).                         (1)

This comparison is sufficient for a lower bound; the following resolvent
condition is not claimed to be necessary for the actual Hamiltonian.

At target E=3/5, set

  D=L_X+2/3-E=L_X+1/15.

The projection Schur complement says that the right side of (1) is at least

The exact full q2 local spectrum and the fact that the unique kernel lies in

With N=120 B2 it verifies exactly

The exact rank-ten B projector is D_B/96, where

  (D_B)_(lm)=(<ray_m,ray_l>)^2,

It commutes with N, and the entire N=60 spectral projector lies in B.
