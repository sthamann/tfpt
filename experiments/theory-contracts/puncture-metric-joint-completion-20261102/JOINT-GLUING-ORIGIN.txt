CENTRAL IDEA

The simplest useful common object is an amplitude with its COMPLETE boundary
data. Composition then integrates the common boundary data once. Higgs values,
fermion labels, orientation, domains, reference factors and their correlations
are parts of that data. It is incorrect to infer a primitive process by first
discarding the Higgs boundary information and then requiring the remaining
fermion kernel to compose as though nothing had been discarded.

This observation is a structural restriction on a proposed origin route, not
a derivation of the original TFPT amplitude. It does not pick the Higgs action
or distribution, identify physical time, produce 3+1 dimensions, or close F1.

SMALLEST DECISIVE TEST, ANALYTIC

Use the already given constant-H Hermitian finite source h_H on the common
original 96-dimensional carrier. This is exactly the R096/v252 constructor,
not a new action. Let mu be any positive normalized measure for which the
following finite-matrix expressions are integrable. Define

 T_t(H)=exp(-t h_H),     K_t=integral T_t(H) dmu(H).

Every full conditional fiber composes: T_2t(H)=T_t(H)^2. Averaging ONCE over
one common H, rather than drawing H independently in the two halves, gives
the exact identity

 K_2t - K_t^2 = integral (T_t(H)-K_t)^2 dmu(H) >= 0.       (1)

Proof: expand the square. No commutation between h_H and h_H' is assumed.
For any t>0, equality in (1) holds exactly when T_t(H) is mu-almost surely
constant: take the trace of the positive integrand to obtain the square of
its Hilbert-Schmidt norm. The selfadjoint logarithm is unique, so equality
is equivalent to h_H being mu-almost surely constant.

Thus demanding ordinary lossless semigroup gluing AFTER static Higgs
marginalization eliminates all nontrivial H-dependent fibers on the support.
It cannot serve as a general origin principle for dynamical Higgs coupling.
The original source may instead retain the Higgs interface variable or an
equivalent sufficient memory. This is the physically relevant alternative.

The same averaged kernel also has a positive time-Hankel form:

 sum_ij <v_i,K_(t_i+t_j)v_j>
   = integral ||sum_i exp(-t_i h_H)v_i||^2 dmu(H) >= 0.     (2)

Hence this elementary positivity does not repair the marginal gluing defect.
Equation (2) is a finite kernel identity, not an identification with physical
OS time or a proof of a positive-energy spectrum of the unshifted h_H.
The separately established full even-CAR reflection positivity is also
preserved by positive mixtures when the reflection and algebra are common,
simply by linearity. A mixture need not remain quasifree or primitive.

At small t, (1) reads

 K_2t-K_t^2 = t^2 [ integral h_H^2 dmu - (integral h_H dmu)^2 ] + O(t^3).

For radial H=(0,r), the existing constructor has h_r=h_0+r V in the same
fixed chart. For equal weights at r=0,1, the leading defect is t^2 V^2/4.
The two radii are different gauge-invariant H^dagger H, not merely rotated
weak frames. They are diagnostic background configurations, not complete
different theories.

FIRST ADDITIONAL PREMISE

Equations (1)-(2) assume a common positive normalized static Higgs weight and
the stated constant-H fibers. Exactly this common positive pushforward is
not yet derived from full original Z_adm; R096 preserves that boundary.
The extra assumption needed to turn marginal gluing into a selector would
be that the retained fermion boundary label is already a sufficient statistic
for every subsequent H-dependent process. Equation (1) shows precisely how
that extra memorylessness assumption fails in the stated class.
