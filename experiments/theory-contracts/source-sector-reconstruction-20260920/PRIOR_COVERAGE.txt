Fixed lattice facts already established
----------------------------------------
The exact source-charged-lift contract, PROOF.txt sections 2--5, fixes

  Gamma = Z^10, K = diag(1^9,-1),
  Gamma0 = {x in Gamma : g.x is even},  h = n/2,  f = e9,
  L_h = Gamma0 + Z h,  Gamma = Gamma0 + Z f,

with four Gamma0-classes ordered (0,h,f,h+f).  Their quadratic values are
(0,0,1/2,0), and the two even neighbors Gamma0+Zh and
Gamma0+Z(h+f) are distinguished; the chosen T(E8) lift selects L_h.
The proof also establishes that q and Y annihilate n and z, that the original
odd Gamma fields are absent from the local even neighbor, and that the
complement restores the missing mutual-locality phase.  This is a local
lattice/gluing result.  It does not define a Z2 automorphism of L_h, a
twisted GNS module, or an identification of f+L_h with the original odd
fields.

What remains unproved for the proposed recovery
-----------------------------------------------
1. No existing contract constructs a Z2 automorphism of L_h whose twisted
   module is f+L_h, with the required fusion, locality, spin structure, and
   original Gamma fermion parity.
2. The local L_h extension is bosonic.  A putative f+L_h object would be a
   defect/twisted module or line endpoint until locality and its operator
   algebra are supplied; calling it the old odd Gamma sector would exceed the
   established result.
3. No common source-derived Hamiltonian has been shown to mix the sectors for
   the special autonomous memory hypothesis.  The effective H=aL0+c preserves
   the extension grade, so its QHP and the associated Feshbach memory kernel
   vanish, while a charged field/intertwiner may still satisfy QV_xP != 0.
   This dynamical condition is separate from reconstructing the odd fermion
   field itself; the needed source map and, independently, any nonzero
   Hamiltonian cross-sector corner remain open.
4. No source-derived Majorana/bosonization map is established.  Existing
   sine-Gordon, Ising, and SO(16)_1 statements are conditional continuum or
   vertex-algebra inputs.
5. Existing cos(n.phi) references concern selected n/z cosine diagnostics.
   There is no source coefficient, RG selection, vacuum/domain construction,
   or native interaction proving the required sector recovery.
