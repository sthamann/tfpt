1. Global gauge descent replaces the extra block-permutation premise

The character i^(g.n) equals 1 precisely when n_9=-1, leaving 56. Write

Then c+w+f=3. For an auxiliary vectorlike pair of SU(3)/SU(2) singlets of
common hypercharge y, Y10=(Y8,y,y) gives

 Y10.n = w-2c/3+2y; hence y=c/3-w/2.                 (1)

The full source tensor, including epsilon, must still be kept. With one

       =[[0,h3,-h2],[-h3,0,h1],[h2,-h1,0]].           (4)

For arbitrary COMPLEX h,

 A^dagger A=||h||^2 I-h h^dagger.                   (5)
For h!=0, its singular values are (||h||,||h||,0). Its determinant is zero.
Invertible left/right changes of basis or normalization preserve this rank.
The direct single-Higgs-family reading therefore cannot give three nonzero

There is a more basic statistics issue for a direct common-field 4D reading.
The local Lorentz scalar psi_I^alpha psi_J^beta epsilon_(alpha beta) is

This is not a vanishing theorem for the original three-current correlation,

3. What the selected auxiliary charge permits

At the level of internal representations, let the conjugate massive
components have E:(1,1)_(-1), E^c:(1,1)_(+1). With the original one Higgs
H:(1,2)_(+1/2), the following internal structures are gauge-invariant:

 L H^dagger E^c,       E e^c,       M E E^c.           (6)

The doublet indices in the first term admit the singlet contraction. A
colourless Higgs cannot turn a colour triplet/antitriplet and an auxiliary
singlet into a colour singlet. Consequently there is no analogous direct
coloured-light/singlet-heavy bilinear mixing within this declared class.

This is a concrete possible source of unequal lepton/down response; it is
not the derivation of a nonzero coefficient in (6). The boundary pair remains

an actual spacetime/statistics-preserving map, absent in the present source.
Colour-preserving multivertex loop effects and additional coloured fields
are not excluded by this simple bilinear statement.

4. The precise complementary matrix elements needed to lift the missing mass

Even in the more generous directed-slot reading (4), a genuine coupling to
one massive complementary channel would enter a full chiral MASS block as

 M_full = [[A(h), b], [c^T, M]], M!=0,
 M_eff  = A(h)-b c^T/M.                              (7)

Here b,c,M are unspecified SOURCE MATRIX ELEMENTS, not fitted parameters or
a new dynamical model. Equation(7) is a necessary diagnostic for this matching
class; the preceding statistics obstruction has not been repaired by writing it.

Since adj A=h h^T, the determinant identities are exact:

 det M_full = -(h^T b)(c^T h),
 det M_eff  = -(h^T b)(c^T h)/M.                     (8)

Thus rank is restored iff both overlaps are nonzero. A large complement or
an arbitrary complex heavy mass alone is insufficient. The source must
couple into AND out of the missing family direction. In the full determinant
M times det M_eff, the inverse-M phase of the reduced determinant cancels.
A phase inserted only into M while A,b,c remain fixed does not change the
full chiral MASS determinant. Relative phase information, if present in
this matching class, resides in the actual mixing overlaps as well.

Limit of (8): this is a finite chiral mass-block identity. It is NOT a
calculation of the full Euclidean/source functional, a gauge-field Ward
identity, an anomaly coefficient, or a proof that every other CP observable
is independent of M. Those quantities also need kinetic operators, measure,
backgrounds, fields and regulator. In particular an extra hidden factor may
still matter elsewhere even when it cancels in this particular determinant.

This is the direct operational form of the user's complement proposal for
the actual rank obstruction: derive b and c from the common source, prove
the two overlaps nonzero, and retain M in the full phase accounting.
