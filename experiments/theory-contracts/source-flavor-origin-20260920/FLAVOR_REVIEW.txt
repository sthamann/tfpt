INDEPENDENT BOUNDED CHECK: E6 CUBIC DIRECT-USE TEST (2026-09-20)

Source inspected:
experiments/theory-contracts/compiler-vacuum-current-cubic-20260918/PROOF.txt:66-100,107-124
experiments/theory-contracts/compiler-vacuum-current-cubic-20260918/checker.py:172-188,214-248,250-280
experiments/theory-contracts/compiler-vacuum-current-cubic-20260918/certificate.json:402-414

The certificate is exact and has verdict PARTIAL with mathematical verdict
EXACT_SOURCE_VACUUM_RESPONSE_AND_UNIQUE_E6_CUBIC. It verifies

  C_(A,i)(B,j)(C,k) = d_ABC epsilon_ijk,

with d symmetric, 270 nonzero ordered entries / 45 squarefree polynomial
monomials, complete E6 Ward invariance, Ward rank 44 and invariant dimension
one. The source proof itself says the remaining implication is raw seam state
plus actual local charged operators -> the marked affine E8 vacuum current
algebra, and that physical Yukawa couplings/mass hierarchy are not derived.

The exact neutral Hd index is 12, with doubled root [0,0,0,0,-2,-2,0,0].
Reading the certificate monomial list gives precisely these four 16*16*10
terms (index labels supplied by the contract's marked dictionary):

  (2[e], 24[L]) -1
  (3[Q], 23[d]) +1
  (5[Q], 21[d]) -1
  (8[d], 18[Q]) -1.

Direct-use test, scope fixed: replace the three 27-sector factors by 4D
left-Weyl fields in 16 x 3 and a scalar 10 x 3, with one Higgs family vector
h_k. The coefficient for two fermions is

  T_(A,i)(B,j) = d_ABH epsilon_ijk h_k.

Since d_ABH is symmetric and epsilon_ijk is antisymmetric under the combined
exchange (A,i) <-> (B,j), T is antisymmetric in the combined field labels.
The Lorentz scalar left-Weyl bilinear is symmetric under exchange of its two
Grassmann field slots (the spinor epsilon and Grassmann swap cancel). Hence
this naive same-field zero-derivative Yukawa term vanishes after the required
combined symmetrization.

Even if one restricts first to distinguishable Q,d or L,e slots, the family
matrix is

  M_ij = epsilon_ijk h_k.

For complex h, direct index contraction gives the exact identity

  M^dagger M = ||h||^2 I - h h^dagger.

Therefore h != 0 gives rank(M)=2, det(M)=0, and singular values
(||h||,||h||,0). I checked the orientation numerically for a generic complex
h; the correct outer product for the convention M_ij=epsilon_ijk h_k is
h h^dagger (the conjugate-reversed outer product is wrong). Thus one scalar
family vector cannot supply a full-rank 3x3 Down or lepton mass matrix.

This is a decisive negative only for the direct zero-derivative use of this
specific source cubic with one Higgs-family vector. It does not rule out an
E6 Yukawa with an independent symmetric family tensor, nonlocal or
time-ordered dynamics, complement-generated terms, additional Higgs/source
components, or a different Lorentz/operator contraction. It also does not
turn the exact current cubic into the missing physical F_d(theta),F_e(theta)
origin bridge above.
