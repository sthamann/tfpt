## Why information suggests logarithmic weights

Let I:N_positive -> R satisfy I(mn)=I(m)+I(n) and monotonicity in the
ordinary numerical order. Then I(n)=c log(n), c>=0.
Proof: I(1)=0. Fix m,n>=2; r_k=floor(k log(n)/log(m)). From
m^r_k<=n^k<m^(r_k+1) obtain r_k I(m)<=k I(n)<=(r_k+1)I(m).
Divide by k and take the limit. This also covers the identically zero case.
Without monotonicity the general solution is I(n)=sum_p v_p(n) w_p with
arbitrary real prime weights w_p. Multiplicative additivity alone does not
select log(p). This lemma selects an information functional under its given
order; it does not identify that functional with physical energy or time.

For independent uniform alternatives, counts multiply and information adds;
for maximally mixed quantum states S(I_n/n)=log(n) and tensor dimensions
multiply. Nonuniform probabilities, entangled states and generic physical
Hamiltonians are not determined by dimension alone. Prime dimension does not
mean an individual quantum state has intrinsic algorithmic complexity.

## The exact quantum dictionary, and what it does not prove

On l2(N_positive), H|n>=log(n)|n> on its natural weighted l2 domain.
H is positive self-adjoint. For beta>1,

    Tr exp(-beta H)=sum_n n^(-beta)=zeta(beta).

With V_m|n>=|mn>, V_m is an isometry, not a surjective unitary for m>1.
On finite-support vectors and via the unitary groups,

    [H,V_m]=log(m) V_m,
    exp(itH) V_m exp(-itH)=m^(it) V_m.

Thus scale multiplication is represented by additive energy differences.
Unique factorization identifies n with the finite-support occupation list
(v_p(n))_p, giving H=sum_p log(p) N_p and the bosonic Euler product.
The same diagonal partition sum has a classical statistical interpretation:
the sum alone neither witnesses coherence nor derives quantum theory.

Primary source: Marcolli--Tabuada, *Bost-Connes systems, categorification,
quantum statistical mechanics, and Weil numbers*, arXiv:1411.3223;
https://www.its.caltech.edu/~matilde/MotivicBostConnesNov11.pdf .
See also Cohen, JTNB 11 (1999), pp.15-30,
https://www.numdam.org/item/JTNB_1999__11_1_15_0.pdf .

Physical time evolution is not established for TFPT by defining this H.
Its eigenvalues log(n) are not the nontrivial zeta zeros. Self-adjointness
here therefore does not imply RH; the trace formula above converges only
for beta>1 and cannot be used as a Gibbs trace in the critical strip.

## Primitive cycles versus arithmetic primes

For a suitable dynamical system a zeta Euler product can run over primitive
closed orbits. 'Primitive' means not a repetition of a shorter orbit, not
that its integer period is a prime number. For the full two-symbol shift,
the orbit of periodic word 0001 has primitive period 4. Consequently the
mere appearance of an orbit product cannot identify ordinary arithmetic.
Reference: Baladi, *Periodic orbits and dynamical spectra*,
https://www.math.umd.edu/~dolgop/BalSur.pdf .

An arithmetic trace target must additionally match log(p) lengths and the
correct repetition weights, not just the product's shape.

## Correction to the historical seam exclusion

seam_bc_primon_bridge_probe.py checks finite ladders at N=16,32,64 and
contrasts empirical counting slopes with a primon ladder. A finite fermionic
partition function is indeed entire and cannot equal zeta globally. However
those finite slope samples do not prove an exclusion for every infinite
refinement/scaling limit. The historical 'KILLED at probe level' wording must
not be upgraded to an all-limit theorem. No positive bridge follows either.
