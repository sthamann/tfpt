# An even bridge: parity can be respected, but access and energy are not free

2026-09-12. NON-RH finite-source conditional construction. No native coupling
or free Hamiltonian was changed. A proposed readout is not thereby derived.

## Fixed source and two added readouts

Use the unchanged D=J+B/8 and the clock projector P from boundary_access.py.
Set Q=I-P, b=e_10 and v=Q(e_0+e_6). Exact arithmetic gives

    ||b||^2=1, ||v||^2=7/6, b.v=0,
    span{v,Dv,...,D^5v}=im Q (dimension six).

With {gamma(x),gamma(y)}=2 x.y, define

    F=i gamma(b) gamma(v).

F is Hermitian and fermion-parity even, F^2=(7/6)I. It is a bilinear
cross-observable, not an individually accessible odd field. Its coefficient
matrix b v^T-v b^T has nonzero P-to-Q block. Its clock average is zero.
Thus parity is respected, while invariance under the clock and microscopic
access remain separate obligations.

## Conditional generation of all quadratic observables

Assume the full even boundary quadratic algebra so(im P) is accessible, as
well as F; close the observable algebra under commutators and the original
free evolution. This is an algebraic access assumption, NOT control authority.
Write x wedge y=x y^T-y x^T. Commutators with so(im P) take b wedge v to
x wedge v for every x in im P (dimension ten). Next

    [D,x wedge v]=(Dx) wedge v+x wedge(Dv).

Since Dx is still in im P, the first term is already available. Iterating
gives x wedge D^k v, hence all im P wedge im Q by the verified cyclicity.
For fixed unit x in im P, commutators of x wedge y and x wedge z give,
up to sign, y wedge z for y,z in im Q. Therefore the quadratic Lie algebra
is so(16), dimension 45+60+15=120. Every operator here is parity even.

This is not the full 256-dimensional matrix algebra as a Lie algebra.
Quadratic unitary evolution is Gaussian; it does not supply arbitrary
many-body control. Associative products and commutators, physical control
protocols, and state preparation have different meanings and costs.

## Exact vacuum discriminator on the complete 256-state Fock space

Use the same complex modes as the source, gamma_(2j)=c_j+c_j*,
gamma_(2j+1)=-i(c_j-c_j*). The real vector v has only even coordinates;
let c_v=sum_j v_(2j)c_j and c_b=c_5. A number-neutral counterpart is

    G=c_b* c_v+c_v* c_b.

Both F and G commute with total fermion parity. But [N,G]=0 and
G|vac>=0, whereas F|vac> is a nonzero two-particle vector of norm squared
7/6. These identities were checked on the full 256-state CAR matrices.
The complex hidden vector is also cyclic under the unchanged one-body h:
rank[v8,h v8,h^2 v8]=3. No full u(8) control claim is inferred from that rank.

If a pulse generated by F were independently available, put Fbar=F/sqrt(7/6).
Then exactly

    exp(-i theta Fbar)|vac>
      =cos(theta)|vac>-i sin(theta)Fbar|vac>,
    <N>=2 sin(theta)^2.

The pulse is an EXTRA interaction during application, even though the free
source Hamiltonian is left unchanged in this comparison. Its duration,
strength, charge exchange and energy supply are not source-derived.
At the positive source point the vacuum is the free ground state, so pair
excitation costs energy. Parity conservation alone does not enforce total
particle number or gauge charge conservation.

Conversely G-generated unitaries and spectral measurements leave the empty
vacuum unchanged (every function of G acts as a scalar on it). Number-neutral
access can probe occupied sectors but cannot create occupation from this
vacuum. Neither changing preparation nor postulating a reservoir is free.
