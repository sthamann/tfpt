1. The first literal compression test

Let epsilon be a self-adjoint involution and P_+=(I+epsilon)/2.
If P is an orthogonal projection with Ran P contained in Ran P_+, then

  epsilon P=P, and epsilon restricted to Ran P is I.

Consequently any carrier E contained in Ran P, with its involution defined
as the restriction of this same epsilon, has E_-=0. This proof does not
depend on dimension, regularity, numerical cutoffs or a gap model.

The archived source 01_boundary_kernel_source.tex gives
Pprim=P_Seam,+ P_prim,gap (line256), with P_Seam,+=(I+iC)/2,
and calls H_car the finite carrier selected after primitive admissibility
compression (line276), defining epsilon_car=iC|H_car (line279).
Under the literal inclusion H_car subset Ran Pprim these statements force
E_-=0. In particular the proposed amplitude with a Lambda^2 E_- leg is zero.
This rules out that literal route to a nonzero 3+2 carrier/Yukawa tensor.

If 'carrier' instead denotes an internal coefficient space before the
state projection, the inclusion is not justified and the contradiction
does not apply. But then the restriction formula is not the asserted
construction: an actual map from the source to an internal involution is
required. The archived Paper2 already lists this upstream bridge as open.
This contract is not a new failure of the conditional Lean algebra.

An isometric transport W:E->Ran P does not evade the test: W*epsilon W=I.
Thus 'transport' alone is insufficient without changing the source operator
or the source space. The active top-level source no longer asserts the
archived primitive theorem; it treats the Calderon/interface step as open.

2. Independent test in the actual pure CAR class

Let K be the complexification of a finite real Majorana one-particle space,
J its conjugation, and C a pure self-dual CAR covariance:

  C=C*=C^2, J C J=I-C.

For epsilon=2C-I, J epsilon J=-epsilon. J is therefore an antiunitary
bijection between the plus and minus eigenspaces. Their dimensions agree.
The same holds on any finite J-stable, epsilon-invariant subspace.
Thus this same epsilon cannot have finite signature (3,2) on such a space.
If E=Ran C is the annihilation/polarized complex space instead, then
epsilon|E=I. E need not be J-stable; it does not retain both eigenvalues.

This is conditional on the self-dual CAR relation, not a claim that every
geometric Calderon projector automatically satisfies it. The current v113
kernel does: its covariance is C=(I+iA)/2 with A real antisymmetric and
A^2=-I. Thus conjugation takes C to I-C. At g=5, the real vector carrier
has dimension10 and C has rank5. The involution epsilon_C=2C-I has
complex signature(5,5), while its restriction to Ran C is +I.

3. What the kernel alone can canonically determine

The stabilizer of this pure polarization in O(10) is U(5), acting on E=Ran C
as its complex fundamental representation. Any complex-linear operator
on E natural under all automorphisms of these data must commute with U(5).
Diagonal phases force it diagonal; coordinate permutations force all
diagonal entries equal. Therefore it is scalar. A self-adjoint involution
among these operators is +I or -I, never a 3+2 split.

Given a chosen three-plane W in E one CAN define
epsilon_int=-P_W+P_(Wperp), Y=-(1/3)P_W+(1/2)P_(Wperp).
The choices form U(5)/(U(3)xU(2)), real dimension12.
That is a classification of the missing choice, not its origin.
These twelve coordinates are not claimed as twelve physical parameters:
the planes are related by symmetries of the kernel. The question is a
natural selection or derivation of the reduced structure, not a basis choice.
Additional raw operators may break U(5) and select W. This contract does
not assert that the complete raw datum contains only C, J and the metric.
It says that their polarized kernel alone cannot perform this selection.

4. Minimal consistent correction of the source interface

Keep distinct:
  geometric reflection on boundary sections;
  the pure-CAR polarization C selecting a state;
  an internal carrier involution on a coefficient space E.

An internal involution X on E can preserve a physical admissible space
without coinciding with the involution used to select that space. If they
act on separate known factors, [X,Pprim]=0 is consistent with both signs
of X surviving. Likewise on the realification of E a chosen unitary
internal action extends to creation and annihilation spaces by conjugation.
Neither observation constructs a new raw factor, nor derives X.
The required source theorem must specify the factor/map and X before
invoking the conditional 3+2 algebra. Merely renaming iC does not do this.

More explicitly, for an independently supplied e_car on E=Ran C, the
extension ehat=e_car direct_sum J e_car J^{-1} on E direct_sum JE obeys
[ehat,C]=[ehat,J]=0. This contrasts with J epsilon_C J=-epsilon_C.
The two operators have different transformation laws, not just names.

The missing information may already lie in a FULL source Hamiltonian,
but is not recoverable from its sign alone. For the explicit C in the
checker, take any positive real numbers a!=b and a chosen compatible
ehat, with its projectors Q_- and Q_+. Then

  H_(a,b,ehat)=epsilon_C (a Q_-+b Q_+)

is Hermitian, J H J=-H, and sign(H)=epsilon_C for EVERY such ehat.
The covariance and pure state are unchanged, while the internal energy
eigenspaces vary. One can also replace the3+2 multiplicities by1+4 while
keeping C, J, a=2 and b=3 fixed. The resulting full H spectra differ
(the multiplicities of +/-2 are3 versus1), so this difference is not merely
a unitary change of basis. Thus restoring the source's full operator magnitude is
a possible place to derive the split; guessing it from the polarization
is not. This is an exact fiber of the sign map, not a new physical model
or a claim that any of these Hamiltonians satisfies all raw P1 conditions.

5. Strongest conditional Yukawa compression, with its assumptions exposed

For nonzero finite Hermitian spaces E_-, E_+, require an invariant amplitude
factoring through

  Lambda^3 E_- tensor Lambda^2 E_+ -> C

for S(U(E_-) x U(E_+)), and require the amplitude nonzero.
The subgroup SU(E_-) x SU(E_+) already forces dim E_-=3 and dim E_+=2:
Lambda^k C^n contains a trivial SU(n) representation at k>0 only at k=n,
with k<=n. At n=3,m=2 the full group preserves the product determinant,
so the invariant space is one-dimensional. A nonzero volume form on E_-
then yields injective and surjective contraction E_- -> (Lambda^2 E_-)*.

The exterior degrees (3,2), the two field representations, and nonzero
amplitude are assumptions. Calling it the lowest primitive coupling does
not derive those assumptions from raw P1. Norm and phase require their
respective metric and determinant-trivialization data.
The supplied Lean BoundaryYukawaKernel interface explicitly leaves the
raw-source arrow open. Existing downstream family transport already uses
representation data; it cannot serve as the upstream proof of those data.

6. Correct downstream dictionary, preserved rather than rediscovered

The existing compiler-source-channel-gate-20260918 proves that the complex
Spin(10) half-spinor S+=Lambda^even C5 has no equivariant real16 form.
The correct CAR vector construction is instead

  V10=E_R, V6=Fix(J_6 on Lambda^2 F), V16=V10 direct_sum V6,

where F is a Hermitian four-dimensional space with determinant volume,
and J_6 is the antilinear Hodge real structure (J_6^2=1).
Then the Clifford half-spinor is (16,4)+(conjugate16,conjugate4).
The original D5+D3 construction already supplies this representation
dictionary. It does not supply local fields from mere finite dimensions.
The required source map is a real CAR-linear, symmetry-equivariant local
field realization of V10+V6 with the common state, time and stress, plus
the charged-sector extension. Neither an internal16 nor a finite kernel
automatically provides it. The earlier fixed-source exhaustion obstruction
and the sector-memory result remain in force.

7. Why geometric reflection does not supply the missing operator identity

The archived source also equates the geometric deck lift R_tau with 2P_C-I.
For a product Dirac collar D=gamma_n(partial_x+B), the decaying half-cylinder
solutions are u(x)=exp(-xB)f, giving P_C=1_(0,infinity)(B) on nonzero modes.
A proper rank2 Dirac test uses B=-i sigma3 partial_y, gamma_n=i sigma1.
Then the sign symbol at a nonzero tangential momentum k is sign(k)sigma3.
A geometric reflection x->-x fixing y acts at the boundary through a
pointwise fiber lift r(y), whose symbol is independent of k. It cannot
equal both sigma3 and -sigma3. This symbol obstruction also applies on
compact product collars; the half-cylinder is the explicit local model,
not an assertion that it satisfies the archived compactness condition.

Standard Calderon theory gives a pseudodifferential order-zero projection
with tangential positive spectral principal symbol. Its full lower-order
part can depend on the interior. A geometric deck involution alone does
not determine it by its plus eigenspace. A nonlocal Poisson/Cauchy-data
lift can instead be defined, but that is a separate analytic construction.
See Booss-Bavnbek and Lesch, arXiv:0803.4047, and Booss-Bavnbek/Lesch/Zhu,
arXiv:0803.4160. This does not refute a separately specified nonlocal lift.
The literal equality is in the archived TFPT45 source, not the active
top-level TFPT architecture. No current closed theorem is downgraded here.
