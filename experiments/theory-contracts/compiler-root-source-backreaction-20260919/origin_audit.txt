central phase fibre {1,i,-1,-i}.  The pinned source actually reconstructs all

(compiler-correlated-event-clock source_channel.py:39-68).  On the ray labels
the central commutator of suitable words is erased, whereas the same words on
two C4 registers retain the scalar -1.  Under the stated unmarked/full-
symmetry premises the two joint lifts therefore anticommute, so every common
energy eigenspace is even-dimensional.  This is a genuine obstruction to a
unique ground state for that phaseless 60-label realization.  It is not a
no-go for a marked source, a central reference register, or another linear
lift.

The 240-label construction resolves exactly this cocycle mismatch.  Let

  C_R |alpha> = |i alpha>

on H_R = l2(R(E8)).  For a ray representative z_l define

  |l;q> = (1/2) sum_{k=0}^3 i^(q k) |i^k z_l>.

The relevant state is

  |l;2> = (|z_l>-|i z_l>+|-z_l>-|-i z_l>)/2,
  C_R|l;2> = -|l;2>.

If a native reflection sends z_l to i^s z_{l'}, its source action in sector 2
is the signed permutation

  R_m^(2)|l;2> = (-1)^s |l';2>.

The pair vector v_l=(z_l/2) tensor (z_l/2) obeys the same law,

  (r_m tensor r_m)v_l = (-1)^s v_l'.

This is checked exactly for all source/reflection pairs in
work/root_source_audit_20260919/response_check.py:16-43,81-91.  Thus the phase
fibre supplies the compensating sign that the sixty projective labels lost.

The first additional source premise is already here, before H_E or L_R:

  H_R := l2(R(E8)), with 240 mutually orthogonal root-label kets, and
  R_m|alpha> := |r_m alpha> as the physical source action.

The E8 lattice theorem supplies 240 geometric roots, their norm, and the

1b. Exact exclusion of the pure permutation as an E8_1 root-current action

The 240 orthogonal labels might be compared with the 240 root currents in the
grade-one E8_1 vacuum space.  That comparison fails for the PURE permutation
action used by the source.  The smallest witness already occurs on three
native Gaussian roots (in the source normalization, norm squared 4):

  alpha=(1,1,1,1),
  beta =(1,-1,-1,-1),
  gamma=alpha+beta=(2,0,0,0),

with Re<alpha,beta>=-2.  Direct reconstruction from the pinned source confirms
that all three belong to the 240-root set.  The native coordinate-reflection
word

  g=diag(1,-1,-1,-1)=r_e1 r_e2 r_e3

(with the three flipped coordinate rays) obeys

  g alpha=beta,   g beta=alpha,   g gamma=gamma.           (1b.1)

Let E_delta denote root-current/Chevalley root vectors.  Since gamma is a
root,

  [E_alpha,E_beta]=N_(alpha,beta) E_gamma,   N_(alpha,beta) != 0.

If the source's pure permutation P_g were a Lie automorphism on those root
currents, then applying it to this bracket would give

  P_g [E_alpha,E_beta] = N E_gamma,

whereas bracket preservation and (1b.1) require

  [P_g E_alpha,P_g E_beta]
    =[E_beta,E_alpha]=-N E_gamma.                          (1b.2)

This is a contradiction over C.  Rephasing the root basis cannot repair it
while the action remains pure: for F_delta=s_delta E_delta the new structure
constants still satisfy N'_(beta,alpha)=-N'_(alpha,beta), and a coefficient-one
swap repeats (1b.2).

A genuine phased lift would have to act as

  E_delta -> c_g(delta) E_(g delta)

and this witness forces

  c_g(gamma) = -c_g(alpha)c_g(beta).                      (1b.3)

Thus at least one nontrivial root phase/sign is compulsory.  This is consistent
with the existing native E8 lift rather than a new requirement invented for
this audit: compiler-quartic-holonomy-20260919/checker.py:139-177 pulls the
actual 240 E8 roots into D5+A3 coordinates and checks phase additivity on all
13,440 nonzero root-sum brackets, including neutral opposite-root brackets.
Its PROOF.txt:129-175 also records the phase-correct E8 lift and global Z4
cocycle.  That contract does not identify its phased current lift with the
source's coefficient-one permutation R_m; it shows precisely the additional
cocycle data a current interpretation must carry.

Therefore the pure 240-permutation register is exactly excluded as the native
E8_1 root-current/Weyl action.  It remains a valid abstract pointer Hilbert
space for the finite H_E,L_R model.  Promoting it to a current source requires
replacing R_m by a phase-correct lift, adding the eight Cartan grade-one
currents for the full 248-dimensional adjoint space, and rechecking Omega,
positivity, the phase-sector decomposition and the revival with that changed
operator.  None of those follow from the present finite certificate.

2. Exact connection of Omega to Bell10 and to the A3_1 descendant

Write psi_l=z_l/2 and v_l=psi_l tensor psi_l in Sym^2(C4).  Grouping the four
phase lifts in the submitted definition of Omega gives, without approximation,

  Omega
   = (1/sqrt(240)) sum_{alpha} |alpha> |psi_alpha> |psi_alpha>
   = (1/sqrt(60)) sum_{l=1}^{60} |l;2> tensor v_l.          (1)

The exact projective second moment gives

  sum_l |v_l><v_l| = 6 I_Sym2(C4).                         (2)

This follows both from the pinned projective-design identity
(source_channel.py:152-169) and the independent integer check
(response_check.py:81-86).  Define

  T : H_R,2 -> Sym^2(C4),
  T|l;2> = v_l/sqrt(6).

Then TT* = I_10: T is a canonical coisometry onto Bell10.  Fix the
source-first column-vectorisation convention

  vec_R(T) := sum_(l,a) T_(a l) |l>_R tensor |a>_Bell.

With this convention,

  Omega = (1/sqrt(10)) vec_R(T).                           (3)

Consequently Omega has Schmidt rank 10 with ten weights 1/10 and

  rho_AB = TT*/10 = I_Bell10/10 = P_sym/10,
  rho_R  = (T* T)^T/10.                                   (3a)

The source Schmidt support is therefore im(T^T), not in general im(T*).
This distinction matters because the native frame vectors and the Bell
matrices need not be real in the chosen Pauli-Bell basis.

This is stronger than a dimension match.  T intertwines every native labelled
reflection,

  T R_m^(2) = G_m T,       G_m:=Sym^2(r_m).               (4)

The source reflection matrices R_m^(2) are real signed symmetric involutions.
Transposing (4) therefore gives

  R_m^(2) T^T = T^T G_m^T.                               (4a)

Thus im(T^T) carries the transpose, equivalently the dual/conjugate unitary
Bell action.  One must not replace G_m^T by G_m merely because the source
permutation is real: in the pinned Pauli-Bell basis G_m can have off-diagonal
entries +/-i.

Now apply the previously constructed current-descendant isometry

  E : Sym^2(C4) -> D_10 subset L_A3_1(Lambda2),

where D_10 is the horizontal 10 at affine grade one and h_A3=3/2.  Its explicit
CAR formula, current-mode separator, SU4 equivariance and central phase are in
work/current_origin_20260919.txt:127-234.  The exact lifted singlet is

  Omega_aff := (I_R tensor E) Omega
    = (1/sqrt(60)) sum_l |l;2> tensor E(v_l)
    = (1/sqrt(10)) vec_R(E T).                            (5)

Because the bounded affine involution V_m restricts to Sym^2(r_m) on E(Bell10),

  (R_m^(2) tensor V_m) Omega_aff = Omega_aff              (6)

for every one of the sixty events.  Equations (1)-(6) are the requested exact,
directed operator connection.  The word "singlet" here means invariant under
this finite diagonal reflection action.  It is not automatically an SU4
singlet: 10 tensor 10 contains no SU4 scalar (the invariant evaluation pairs
10bar with 10).  Equations (3a) and (4a) show precisely how the finite source
partner supplies the dual/conjugate Bell action.  They do not extend that
source action to a full SU4 representation.

4. Preparation verdict

The new source does not naturally prepare the E8 grade-two sector.

First, even inside the finite 240-label model, the Hamiltonian commutes with
the phase operator C_R (equivalently all four source sectors are dynamically
invariant).  The uniform coherent root state

  |u_240>=(1/sqrt(240)) sum_alpha |alpha>

lies in sector 0 and can never evolve into the sector-2 ground vector Omega.
More generally, for a closed autonomous H with ground projector Q_Omega,

  Tr(Q_Omega rho(t)) = Tr(Q_Omega rho(0)).

Uniqueness of the lowest eigenvector is therefore a spectral selection, not a
unitary preparation rule.  Cooling, dissipation, an adiabatic initial
condition, or an explicit sector-2 boundary preparation would be additional
physics.  The response certificate states this same ground-population caveat
(response_check.py:92-102).

Second, even if Omega is supplied as an initial state, tracing out the source
prepares I_D10/10, not a chosen pure Bell descendant.  Retaining the source
gives the pure finite-group invariant Omega_aff in H_R,2 tensor D_10, which is
not yet a vector of the E8 vacuum Hilbert space.  The extra map D above, a D5
state/readout rule, and an actual preparation dynamics remain missing.

This conclusion is independent of whether L_R has a later exact revival that
restores earlier event steps at selected clock times.  A revival can repair a
stroboscopic channel equality; it cannot move sector-0 initial data into the
conserved sector 2 or create the absent D5 carrier map.
