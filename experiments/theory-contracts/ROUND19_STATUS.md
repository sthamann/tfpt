## Main outcome: an interaction, not a tensor sum of separate models

The [coupled charge parent](coupled-charge-parent-round19/PROOF.md) adds a
positive finite-range endpoint interaction to the existing positive local
scalar/gravity/auxiliary base B and the complete E8-metric electric parent:

    V=lambda/(2N) sum_(x,i) e(F_i(x))[phi_x^2+phi_(x+ei)^2],
    X=B+H_charge+V,  lambda>=0.

The countably infinite electric Hilbert space is retained. Each fiber is a
positive self-adjoint Schrodinger operator; its graph-summable direct sum
defines complete unitary dynamics. Exact Hodge reduction gives

    X(n)=B+C(n)(1+lambda R),
    R=(1/N)sum_x phi_x^2,  C(n)=sum_i e(n_i).

Charge changes the scalar mass by 2lambda C(n)/N. Conversely a physical
charge shift has energy difference [C(n+r)-C(n)](1+lambda R), so its energy
depends on the scalar state. A superposition of zero and unit charge coupled
to a product scalar Gaussian has the exact short-time charge purity

    P(t)=1-lambda^2 t^2/(4N)+o(t^2).

This establishes entanglement for lambda>0 at every fixed finite N, in the
time generated by X. It is not automatically a square-root clock purity
coefficient or a thermodynamic rate. Charge remains conserved: this is
reciprocal conditional dynamics, not propagating charged pair creation.
