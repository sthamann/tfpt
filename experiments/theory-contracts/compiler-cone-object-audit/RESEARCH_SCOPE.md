## Known mathematics versus the new local derivation

The Clifford/twisted-algebra framework is standard:
[Elduque and Rodrigo-Escudero](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.07002).
The Hermitian-matrix/Jordan/Lorentz connection is also standard; an accessible
mathematical exposition is [Baez, Week 162](https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/week162.html).
Arithmetic order theory and the maximal-order/lattice correspondence can be
found in [Voight's Quaternion Algebras](https://jvoight.github.io/quat.html).

The relationship between local quantum filtering, Lorentz transformations and
normalization appears explicitly in the original research of
[Verstraete, Dehaene and De Moor](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0011111).
It is not a new physical identification of filter normalization with time.

Here the mathematical additions are the explicit compiler overorder chain,
its conditional uniqueness, its trace-lattice E8 reconstruction with an actual
marked source isometry, and exact process/Clock discriminators. No worldwide
novelty claim is made. The cited literature is context; the finite identities,
lattice maps and scoped obstruction arguments are given explicitly in the
accompanying proofs and checker.
