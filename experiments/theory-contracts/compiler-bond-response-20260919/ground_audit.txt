Decision
========

The new unperturbed statement is strongly supported, but it is not yet an
exact full-space theorem on the supplied evidence.

After an exact Z4 Fourier decomposition of each 240-root source, an independent
matrix-free Hermitian replay of all sixteen 230,400-dimensional blocks finds

  E0/kappa = 1/2  in source sectors (2,1) and (1,2),
  one lowest Ritz vector in each mirror sector,
  next level  = 3/5 in sector (2,2),
  observed global gap = 1/10.

The two mirror-sector next levels are 7/10.  The largest recorded Ritz residual
in the decisive sectors is below 5e-13.  This reproduces the claimed energy,
twofold ground space, and gap without constructing the 3,686,400-dimensional
matrix.  It is numerical spectral evidence, not an exact lower-bound
certificate.

Weak perturbation
=================

Let

  H(epsilon) = kappa H0 + epsilon kappa B,
  B = H_E1 + H_E2 + V,
  V(alpha,beta)=1-Re <psi_alpha|psi_beta>.

The supplied two-state compression gives

  PBP = (5/2) I - sigma_x/8,

so first order splits the doublet by epsilon kappa/4.  This compression alone
does not prove uniqueness: an off-P leakage/resolvent bound is necessary.

There is, however, a short exact conditional argument once the claimed
unperturbed full-space gap delta=kappa/10 is assumed.  The exact residual Gram is

  PBQBP = (55/64) I + (3/8) sigma_x,

with parity values 79/64 and 31/64.  Positivity of B and the Schur complement
give

  gap/kappa >= epsilon/4
      - 31 epsilon^2 / [64(1/10 - 21 epsilon/8)].

At epsilon=1/65 the right side is exactly epsilon/8, and it is no smaller for
0<epsilon<=1/65.  Therefore the stated epsilon<=1/200 uniqueness and
gap>=mu/8 follow conditionally from the exact H0 ground/gap theorem.  They do
not supply the missing H0 theorem themselves.
