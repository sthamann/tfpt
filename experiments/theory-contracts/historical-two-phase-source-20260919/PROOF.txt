```text

```

The unavoidable potential energy in at least one endpoint is
`1.1481519154814445e-8 GeV^4`, or `8.85740538200542e29` times the declared
recombination comparison density. These values are a necessary consistency
bound, not a cosmological likelihood or a claim about all axion/ALP models.

For phases `theta`, positive-definite kinetic matrix `K`, QCD charge vector
`N`, and photon vector `c`, with only the QCD rank-one quadratic term, a
QCD-flat photon-active direction exists exactly when

```text
exists v: N^T v = 0 and c^T v != 0
iff c not in span(N).
```

In canonical coordinates its photon coupling obeys

```text
g_L^2 = c^T K^-1 c - (c^T K^-1 N)^2/(N^T K^-1 N) >= 0.
```

The short proof is to pass to canonical coordinates
`n=K^(-1/2)N` and `e=K^(-1/2)c`. The QCD mass operator is
`chi*n*n^T`, hence has rank one for `N != 0`; its orthogonal complement is
QCD-flat. The squared photon coupling projected to that complement is exactly
the displayed `g_L^2`. Cauchy--Schwarz gives nonnegativity, with equality
exactly when `e` is parallel to `n`, equivalently when `c` is parallel to `N`
in the `K^(-1)` metric. Thus a nonzero light coupling requires the stated
nonparallel alignment.

For the same-well cosine bound, if endpoint separation is `delta` then at
least one endpoint has `|theta| >= delta/2` by the triangle inequality.
Because `1-cos(theta)` is monotone in `|theta|` on `[0,pi]`, that endpoint has
potential at least `chi*(1-cos(delta/2))`; symmetric endpoints
`theta_e=-delta/2`, `theta_o=delta/2` attain the bound. This is the exact
minimax endpoint argument used by the numerical control.

For the historical two-phase convention `N=(0,1)`, `c=(g0,0)` and
`K=[[A,B],[B,D]]`, this yields `g_L^2=g0^2/A`,
`m_H^2=chi*A/(A*D-B^2)`, and
`g_H^2=g0^2*B^2/(A*(A*D-B^2))`. Thus kinetic mixing alone does not remove the
QCD-null direction; the result still requires the source to derive `K`, `N`,
`c`, all additional potential rows, and the compact phase lattice.

The explicitly moving historical minimum also has the exact balance term

