Question and decisive test
-------------------------
Can the existing static four-phase register supply the missing coherent
fermion interaction merely by retaining and then tracing its phase sectors?
The required output is a reversible evolution on the same full matter
operator algebra, including its charged fields. A non-Gaussian mixture, a
single prepared state, or an effective operator on a newly chosen code
subspace is not this output. This question is the register-trace possibility
left explicitly separate in clock-interaction-provenance, section 3.

The source is verification/v1033_charged_disorder.py, functions
register_hamiltonian and seam_forward. It has

 H_R = sum_(r=0)^3 Pi_r tensor h_r,
 h_r = b + i^r a + (-i)^r a^dagger,
 Pi_r=|r><r|,  Z=diag(1,i,-1,-i).

Its shift appears in the disorder observable, not in H_R. Thus every Pi_r
is conserved. This source is written on C4 tensor the one-particle space V.
When we discuss elementary many-fermion fields below, the lift to
C4 tensor Fock(V), with H_r=dGamma(h_r)+s_r I, is an explicit conditional
extension. It is NOT Fock(C4 tensor V), and is not selected by the source.
No new Hamiltonian, reset law, or ground-state selection is inserted.

1. Exact inclusive channel, including register coherences
-------------------------------------------------------
Let the initially independent register have any density matrix sigma and
the matter input any density matrix rho. For a time-independent block H_r,
U_r(t)=exp(-it H_r); the proof also allows blockwise time-dependent
quadratic H_r(t), with their actual time-ordered unitaries. Then

 U(t)(sigma tensor rho)U(t)^dagger
   = sum_(r,s) sigma_rs |r><s| tensor U_r rho U_s^dagger,

 Phi_t(rho)=Tr_R(...)=sum_r p_r U_r rho U_r^dagger,
 p_r=sigma_rr.

All offdiagonal register coherences vanish in this inclusive trace. A pure
coherent register is covered, not only a classical initial mixture. The
joint state may nevertheless be entangled; only its reduced channel is
being characterized.

2. When this channel is unitary
------------------------------
Suppose Phi_t(rho)=V rho V^dagger for every input rho on the full matter
space. Its unnormalized Choi matrix is

 J(Phi_t)=sum_r p_r |U_r>><<U_r|=|V>><<V|.

For any vector w orthogonal to |V>>, positivity gives
 sum_r p_r |<<w|U_r>>|^2=0.
Therefore every U_r with p_r>0 is proportional to V; unitarity fixes the
proportionality to a phase. Conversely proportional occupied branches
plainly give a unitary channel. This is an elementary standard quantum-
channel fact, proved here to fix its exact application and assumptions.

Under the conditional quadratic Fock lift, every U_r is a fermionic
Gaussian unitary, since its conjugation acts linearly on the elementary
CAR fields. Any proportional V has the same action. Hence:

 A deterministic unitary obtained from this static register on the full
 elementary CAR algebra is Gaussian, up to a scalar phase.

If the reduced channel is unitary over a time interval starting at zero,
its infinitesimal generator is the quadratic average sum p_r H_r up to a
scalar. A nonquadratic choice of a matrix logarithm at one isolated
recurrence time does not establish such a continuous interaction.

The distinction from the earlier purely fermionic Gaussian-integration
theorem matters: Phi_t need not itself be a Gaussian channel. What is
excluded is a non-Gaussian UNITARY channel from these ingredients.

3. Coherent return, memory and measurements
-----------------------------------------
If the register returns to an input-independent state and the matter
undergoes the same unitary for every input, its reduced channel is already
covered by section 2. Any register-only trace-preserving operation performed
after the last joint interaction leaves the matter marginal unchanged.
A final register rotation or unconditioned reset therefore cannot evade
the result. We do not claim that repeated resets at intermediate times
are the same process as retention: with the same static branch structure
they give mixtures of products of Gaussian unitaries instead, which obey
the same rank-one implication whenever the total channel is unitary.

For a pure initial register with amplitudes a_r, postselection of a final
register vector m produces K_m=sum_r <m|r>a_r U_r. Such coherent sums need
not be Gaussian. This is a conditional instrument, not the inclusive
channel above. A proposed measurement/feed-forward or additional control
must be specified and physically derived; it is not supplied by a shift
being present merely as an observable.

Initial correlations restrict the admissible input family and need not
define the same completely positive map on arbitrary matter inputs. Also,
on an encoded subspace or a fixed superselection block the branch unitaries
may agree there without agreeing on the full CAR algebra. These cases are
outside the claimed exclusion. A physical reconstruction using them must
transport its charged-sector intertwiners and time, not only neutral traces.

4. A source-level purity diagnostic
----------------------------------
For a pure matter input P=|psi><psi|, write Hbar=sum_r p_r H_r. Taylor
expansion gives

 Phi_t(P)=P-it[Hbar,P]
          -t^2/2 sum_r p_r[H_r,[H_r,P]]+O(t^3),

 Tr Phi_t(P)^2 = 1-2t^2 sum_r p_r Var_psi(H_r-Hbar)+O(t^3).

One proof differentiates Tr rho(t)^2 twice at zero and uses
 Tr(P[H,[H,P]])=2 Var_psi(H). The difference of second moments is
 sum_r p_r Var(H_r)-Var(Hbar)=sum_r p_r Var(H_r-Hbar).
No commutativity of H_r or of Hbar with H_r is assumed.

For the uniform four phases, H_r=B+i^r A+(-i)^r A^dagger and Hbar=B.
Since the averages of i^r and i^(2r) vanish,

 sum_r p_r Var_psi(H_r-Hbar)
  = <AA^dagger+A^dagger A>_psi-2|<A>_psi|^2.

The checker uses the actual original source at nx=3, ny=1 and a single
particle in the departing seam orbital. These are a diagnostic size and
preparation, not a predicted vacuum. The original seam matrix has squared
column norm 1/2, and on this vector A^dagger psi=0 and <A>=0. Thus

 Tr rho_matter(t)^2 = 1-t^2+O(t^3)

in the source's dimensionless time convention. This is an exact Taylor
coefficient, not a bound or a calibrated prediction at macroscopic time.
The on-site and nonseam terms are retained: their common B contribution
cancels from this purity coefficient, not from the full dynamics.

5. Real four-point non-Gaussianity without a coherent quartic Hamiltonian
----------------------------------------------------------------------
On the conditional Fock lift of the same one-particle preparation the
state has N_f=1 at all times. Let C_ij=<c_i^dagger c_j>. Then

 sum_(i<j)<n_i n_j>=0,  Tr C=1,
 sum_(i<j)(C_ii C_jj-C_ij C_ji)=(1-Tr C^2)/2.

The total Wick defect is therefore exactly

 D4 = sum_(i<j)[<n_i n_j>-(C_ii C_jj-C_ij C_ji)]
    = -(1-Tr rho_matter^2)/2
    = -t^2/2+O(t^3)

for the diagnostic above. The state really fails Wick factorization after
the register is discarded. This does not contradict section 2: the
evolution is a mixture, not a closed unitary interaction on the matter.
It directly tests why an observed connected four-point contribution is
not by itself the missing Hamiltonian derivation.

More generally, when each occupied branch is gauge-invariant quasifree,
the reduced fourth cumulant equals

 Cov_p(C^(r)_ik,C^(r)_jl)-Cov_p(C^(r)_il,C^(r)_jk)

with the convention <c_i^dagger c_j^dagger c_l c_k>. It measures branch
covariance. No assertion that a convex mixture of Gaussian states is
Gaussian is made.

6. Consequence for the original research question
------------------------------------------------
Keeping a full determinant, a coherent initial register and its four
phases does not alone manufacture a reversible nonquadratic field
generator. The unchanged v1033 source has static sectors. To escape this
specific construction one must change an explicit premise: derive sector-
changing dynamics, supply an actual nonquadratic branch, or construct a
different encoded/charged-sector realization with its state and common
time. A nonzero [H,Pi_r] is a useful necessary structural change for the
first of these routes, not a sufficient proof of an interaction, E8 phase,
vacuum selection or relativistic limit.

Conserved Pi_r does not mean every register observable is stationary. For
S=sum_r |r+1><r|, the full Heisenberg evolution is exactly

 S(t)=sum_r |r+1><r| tensor U_(r+1)(t)^dagger U_r(t).

There is therefore a genuine sector-changing PROBE response in the full
source algebra even when no such transition occurs in the Hamiltonian.
The original disorder construction already uses this distinction. Our
theorem does not exclude that response, call the entire joint model free,
or demand that every physically valid reduced description be closed and
unitary. It excludes the claimed deterministic matter-only interaction
in the specified reduction. Approximate infrared dynamics in a correlated
encoded sector requires a separate error-controlled argument.

The existing relative-phase compiler coupling and the electric rotor are
separate examples with genuinely noncommuting source dynamics. Their
source/charge/time map into the boundary theory remains unproved. This
result does not transfer their properties to v1033, nor derive their
couplings from P1/P2. It also does not exclude open-system descriptions of
an interacting world or a nontrivial encoded infrared sector.
