VERDICT

The target here is actual unitary equivalence of the oriented physical heat

It is not unitary equivalence of the doubled HS vector or its Liouvillian.
It is not merely the presence of a flat parent term in an interacting H.

1. EXACT HYPOTHESES

Let E>=0 be selfadjoint on the original excitation one-particle space,
and assume Tr exp(-xE)<infinity for EVERY real x>0. Let
H=dGamma(E) on its full fermionic Fock space. This is exactly the given
Clifford source in SEED-FIELD.txt, not a newly chosen Hamiltonian.

The hypothesis implies finite m=dim Ker E and a discrete positive spectrum
epsilon_j>0 with finite multiplicities, listed with those multiplicities.
There are only finitely many epsilon_j in every bounded interval. In
particular no infinite collection of zero or near-zero modes is hidden.

Suppose for contradiction there is a unitary W onto C^d tensor K_R with

 W H W^* = Delta(I-P) tensor I + I tensor H_R,          (1)

where P is rank one, d>2, Delta>0, and H_R is selfadjoint. Equality means
the actual selfadjoint generator/closed form, or equivalently its whole
heat semigroup; it is not a formal equality of a selected matrix block.

Since H>=0 and Delta(I-P) has a zero eigenvector, (1) forces H_R>=0.
This can be read by restriction to that reducing zero-factor subspace,
or by the joint spectral calculus; no subtraction of unbounded operators
on an unproved common domain is needed.

2. THE FULL FREE HEAT TRACE IS HOLOMORPHIC AND ZERO-FREE

For Re z>0, occupation-number summation gives

 Z_free(z)=Tr exp(-zH)
   =2^m product_j (1+exp(-z epsilon_j)).               (2)

The absolute occupation sum is Z_free(Re z)<infinity. Indeed the finite
products increase at real x>0 and are bounded by
2^m exp(sum_j exp(-x epsilon_j)). Thus the full trace exists, and ordinary
absolute summation proves (2) also for complex z.

On a compact subset of the right half-plane choose a>0 with Re z>=a.
The sum of |exp(-z epsilon_j)| is uniformly bounded by the convergent
sum exp(-a epsilon_j). Split off finitely many terms so the remaining
|exp(-z epsilon_j)|<=1/2. The analytic branch of log(1+w) on |w|<1 has
|log(1+w)|<=2|w| on this tail. Its logarithmic series therefore converges
normally, and its exponential is holomorphic and never zero. Every finite
prefix factor is also never zero, since |exp(-z epsilon_j)|<1.

Consequently

 Z_free(z) is holomorphic and nonzero for Re z>0.      (3)

The finite zero modes contribute the nonzero constant 2^m. This is a proof
of nonvanishing of the INFINITE product; nonvanishing of each finite factor
alone would not have been sufficient.

3. THE REST TRACE CANNOT SUPPLY A COMPENSATING POLE

At every real x>0 the supposed tensor decomposition gives

 Z_free(x)=[1+(d-1)exp(-x Delta)] Tr exp(-xH_R).

Hence exp(-xH_R) is trace class for every x>0. Equivalently its trace-class
property follows by compression to the finite factor's zero eigenvector.
This conclusion does not assume a rest model or fit a rest density.

For Re z>0 the rest trace

 Z_R(z)=Tr exp(-zH_R)

is holomorphic. One may use its absolutely normally convergent eigenvalue
sum: on Re z>=a its terms are dominated by Tr exp(-aH_R); derivatives are
controlled on smaller compact sets by Tr exp(-(a/2)H_R). There are no poles
anywhere in this half-plane. The zero operator rest Hilbert space is excluded
by the nonzero original Fock space/unitary W.

Trace invariance and tensor spectral calculus therefore give

 Z_free(z)=[1+(d-1)exp(-z Delta)] Z_R(z)               (4)

throughout Re z>0, either directly by absolute summation or by the identity
theorem from the real positive axis. No complex physical temperature is
postulated; complex z is a mathematical probe of the same real generator.

At

 z_*=[log(d-1)+i pi]/Delta,

one has Re z_*>0 and 1+(d-1)exp(-Delta z_*)=0. Since Z_R(z_*) is finite,
(4) gives Z_free(z_*)=0, contradicting (3). This proves the claim.

For original DET16, d=65536 and Delta=2, so the same explicit point is
z_*=[log(65535)+i pi]/2. The phase in the cat projector does not change its
rank-one/flat-excited spectrum and has no effect on this argument.

4. A SINGLE EXACT POSITIVE STEP ALREADY SUFFICES

Suppose at the actual fixed a>0 one has the exact operator equality

The original heat is positive and injective. Its ground norm is one.
Compression to the rank-one ground factor implies R is positive,

logarithm H_R=-(1/a)log R>=0. No bounded inverse of R is required.
The unique selfadjoint logarithm of (5) gives (1). Positive fractional
powers then give the same full semigroup, with the SAME W.

If the single step is normalized, its exact faithful density factorization
likewise yields the generator up to a real scalar by its selfadjoint log.
That scalar can be absorbed into H_R, or retained as a global offset.
The real heat traces of the rest at all positive times follow from the
already given free source, not from assuming arbitrary powers of a generic
trace-class operator are trace class. No new beta family is fitted.

5. SCOPE AND FIRST PLACES THE ARGUMENT WOULD NOT APPLY

An energy offset multiplies Z by exp(-cz); positive time rescaling replaces
epsilon_j by a positive multiple. Neither produces a right-half-plane zero
or cancels the contradiction. The scalar factors allowed by one autonomous
heat semigroup are not arbitrary independently reset source normalizations.

d>2 and Delta>0 are essential. At d=2 the flat factor is an ordinary one-mode
fermion and its zeros lie on Re z=0. At Delta=0 there is no positive gap.
For a more general two-level factor the same argument applies whenever the
excited multiplicity is greater than its ground multiplicity; this is not
needed for the original rank-one DET16 application.

The result concerns the FULL unprojected trace. An actual Gauss, parity,
charge or other sector restriction replaces that trace by a projected trace
and need not retain the product (2). We do not assert that the actual
projected original source is zero-free. Keeping all components of a source
is a hypothesis here, not a license to remove its physical projection.

Likewise integrating over fluctuating Phi, taking an infinite-volume state
without a trace-class heat, adding interactions, or tracing out a coupled
rest does not preserve this proof automatically. A flat parent term plus
noncommuting additional couplings does not imply the tensor factor (1).
This excludes exact equality to the literal tensor-factor source; it does
not exclude such a term, a compression, an effective approximation or a
different interacting source. Relative partition-function quotients alone
are not substituted for the actual heat trace in (2).

An arbitrary unitary on HS that mixes the two face/unvectorization slots
does not automatically intertwine physical Q(s). The actual oriented field
legs and sewing must give the operator equivalence (1) or (5). This review
makes no identification of H with L_H-R_H or with a modular generator.
