1. DOMAIN AND OPERATOR IDENTITY

Let h=gamma D0+V on H1, with constant Hermitian involution gamma and bounded
Hermitian multiplication V. Bounded perturbation keeps the original H1 domain
and selfadjointness. V need not anticommute with gamma for this theorem.
The actual original Higgs family is contained in this larger bounded class.
F is odd, Hermitian and spatially W1,infty; it therefore preserves H1.
Its compact time support and integrable time derivative justify integration
by parts against Dom(h) vectors. Time evolution preserves Dom(h).

On that domain the weak anticommutator is
  {h,A}=int U_t[-i gamma partial_thetaF+{V,F}]U_t* dt = G,
and integration by parts gives
  [h,A]= i int U_t(partial_tF)U_t*dt = iDt.
The sign is positive i: 0=int d_t(U_tFU_t*)=i[h,A]+Dt.

For u,v in Dom(h), adding forms gives
  2<hu,Av>=<u,(G+iDt)v>.
Selfadjointness implies Av belongs to Dom(h*)=Dom(h), and
hAv=(G+iDt)v/2. For arbitrary v approximate with v_n in Dom(h).
A v_n and the bounded right hand side converge. Closedness of h then gives
A(H) subset Dom(h) and the bounded operator identity on all H.
This is sufficient; no formal manipulation with an unproved product domain
or graph-core assertion remains.

Writing a0=int||F||, a1=int||partial_thetaF||, at=int||partial_tF||,
M=||V|| gives
  ||A||<=a0,
  ||hA||<=(a1+2Ma0+at)/2,
  ||(D0+i)A||<=(1+2M)a0+(a1+at)/2.
The order of gamma in D0A=gamma(hA-VA) is correct. The free periodic quarter
resolvent belongs to S2 in this ONE-dimensional circle, hence
  ||A||2 <= ||(D0+i)^-1||2 [(1+2M)a0+(a1+at)/2].
The internal multiplicity factor d in r_alpha² is correct. A zero eigenvalue
of h is harmless. No eigenvalue sign split, fixed positive gap, constant
background or density of separable space/time probes is needed.

This argument would not cover a variable gamma, an altered principal symbol,
or an automatic higher-dimensional S2 conclusion. The revised proof says so.
