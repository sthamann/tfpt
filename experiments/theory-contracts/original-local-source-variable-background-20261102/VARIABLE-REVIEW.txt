The proposed anticommutator/commutator proof is correct, with one domain
clarification: define the time integral initially in the strong/weak operator
sense, not as an unproved norm-Bochner integral in B(H). The two weak
identities then prove the required H1 range and the bounded hA identity.
The log-MGF inequality is also correct without [C,A]=0. It yields all
single-force exponential moments and uniform integrability. All ORDERED
mixed force moments follow from the existing determinant source by a short
locally uniform complex-analytic argument, not from a noncommutative
Holder inference using only the separate scalar MGFs.

2. Anticommutator identity, time-commutator sign and domains

For each t, multiplication by F(t) preserves H1. On H1,
 {h,F(t)}=B(t),
 B(t)=-i gamma partial_theta F(t)+{V,F(t)}.               (1)
The derivative terms cancel exactly because {gamma,F}=0. The scalar twist
alpha also cancels in that anticommutator. B(t) is bounded; for real force
tests it is selfadjoint, since gamma partial_theta F is antihermitian.

Define
 A=int exp(ith) F(t) exp(-ith) dt,
 B_int=int exp(ith) B(t) exp(-ith) dt,
 D_int=int exp(ith) partial_t F(t) exp(-ith) dt.          (2)
These exist as strong operator integrals by strong continuity and compact
support, with ||A||<=int ||F(t)||dt. Strong integrability is sufficient.
For example an odd constant matrix conjugated by an unbounded Dirac
generator need not vary continuously in operator norm, so norm-Bochner
integrability should not be assumed without argument. A=A*.

Test between psi,phi in H1. Moving h onto the test vectors justifies
integration of (1) as a form identity:
 {h,A}=B_int.                                          (3)
The time derivative of the conjugated insertion, again as an H1 form, is
 d_t[exp(ith)F(t)exp(-ith)]
   =i[h,exp(ith)F(t)exp(-ith)]
      +exp(ith)partial_t F(t)exp(-ith).
Its time integral vanishes at both endpoints. Therefore the correct sign is
 [h,A]=i D_int.                                        (4)
Adding the two forms gives, for phi in H1,
 2 <h psi,A phi>=<psi,(B_int+i D_int)phi>.
The right side is bounded in ||psi||. Selfadjointness of h therefore
implies A phi in Dom(h) and
 2hA phi=(B_int+i D_int)phi.
Now approximate arbitrary phi by H1 vectors. Boundedness of A and the
displayed right side, together with closedness of h, proves
 A(H) subset Dom(h), 2hA=B_int+i D_int in B(H).          (5)
In particular this conclusion was derived, not presupposed when using
the weak identities.

3. Uniform Hilbert-Schmidt estimate, no spectral diagonalization required

Write f0=int||F||dt, ftheta=int||partial_theta F||dt,
ft=int||partial_t F||dt. From (1)-(5),
 ||hA|| <= (ftheta+ft)/2+M f0,
 ||D0 A||=||gamma(hA-VA)|| <= (ftheta+ft)/2+2M f0.
Since
 A=(D0+i)^-1 (D0 A+iA),
and the resolvent on this original one-dimensional compact circle obeys
 ||(D0+i)^-1||_2^2
   =d_int sum_(n in Z) [1+(n+alpha)^2]^-1 <infinity,
we obtain
 ||A||_2 <= ||(D0+i)^-1||_2
             [(ftheta+ft)/2+(2M+1)f0].                (6)
This proves S2 for all such actual space/time force tests, uniformly over
the bounded original background class ||V||<=M. It even works for bounded
selfadjoint spatial V, since no derivative of V entered. No parameter,
mode weight or time law has been fitted.

The extension concerns SMOOTH spatial force tests. The older R080 proof
also allowed bounded rectangular spatial windows at constant background;
(6) does not remove its spatial derivative requirement or replace that
older stronger nonsmooth-test result.

4. Actual Galerkin recomputation, if used by the native controls

If A_N means P_N A P_N, S2 convergence is immediate. If it is recomputed
using h_N=P_N h P_N and F_N=P_N F P_N, the limit can also be justified.
Use the extension htilde_N=D0 gamma+P_N V P_N on the whole space. Fourier
P_N commutes with D0 and gamma. Bounded P_N V P_N converges strongly to V,
uniformly in norm, so htilde_N converges strongly in resolvent sense to h;
the corresponding unitary groups converge strongly on compact time sets.
Thus A_N -> A strongly. The SAME finite-dimensional identities give a
uniform bound on ||(D0+i)A_N||. Hence
 ||(1-P_K)A_N||_2
  <=||(1-P_K)(D0+i)^-1||_2 sup_N||(D0+i)A_N|| ->0
as K->infinity, uniformly in N. Selfadjointness of A_N supplies the
analogous right tail. Strong convergence on each fixed finite corner
becomes norm/HS convergence there; combining both tails proves
 A_N -> A in S2.                                      (7)
This is a consistency proof for the actual recomputed cutoff, not a new
choice of background. A thermal C_N recomputed from h_N converges strongly

5. Exact log-MGF proof, including noncommuting C and A

In a finite CAR cutoff let 0<=C_N<=I be the actual covariance, A_N=A_N*,
and W_N=dGamma(A_N)-Tr(C_N A_N) I. The finite quasifree trace formula gives
 log omega_N(exp(s W_N))
 =-s Tr(C_N A_N)+log det[I+C_N(exp(sA_N)-I)], s real.
Use Sylvester's determinant identity to write the determinant with
 Y_N=I+C_N^(1/2)(exp(sA_N)-I)C_N^(1/2)>0.
Indeed Y_N>=exp(-|s| ||A_N||)I, so its real logarithm is legitimate.
The elementary positive-matrix inequality logdetY<=Tr(Y-I) yields
 log omega_N(exp(s W_N))
 <=Tr[C_N(exp(sA_N)-I-sA_N)]
 <=(s^2/2) exp(|s| ||A_N||) Tr(C_N A_N^2)
 <=(s^2/2) exp(|s| ||A_N||) ||A_N||_2^2.              (8)
The middle inequality follows by scalar Taylor remainder on the spectrum
of selfadjoint A_N and then positive functional calculus. [C_N,A_N]=0
was never used. The same proof applies to either cutoff construction
above. All constants are uniform on ||V||<=M by(6).

For every t>0, (8) on both signs implies
 sup_N omega_N(exp(t|W_N|))
 <=2 exp[(t^2/2)exp(t a)b^2],                          (9)
where a bounds ||A_N|| and b bounds ||A_N||_2. For each p>0,
 sup_N omega_N(|W_N|^p 1_(|W_N|>R))
 <=the RHS(9) sup_(x>R) x^p exp(-t x) ->0.
Thus single-force spectral distributions have all moments and uniformly
integrable powers. Weak convergence from the existing characteristic
source plus (9) gives convergence of all their moments; a stronger
exponential bound gives convergence of each fixed real MGF as well.

6. All ordered mixed moments: use the existing complete source

Separate scalar MGFs alone do not prove arbitrary noncommuting ordered
moment convergence. For actual forces A1,...,Ar use the existing R080
centered source, now with complex z_j:
 U(z)=product_j exp(z_j A_j), L(z)=sum_j z_j A_j,
 chi(z)=det2[I+C(U(z)-I)] exp Tr C[U(z)-I-L(z)].       (10)
For imaginary z it is the old ordered unitary word, with original order.
For real z in a finite cutoff it is the ordered centered exponential word.
No independent infinite linear trace or uncentered determinant is used.

Put S=sum_j |z_j| ||A_j||, B=sum_j |z_j| ||A_j||_2.
Telescoping the product gives ||U-I||_2<=exp(S)B.
For U(t)=product_j exp(t z_j A_j), U(0)=I, U'(0)=L.
Every term of U''(t) contains two S2 factors, so
 ||U''(t)||_1<=exp(t S) B^2.
Integrating its second-order remainder yields
 ||U-I-L||_1<=(exp(S)/2) B^2.                         (11)
The estimates hold on every complex compact set, uniformly in the actual
cutoffs and bounded background class. S2 continuity of the exponential
product and S1 continuity of the remainder, with det2 continuity, give
locally uniform convergence of(10). All functions involved are entire in
the finitely many z_j. Cauchy's formula therefore passes every ordered
mixed derivative to the limit. This supplies the full ordered moment
functional, not just Gaussian cumulants or symmetrized responses.
The positive real unitary-word kernel and the existing Stone realization
are retained. An additional essential-selfadjointness core claim is not
needed or inferred from this calculation.

7. First assumptions, scope and novelty

The changed premise is the SAME original force source at bounded variable
spatial H(theta). Within the given circle/source class, the smoothing
proof is analytic and uniform; it avoids mode-by-mode diagonalization.
No new geometry, coupling, state rule, thermal calibration, probability
law for H, Gaussianization or physical active-clock assumption is added.
If an original positive H measure is later used and is supported on this
bounded class, the uniform estimates pass through that existing integral.
No such support restriction or new measure is selected here. Unbounded
background ensembles need their own actual integrability control.

The same zero-order Hermitian V also preserves the original circle
finite-propagation argument: it cancels in the energy-current identity.
This retains the given cylinder locality where the R080 spacelike support
hypotheses hold; it derives no physical spatial dimension or Lorentz
invariance. The Hilbert-Schmidt step uses the given one-dimensional
compact Dirac resolvent and is not a3+1 source theorem.

R080 already supplies the S2 constant-background det2 source, centering,
trace correction, whole ordered words and supported circle net. The
present useful extension is the direct variable-background anticommutator
bound and its uniform exponential-moment control. It does not repair or
rederive the primitive H law, absolute Z_adm/Z_ref, Euclidean physical OS
reflection, Γ background normalization or full TFPT origin. Those claims
are outside the theorem, not negative conclusions from this review.
