At a finite charged regulator let H_F=dGamma(h) and beta=2ell. Then exactly

This follows either by diagonalizing h in the actual charged Fock trace or
by the finite Klich trace identity. It is a finite trace statement with the
original fermionic occupation order, not a change of the given time.

The AP first-order Green kernel of partial_r+h on a circle of length beta
has, for0<r<beta,

Therefore an ordered Grassmann contraction at0+ is a(h) even though the usual
occupation <a^dag a> is c(h). Both are correct and belong to the SAME source.
Their names or numerical signs must not be interchanged during transport.
In particular

 log((1-c)c^-1)=+beta h.

because R_f(0)=||f||² and R_f'(0)=0. The second logarithmic Dyson coefficient
is minus the ordered covariance integral. Hence each original singular pair
