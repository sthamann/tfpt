2. COMPANION PROOF WITH ALL NONCOMMUTING MATRICES RETAINED

Set S=sqrt(C), the positive invertible square root. The companion
 T=[[-2B,S],[S,0]]
is Hermitian. It has no zero eigenvalue: T(x,y)=0 implies Sx=0, hence
x=0 and Sy=0, hence y=0. For every nonzero v,

 Q_n(v)x=0
 iff T (x,Sx/v)=v(x,Sx/v).                              (2)

Conversely the bottom companion equation gives y=Sx/v and the top
equation gives (1), without interchanging B and C. Thus all characteristic
roots are real and nonzero. There are exactly d positive and d negative
roots counted with multiplicity for a d-dimensional block: continuously
replace B by tB; no eigenvalue can crosszero, and at t=0 the companion
has eigenvalues +/-sqrt(spec C). This supplies the existence/inertia
detail behind section4's sentence.

Within THIS exact positive Hermitian quadratic class, if ALL complete
characteristic roots are exactly +/-c, the converse factorization can
also be proved: the Hermitian companion has T^2=c^2 I; its lower-right
block is C=c^2 I and its offdiagonal block is -2B sqrt(C)=0, so B=0.
That restricted equivalence needs all physical characteristic branches,
with no hidden omitted block. It is not a replacement for the broader
source/pole typing in section2.

3. THE ERROR BOUND IS CORRECT

For any normalized x in ker Q_n(v), v>0, put
 r=v/c, b=<x,Bx>/c, s=<x,Cx>/c^2.
Hermiticity gives real b,s; the actual residual hypotheses give
 |b|<=eta, 1-epsilon<=s<=1+epsilon.
Taking the scalar expectation of Q_n(v)x=0 yields
 r^2+2br-s=0.
Its positive root -b+sqrt(b^2+s) increases with s and decreases with b
because s>0. Therefore exactly

 sqrt(eta^2+1-epsilon)-eta <=v/c
                  <=sqrt(eta^2+1+epsilon)+eta.            (3)

The lower endpoint is strictly positive. The eta=0 and epsilon=eta=0
specializations in the source are correct. This is an enclosure for the
principal characteristic ratios of the stated Hermitian block, not a
claim about finite-momentum massive group velocities, damped complex
thermal poles or the UV front of a nonlocal effective kernel. The source
already says the relevant latter distinction explicitly.
