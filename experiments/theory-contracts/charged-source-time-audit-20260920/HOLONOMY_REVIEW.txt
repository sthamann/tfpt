2. Independent proof of positivity, kernel and gap
===================================================

For a charge/oscillator basis vector write

  x=|p|^2/2,  N=N_osc in Z_>=0,
  H_delta(p,N)=N+x-(s.p)/2.

Here |s|^2=2 and therefore |s/2|^2=1/2.  Every nonzero E8 lattice vector has
|p|^2>=2, so x>=1.  Cauchy--Schwarz gives

  (s.p)/2 <= |s/2| |p| = sqrt(x),
  H_delta >= N+x-sqrt(x) >= 0.

For zero energy, N=0.  If p is nonzero, both inequalities must be equalities.
Since x>=1 and x-sqrt(x)=0 only at x=1, p must have norm squared two and be
positively parallel to s/2.  Norm then fixes p=s.  Thus the only zero modes are
p=0 and p=s.  The negative parallel vector -s has energy 2, not zero.

For x>=2 the bound is x-sqrt(x)>=2-sqrt(2)>1/2.  Oscillators cost positive
integers.  The root census below contains energy 1/2, so the full conditional
target gap above the two-dimensional kernel is exactly 1/2.

This proof uses the complete E8 lattice, not just a finite root enumeration.
It does not prove that this lattice Hilbert space, its Hamiltonian or either
zero state is realized or selected by the microscopic source.

===========================

The 112 integer roots are +/-e_i +/-e_j, i<j.  Their coordinate sums and
energies 1-(sum_i p_i)/4 are

  sum +2: 28 roots at 1/2,
  sum  0: 56 roots at 1,
  sum -2: 28 roots at 3/2.

The 128 spinor roots are (1/2)(+/-1,...,+/-1) with an even number k of minus
signs.  Their energy is k/4.  For k=0,2,4,6,8 the multiplicities are
1,28,70,28,1.  Adding the two families gives

  {0:1, 1/2:56, 1:126, 3/2:56, 2:1}.

An independent exact enumeration reproduced all 240 roots and this counter.
The eight Cartan creation modes do not change P and hence remain at energy 1.

4. Mode commutator and the convention that must be stated
==========================================================

Use charge momentum P and physics/affine mode labels for which

  [P,V_{p,n}]=p V_{p,n},
  [L0,V_{p,n}]=-n V_{p,n}.

Then, on the common finite-energy core,

  [H_delta,V_{p,n}]=(-n-(s.p)/2)V_{p,n}.              (1)

For the E8 root currents this is unambiguous because every root field has
ordinary conformal weight one.  If one instead uses the VOA nth-product
convention Y(e^p,z)=sum_m (e^p)_m z^(-m-1), the L0 term is
(|p|^2/2-1-m); equation (1) is recovered after translating the mode label.
This convention warning prevents a spurious sign or h-1 discrepancy.

The source orientation agrees with (1).  In microscopic-charged-car-limit,
Psi=a(Rg) satisfies [Q,Psi]=-Psi and its adjoint has charge +1.  The r=1 top
edge has a=1/4 and therefore H=L0-Q/4.  Reversing the background to r=3 gives
the polynomial E_top,3(q)=q^2/2+q/4, i.e. the opposite chemical sign.  Thus
ordinary charge conjugation p->-p exchanges the two background orientations:
it maps H_delta to H_-delta.  It is not a symmetry of one fixed r=1 sector.

5. The blind operator class
===========================

For a number-conserving bilinear current E^{ab}_n the lattice charge is
p=e_a-e_b.  Since s.p=0,

  [H_delta,E^{ab}_n]=-n E^{ab}_n,

exactly as for L0.  Any frequency, bracket or time-response test using only
these U(8)-type currents is blind to the quarter-holonomy term.

The missing operators see it immediately:

  pair current p=e_a+e_b:  [H_delta,V_{p,n}]=(-n-1/2)V_{p,n};
  spinor p=s:              [H_delta,V_{s,n}]=(-n-1)V_{s,n};
  conjugate p=-s:          [H_delta,V_{-s,n}]=(-n+1)V_{-s,n}.

Consequently a common-frequency result proved on number-conserving E^{ab}
does not establish a common-frequency action on the E8 extension.  A decisive
source test must contain at least one operator with nonzero Q_total, its
adjoint, and a nonzero bracket/composition path.  Pair currents already detect
the problem; the spinor field tests the actual intersector carry.

6. Fixed-holonomy failure of actual C/J covariance
==================================================

Let R be an orthogonal lattice clock.  A lift sending p to Rp takes H_delta to
the Hamiltonian with the correspondingly rotated chemical vector.  A fixed
background is invariant only if R fixes delta=s/2 (equivalently the appropriate
transpose fixes it, depending on the active/passive convention).

The actual exact clock certificate rules this out.  Each of C, J and compiler-c
has zero fixed Cartan dimension.  More concretely,

  C s=(0,-1,0,0,0,0,0,1),
  J s=(-1/2,1/2,-1/2,1/2,-1/2,1/2,-1/2,1/2).

Both are E8 roots.  Direct substitution gives

  H_delta(s)=0,  H_delta(C s)=1,  H_delta(J s)=1.

Thus neither clock can commute with this fixed H_delta.  The exact certificate
also gives det(I-C)=1 and J^2=-I, so there is no nonzero fixed-vector repair
inside the same Cartan space.  This is the concrete instance of the previously
proved general rule: a diagonal affine derivation can contain alpha(h), but
actual C covariance forces h=0.  Here h=-s/2 is nonzero.

Do not confuse Coxeter C in this paragraph with ordinary charge conjugation.
Both fail for a fixed oriented holonomy, but for different reasons: the clocks
rotate s, while charge conjugation reverses it.

7. Sector-relative constants do not preserve one scalar-frequency field
==========================================================================

Let Pi_0 project to the D8 sector and Pi_1 to the spinor coset D8+s, and try

  H' = H_delta + c_0 Pi_0 + c_1 Pi_1.

This can move the relative vacuum energies.  For example c_1-c_0=1 lifts the
p=s target vacuum from zero to one.  However an intersector vertex V_p with
p in D8+s toggles the two sectors.  On an input from sector b,

  [c_0 Pi_0+c_1 Pi_1,V_p] Pi_b
    =(c_(1-b)-c_b) V_p Pi_b.

The two values are +(c_1-c_0) and -(c_1-c_0).  They are one state-independent
scalar only when c_1=c_0, in which case the relative shift does nothing.
Therefore a nontrivial sector constant cannot simultaneously

  * remove the second vacuum,
  * keep one scalar-frequency action on the full intersector field,
  * retain its adjoint, and
  * retain repeated charge carry/composition.

One may restrict to a one-way component and absorb one sign into its mode
label, but its adjoint has the opposite sign and the second application exposes
the sector dependence.  That is a sector-covariance cocycle or a pair of
oriented fields, not the single H=aL0+c action asserted in the attachment.
It would need its own microscopic derivation and common-domain proof.

8. What can be repaired, and what each repair costs
====================================================

(a) Co-transform the background.  Enlarge the theory to the C/J orbit of
delta and let clocks permute background sectors.  This restores covariance of
the family {H_Rdelta}, not invariance of one fixed source Hamiltonian.  A source
construction, superselection rule and physical state must select or sum the
backgrounds.

(b) Pair opposite source orientations.  The actual r=1 and r=3 top sectors,
or a top/bottom construction with compensating offsets, can cancel a total
chemical term.  This may restore a global charge-conjugation action.  It does
not derive eight channels, the E8 lattice restriction, the cocycle, or C/J
implementers, and it cannot be replaced by deleting the compensating edge.

(c) Demand physical C/J invariance on one affine E8 target.  Since the joint
fixed Cartan space is zero, the only linear chemical vector is delta=0, leaving
H=aL0+c after time calibration and vacuum normalization.  Getting delta=0 from
the oriented quarter-holonomy source requires a derived compensation or
spectral-flow dictionary; simply dropping -Q_total/4 erases the actual source.

(d) Keep H_delta and accept the symmetry breaking.  This is a consistent
positive target Hamiltonian with two ground states and the spectrum above, but
then it is not the clock-covariant common E8 time assumed by the attachment.

(e) Declare a shifted Virasoro element.  This is standard shifted-lattice-VOA
mathematics, not a repair preserving the original unitary E8_1 conformal
structure.  Dong--Mason's formula gives weights
N+|p|^2/2-(h,p) and central charge rank(L)-12|h|^2.  Here h=s/2 has norm squared
1/2, so c changes from 8 to 2; with the original adjoints the shifted Virasoro
modes also fail the original unitary adjoint relation.  The same warning is
already recorded in half-charge-energy-bridge Section 5.

11. Follow-up: exact time-dependent clock transport at the target level
=======================================================================

The proposed construction is algebraically correct.  It gives an exact
time-dependent covariant action on the already stipulated E8 target.  Its
correct interpretation is crucial: it is Heisenberg transport of a chosen
lattice lift, not a proof that the fixed clock is a symmetry of H_delta and not
a derivation of either the clock or H_delta from the microscopic source.

11.1 Sign and operator identities
---------------------------------

Let g be an orthogonal E8 lattice isometry and choose a normalized unitary
lift

  U_g |p> = eta_g(p) |g p>,  eta_g(0)=1,  |eta_g(p)|=1.

With P a column of charge operators, this ket convention gives

  U_g P U_g^dagger = g^(-1) P,
  U_g H_delta U_g^dagger = H_(g delta),

where H_delta=L0-delta.P and U_g commutes with L0 up to the natural action on
oscillators.  Therefore U_g is a static intertwiner between the background
Hamiltonians H_delta and H_(g delta).  It is a symmetry of one fixed
Hamiltonian only when g delta=delta.

Define, exactly as proposed,

  W_g(t)=exp(i t H_delta) U_g exp(-i t H_delta).

Since H_delta and H_(g delta) commute, their difference is

  H_delta-H_(g delta)=(g delta-delta).P,

and hence

  W_g(t)=exp[i t (g delta-delta).P] U_g.                (2)

On a charge ket,

  W_g(t)|p>
   =eta_g(p) exp{i t [E_delta(gp)-E_delta(p)]}|gp>,

because

  E_delta(gp)-E_delta(p)
   =delta.p-delta.(gp)
   =(g delta-delta).(g p).

Thus both the sign and the placement of the phase in (2) are correct.  Moving
the phase to the right of U_g instead gives the equivalent expression

  W_g(t)=U_g exp[i t (delta-g^T delta).P].

These formulas also make the earlier mismatch explicit rather than removing
it: for p=s and g=C or J the phase frequency is E(gs)-E(s)=1.

11.2 The inherited lattice-cocycle phase
----------------------------------------

Write the twisted lattice multiplication as

  e_p e_q = epsilon(p,q) e_(p+q).

A charge phase eta_g defines a lift of g as an algebra automorphism precisely
when

  epsilon(p,q) eta_g(p+q)
   =eta_g(p) eta_g(q) epsilon(gp,gq).                   (3)

For a normalized bilinear cocycle epsilon, the ratio between epsilon and its
g-transform has the same commutator because g preserves the integral lattice
pairing.  It is therefore a coboundary on the free lattice, and an inherited
quadratic/character choice of eta_g can solve (3).  The choice is not unique:
multiplying eta_g by a lattice character gives another lift.  This is the
standard character kernel of lattice-isometry lifts, not a physical ambiguity
resolved by the present Hamiltonian.

The extra time factor is

  chi_(g,t)(p)=exp{i t [delta.p-delta.(gp)]}.

It is a character of p because the quadratic L0 contribution cancels under
the orthogonal isometry.  Consequently eta_g,t=chi_(g,t) eta_g obeys the same
cocycle equation (3).  The Heisenberg dressing therefore preserves all
charge-addition and cocycle phases already present in the chosen lift.  It
does not repair a wrong or absent eta_g.

Adjunction must still be inherited rather than inferred from (3) alone.  With
e_p^dagger=kappa(p)e_-p in the selected cocycle convention, the finite check is

  conjugate(eta_g(p)) kappa(gp)
   =kappa(p) eta_g(-p),

with the exact placement of kappa fixed by that convention.  A root-pair
enumeration tests only the enumerated set unless eta_g is given on a lattice
basis and (3), bilinearity and the quadratic refinement prove its extension to
all lattice charges.

11.3 Composition and clock orders
---------------------------------

Conjugation by one common unitary evolution preserves products exactly:

  W_g(t) W_h(t)
   =exp(i t H_delta) U_g U_h exp(-i t H_delta).

Thus, if the chosen lifts form an exact representation,

  U_g U_h=U_(gh)  implies  W_g(t)W_h(t)=W_(gh)(t).

More generally lattice lifts may satisfy

  U_g U_h=K_(g,h)(P) U_(gh),

where K_(g,h) is an inherited lattice character.  Since K(P) commutes with
H_delta, the same character kernel appears after dressing:

  W_g(t)W_h(t)=K_(g,h)(P)W_(gh)(t).

No new projective or character defect is generated.  Likewise

  W_g(t)^N=exp(i t H_delta) U_g^N exp(-i t H_delta).

If the pinned finite lift checks establish U_C^30=I and U_J^4=I exactly, then
W_C(t)^30=W_J(t)^4=I for every fixed t.  If the undressed powers close only to
a character, the dressed powers close to that same character; time transport
does not promote them to exact orders.

11.4 What kind of symmetry this is
----------------------------------

In the Heisenberg convention alpha_t(A)=exp(i t H)A exp(-i t H), equation (2)
is simply

  W_g(t)=alpha_t(U_g).

Every unitary U admits this construction for every Hamiltonian.  Therefore
existence of W_g(t), preservation of its group law and preservation of lift
orders are exact but kinematic facts.  They show that the clock operation can
be transported consistently along the same target time evolution.  They do
not show

  [H_delta,U_g]=0,
  alpha_t composed with Ad(U_g)=Ad(U_g) composed with alpha_t,
  preservation of energy eigenspaces or of the two-dimensional ground space,
  or selection of H_delta from a symmetry commutant.

Indeed W_C(t) and W_J(t) still send the zero-energy charge ket |s> to the
energy-one kets |Cs> and |Js>, up to the displayed time phase.  A phase cannot
change their energy.  Calling W a fixed-H symmetry would therefore be false.

There are two legitimate readings:

  (a) Background covariance: U_g statically identifies the member H_delta of
      a Hamiltonian family with H_(g delta).  Then g acts on the holonomy
      background as well as on charge.

  (b) Time-dependent frame: W_g(t) is the time-evolved implementation of the
      chosen initial clock operation on one fixed Hilbert space.  The resulting
      automorphism Ad(W_g(t)) is alpha_t Ad(U_g) alpha_-t.

Neither reading is an ordinary conserved internal symmetry.  Under the
standard Schrodinger convention i d|psi>/dt=H|psi>, the operator that maps a
solution with initial datum |psi_0> to the solution with initial datum
U_g|psi_0> is instead

  S_g(t)=exp(-i t H_delta) U_g exp(i t H_delta)=W_g(-t).

This too exists for every U_g.  It is useful bookkeeping for histories, but
without an independently specified action on backgrounds, observables,
sources and allowed controls it has no selection power.

11.5 Exact target advance and strict boundary
---------------------------------------------

The constructive advance is real but narrow:

  * fixed-clock noncommutation does not make the abstract clock action
    inconsistent with target time evolution;
  * the chosen cocycle lift has an exact time-dependent conjugate with the same
    multiplication law, adjunction and possible character kernel;
  * the clock mismatch is represented by an explicit Cartan character rather
    than hidden or erased.

It does not remove the two target vacua, restore fixed-background C/J
invariance, prove that W_g(t) is local or generated by an allowed physical
control, select vL0 from the larger Hamiltonian family, or construct the
many-body source half-charge field.  In particular, the tautological existence
of alpha_t(U_g) must not be reported as a derivation of common physical clocks
from P1/P2.

The strongest honest statement is:

  GIVEN the conditional E8 target, H_delta and a pinned cocycle-compatible
  lift U_g, there is an exact time-dependent covariant lift W_g(t) carrying the
  same group/character law under the same target evolution.  Whether this
  transported operation is a physical clock transformation of the raw source,
  and whether the holonomy background must co-transform, remain open.
