Verdict: OPEN, but the first missing arrow is now sharply typed.

QUESTION
========

What does the actual P1/P2 source supply, what is derived from it, and where is
the first still-unproved arrow to a local one-particle/CAR source with an
internal rank-eight multiplicity?
In particular: does primitive admissibility or the Yukawa contraction construct
the local source with its rank-eight internal multiplicity, or does it already
assume a finite internal representation?


SHORT ANSWER
============

The raw one-sided datum is

  T_boundary = (A_+, H_+, D_+, J, Gamma, B_Sigma).

From it the archival source constructs the double, the deck/reflection data,
the Calderon projector/involution, and a primitive admissibility projection
P_prim.  The current P1 adds a primitive reflection-positive seam with
[u_Sigma]=1 and c_3=1/(8 pi).  Current P2 supplies a five-slot readout
(3 colour + 2 weak) as an interface axiom; the later charge theorem is
conditional on that carrier.

None of these definitions constructs a canonical internal rank-eight
multiplicity bundle or local scaling sector inside the raw boundary Cauchy
space.  The archival source first says “let
H_car denote the finite-dimensional carrier block selected after primitive
admissibility compression”; it does not define the selection.  Its own final
boundary-kernel definition explicitly contains no carrier split, family
geometry, determinant class, or transport data.

There is also an earlier exact typing test.  Since
P_prim=P_Seam,+ P_prim,gap and P_Seam,+=(1+iota_C)/2, iota_C acts as +1 on
Ran(P_prim).  Therefore, if H_car is literally a subspace of Ran(P_prim), the
declared restriction epsilon_car=iota_C|H_car is +I, so E_-=0.  The intended
3+2 split requires H_car to live before that seam-even compression or to be
connected by a separately defined map that does not factor through the
seam-even range.  No such map is currently displayed.

Likewise, the Lean `BoundaryYukawaKernel` already contains omega together with
injective and surjective contraction.  It is exactly equivalent (under the
stated nontriviality hypothesis) to rank(E_-)=3.  The Lean file expressly does
not prove

  raw TFPT boundary kernel -> BoundaryYukawaKernel.

The converse builds omega from a chosen basis once rank 3 is already known, so
it cannot be used as an origin proof.  `SeamWindingData 3 2` similarly already
has the ranks 3 and 2 as type parameters and the determinant/orientation/
primitivity equations as fields.  It proves the charges (-2,3) only after that
input; it does not derive `SeamWindingData (3,2)` from [u_Sigma]=1.

Three objects must be kept distinct:

  (a) a finite internal polarization/multiplicity K_int ~= C^8;
  (b) an infinite-dimensional local source space, locally of the form
      L^2(Sigma) tensor K_int, or more generally L^2-sections of a rank-eight
      multiplicity bundle;
  (c) the later mu4 simple-current/vertex extension from D5_1 x A3_1 to the
      E8 level-one net.

A globally finite-rank projector on the whole Cauchy space cannot produce
eight propagating local channels: it would retain only finitely many global
modes and destroy the local field space.  The earliest missing carrier arrow
is the typed pre-/post-compression map that preserves both carrier signs.  At
the later rank-eight source stage, the missing arrow is a canonical,
symmetry-compatible local multiplicity bundle, symbol sector, or local scaling
sector in the raw Calderon/admissibility data.  The current “fixed rank-8
polarization K_Sigma” is still a premise unless read as such an internal fiber.
The Yukawa map is another upstream carrier-rank arrow; even closing it would
not by itself construct the local source or the E8 vertex extension.


1. ACTUAL INPUT / DERIVED / ASSUMED LAYERS
==========================================

Layer A — raw one-sided analytic datum (input class)
----------------------------------------------------

The original boundary source defines

  (A_+, H_+, D_+, J, Gamma, B_Sigma),

with a compact oriented manifold with boundary encoded by the centre,
self-adjoint tangential B_Sigma compatible with J and Gamma, collar form

  D_+ = gamma_n (partial_n + B_Sigma),

and reflection regularity sufficient to form the double and Calderon data.
Source: `_archive/tfpt-45/source_extracts/01_boundary_kernel_source.tex`,
lines 65-80.

The almost-commutative presentation

  H ~= L^2(M_tilde,S) tensor H_int

is explicitly introduced only as a chosen realization, not as an output of
the raw boundary datum.  Hence H_int and its dimension cannot be counted as
already derived.  Source: same file, lines 83-91.

Layer B — exact constructions from the raw datum
------------------------------------------------

The double, the deck involution, D_ref/D_rel, the Calderon projector P_C and

  iota_C = 2 P_C - 1

are constructed from the one-sided analytic data, subject to the declared
reflection-regularity hypotheses.  Source: same file, lines 42-55 and 94-170.

The primitive complex is then defined on an enlarged Hilbert space.  It uses

  P_Sigma^+ = (1+iota_C)/2,

APS/relative conditions, ghost variables, positive penalty parameters,
epsilon_adm, and the projection

  P_prim = Pi_ker(Delta_prim).

Source: same file, lines 192-271.  This is a genuine analytic compression, but
it does not construct a rank-eight internal multiplicity bundle or a local
rank-eight scaling sector.  The construction also contains
declared realization choices (tensor-sector split, epsilon_adm, penalty
operators/commuting-sector assumptions); positivity makes the kernel
insensitive to the numerical positive weights, but it does not create a
canonical eight-dimensional internal fiber over every local/momentum mode.

Layer C — P1/P2 inputs and their exact downstream results
---------------------------------------------------------

The current architecture states:

  P1: primitive reflection-positive oriented seam, [u_Sigma]=1,
      c_3=1/(8 pi);
  P2_interface: a five-slot carrier readout, 3 colour + 2 weak;
  P2_algebra: conditional algebraic rigidity once that carrier is supplied.

Source: `tfpt_1_architecture_e8.tex`, lines 155-192.  The text itself calls P1
and P2 inputs.  P2_interface is not a construction of five modes from B_Sigma.

The archival boundary source agrees with this boundary: its final primitive
kernel is

  T_kernel^boundary =
    (A,H,D,J,Gamma,tau_double,iota_C,P_prim,[u_Sigma],c_3),

and it explicitly says that no carrier split, hypercharge, family geometry,
determinant class, transport pole, or vacuum datum is part of the definition.
Source: `01_boundary_kernel_source.tex`, lines 700-731.

Layer D — first unconstructed finite carrier
--------------------------------------------

The same archival source later begins with

  “Let H_car denote the finite-dimensional carrier block selected after
   primitive admissibility compression”

and restricts iota_C to it to define E_- and E_+.  No spectral projection,
universal property, functor, or uniqueness proof selects H_car from P_prim.
Source: `01_boundary_kernel_source.tex`, lines 274-299.

The literal same-space reading is not merely unspecified; it collapses the
negative block.  From lines 204-261,

  P_Seam,+ = (1+iota_C)/2,
  P_prim = P_Seam,+ P_prim,gap.

Hence

  P_Seam,+ P_prim = P_prim,
  iota_C P_prim = P_prim.

For every v in Ran(P_prim), iota_C v=v.  Thus, over the complex Hilbert space,
if

  H_car subset Ran(P_prim),

then

  epsilon_car=iota_C|H_car=+I,
  E_-=ker(epsilon_car+I)=0,
  E_+=H_car.

The conclusion is unchanged if H_car is produced by any selector whose range
lies in Ran(P_prim), or by an injective iota_C-intertwining map into that range.
The later formula P_adm=P_prim P_sing P_Theta cannot restore the negative block,
because its range is still contained in Ran(P_prim).  This is a conditional,
local typing result about the displayed compression chain; it is not a global
no-go for P1/P2.

The source mentions at lines 172-179 that only the displayed restriction and
“transport maps” may relate the involutions, but neither that passage nor the
audited later carrier/QFT sources give a carrier transport map that changes
this conclusion.  The chosen
almost-commutative factorization at lines 149-154 also does not repair it:
H_int is expressly not a reconstruction output, and no map is defined that
induces iota_C on an internal carrier factor.  The later QFT source
(`04_qft_source.tex`, lines 1880-1925) asserts the finite carrier block and its
functorial preservation but again supplies no inclusion, quotient, partial
isometry, or factor map from the pre- or post-compression Cauchy space.

The Lean interfaces preserve the same gap.  `CalderonInterface.lean`, lines
43-48 and 66-70, packages an arbitrary finite involution as the future target
of an analytic boundary theorem.  `CalderonProjector.lean`, lines 53-76,
packages an idempotent directly in the carrier endomorphism ring.  Neither
formalizes the global Calderon projector -> finite carrier map, and neither
proves that the carrier is a subspace of Ran(P_prim).

There are two type-consistent readings, but the source must choose and define
one:

  (A) Pre-compression subspace: an iota_C-invariant finite carrier subspace of
      the full doubled Cauchy space, with both signs present.  “Selected after”
      then describes logical order, not inclusion in Ran(P_prim).  The source
      needs its selector/inclusion Q_car or j_car and must state that it does
      not factor through P_Seam,+.

  (B) Abstract carrier/factor: a finite multiplicity space reached from the
      pre-compression boundary space by a named quotient, partial isometry or
      factorization map T_car, with

        epsilon_car T_car = T_car iota_C

      on the declared domain.  If T_car factors only through Ran(P_prim), this
      equation again forces epsilon_car=+I on its image, so the domain must
      retain both iota_C signs or the carrier involution must have a distinct
      explicitly derived origin.

The smallest missing map at this point is therefore not “some finite block”,
but the typed pre-/post-compression relation

  (H_Cauchy,iota_C,P_prim) -> (H_car,epsilon_car)

showing exactly where both carrier signs survive.

Essentialization does remove summands with zero seam flow, trivial carrier
polarization, zero determinant degree and zero primitive Yukawa type.  This is
why arbitrary invisible spectator direct sums are not valid countermodels.
But essentialization is a quotient criterion, not a construction of H_car;
some of its tests already mention carrier polarization and Yukawa type.
Source: same file, lines 523-560.

The claimed unique defect-minimal representative is also insufficient as a
selector theorem.  Its written proof establishes existence of a minimizer by
well-foundedness and removes contractible spectators, but the asserted
uniqueness/classification is not established there.  It does not exhibit the
finite projection.  Source: same file, lines 646-683.


2. WHAT THE YUKAWA AND WINDING INTERFACES ACTUALLY PROVE
========================================================

Boundary Yukawa interface
-------------------------

`BoundaryYukawaKernel K E` has four fields:

  omega : Lambda^3 E -> K,
  contraction injective,
  contraction surjective,
  E nontrivial.

Source: `experiments/lean4-carrier-rigidity/TfptCarrier/
BoundaryYukawaKernelInterface.lean`, lines 69-93.

The exact Lean theorem is

  BoundaryYukawaKernel K E -> finrank_K(E)=3,

and conversely any rank-three E admits such a package by choosing a basis.
Thus nonemptiness of the interface is equivalent to rank 3.  Source: same
file, lines 100-134.

The file explicitly states that it formalises only the packaged-interface
implication and does not formalise

  TFPT boundary kernel -> BoundaryYukawaKernel K E.

It also says canonicity from TFPT boundary data remains upstream physics.
Source: same file, lines 18-57.

This is the strongest exact positive Yukawa result: if the raw boundary really
produces a canonical omega with injective and surjective contraction, then
rank(E_-)=3 follows without a separate rank assumption.  But building omega
from a basis after assuming rank 3 is the converse, not the missing origin
arrow.

Seam winding interface
----------------------

`SeamWindingData m n` already stores q_-, q_+, determinant trace zero,
orientation signs, and gcd=1.  It is a typed renaming of the primitive oriented
determinant-carrier input.  The file explicitly does not formalise

  [u_Sigma]=1 -> SeamWindingData(m,n).

Source: `experiments/lean4-carrier-rigidity/TfptCarrier/
SeamWindingInterface.lean`, lines 31-45 and 56-126.

Only after specializing to `SeamWindingData 3 2` does Lean force
(q_-,q_+)=(-2,3).  The ranks are parameters, not outputs.  Source: same file,
lines 128-135.

Carrier paper
-------------

The carrier source derives the 3+2 normal form from a collection of carrier/
Higgs/Yukawa hypotheses.  The key negative-block step uses the canonical
contraction E_- tensor Lambda^2 E_- -> Lambda^3 E_- together with a primitive,
no-spectator/top-form condition.  This downstream algebra is compatible with
the Lean correction above, but the paper does not supply the raw-boundary map
that canonically creates that top form.  The family transport construction is
also downstream: it assumes the four-punctured mu4 geometry and the already
fixed family rank.  Sources:

  `_archive/tfpt-45/source_extracts/02_carrier_source.tex`,
    lines 8-74, 143-159, 2400-2562;
  `_archive/tfpt-45/source_extracts/03_em_flavor_source.tex`,
    lines 1188-1320.


3. STRONGEST POSITIVE CHAIN ALREADY ESTABLISHED
===============================================

The following conditional chain is real and should be retained:

  a. A canonical boundary-produced omega on E_- with injective and surjective
     contraction forces rank(E_-)=3 exactly in Lean.

  b. Given the compact Higgs/top-form input on E_+, the positive block has
     rank 2.

  c. Given `SeamWindingData 3 2`, determinant trace zero, orientation and
     primitivity force the unique charge pair (-2,3).

  d. Given the D5 + A3 lattice data and mu4 glue, the E8 rank-eight lattice/net
     completion is exact.

  e. On the multiplicity-free three-character H^1 block, mu4-equivariance plus
     the three eigenvalues determines the transfer operator exactly, not only
     its spectrum.  `QGEO.REDUCE.01` records this finite theorem.

  f. Given a quasi-free covariance on the full local one-particle space
     H_source (for example L^2(Sigma) tensor K_int with dim K_int=8),
     Wick/Araki reconstruction fixes the quasi-free state and its
     correlations.

These are conditional downstream theorems.  They do not construct the
local source space or its internal rank-eight multiplicity from the raw
boundary datum.

This distinction is visible in `verification/v113_quasifree_kernel.py`, lines
149-170: the “seam level” creates a 16-by-16 standard Majorana matrix by hand,
then verifies that its polarization has rank 8.  The script labels the premise
merge “[P] (recorded, not claimed)”.  It checks a finite internal target after
choosing the 16 real Majoranas; it does not derive the internal factor from
B_Sigma and does not construct the infinite-dimensional local boundary
one-particle space.

The current ledger is therefore correctly open:

  QGEO.KERNEL.01:
    raw C_Sigma = U^{-1} C_mu4 U as an operator.

Its reduced form still assumes that the full raw operator reduces to the
three-dimensional moving transfer block plus a fixed rank-eight polarization
K_Sigma.  Sources: `verification/status_ledger.csv`, rows 257-258;
`verification/v178_marks_kernel_attempt.py`, lines 30-55.

The later source/RG audit independently locates the same two smaller holes:

  raw boundary kernel -> BoundaryYukawaKernel K E_-,
  [u_Sigma]=1 -> SeamWindingData(3,2),

and stresses that closing the first gives an internal rank, not eight
propagating CAR fields.  Source:
`experiments/theory-contracts/source-rg-clock-bridge-20260920/PROOF.txt`,
lines 212-234.


4. FIRST STILL-UNPROVED LOCAL SOURCE ARROW
==========================================

The missing theorem should be stated as a local source functor/selector, not
as a global finite-dimensional eigenspace or a dimension coincidence.  A
correct target has the form

  LocSel_Sigma(T_boundary, P1, P2)
    = (E_int -> Sigma, H_source, p_source, rho_Sigma, U_Sigma),

Required output:

  1. E_int is an intrinsically selected Hermitian/real graded multiplicity
     bundle of fiber rank 8 over the seam (or over the relevant cosphere/
     momentum space), or an equivalent rank-eight local scaling sector.  A
     trivialization E_int ~= Sigma x C^8 must be derived if the target uses
     one; it is not automatic.

  2. H_source is the infinite-dimensional local one-particle space

       H_source = L^2(Sigma,E_int)

     in the bundle formulation, locally L^2(Sigma) tensor C^8.  The raw
     Calderon projection/covariance acts on H_source as an order-zero
     pseudodifferential or local scaling-limit operator.  “Rank 8” refers to
     the fiber/multiplicity, not to the Hilbert-space rank of H_source or of
     the full Calderon projector.

  3. The symbol projector p_source(x,xi), or the corresponding local scaling
     sector, is selected from the raw data and is invariant/intertwining under
     J, Gamma, iota_C, the raw order-four clock rho_Sigma and the relevant raw
     time/boundary evolution at the level required by the target theorem.

  4. U_Sigma is a localization-preserving unitary/module equivalence, fixed
     up to the declared target symmetry, from H_source to the standard local
     D5+A3 source.  It must intertwine the full source covariance/operator,
     the grading/reality/clock structures, and multiplication by local support
     functions.  Schematically,

       U_Sigma C_Sigma U_Sigma^* = C_mu4,
       U_Sigma J_Sigma = J_target U_Sigma,
       U_Sigma Gamma_Sigma = Gamma_target U_Sigma,
       U_Sigma rho_Sigma = rho_mu4 U_Sigma.

     A dynamics claim additionally needs the corresponding full local
     generator intertwining, rather than only a covariance or sign-projector
     match.

  5. The local CAR net is then built from interval/test-function subspaces,

       A_CAR(I) = CAR(L^2(I,E_int)),

     with the required graded locality.  This is still distinct from the E8
     vertex/net extension.  The latter is the downstream index-4 mu4
     simple-current extension of the D5_1 x A3_1 net and must use the already
     established lattice/net theorem; it is not created by a rank-eight
     finite matrix.

No current definition supplies this local bundle/symbol/scaling-sector map.
The existing downstream rigidity chain therefore cannot establish the raw
operator arrow.  This statement is more precise than a generic “P1/P2 do not
imply E8” claim and does not rely on forbidden spectator countermodels.


5. YUKAWA ORIGIN GAP AND POST-RANK NORMALIZATION
================================================

Once a genuine finite internal carrier fiber (or carrier multiplicity bundle)
has been selected inside the local source, a determinant-line trivialization
is a possible normalization datum:

  tau_Sigma : det(E_-) tensor det(E_+) -> C,

together with a canonically normalized weak top form

  nu_+ : det(E_+) -> C.

If n=rank(E_-), these data produce only the top-degree form

  omega_top,Sigma(x) = tau_Sigma(x tensor nu_+^{-1}(1)),
  x in det(E_-) = Lambda^n E_-.

This is a form on Lambda^n E_-, not a trilinear form on Lambda^3 E_-.
Only after an independent theorem has already established n=3 may one identify
det(E_-)=Lambda^3 E_- and use omega_top,Sigma to normalize or phase-fix a
Yukawa trilinear form.  Using that grade identification to derive n=3 would be
circular for exactly the same reason as the basis-built Lean converse.

Therefore this determinant-line route does not prove

  raw boundary kernel -> BoundaryYukawaKernel K E_-.

The genuine missing origin remains a boundary construction of an alternating
trilinear form

  omega_Sigma : Lambda^3 E_- -> C

together with injective and surjective contraction (CI/CS), without first
assuming rank(E_-)=3; alternatively an independent rank-three theorem must be
given first, after which the determinant line is only a normalization route.

To close the Lean interface one must prove from the raw boundary/kernel data:

  - independence from bases, collar charts and presentation;
  - covariance under the raw J, Gamma, iota_C and clock action;
  - a fixed phase/normalization from reflection orientation or another already
    present datum;
  - injectivity and surjectivity of contraction;
  - compatibility with essentialization.

The present winding interface supplies determinant trace zero and primitivity
only after a finite carrier is given.  It does not supply tau_Sigma, nu_+, or
their phase.  Even if those normalizations were supplied, they would close
only the post-rank normalization step unless rank 3 had already been proved by
an independent source argument.  No new origin chain is claimed from the
determinant-line grade identification.  This route also does not construct the
local source functor above or the later E8 vertex extension.


6. SMALLEST DECISIVE ORIGINAL-MAP TEST
======================================

The next test must operate on an explicit raw B_Sigma/DtN datum, not on a QWZ
substitute and not on a preselected E8 matrix.

Test 0 — same-space carrier/parity typing (earliest exact gate)
----------------------------------------------------------------

Before any spectrum or dynamics calculation, require the proposed carrier map
to state its domain and codomain.  Check the identity

  P_Seam,+ P_prim = P_prim.

If the proposal declares H_car subset Ran(P_prim), or embeds H_car into that
range while intertwining epsilon_car with iota_C, stop: the exact projector
identity gives epsilon_car=+I and E_-=0.  Such a proposal cannot be the stated
3+2 carrier.

Acceptance requires one of the two explicitly typed alternatives already
listed above: a pre-compression iota_C-invariant carrier selector whose image
contains both signs, or a named pre-compression transport/factor map that
induces a nontrivial epsilon_car.  Merely saying “selected after compression”
or wrapping an arbitrary finite involution in the Lean certificate does not
pass this gate.  This test refutes only the literal same-space compression
chain, not P1/P2 or a correctly typed alternative.

Test A — raw clock covariance (first obstruction)
-------------------------------------------------

Given the raw order-four clock rho_Sigma on the raw Cauchy space, compute

  [rho_Sigma, Lambda_Sigma]

or the off-character matrix elements of the bounded sub-principal part after
the universal |k| principal symbol is removed.

The exact quasi-free state-invariance condition is

  omega o rho_Sigma = omega
    <=> [rho_Sigma,C_Sigma]=0

for a unitary one-particle implementer rho_Sigma and covariance C_Sigma.  If

  C_Sigma = (1 + sgn H_1)/2,

then

  [rho_Sigma,H_1]=0  =>  [rho_Sigma,C_Sigma]=0.

The converse is false for a general self-adjoint H_1: the sign projector knows
the positive/negative spectral subspaces but not the energy magnitudes inside
them.  It becomes an equivalence only with an extra proved hypothesis making
H_1 recoverable from C_Sigma, or otherwise fixing the magnitude/spectral
action within each sign sector.  The cited passage at
`tfpt_research_contracts.tex`, lines 450-473, states a reduction to
`[rho,H_1]=0`; this audit uses it only as the stronger sufficient operator
test unless those extra hypotheses are supplied.

For the narrower model Lambda=|k|+M_f, where rho is the quarter rotation and
M_f is multiplication by the curvature function f, the original Fourier
calculation is exact: [rho,Lambda]=0 iff f has Fourier support only in modes
0 mod 4.  Four equally spaced mark-local contributions have this support.
Source: `tfpt_research_contracts.tex`, lines 474-486.

Acceptance: all off-character blocks vanish for the actual raw datum, with
rho_Sigma obtained from P1/P2 rather than fitted after diagonalization.

Failure: one nonzero off-character block refutes the claimed clock covariance
for that raw datum.  Passing this test does not yet produce the local
rank-eight multiplicity sector.

Test B — intrinsic local multiplicity/symbol sector (correctly typed test)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Use the principal and sub-principal symbol of the actual raw Calderon/DtN
operator, or its rigorously defined local scaling limit.  Construct a candidate
equivariant symbol projector

  p_int(x,xi) : E_raw(x,xi) -> E_raw(x,xi)

whose range has constant internal rank 8 over the relevant seam/cosphere
sector.  Equivalently, construct a rank-eight multiplicity bundle E_int and a
local module embedding

  L^2(Sigma,E_int) -> H_Cauchy^ess.

Test:

  - constant fiber rank 8, without truncating spatial/momentum modes;
  - compatibility with the Calderon principal symbol and bounded
    sub-principal operator;
  - equivariance under J, Gamma, iota_C and rho_Sigma;
  - stability under the admissible collar changes and essentialization
    already allowed by the source class;
  - a localization-preserving intertwiner to the standard D5+A3 local source;
  - zero full-operator, clock and declared time-evolution defects on that
    infinite-dimensional source sector.

Acceptance: the raw source canonically supplies a rank-eight fiber/local
scaling multiplicity together with the infinite-dimensional local module and
the required full-operator intertwiners.

Diagnostic outcome: nonconstant fiber rank, failure of equivariance or
localization, or inequivalent source-module identifications not removed by
declared target symmetries.  This locates the failure of the proposed source
map for the tested datum; it is not a universal P1 impossibility theorem.

Optional Test B2 — internal Riesz selector only after factorization
-------------------------------------------------------------------

A Riesz projection is relevant only if an independent theorem first proves a
local factorization or direct-integral decomposition such as

  H_source ~= L^2(Sigma) tensor K_aux

and identifies an internal operator M_int on K_aux that commutes with the
local spatial algebra.  An isolated rank-eight spectral cluster of M_int may
then define a finite internal projector p_8 on K_aux.  The full local
projection is

  1_{L^2(Sigma)} tensor p_8,

which has infinite Hilbert-space rank and retains all local propagating modes.
Its symmetry, homotopy and essentialization properties may then be checked.

A finite-rank Riesz projection on the entire Cauchy space is not an acceptable
source of eight local CAR channels.  Without the independently proved
factorization, absence of a global isolated eight-dimensional eigencluster is
not an obstruction to the correct local-source map and creates no additional
necessary gap.


7. CAN THE DERIVATION BE CLOSED NOW?
====================================

No.  The canonical sources define a class of raw boundary data, but they do
not give one explicit B_Sigma/DtN operator from which Test A or Test B can be
run.  They also do not define the local rank-eight multiplicity/symbol sector
or the boundary determinant-line map.  Consequently the missing arrow cannot
be completed by further finite-dimension algebra alone.

The precise minimum needed to continue is:

  (i) a typed map relating the pre-compression boundary Cauchy space to H_car
      and proving where both epsilon_car signs survive;
  (ii) one explicit admissible raw boundary datum, including domains and the
       raw clock action;
  (iii) an intrinsic rule selecting the local rank-eight symbol/multiplicity
        bundle or scaling sector while retaining the infinite local mode space;
  (iv) the source-defined normalization that fixes the localization-preserving
       U_Sigma and, separately, the genuine omega_Sigma origin; determinant-line
       data enter only as post-rank normalization.

Items (i)-(iii) are an operational decomposition of the already open raw
operator/source arrow and the already identified Yukawa-origin arrow.  They
are not asserted as additional independent postulates or new gaps.

Once (i) and a candidate rule for (ii) are supplied, Tests A and B are small,
decisive, and directly tied to the original operator map.  Until then the
honest status is:

  - raw one-sided datum -> Calderon + primitive admissibility: constructed;
  - literal H_car subset Ran(P_prim) with epsilon_car=iota_C|H_car: excluded
    by the exact seam-even projector identity (it gives E_-=0);
  - a typed pre-compression/factor map yielding both carrier signs: open;
  - boundary-produced canonical Yukawa trilinear with CI/CS: open;
  - winding-one -> typed (3,2) determinant carrier: open;
  - raw Cauchy space -> local one-particle module with canonical rank-eight
    internal multiplicity: open;
  - conditional carrier/charge/E8/net rigidity after these inputs: strong and
    substantially exact.
