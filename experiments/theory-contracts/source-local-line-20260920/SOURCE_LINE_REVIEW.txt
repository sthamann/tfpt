INDEPENDENT REVIEW OF SOURCE_LINE_PROOF.txt
20 September 2026

Verdict: PASS for S1--S9 with the stated joint scaling and CAR-word scope.
No normalization or time-sign correction is required.  This is a direct
one-copy source limit, not a rank-eight construction or a full local-net
theorem.

1. Source symbol and residual (S1--S2)
======================================

The imported blocks give

  SZ + exp(-ip)TX + exp(ip)TX^dagger
    = (1-cos p)SZ-sin p SX.

The top vector has SX eigenvalue +1, SZ r_top=chi_top, and the transverse
term vanishes because TY^dagger r_top=0.  Hence S1 has the correct signs.
After D_a=h(ak)/a, the residual components along the orthonormal vectors
r_top and chi_top are k-sin(ak)/a and (1-cos(ak))/a.  The stated epsilon is
a valid triangle bound on their Euclidean norm.  source_exact_check.py
independently verifies the exact squared norm against the imported source.

2. Occupation and time sign (S3--S6)
====================================

The spectral-projector estimate is correct on both sides of zero.  For k>0,
the target energy -k is negative and the wrong spectrum [0,infinity) is at
distance at least k; for k<0, the wrong negative spectrum is at distance at
least |k| from -k>0.  The strict projector 1_(D<0) treats a possible zero
eigenvalue on the correct, bounded side.  The shifted finite mesh contains no
k=0.

The time sign is also correct:

  D_a r_top approximately -k r_top
  implies exp(-itD_a)r_top approximately exp(+itk)r_top.

Duhamel gives |t| epsilon without a gap assumption.  Splitting the matrix
element into the unitary residual and the occupation error gives S5, and
epsilon^2/k^2=(a|k|/2+a^2 k^2/6)^2 gives exactly the uniform bound S6.

3. Fourier normalization and the joint limit (S7--S9)
======================================================

The seam phase i^r obeys exp(ipN)=i^(-r) in the source hopping convention,
so p=2pi(j-r/4)/N and k=(j-r/4)/R have the stated sign.  The physical
circumference is Na=2piR, hence the k-mesh spacing is 1/R.  Therefore
R^(-1/2) in S7 is the correct unitary Riemann-sum normalization and gives S8.
Using N=2piR/a with the normalized discrete inverse Fourier transform also
reproduces the stated local real-space scale sqrt(a) f(ax).

The proof of S9 is sufficient for Schwartz f,g in the declared joint limit

  R -> infinity,  a -> 0,  pi/a -> infinity.

On a fixed band, S6 is uniform on bounded time intervals.  The shifted
Riemann sum crosses only one bounded jump at k=0, which does not affect the
limit and is O(1/R) for the smooth band weight.  Outside the band,
|<r,P exp(-itD)r>|<=1; shifted Riemann sums of the Schwartz majorant are
uniformly small for sufficiently large R, while pi/a -> infinity removes the
Brillouin cutoff.  This closes the advertised band-then-tail argument.

Removing P gives the full time-dependent CAR kernel; subtracting S9 gives
the complementary covariance.  Gauge-invariant quasifreeness then reduces
every fixed finite charged word to finitely many convergent two-point
kernels.  The result is uniform only for a fixed number of insertions and
bounded times, as claimed.

4. Locality and channel count
=============================

The locality conclusion is exactly graded CAR locality for the declared
smeared embeddings: disjoint limiting real-space supports have zero CAR
pairing.  It does not establish convergence of all source observables,
bounded local algebras, Haag duality, a completed conformal net, or a
source-to-E8 intertwiner.  SOURCE_LINE_PROOF.txt states this boundary
correctly.

The exact identities h(0)^3=h(0), rank h(0)=14 and trace h(0)=0 imply
multiplicities 7,2,7 at -1,0,+1.  The complete zero projector is the two
edge-row span, and its derivative compression diag(-1,+1) gives one
low-momentum chiral branch per edge.  This is a low-momentum statement for
the fixed width-eight source.  It neither turns the eight spatial rows into
species nor excludes different sources or unrelated high-momentum scalings.
