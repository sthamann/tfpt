THE SAME SOURCE ON A GROWING CIRCLE: CHARGED LOCAL LINE LIMIT
20 September 2026 — UR.SOURCE.LOCAL_LIMIT.01

Question and answer
===================

Does the quarter-holonomy mismatch found on a fixed circle also obstruct a
local charged field limit when the circle itself grows? No, for the precise
one-copy source below. Its original top-row charged CAR observables converge
to a single chiral free fermion on the line with their common time evolution.
The holonomy becomes locally invisible. This does not create eight species,
nor derive this auxiliary QWZ source from the abstract TFPT boundary datum.

The genuinely new comparison is the limit geometry: previous calculations
kept the physical circle radius fixed and refined its lattice. Here both
R -> infinity and a=2*pi*R/N -> 0. The seam multiplier i^r, source mass 1,
width 8 and all hopping matrices are unchanged. Choosing this joint limit
is an explicit physical scaling assumption, not a consequence of P1/P2.
Velocity and hbar are set to one. No numerical value of the speed of light
is predicted.

1. Actual operator, not a dispersion fitted to eigenvalues
==========================================================

Use verification/v1033_charged_disorder.py, qwz_cylinder, at width W=8 and
mass 1. Write its actual blocks as

 TX=-(i sigma_x+sigma_z)/2,
 TY=[[-1,-1],[1,1]]/2.

The 16-by-16 Hermitian Fourier strip has diagonal block

 (1-cos p) sigma_z - sin p sigma_x

and TY/TY^dagger between adjacent transverse rows. The seam sector r=1 or 3
sets the momentum mesh

 k_j=(j-r/4)/R,      p_j=a k_j=2*pi*(j-r/4)/N.

Take one set of N consecutive j such that p_j lies in a Brillouin interval
centred at zero. At fixed physical momentum this interval exhausts the line
as a -> 0. The physical one-particle generator is D_a(k)=h(a k)/a.

Let r_top have (1,1)/sqrt(2) only at row y=7 and let chi_top have
(1,-1)/sqrt(2) there. Direct substitution into the imported primitive blocks
gives the exact identity

 h(p) r_top = -sin p r_top + (1-cos p) chi_top.             (S1)

The transverse contribution vanishes; chi_top and r_top are orthonormal.
Consequently

 ||(D_a(k)+k)r_top||^2
    = (k-sin(a k)/a)^2 + ((1-cos(a k))/a)^2,

 ||(D_a(k)+k)r_top|| <= eps_a(k),
 eps_a(k)=a k^2/2+a^2 |k|^3/6.                            (S2)

Only |1-cos x|<=x^2/2 and |x-sin x|<=|x|^3/6 are used.
The estimate is on the actual row vector at the actual full strip operator;
it does not discard a transverse subspace or replace source coefficients.

2. Filled source state and time in the same estimate
====================================================

Let P_a(k)=1_(D_a(k)<0) be the negative-energy projection used by the source.
For any self-adjoint D and normalized r with ||(D+k)r||<=eps, the spectral
theorem gives, for k nonzero,

 |<r,1_(D<0)r>-1_(k>0)| <= eps^2/k^2.                    (S3)

Proof: if k>0, the wrong, nonnegative spectrum is at distance at least k
from the target eigenvalue -k; if k<0, the negative spectrum is at distance
at least |k|. Integrating (lambda+k)^2 against r's spectral measure bounds
the wrong-side weight. Possible zero eigenvalues obey the same estimate.
No separate assumption about a uniform spectral gap at k=0 is needed.
The quarter-shifted finite mesh never contains k=0.

Duhamel's formula on this finite strip also gives

 ||(exp(-itD_a(k))-exp(itk))r_top|| <= |t| eps_a(k).       (S4)

Since P_a is a contraction commuting with D_a, (S3)-(S4) imply

 |<r_top,P_a exp(-itD_a)r_top>-1_(k>0)exp(itk)|
       <= |t| eps_a(k)+eps_a(k)^2/k^2.                   (S5)

On every fixed band |k|<=K and bounded time interval |t|<=T, this is bounded
uniformly by

 T(a K^2/2+a^2 K^3/6)+(a K/2+a^2 K^2/6)^2 -> 0.         (S6)

The formula simultaneously controls the charged covariance and its actual
source time response. In particular a static sign-projector comparison is
not being used to infer an unspecified Hamiltonian.

3. Smearing, CAR and finite-time words
=====================================

Use unitary Fourier convention ||f||_2^2=integral |fhat(k)|^2 dk.
For Schwartz test functions f on the line define the top-row lattice vector
I_(R,a)f by Fourier components

 (I_(R,a)f)_j=R^(-1/2) fhat(k_j) r_top.                  (S7)

With the seam placed outside the fixed observation region this is the
Fourier version of the usual sampled, locally normalized edge smearing.
For smooth compactly supported f it converges to real-space samples
sqrt(a) f(a x) r_top; periodization and alias tails vanish. Thus it is not
a global zero-mode probe. The Fourier definition (S7) fixes the regulator
and suffices for the convergence statements below.

Because the mesh spacing is 1/R and its cutoff pi/a tends to infinity,

 <I f,I g> = (1/R) sum_j conjugate(fhat(k_j)) ghat(k_j)
               -> <f,g>.                               (S8)

Let C_(R,a)=1_(D_(R,a)<0) on the complete one-copy finite source. In the
original filled quasifree state, its two-point kernel obeys

 <I f,C_(R,a) exp(-itD_(R,a)) I g>
      -> integral_0^infinity conjugate(fhat(k)) ghat(k)
                              exp(itk) dk.             (S9)

Proof: first restrict to |k|<=K. Equation (S6) makes the replacement of the
actual source matrix element by 1_(k>0)exp(itk) uniformly small. The
remaining shifted Riemann sum tends to the integral; its jump at zero has
finite size and contributes O(1/R) for smooth band weights. Then control
both original and limiting tails by |fhat*g hat|, since the projection and
unitary have norm at most one. Schwartz decay makes these tails uniformly
small as K increases. This proves (S9), uniformly on bounded time intervals.
Polarization gives different test functions. The same argument without C
gives the unfilled CAR kernel and hence the complementary covariance.

The lattice annihilation operators a(I f) are bounded, with norm ||I f||.
Their CAR converge by (S8). The source state is gauge-invariant quasifree;
every fixed finite product of the charged operators, their adjoints and
their time translates is a finite Wick determinant made from the kernels
in (S8)-(S9). Therefore all such finite word expectations converge, with
nonzero limits when the corresponding continuum contraction is nonzero.
Disjoint real-space smearing supports have vanishing limiting CAR pairing.
The continuum generator is i partial_x (eigenvalue -k), a single chiral
fermion's one-particle motion. This proves a concrete local field response,
not merely a sequence of small eigenvalues.

This is convergence for the declared smeared CAR family in its quasifree
state. It does not classify every scaling observable of the full source,
construct an E8 spinor vertex, or establish a rank-eight local net. A
completed net reconstruction would require its own specified embeddings
and uniform estimates; none is silently imported here.

4. The number of channels is checked on the same source
======================================================

At p=0, h0^3=h0, rank h0=14 and trace h0=0 exactly. Hence

 spec h0={(-1)^7, 0^2, (+1)^7}.

The complete zero projector is

 1-h0^2 = |r_top><r_top|+|r_bottom><r_bottom|,

where r_bottom is (1,-1)/sqrt(2) on row y=0. On this two-dimensional space,

 P0 h'(0) P0 = diag(-1,+1)

in the top/bottom basis. The fourteen other branches are separated from
zero at p=0. Thus the local low-momentum scaling sector has one chiral
channel per edge, with opposite velocities. The eight transverse rows
are spatial rows, not eight independent fermionic species. This exact
count concerns the specified source/low-momentum limit; it is not a
no-go theorem for every admissible TFPT source or an exhaustive analysis
of unrelated high-momentum scaling prescriptions.

Eight copies would have eight channels by construction, but making those
copies would add the very source multiplicity that still needs a derivation.

5. Executed diagnostics and a control that distinguishes the limits
==================================================================

source_line_check.py imports TX, TY and SZ from the actual source. It uses
only its exact 16-by-16 Fourier strips, both r=1 and r=3, and the Gaussian
weight |fhat(k)|^2=exp(-k^2)/sqrt(pi), |k|<=6. The omitted Gaussian tails
are below 3e-17 at the continuum level and immaterial at the shown scale.
The numerical results are floating-point diagnostics, not interval proofs.

 R       a approximately    covariance error       response error at t=.7
 4       .0156201           .0354010                .03544 (worst r)
 8       .00781248          .0176482                .01765
 16      .00390624          .00881762               .00882
 32      .00195312          .00440800               .00441

The limiting filled weight is 1/2; the limiting time response is
.4423529524717418+.1821032094531042 i. Both holonomies approach these values.
The residual bound (S2) holds at every tested mesh point.

At fixed R=4, refining N from 1609 to 6434 leaves the r=3 minus r=1
occupation difference about .07080191512. Thus lattice refinement alone
does not remove the holonomy. Enlarging the physical circumference is the
new assumption doing work in the positive result.

6. How this joins the charged E8 target without conflating two theorems
=====================================================================

LOCAL_LIMIT_REVIEW.txt separately treats the already stipulated rank-eight
E8 lattice target with H_(delta,R)=(L0-delta.P)/R, delta=(1/4)^8.
Its root-field spectral measures tend to omega d omega; its charged
two-point function tends to tau^-2. Its actual C/J clock defects vanish
on fixed physical-energy windows, while they remain nonzero at finite R.
Finite separated vertex words have the plane limit. The two finite-circle
ground charges become indistinguishable for those words, not by a proof
identifying their global Hilbert-space states.

Those are positive conditional target results. Section 3 supplies a
source-faithful charged limit for ONE channel. Together they show that a
fixed-circle holonomy failure is not an unavoidable local obstruction.
They do not supply the missing map from the native boundary to eight
channels or the microscopic half-charge spinor with its cocycle and carry.

The actual first origin problem is therefore not to tune away a finite-circle
chemical shift. It is to derive the local source multiplicity, field
extension and their localization/time action from the original boundary.
SOURCE_AUDIT.txt records the actual first assumed finite carrier and the
Lean interfaces that already package their rank assumptions.

An internal eight-dimensional polarization, an infinite-dimensional
one-particle space such as L2(line) tensor C8, and an E8 lattice vertex
extension are different objects. A rank-eight GLOBAL Riesz projection
would produce finitely many modes, not eight propagating local fields.
An internal Riesz projection can help only after an intrinsic internal/local
factorization is proved. A symbol multiplicity bundle or a source-defined
local scaling sector is another possible form of the missing construction.

7. Proven boundary and open origin
=================================

Proven here, under the explicitly declared source and scaling:
 - same-source charged covariance AND time evolution converge locally;
 - the twist disappears locally while the response stays nonzero;
 - the source has one low-momentum chiral channel per edge;
 - the conditional E8 target's clock obstruction is finite-circle specific.

Still open:
 - why the raw P1/P2 boundary yields this source, its scaling and the selected
   multiplicity needed by the target;
 - the canonical carrier/Yukawa and local source/operator intertwiners;
 - native half-charge fields and common-time E8 completion;
 - one common 3+1-dimensional chiral, interacting and gravitational parent.

The standard strategy of constructing continuum correlations from lattice
fermions is established. The present contribution is the explicit audit of
this original source and of the earlier finite-circle conclusion. See
Osborne and Stottmeister, Conformal field theory from lattice fermions,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.13834 ; this paper is context, not a substitute
for (S1)-(S9). For the parent QWZ topological model see Qi, Wu and Zhang,
https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0505308 . No physical claim is promoted.
