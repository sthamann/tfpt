LOCAL LARGE-RADIUS REVIEW OF THE QUARTER-HOLONOMY E8 TARGET
20 September 2026

Verdict: PASS, WITH A STRICTLY LOCAL AND CONDITIONAL SCOPE.

The finite-circle obstruction in charged-source-time-audit-20260920 is real at
every finite radius, but it does not survive on fixed, separated, locally
normalised E8 root fields when R tends to infinity.  The charged response does
not disappear: its two-point function tends to 1/tau^2 and finite neutral
vertex words tend to the ordinary plane lattice correlators.  What disappears
is the O(1/R) chemical-frequency offset produced by the fixed quarter
holonomy.  Times of order R, fixed global modes, charge sectors growing with R,
and the global clock action still retain the holonomy.

This is a direct limit calculation on the already stipulated E8 target.  It is
not a new physical source, a derivation of eight channels, a microscopic
spinor field, an infinite-volume construction, a proof of bounded local-net
equivalence, or a closure of T1, T2 or T8.

2. Vague convergence of the local spectral measure
====================================================

The locally normalised weight-one root field has vacuum spectral measure

  mu_R,a=(1/R^2) sum_{n>=1} n delta_((n-a)/R).

For f in C_c^1([0,infinity)) let g(omega)=omega f(omega), h=1/R and
beta=1-a in [0,2].  With x_k=(k+beta)h,

  integral f d mu_R,a
    = h sum_{k>=0} g(x_k) + a h^2 sum_{k>=0} f(x_k).

The first term is a shifted Riemann sum.  Comparing each interval
[x_k,x_k+h] and accounting for the missing interval [0,beta h] gives

  |h sum g(x_k)-integral_0^infinity g(omega)domega|
    <= h ||g'||_1 + 2h ||g||_infinity.

Also

  h sum |f(x_k)| <= ||f||_1+h||f'||_1.

Therefore, uniformly for all five root values of a,

  |integral f d mu_R,a - integral_0^infinity omega f(omega)domega|
    <= (||g'||_1+2||g||_infinity+||f||_1)/R
       + ||f'||_1/R^2.                                  (1)

Thus mu_R,a converges vaguely to omega d omega.  This is a quantitative
compact-frequency statement, not only pointwise convergence of spectral
lines.

For a=1 the n=1 atom is at zero energy.  Its residue is exactly 1/R^2, so it
vanishes vaguely.  This is the local fate of the finite-circle state
V_{s,-1}|0>: it does not justify identifying the two finite-R ground vectors,
but its weight in this locally normalised two-point measure goes to zero.


3. Exact two-point function and its two inequivalent limits
============================================================

For coincident spatial position and tau>0,

  G_R,a(tau)
    = integral exp(-tau omega) d mu_R,a(omega)
    = exp(a tau/R)/(4R^2 sinh^2(tau/(2R))).              (2)

The geometric-series identity behind (2) is

  exp(a t) exp(-t)/(1-exp(-t))^2
    = sum_{n>=1} n exp(-(n-a)t),  t=tau/R.

At fixed physical tau,

  G_R,a(tau)
    = 1/tau^2 [1+a tau/R+(a^2/2-1/12)tau^2/R^2
                +O(R^-3)].                              (3)

The limit is the nonzero charged weight-one response 1/tau^2.  For a=0 the
leading finite-size error improves to O(R^-2); charged roots with a nonzero
have the expected O(R^-1) chemical correction.

The negative control is tau=cR.  Then

  G_R,a(cR)=R^-2 exp(ac)/(4 sinh^2(c/2)),

and its ratio to the plane expression 1/(cR)^2 is

  exp(ac)c^2/[4 sinh^2(c/2)],                            (4)

which is independent of R and generally not one.  More specifically, the
charged-to-neutral ratio at the same c is exp(ac), so the holonomy itself,
not only cylinder geometry, remains visible on this time scale.


4. Fixed finite vertex words at separated insertions
=====================================================

Let p_1,...,p_N be E8 lattice charges with sum p_i=0.  Fix a radial order and
pairwise separated Euclidean points w_i=tau_i-i x_i.  In the chosen cocycle
and branch convention the locally normalised cylinder word is

  G_R = epsilon_product
        exp[-sum_i tau_i(delta.p_i)/R]
        product_{i<j}
          [2R sinh((w_i-w_j)/(2R))]^(p_i.p_j).           (5)

All pairings are integers, and fixing the radial order fixes the cocycle and
branch.  Equation (5) follows directly from the lattice zero-mode algebra and
the exponential map; it does not assume a new source dynamics.

Put A=sum_i tau_i(delta.p_i), z_ij=w_i-w_j and m_ij=p_i.p_j.  Uniformly on a
compact configuration set away from coincident points,

  log(G_R/G_infinity)
    = -A/R + sum_{i<j} m_ij z_ij^2/(24R^2)+O(R^-4),

where

  G_infinity=epsilon_product product_{i<j}(w_i-w_j)^(p_i.p_j).             (6)

Hence every fixed neutral vertex word has the plane limit, with relative
error O(R^-1), or O(R^-2) when A=0.  This includes genuinely charged
intermediate fields.  For example the actual root triple

  p_1=s,
  p_2=(-1,-1,0,0,0,0,0,0),
  p_3=-(p_1+p_2)

has p_i.p_j=-1 for every pair and
delta.p_i=(1,-1/2,-1/2); it is not a neutral-current-only test.

Cartan currents follow by differentiating the same free-boson factors.  Away
from diagonals,

  [2R]^-1 coth(z/(2R)) = 1/z + z/(12R^2)+O(R^-4),

so any fixed finite number of Cartan derivatives preserves the limit and its
uniformity on compact separated configurations.


5. The two finite-circle ground charges are locally indistinguishable here
============================================================================

Let |q> be a fixed lattice-charge vacuum.  For a neutral word, the diagonal
matrix element differs from the q=0 value by the zero-mode character

  exp[sum_i (q.p_i)w_i/R],                               (7)

up to the already fixed cocycle convention.  It tends to one for fixed q and
fixed insertions.  In particular, q=0 and q=s give the same limiting neutral
vertex-word correlations even though they are orthogonal ground vectors at
every finite R.

The off-diagonal terms also vanish.  For a word of total charge
P=sum_i p_i, the conformal map gives an overall radius power

  R^(-|P|^2/2).                                         (8)

One way to see (8) is to combine the factors R^(-|p_i|^2/2) from local
primary normalisation with the pair factors: the total exponent is

  sum_i |p_i|^2/2 + sum_{i<j} p_i.p_j = |P|^2/2.

A matrix element between |0> and |s> requires P=+s or P=-s.  Since |s|^2=2,
it is O(R^-1).  Derivatives do not change the net-charge power.  Therefore,
for any R-independent normalised coefficients alpha,beta,

  |psi_R>=alpha|0>+beta|s>,

all fixed finite current/vertex-word expectations considered above converge
to the same plane-vacuum values: neutral cross terms vanish by charge
conservation, diagonal q=0,s values merge by (7), and the possible P=+/-s
cross terms vanish by (8).

This proves only word-by-word local indistinguishability on this algebraic
domain.  It does not prove equality of the two limiting states on a completed
von Neumann net, normality/quasi-equivalence, a unique infinite-volume GNS
vacuum, or the existence of a normalisable global charge-groundstate limit.
Those conclusions require a constructed net and uniform energy/locality
control.  Thus the old finite vacuum doubling is not by itself a local vacuum
doubling, but it has not been globally erased.


6. C/J defect on controlled energy windows
===========================================

Let U_g be an orthogonal E8 lattice lift.  With the ket convention of the
prior audit,

  U_g H_delta U_g^dagger=H_(g delta),

so, up to the equivalent transpose convention,

  H_R U_g-U_g H_R
    = R^-1 (g delta-delta).P U_g.                        (9)

On a fixed charge/mode sector this is O(R^-1).  A broader estimate holds on
physical energy windows that grow with R.  Let

  F_R(E)=1_(L0<=R E).

Since |P|^2<=2L0 and U_g preserves L0,

  ||(H_R U_g-U_g H_R)F_R(E)||
    <= |g delta-delta| sqrt(2E/R).                       (10)

For the actual C and J of charged-source-time-audit,

  |C delta-delta|=|J delta-delta|=1,

because delta=s/2, the images are roots orthogonal to s, and both delta and
its image have norm squared 1/2.

There is also a bound on the actual H_R cutoff K_R(E)=1_(H_R<=E).  The proved
dimensionless comparison L0<=2(1+H_delta)=2(1+R H_R) gives

  ||(H_R U_g-U_g H_R)K_R(E)||
    <= 2|g delta-delta| sqrt(1+R E)/R.                   (11)

Duhamel's formula multiplies the right sides of (10) or (11) by |t| for

  ||(exp(-itH_R)U_g-U_g exp(-itH_R))F||.

Thus the clock defect vanishes on each fixed physical-energy window, although
only as O(R^-1/2) when the window admits charges of order sqrt(R).  This is not
global operator-norm convergence: P is unbounded, and no bound is uniform for
t of order R.  At finite R the exact statements from the prior audit remain:
U_C and U_J do not commute with H_R, and they move |s> from energy 0 to 1/R.

8. Exact claim boundary
=======================

The earlier finite-circle statement

  H_delta != L0, two ground charges, fixed C/J noncommutation

remains exact.  What this review proves is the narrower implication

  fixed separated locally normalised fields + R to infinity
    => plane E8 vertex-word correlations and vanishing holonomy defect.

Accordingly, the finite-circle mismatch does not by itself obstruct local E8
time covariance on the decompactified line.  It also does not establish that
covariance from the TFPT source.  The first still-open causal edge is the same:
construct the rank-eight charged local target, its half-charge field and its
common time from the marked P1/P2 source, with uniform locality and energy
bounds.  No source winding has been changed, no fixed-R symmetry has been
asserted, and no T1--T8 or ledger status is upgraded.


Primary mathematical context
============================

The cylinder/plane calculation above is derived explicitly and needs no new
theorem.  For the stronger step from energy-bounded vertex fields to a local
conformal net, which is deliberately not claimed here, see Carpi, Kawahigashi,
Longo and Weiner, "From vertex operator algebras to conformal nets and back",
https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.01260.  For energy bounds under suitable unitary VOA extensions,
see Carpi and Tomassini, "Energy bounds for vertex operator algebra
extensions", https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.14097.  Those results state additional hypotheses;
they do not supply the missing TFPT microscopic extension or make this
word-level limit a full local-net theorem.
