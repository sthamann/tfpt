1. THE COUPLED-CELL TENSOR AND ITS TRANSFER

Let P_0=I and P_1,...,P_15 be Hermitian two-qubit Paulis, normalized by
Tr(P_a P_b)=4 delta_ab. Put c_0=1/2, c_a=1/sqrt(20) for a!=0 and
B_a=c_a P_a. The proposed isometry and parent rule are added assumptions;
they are not inferred from P1/P2 by this computation.

The Pauli completeness identity is
  sum_a P_a X P_a = 4 Tr(X) I.
Consequently
  E(X)=sum_a B_a X B_a^dagger=(X+Tr(X)I)/5,
  sum_a B_a^dagger B_a=I.
The entire operator basis is checked exactly. E has eigenvalues 1 on I and
1/5 on all 15 traceless directions. The periodic MPS therefore satisfies
  ||sum_word Tr(B_a1 ... B_an)|word>||^2
     =Tr(E^n)=1+15*5^(-n).
This trace is the trace of the 16-dimensional transfer superoperator.
The transfer correlation scale is xi=1/log(5) in lattice cells. Some particular
correlators can vanish or decay faster; this is the nontrivial transfer scale.

2. ALL-LENGTH GROUND SPACES AND OPEN ISOSPECTRALITY

Write the Pauli multiplication as e_a e_b=alpha(a,b)e_(a+b), with a,b in
V=F2^4 and normalized U(1) cocycle alpha. The positive c_a never vanish.
In each total-label sector t the allowed two-site vector is
  q_t^alpha = D_t^(-1/2) sum_a c_a c_(a+t) alpha(a,a+t)|a,a+t>,
  D_t=sum_a c_a^2 c_(a+t)^2.
Different t have disjoint support. Q_2=sum_t |q_t><q_t| is an ORTHOGONAL
rank-16 projector, and h=I-Q_2. For the full Pauli algebra this equals the
two-site MPS parent projector obtained from B_a.

For an open word define F_alpha by
  e_a1 ... e_an = F_alpha(a1,...,an)e_(a1+...+an).
Then U_n|word>=F_alpha(word)|word> is a diagonal UNITARY. If an adjacent pair
is changed while preserving its total label, associativity says that the
ratio of full word phases is the ratio of its local pair phases. Hence
  U_n Q_j^0 U_n^dagger=Q_j^alpha
simultaneously for every open bond. This proves complete open-chain spectral
equivalence for every n; it is not an extrapolation of finite spectra.

After dividing wavefunction coefficients by the positive product of c_a, the
open local constraints let us replace any adjacent pair by the identity and
its product, with the specified cocycle. Every allowed word amplitude is thus
  f(e_a1 ... e_an)
for one linear functional f on the 16-dimensional twisted group algebra A_alpha.
Conversely each such f satisfies every open constraint. This gives exactly 16
open ground states for n>=2. The injective MPS proof is equivalent for the
full Pauli algebra M4(C).

The cyclic closing bond additionally demands f(xy)=f(yx). It follows, for
directed rings n>=3, that
  dim ker H_ring=dim A_alpha/[A_alpha,A_alpha]=dim Z(A_alpha).
If e_a e_b=(-1)^(b(a,b)) e_b e_a, a central basis element has label in rad b.
Therefore
  dim ker H_ring=|rad b|=2^(4-rank_F2 b).
The full Pauli cocycle is nondegenerate and gives one state, represented by
the matrix trace. Rank two gives four; rank zero gives sixteen. The independent
64-form census gives 28 rank-four, 35 rank-two and one rank-zero form.

Important type correction: when the cocycle is removed, the algebra is C^16,
not M4(C). Sixteen linearly independent commuting 4x4 matrices cannot replace
the same Paulis. The valid comparison is between the two specified algebraic
parent constructions on the SAME 16-label physical cells, with different
virtual algebra representations. It is not the same bond-four injective MPS
with its multiplication phases erased inside M4(C).

The physical-to-virtual one-site map is invertible but NONUNITARY, because
c_0 differs from c_a. It preserves dimensions of constraint spaces, not
energies. The open spectral equivalence above uses the separate U_n proof.

3. AN EXACT SMALL CERTIFICATE FOR THE UNIFORM GAP

No 4096-dimensional diagonalization is needed. The preceding open-word
unitary removes alpha on three sites. Work in a fixed total-label sector
c=a+b+d. The two allowed projection ranges each have dimension 16.
Set w_a=c_a^2, D_0=1/10 and D_t=3/50 for t!=0. Their overlap matrix is
  M_c(t,u)=sqrt(w_(c+t)/D_t) w_(c+t+u) sqrt(w_(c+u)/D_u).
It is real symmetric and similar, via an invertible diagonal matrix, to
  L_c(t,u)=w_(c+t+u) w_(c+u)/D_u,
which has exact rational entries. This similarity is used only to compute
eigenvalues of the symmetric overlap, not to change a physical Hamiltonian.

Because w distinguishes only zero from nonzero labels, GL(4,2) relabeling
reduces all sectors to c=0 and c!=0. Their exact characteristic polynomials are
  c=0: (x-1)(x-1/6)^14(x-5/12),
  c!=0: (x-1)(x-1/6)^6(x+1/6)^7(x^2+x/60-5/72).
The last roots are (-1 +/- sqrt(1001))/120. Their absolute values are strictly
below 5/12 (already sqrt(1001)<49 suffices). Singular values of the symmetric
overlap are its absolute eigenvalues. There is one overlap singular value 1
in each of the 16 total sectors and the largest remaining singular value is
5/12. For two projections the nontrivial eigenvalues of 2-Q12-Q23 are
1 +/- singular_value. The exact three-site gap is therefore 7/12.

Writing h_j=I-Q_j, this gives
  {h_j,h_(j+1)} >= -(5/12)(h_j+h_(j+1)).
Nonoverlapping terms commute and their products are positive. Each h_j occurs
in at most two adjacent pairs, so
  H_n^2 >= H_n-(5/6)H_n=(1/6)H_n.
For n=3 on a directed ring, every pair is adjacent and the same cyclic
three-site estimate applies. Open n=2 is the single-projector case.
This establishes the stated size-independent lower bound with fixed norm-one
terms. It does NOT establish a gapless/chiral limit. Increasing n at fixed
local normalization keeps all positive energies above 1/6. A different scaling
or Hamiltonian would require a new derivation.

5. THE ADDITIONAL CONTINUOUS SYMMETRY AND THE CENTRAL OBSTRUCTION

For every U in U(4), conjugation fixes I and rotates the 15-dimensional
traceless Hermitian matrix space orthogonally. Since all traceless c_a are
equal, there is a physical unitary R(U)=1 plus Ad(U) such that
  U B_b U^dagger=sum_a R(U)_ab B_a.
Products telescope the intervening U^dagger U; taking their trace proves MPS
invariance. Unitarity preserves the two-site support and its orthogonal
projector, so the parent Hamiltonian has this symmetry too. The scalar center
acts trivially; the effective continuous group is PSU(4).

An exact additional U=diag((3+4i)/5,1,1,1) is checked on all 16 matrices.
The universal argument holds for arbitrary U, not just this example. The
virtual bond transforms as the fundamental 4; under PSU(4) it is projective.
This connects the phase-sensitive open edges with the standard one-dimensional
symmetry-protected-phase mechanism. It is an internal/global symmetry, not
a derived local gauge field or a chiral E8 edge anomaly.

Now consider the native Gaussian central generator z=iI_4. On amplitude
labels V=C4 viewed as R8 it acts as J, with J^2=-I. On neutral matrices
Ad_z(X)=X. A real-linear equivariant current candidate T:V -> M4(C) must satisfy
  T(Jv)=T(v), hence T(-v)=T(v), hence T=0.
The same obstruction holds on ANY finite tensor product of the neutral event
registers. In the sequential isometry, retaining the terminal C4 memory adds

This does NOT mean Pauli composition phases are unobservable. Relative word

A possible nonzero charged map would require relative sectors, for example
