UR.SOURCE.GRADED_FLAVOR_AUDIT.20260920
20 September 2026 -- exact bounded adjudication; PARTIAL

Question and pinned scope
=========================

Does the already calculated source cubic or an already source-derived
CAR/current operation supply the missing graded source-to-Yukawa map, a
symmetric family tensor, or the complementary mixing required to lift the
rank defect?  This audit uses pinned snapshots of:

* UR.COMPILER.VACUUM_CURRENT_CUBIC.06;
* the live draft UR.SOURCE.FLAVOR_ORIGIN.01;
* UR.COMPILER.CURRENT_DESCENDANT.16 and CURRENT_PRODUCT.21; and
* the implemented 96-dimensional finite triple v252.

The source files are not edited.  `source_pins.json` fixes their bytes and the
repository HEAD.  Cross-lane corrected-coframe and native-C/J quadratic-family
artifacts are separately byte-pinned.  `checker.py` performs 104 exact bounded
checks and writes `certificate.json`.  The stale theory-graph wrapper was not
rebuilt.

Verdict
=======

The latest source-flavor draft is correct on its narrow negative statement:
the ungraded native bosonic tensor

  C_(A,i)(B,j)(H,k) h_k = d_ABH epsilon_ijk h_k                  (1)

cannot be substituted directly for a local zero-derivative all-left-handed
Weyl Yukawa coefficient.  The internal E6 tensor d_ABH is symmetric, while
the family factor is alternating, so (1) is alternating under the combined
exchange (A,i)<->(B,j).  The Lorentz scalar of two left-handed Grassmann
fields is symmetric in the complete component labels.  Their contraction is
therefore zero.

This applies to the actual common Spin(10)-16 times family inclusion, not only
to two fields bearing the same informal species name.  Q, d^c, L and e^c are
coordinates of the common left-handed multiplet.  Declaring two independently
ordered field spaces could avoid this particular symmetrisation, but the
source has not derived such a split, its grading, or its map to the common
multiplet.  It cannot be inserted as a loophole.

The original cubic itself remains valid.  It is a bosonic three-current
correlator with ordered modes, where antisymmetry is supplied by the current
bracket.  The audit does not refute the unique E6 cubic, its 45 monomials, its
40 (16,16,10) slots, or its actual down/lepton coefficients.

But that negative result is not the end of the existing algebra.  The fixed
3+2 carrier already supplies a discrete weak-occupation mark S: +1 on the
weak doublets Q,L and -1 on the singlets u^c,d^c,e^c,nu^c.  On both actual
neutral-Higgs internal matrices d_H extracted from the 45 pinned monomials,

  {S,d_H}=0.                                             (1a)

Therefore S d_H is alternating internally.  Since A(h) is also alternating,

  Y_mark(h) = (S d_H) tensor A(h)                        (1b)

is symmetric on the complete internal-plus-family labels.  It also
anticommutes with S.  Thus an already source-marked algebraic repair exists;
an unknown independent symmetric family tensor is not required merely to fix
exchange parity.

This S is a weak-occupation mark, not CAR fermion parity, and not the
restriction of the v252 grading to the all-left-handed source multiplet.  All
source Weyl fields remain Grassmann odd.  In v252 the grading flips on
antiparticles, so the all-left subspace made from left particle fields and
charge-conjugated right fields has gamma=+ throughout.  The match to v252 is
only obtained after the typed conversion from the all-left coefficient to the
particle-space Hermitian Dirac operator, explained below.  No current-to-CAR
or boson-to-fermion intertwiner follows from S.

This is the strongest positive result of the audit.  It remains a conditional
operator descent: the current source has not yet shown that its bosonic mode
ordering becomes a local 3+1D fermion vertex with S inserted on the specified
leg.  Its rank also remains deficient.  A second positive algebraic structure,
the two-current contraction, repairs symmetry by a different route but has the
same one-family null direction.  No inspected source operation supplies the
nonzero complementary overlaps.

1. Exact exchange statement
===========================

For one Higgs-family vector h define

  A(h)_ij = epsilon_ijk h_k
          = [[0,h3,-h2],[-h3,0,h1],[h2,-h1,0]].       (2)

Then A(h)^T=-A(h), A(h)h=0, det A(h)=0 and

  adj A(h) = h h^T.                                  (3)

Let I=(A,i) and J=(B,j) be complete internal-plus-family indices.  For a
left-handed scalar bilinear

  B_IJ = psi_I^alpha psi_J^beta epsilon_(alpha beta),

Grassmann exchange and spinor-epsilon exchange each contribute a minus sign,
so B_JI=B_IJ.  But d_ABH A(h)_ij is alternating in I,J.  Summation therefore
gives zero pairwise, including when I and J label different Standard-Model
components inside the unified field.

This matches the standard two-component-field rule that the Yukawa tensor is
symmetric in its two left-handed fermion labels; see Dreiner, Haber and Martin,
"Two-component spinor techniques and Feynman rules", arXiv:0812.1594,
https://arxiv.org/abs/0812.1594.  The E6 tensor inventory and its physical
Yukawa uses are separately documented by Deppisch, "E6Tensors",
https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.05920.  These references support the field-theory
typing; the TFPT coefficient and rank tests are the calculations here and in
the pinned source certificate.

There is a necessary precision at this point.  After gauge breaking, a charged
Dirac mass is usually written as a rectangular block M_ch between the charge
-1 left-handed fields and the charge +1 left-handed conjugate fields.  M_ch
itself need not be symmetric.  The complete all-left-handed mass tensor is

  [[0,M_ch],[M_ch^T,0]],                              (3a)

which is symmetric.  Therefore an antisymmetric 3-by-3 A(h) is not forbidden
as a rectangular Dirac block.  What is forbidden is substituting the entire
native ordered-current tensor directly as the complete coefficient: its
swapped current ordering supplies the opposite sign, whereas (3a) requires
the transpose completion with the same coefficient.

The carrier mark supplies the algebraic sign needed for precisely this
completion.  On a doublet-to-singlet entry S contributes +, while on the
swapped singlet-to-doublet entry it contributes -.  That minus combines with
A(h)^T=-A(h), producing the same transposed coefficient required in (3a).
What remains open is whether the physical source-to-field map actually applies
this mark to the ordered current vertex.  The corrected auxiliary coframe gives
representation slots in which the resulting rectangular 3+1 block is
statistically consistent.  It does not generate its matrix elements.  The
pinned C/J-invariant quadratic action in fact factorizes, so natural
elimination of that pair produces no such mixing; Section 5 states the exact
limit.

2. Canonical marked one-vertex repair on the actual source slots
================================================================

The mark does not come from a continuous flavor fit.  For each actual Spin16
weight in the pinned E6 basis, take its five carrier occupations.  The parity
of the two weak occupations is odd exactly for Q and L and even exactly for
u^c,d^c,e^c,nu^c.  Choosing odd -> + and even -> - defines S, up to an overall
irrelevant sign.  There are eight states of each sign.

The down-type neutral Higgs slot is pinned index 12 and the up-type slot is
index 14.  Their actual symmetric 16-by-16 coefficient matrices d_d,d_u each
have rank 8 and only connect opposite S sectors.  The checker verifies from
the 45 monomials, without a guessed dictionary,

  d_H^T=d_H,  rank d_H=8,  {S,d_H}=0,
  (S d_H)^T=-S d_H.                                  (3b)

It also verifies the actual charges and colours: the down slot contains one
e^c-L pair and three colour-complementary Q-d^c pairs; the up slot contains
one nu^c-L pair and three colour-complementary Q-u^c pairs.  Their
hypercharges sum respectively to +1/2 and -1/2, cancelled by the selected
neutral Higgs component.  This rules out a dimension-only identification.

Combining (2) and (3b),

  Y_mark(h)^T = Y_mark(h),
  {S,Y_mark(h)}=0.                                   (3c)

The relation to v252 requires care about the spaces.  On the all-left Weyl
space

  V_AL = V_L plus V_(R^c),

a complex rectangular charged block M_ch is completed as the symmetric
coefficient

  C_AL = [[0,M_ch],[M_ch^T,0]].                      (3c1)

After the ordinary charge-conjugation/relabeling R^c -> R, the v252 particle
Hilbert space is

  H_particle = H_L plus H_R,

and its Hermitian Dirac operator is instead

  D_particle = [[0,M_ch],[M_ch^dagger,0]].           (3c2)

With gamma_particle=diag(+I,-I), equation (3c2) is Hermitian and
{gamma_particle,D_particle}=0.  The source mark S becomes this +left/-right
label after relabeling, but it was not v252 gamma on V_AL: v252 assigns + also
to the R antiparticle states used for R^c.  The checker constructs both
transpose and adjoint completions explicitly.  This is a typed algebraic
conversion, not a derived source/96D intertwiner.

The repair preserves rank.  For generic h,

  rank Y_mark = rank(d_H) rank(A(h)) = 8*2 = 16 of 48. (3d)

Every individual charged species block therefore still has family rank at
most two.  S changes exchange type and grading, not the missing singular
value or mass hierarchy.

Conditional one-spurion strengthening.  Assume additionally that h is the
only commuting family triplet spurion, the coefficient is defined for every
h != 0, exactly SU(3)-equivariant, and has exact charge +1 under a continuous
U(1) with h -> exp(i theta)h.  At h=r e3 the SU(2) stabilizer fixes exactly two lines in
the space of two-lower-index bilinear coefficients.  Covariantly these are

  f(h^dagger h) epsilon_ijk h_k,
  g(h^dagger h) bar(h)_i bar(h)_j.                  (3e)

The first has U(1) grade +1 and is A(h); the second is symmetric but has grade
-2.  Neutral radial functions of h^dagger h do not change those grades.
Thus exact grade +1 retains only A(h), and every such one-spurion coefficient
has rank at most two.

This is deliberately conditional.  The charges +1 and -2 coincide modulo
three, so SU(3) triality alone does not exclude the symmetric line.  An
independent charged spurion, a distinct derivative jet, a nonlocal field, or
a coefficient not uniquely defined at nonzero h lies outside the lemma.
Singular behavior only at h=0 does not evade the nonzero-orbit stabilizer
argument.  The checker proves the two-dimensional stabilizer fixed space and
both charge statements; it does not promote the extra U(1) or the one-spurion
premise to source-derived facts.  The independent 12-check derivation in
`joint/ONE_BACKGROUND.md` reaches the same conditional conclusion.

3. Positive two-vertex contraction, and its exact limit
========================================================

The affine source has an invariant current two-point form.  Contracting two
copies of the family vertex through a symmetric family propagator P gives

  T(h;P) = A(h) P A(h)^T.                            (4)

Equation (4) is symmetric for every P=P^T, so it has the correct exchange
type for a left-handed bilinear.  With the canonical orthonormal current
metric P=I,

  T(h;I) = (h^T h) I - h h^T.                       (5)

But T(h;P)h=0 and det T(h;P)=0.  A different vector k at the second vertex
does not help a single contraction:

  det[A(h) P A(k)^T]=0,                              (6)

because each exterior factor has rank at most two.  Thus the positive
two-vertex possibility repairs statistics but leaves one exactly massless
family.  For h=k and P=I the two nonzero eigenvalues remain equal in the
ordinary non-isotropic case.

The obstruction survives an arbitrary matrix-valued internal propagator and
same-vertex resummation.  For every F,

  [A(h) F A(h)^T] h = 0,
  [A(h) + A(h) F A(h)] h = 0.                       (6a)

Thus any diagram whose external family legs retain the same A(h) has the same
massless direction, regardless of what is resummed between them.  A genuinely
different external vertex can evade (6a); equation (7) is the smallest exact
witness.

The rank obstruction is not universal to every sum of source channels.  Two
noncollinear contractions can be full rank.  The exact witness

  A(e1)A(e1)^T + A(e2)A(e2)^T = diag(1,1,2)          (7)

has determinant 2.  Equation (7) is a target condition, not a derived TFPT
Yukawa: the current source must independently produce both backgrounds,
their relative weight and phase, the fermionic chirality flip, and the same
3+1D local scaling limit.  Repeating the same h with different propagators
cannot help, because A(h) remains the common exterior factor.

The affine current identities used by the pinned source are standard current
algebra.  The original reference is Knizhnik and Zamolodchikov, "Current
Algebra and Wess-Zumino Model in Two Dimensions",
https://strings.itp.ac.ru/Papers/knizhnik1984.pdf.  That theory supplies the
bosonic current contraction; it does not itself turn (4) into a TFPT fermion
mass term.

4. Why canonical CAR, a derivative, and the native current descendant do not close it
======================================================================================

The canonical CAR two-point tensor pairs a family 3 with its conjugate bar3,
delta_i^barj.  It is the kinetic/current channel.  A lower-lower delta_ij on
3 tensor 3 is not SU(3)-invariant: the centre acts on it with charge two.
Consequently the CAR metric alone is not the missing holomorphic symmetric
family tensor.

The native A3 current zero modes are grading-even.  Embedded on left and
right particle chiral copies they commute with gamma.  They cannot themselves
be the v252 Dirac/Yukawa block.  The marked tensor (1b), once converted by
(3c1)-(3c2), passes the particle-space oddness test, but using it as a source
interaction requires a current-to-field descent that inserts S on the vertex;
this physical operation is not supplied by a current zero mode or its
polynomial.  S is also distinct from CAR fermion parity: all components of the
Weyl multiplet are fermion-odd, while S takes both signs inside that multiplet.
A relative
derivative can also reverse an exchange parity by contributing relative
momentum, but it vanishes in the zero-momentum mass limit.  It defines a
derivative interaction, not a zero-derivative Yukawa.

CURRENT_DESCENDANT.16 and CURRENT_PRODUCT.21 provide a real positive result:
an exact Sym^2(C4)=10 descendant and a response operator

  G = (3/4) I_50 + (9/4) P_10.                       (8)

This still does not select a coherent symmetric tensor.  G is an element of
End(bar5 tensor Sym^2(C4)), not a vector in Sym^2(C4).  The SU(4) central
element iI acts as -I on Sym^2(C4), so an SU(4)-invariant coherent vector in
that 10 must be zero.  Restricting 4=3+1 only after choosing an anchor gives

  Sym^2(4) = Sym^2(3) + 3 + 1,                       (9)

but neither a vector in Sym^2(3) nor the cross-term 3 is selected by (8).

An individual decomposable source ray v v^T also cannot serve as the desired
complement.  Write v=(s,t) in 3+1 form.  Its blocks are

  S=s s^T,  b=s t,  M=t^2,

and, when M is nonzero,

  S - b b^T/M = 0.                                  (10)

The full 4-by-4 tensor remains rank one.  The uniformly averaged frame in
(8) is a quadratic response operator and does not provide a coherent sum of
rays with phases.  Choosing such a sum would be a new source-state rule.

5. Complement theorem and the corrected pair's exact dynamical limit
=====================================================================

For the generous directed block used in the live draft,

  M_full = [[A(h),b],[c^T,M]],  M != 0,

the exact identities are

  det M_full = -(h^T b)(c^T h),
  det[A(h)-b c^T/M] = -(h^T b)(c^T h)/M.             (11)

Both overlaps must be nonzero.  Multiplying the reduced determinant by M
recovers the full determinant, so a phase residing only in M cancels from
this full finite-block determinant.  The checker independently reproduces
(11).  No pinned native current/CAR construction selects b and c, and the
single Bell ray fails by (10).  More strongly, deriving either overlap only
through the old exterior family vertex cannot work:

  b=A(h)u  implies h^T b=0,
  c^T=v^T A(h) implies c^T h=0.                     (11a)

The complementary channel must therefore couple outside the image selected by
the same A(h), or use a genuinely independent external source vertex.

The corrected auxiliary coframe makes the pair kinematically admissible: its
signed hypercharges are (-1,+1), rather than the old color-like
(-1/3,+1/3), and it preserves the E8 split with a positive permutation-
invariant energy example.  This is a representation result only.
The two corrected ten-dimensional clock lifts do not preserve the fixed Y10
marking.  They can therefore be carried in the same dictionary, but are not
thereby internal hypercharge-preserving time evolutions.

For both coframes, the pinned C/J-invariant quadratic energy family is exactly

  V_W = blockdiag(a G_E8, M_2),                     (12)

because the common invariant symmetric form on E8 is one-dimensional and the
native C/J action has no fixed E8 vector that could form a cross block.  The
only declared cosine is pair-only.  The corresponding restricted Hamiltonian
therefore has the factor form

  H = H_E8 tensor I + I tensor H_pair.              (13)

Let P=I_E8 tensor |vac><vac|, where |vac> is an actual eigenvector of
H_pair, and Q=I-P.  Then [H,P]=0 and

  Q H P = 0.                                        (14)

Natural Feshbach/Schur elimination of this massive pair consequently produces
no E8-pair self-energy and no b,c mixing in the pinned restricted action.
This does not say that b,c vanish in every field basis, nor does it exclude a
source-derived nonfactorizing higher interaction.  The checker includes an
explicit cross-term control with QHP nonzero to show exactly what additional
operator structure would change (14).

6. Exact missing operator contract
==================================

The remaining question is now small enough to state as an acceptance gate.
A successful source-to-Yukawa descent must first realize the now-explicit
candidate Y_mark=(S d_H) tensor A(h) as a local fermionic operator from the
pinned common source, without taking Yukawa matrices as inputs.  It must then
lift its rank by one of the following:

  A. a source-selected sum of at least two independent two-vertex channels

       Y = sum_r A(h_r) P_r A(k_r)^T,

     with the actual source weights/phases and det Y != 0; or

  B. an actual complementary graded channel with blocks b,c,M satisfying
     (h^T b)(c^T h) != 0.

Both routes require the ordered-sector descent just described: the bosonic
current mode ordering must become one rectangular chiral block, S must supply
the certified sign, and the source reality/adjoint must produce its transpose
completion.  Merely writing S beside the finite tensor does not prove that
the 3+1D source dynamics executes this operation.

In either route the same constructed operator must pass all five tests:

  1. EXCHANGE: Y is symmetric on the complete left-handed component labels
     (already passed algebraically by Y_mark);
  2. GRADE: after the typed conversion (3c1)-(3c2), the Hermitian particle
     operator anticommutes with the v252 chirality grading (passed
     algebraically; no source/96D intertwiner inferred);
  3. REPRESENTATION: it intertwines the actual global gauge/family actions;
  4. MASS: its zero-momentum family block has nonzero determinant and the
     required nondegenerate singular spectrum; and
  5. PROVENANCE: backgrounds, propagator, relative phases and normalization
     are generated by the common source/state/time, with a local 3+1D limit.

The smallest decisive next test is therefore not another mass fit.  It is to
derive a nonfactorizing marked kernel from the common source and show either
two independent external family channels or QHP != 0 for the actual massive
pair.  Then evaluate the five quantities above and ablate each independent
channel.  Failure of the typed grade, determinant, or QHP test stops the route
before any CKM/PMNS or phase claim.

Scope
=====

Exact under the pinned finite tensors and stated current-module premises:
the canonical marked one-vertex repair (1a)--(3d), exchange parity, the
two-vertex identities, same-external-leg rank obstruction, response-object
typing, actual colour/charge slots, the transpose-to-adjoint v252 conversion,
the corrected auxiliary charges, and QHP=0 for the restricted factorized
quadratic action.  Conditional/open: physical marked current-to-field descent,
rank-lifting source channel, nonfactorizing chirality-changing interaction,
selection of multiple backgrounds or complement, locality, measure,
continuum and observed couplings.  No T1--T8 gate is closed, no ledger/paper
promotion is made, and no complete TFPT/TOE claim follows.
